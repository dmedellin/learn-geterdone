@art look-alike-pair
Two patients. Same name, same birth year, same town. Different people.

@art thread-of-identity
Compare their fields and you merge them. Meanwhile one patient who moved looks like two. Every attribute can change, and the person stays the same.

@quote text="The model must define what it means to be the same thing." cite="Evans, Domain-Driven Design, Chapter 5"
That's an entity. The model must define what it means to be the same thing.
