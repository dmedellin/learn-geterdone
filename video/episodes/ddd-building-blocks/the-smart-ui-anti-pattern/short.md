@words text="An anti-pattern he recommends"
Eric Evans names an anti-pattern, and then recommends it.

@art screen-apps
The Smart UI puts every rule in the screen that needs it. For simple data entry, with a team new to modelling, it's quick and it's right.

@art rule-copies
The cost: one bike-share fare rule, copied into three screens, means three edits every time it changes.

@art fork-road
And there's no road back. It's a fork, not a starting point.
