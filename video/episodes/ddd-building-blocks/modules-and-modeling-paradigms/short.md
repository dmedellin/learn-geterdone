@words text="4/9 or 7/9"
Same seed bank, same nine dependencies. Packaged one way, four stay inside their modules. The other way, seven.

@art tiered-split
The first splits every concept into a logic package and a data package. Every crossing is a concept reaching its own data.

@art concept-modules
The second packages by concept. Evans's rule: if your model tells a story, the modules are chapters.
