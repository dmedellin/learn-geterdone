@art estimate-range
Two facts about your team can halve your estimate, or nearly double it.

@math items="strong team: 100 × 0.76 × 0.71 ≈ 54@strong team;;weak team: 100 × 1.34 × 1.42 ≈ 190@weak team"
Start from a hundred staff months. A strong team of programmers and analysts makes it about fifty four. A weak team makes it about a hundred and ninety.

@words text="An estimate is a range"
So give a range, and tighten it as you learn about the team and the product.
