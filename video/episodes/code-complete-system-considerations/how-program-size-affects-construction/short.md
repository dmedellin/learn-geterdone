@art team-web
Double your team, and the conversations more than double.

@math items="paths = n · (n − 1) / 2@n times n minus one;;5 people → 10 · 10 people → 45@forty five"
Each pair of people is one path. That's n times n minus one, halved. Five people have ten paths. Ten people have forty five.

@words text="That's why large projects write documents"
Every path is talk, and a chance to be misunderstood. That's why large projects formalise communication.
