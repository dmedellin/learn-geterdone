@art quarter-shrink
Double the pieces, and this error falls to exactly a quarter.

@math items="T₄ − 1/3 = 1/96@four pieces;;T₈ − 1/3 = 1/384@With eight;;error = h²/6@h squared over six"
Use the trapezoid rule on t squared, from zero to one. With four pieces it's high by one ninety sixth. With eight, by one over three hundred and eighty four. The error is exactly h squared over six.

@words text="halve h → the error divides by 4"
Halve the width, and h squared falls to a quarter. Left and right sums only manage a half.
