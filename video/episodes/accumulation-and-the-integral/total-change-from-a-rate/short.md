title: Rate times time, and twice the answer
---

@art end-rate-box
A tap opens slowly. Its rate at the end, times the time, gives you twice the real total.

@math items="rate 2t for 2 minutes@two t litres;;end rate × time:  4 × 2 = 8@The rate at the end;;exact total:  4@the real total"
The tap delivers two t litres per minute, for two minutes. The rate at the end is four, and four times two is eight. But the real total is four.

@words text="The rate was 4 only at the very end"
The rate was four only at the last instant. At the start, it was zero.
