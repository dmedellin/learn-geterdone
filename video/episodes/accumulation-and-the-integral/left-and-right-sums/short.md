You can know the gap between two estimates before you compute either one.

@math items="Rₙ − Lₙ = h·(f(b) − f(a))@right sum minus;;t²,  n = 4:  (1/4)·(1 − 0) = 1/4@For t squared"
The right sum minus the left sum is the width, times the change in the rate from end to end. Every rate in the middle cancels. For t squared from zero to one, in four pieces, that's a quarter.

@words text="15/32 − 7/32 = 1/4"
And sure enough, fifteen thirty seconds minus seven thirty seconds is a quarter.
