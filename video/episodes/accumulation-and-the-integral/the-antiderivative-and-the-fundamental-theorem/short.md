@art area-under-parabola
Here's how to get an exact total without adding up a single piece.

@math items="(t³/3)′ = t²@t cubed over three;;F(1) − F(0) = 1/3@F of one"
The rate is t squared, from zero to one. Find a function whose derivative is t squared: t cubed over three. Then take F of one minus F of zero. That's a third.

@words text="7/32,  35/128,  155/512  …  → 1/3"
Every sum you could compute was closing in on that third. One subtraction lands right on it.
