@art halving-errors
Double the pieces, and the error halves. Almost. But not quite.

@math items="4 → 8:  44/23 ≈ 1.913@forty four;;8 → 16:  92/47 ≈ 1.957@ninety two"
For t squared from zero to one, the error ratio from four pieces to eight is forty four twenty thirds. From eight to sixteen, it's ninety two forty sevenths. Below two, and creeping toward it.

@words text="Exactly 2 only for a straight line"
Only a straight line rate gives a ratio of exactly two, every time.
