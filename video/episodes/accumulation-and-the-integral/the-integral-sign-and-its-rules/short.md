title: The integral of a product, refuted in one line

@art product-mismatch
Is the integral of a product the product of the integrals? One example says no.

@math items="∫₀¹ t·t dt = ∫₀¹ t² dt = 1/3@t times t;;∫₀¹ t dt = 1/2@t alone;;1/2 · 1/2 = 1/4@a half times a half"
From zero to one, the integral of t times t is the integral of t squared, a third. The integral of t alone is a half. And a half times a half is a quarter.

@words text="1/3 ≠ 1/4"
A third isn't a quarter. Sums and constants pass through the sign. Products don't.
