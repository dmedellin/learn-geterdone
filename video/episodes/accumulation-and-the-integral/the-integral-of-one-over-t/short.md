Try to integrate one over t with the power rule, and you'll divide by zero.

@math items="L₄ ≈ 0.7595@left sum;;R₄ ≈ 0.6345@the right sum;;total ≈ 0.693147@sits between"
So use the sums instead. From one to two, in four pieces, the left sum is about zero point seven six, and the right sum about zero point six three. The total sits between: about zero point six nine three.

@words text="∫₁² dt/t = ln 2"
That number is l n two, the natural log of two. And it isn't a fraction at all.
