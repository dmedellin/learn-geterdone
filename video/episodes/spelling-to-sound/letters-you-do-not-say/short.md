title: Silent letters sit in six places

@art six-places
Knee, write, climb. Silent letters aren't random. They sit in six places.

@list items="kn  ·  knee@K-n|wr  ·  write@w-r|mb  ·  climb@M-b|mn  ·  autumn@m-n|l  ·  walk, calm@the l"
K-n at the start says n, as in knee. And w-r says r, as in write. M-b at the end drops the b, and m-n drops the n, as in autumn. And the l is silent in walk and calm.

@words text="19 of 19" sub="one letter drops in every word"
In the lab, those six places are right on all nineteen words.
