I before e, except after c. It's wrong about four times in ten.

@words text="36 of 58" sub="62.1%"
On the fifty-eight words in the lab with i-e or e-i, the saying is right on thirty-six. That's sixty-two percent.

@words text="science · efficient · species" sub="ie after c"
And the c part is the weakest. Science, efficient and species all have i-e after a c.

@words text="believe" sub="words said with ee: 14 of 18"
Keep the rule for words said with ee, like believe, and it's right on fourteen of eighteen.
