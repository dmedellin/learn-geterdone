@art add-e
Hop becomes hope, with one letter you never say.

@words text="hop → hope · bit → bite · tub → tube" sub="the vowel says its name"
The silent e makes the vowel say its own name. Bit, bite. Tub, tube. In the lab, it works on a hundred and thirty-one of a hundred and forty words.

@math items="have, give@Have and give;;come, some, love, none@Come, some;;lose, move, prove@Lose, move"
The nine that break it are everyday words. Have and give. Come, some, love and none. Lose, move and prove.
