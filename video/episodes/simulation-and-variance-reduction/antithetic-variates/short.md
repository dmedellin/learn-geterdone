@art mirror-pair
Pairing each random draw with its opposite can make variance vanish, or double it.

@art step-monotone
If the quantity rises with U, U and one minus U pull opposite ways. In the lesson's example, every pair mean is exactly a half, with zero variance.

@art v-symmetric
But if it's symmetric about a half, both halves give the same number. Two draws, no better than one.
