@art four-for-half
Doubling your simulation runs does not double your precision.

@math items="SE = σ/√n@sigma over root n;;SE(n) / SE(4n) = 2@exactly two"
The standard error of an average is sigma over root n. So quadrupling n divides it by exactly two.

@art narrowing-band
Half the width costs four times the runs. One more decimal digit costs a hundred times. That's the rate, and buying draws can't beat it.
