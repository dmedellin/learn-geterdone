title: Three firms, same profit rate, very different exploitation

@art three-capitals
Three firms all earn twenty per cent. Their workers' unpaid labour varies fourfold.

@math items="300c + 200v:  s′ = 50%@fifty;;450c + 50v:  s′ = 200%@two hundred"
Each has five hundred of capital and makes a hundred. With two hundred of wages, the rate of surplus value is fifty per cent. With fifty of wages, two hundred per cent.

@words text="p′ = 20% for all three"
The rate of profit divides by the whole capital, so the exploitation can't be seen in it.
