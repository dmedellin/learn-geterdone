title: Two firms exploit equally. One earns more. Why?
---

@art two-businesses
Two firms exploit their workers exactly equally. One earns ten per cent, the other eight and a third.

@math items="A:  1,000 / 10,000 = 10%@nine thousand;;B:  1,000 / 12,000 = 8⅓%@eleven"
Both pay a thousand in wages and get a thousand of surplus value. A works with nine thousand of constant capital, B with eleven.

@words text="Business ability, or exploitation?"
Marx says the gap persuades the capitalist that his profit comes from his own skill, not from unpaid labour.
