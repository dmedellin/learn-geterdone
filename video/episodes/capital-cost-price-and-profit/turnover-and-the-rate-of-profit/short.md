title: Same surplus, double the profit rate: turnover

@art twice-and-once
Two capitals make the same profit in a year. One shows twice the rate.

@math items="A:  40 on 100 advanced = 40%@forty per cent;;B:  40 on 200 advanced = 20%@twenty per cent"
Engels' capital A advances a hundred and turns it over twice. B advances two hundred, once. Both make forty. But A's rate is forty per cent, and B's twenty per cent.

@words text="Advanced once, used twice"
The rate is on the capital advanced, not the capital turned over.
