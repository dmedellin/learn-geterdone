title: Exploitation rises, profit rate falls: how?
---

@art overshoot
Exploitation rises by half, and the profit rate falls. Here's how.

@math items="80c + 20v + 20s:  s′ = 100%,  p′ = 20%@eighty c;;90c + 10v + 15s:  s′ = 150%,  p′ = 15%@ninety c"
Eighty c, twenty v, twenty s: a hundred per cent, and twenty per cent profit. Ninety c, ten v, fifteen s: a hundred and fifty per cent, and only fifteen.

@words text="p′ = s′ · v / C"
The share of wages halved, which outweighed the rise in exploitation.
