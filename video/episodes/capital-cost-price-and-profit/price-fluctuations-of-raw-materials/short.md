@art cotton-doubles
Cotton doubles, and you pass every penny on. Your profit rate still falls.

@math items="before:  100 / 500 = 1/5@a fifth;;after:  100 / 600 = 1/6@a sixth"
Marx's capital makes a hundred on five hundred: a fifth. Cotton doubles, adding a hundred. Sell at the new value, and the profit's still a hundred, but now on six hundred: a sixth.

@words text="Same surplus, larger capital"
And if the price can't rise at all, the whole profit is gone.
