title: Sell below value, and still make a profit
---

@art price-range
You can sell something below its value, and still make a profit.

@math items="value £600;  cost price £500@six hundred;;sell at £560:  profit £60@sixty"
Marx's commodity is worth six hundred, but cost its owner only five hundred. The surplus value cost him nothing. Sell at five hundred and sixty, and he makes sixty, while selling forty below value.

@words text="Practical playroom"
That gap between cost and value is where, Marx says, competition lives.
