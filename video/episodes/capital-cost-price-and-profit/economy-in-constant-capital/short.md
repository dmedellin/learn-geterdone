@art same-number
Save twenty pounds with a better boiler, or by skipping the guard rails. Same result.

@math items="400c + 100v + 100s:  20%@twenty per cent;;c − £20:  100/480 = 5/24@five twenty fourths"
Marx's capital earns twenty per cent. Cut constant capital by twenty pounds, either way, and the rate becomes five twenty fourths.

@words text="A saving in buildings"
The rate of profit can't tell a better machine from a room too small to breathe in. That's Marx's point.
