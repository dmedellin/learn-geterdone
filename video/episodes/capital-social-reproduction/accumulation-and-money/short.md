If every capitalist saves up at once, who's left to buy?

@math items="hoarders sell 500;  investors buy 0@five hundred;;500 unsold@sold to nobody"
To invest, Marx says, you first sell your surplus and keep the money. Let every accumulating capitalist hoard at once, and five hundred of surplus product is sold to nobody.

@words text="A balance is an accident"
It clears only if others are spending older hoards, in the same amount, at the same time.
