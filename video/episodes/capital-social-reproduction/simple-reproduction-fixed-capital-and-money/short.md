@art two-sections
No growth, no credit, no change. Marx still finds a crisis.

@math items="wear hoarded 200;  renewals 100@two hundred;;gap:  100 − 200 = −100@a hundred"
Some capitalists hoard their machines' wear as money. Others spend old hoards on new machines. Hoard two hundred and renew only a hundred, and a hundred of means of production has no buyer.

@words text="Balanced in value, still unsold"
The scheme balances in value, and still the product doesn't clear.
