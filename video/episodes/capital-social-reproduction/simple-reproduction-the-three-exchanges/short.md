@art between-departments
Machine makers can't eat machines. So who feeds them?

@math items="I(v + s) = 1000 + 1000 = 2000@two thousand;;IIc = 2000@department two"
Department one's workers and capitalists spend two thousand of income on consumer goods. Department two needs two thousand of means of production to replace what it used up. They swap.

@words text="I(v + s) = IIc"
That's Marx's condition for simple reproduction. Break it, and part of one department's product has no buyer.
