In Marx's growth scheme, consumer goods industries don't choose their investment. It's computed for them.

@math items="I's demand on II:  1000 + 100 + 500 = 1600@sixteen hundred;;II must add:  1600 − 1500 = 100c + 50v@a hundred"
Department one accumulates half its surplus. It spends sixteen hundred on consumer goods. Department two's c is fifteen hundred, so it must add a hundred, and fifty of wages.

@words text="If things are to proceed normally"
Give it too little surplus value, and the year can't balance.
