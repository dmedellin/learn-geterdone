@art coal-two-ways
Is coal a means of production? Marx says that depends on where it's burned.

@words text="Boiler: Dept I · Grate: Dept II"
Under a mill's boiler, it's used up making something else, so it's department one. In a kitchen grate, it goes into a household, so it's department two.

@art two-departments
Society's product splits in two, by use, not by what a thing is. That one line is all Marx's question needs.
