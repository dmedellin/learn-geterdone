Five survey questions. The pilot said one idea. The real data said two.

@words text="How do teams learn of failures?"
Accelerate's authors asked five items about how teams learn of failures: from people like customers, or from systems like alerts and monitoring.

@words text="Proactive failure notification"
In the larger data, the tests split them in two. Notification from systems, proactive failure notification, predicted software delivery performance. One question would have hidden that.
