@art point-cloud
The algorithm that found Accelerate's high performers had no idea what good meant.

@words text="Four measures in, groups out"
The authors fed four delivery measures into a clustering algorithm. It groups teams that sit close together, and nothing more.

@art three-clusters
Out came three groups, which differ on all four measures. Calling them high, medium and low was the researchers' reading afterwards, not the algorithm's.
