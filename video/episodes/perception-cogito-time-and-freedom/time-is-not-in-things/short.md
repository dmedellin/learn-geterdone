@art witness-bank
Time flows like a river. Or does it?

@math items="bank: past w5–w7, future w1–w3@bank;;boat: past w1–w3, future w5–w7@boat"
Watch the same water from the bank, and the water gone by is past. From a boat, the water behind you is. Same water, opposite answers.

@words text="Time presupposes a view of time."
Take the observer away and nothing is past or future at all. Merleau-Ponty: time presupposes a view of time.
