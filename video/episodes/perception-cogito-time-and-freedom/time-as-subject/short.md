Are you something that lives in time? Merleau-Ponty says it's stranger than that.

@words text="We are saying that time is someone"
Time isn't arrayed before you, like a spectacle. Time is someone. You are the passing itself.

@words text="What does not pass in time is the passing of time itself"
Like a fountain, whose water changes while its form stays. What does not pass in time is the passing of time itself.
