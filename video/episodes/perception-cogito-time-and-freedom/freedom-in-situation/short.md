@art weighted-past
Are you free, or shaped by your past? Merleau-Ponty refuses the choice.

@words text="never determinism and never absolute choice"
There is never determinism and never absolute choice. I am never a thing, and never bare consciousness.

@words text="We choose our world and the world chooses us."
Twenty years of a habit has weight, but it isn't a fate. I'm free by means of my motives, not in spite of them. We choose our world and the world chooses us.
