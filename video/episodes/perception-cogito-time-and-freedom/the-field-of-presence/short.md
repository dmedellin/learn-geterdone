title: The moment just past is not a memory

@art pebble-water
Is the moment that just passed a memory? Merleau-Ponty, after Husserl, says no.

@math items="C present: B at depth 1, A at depth 2@depth one"
It's still held, sinking. With C present, B is held at depth one, and A at depth two, through B.

@words text="as I see a pebble through the mass of water which moves over it"
You don't recall A from a copy. You see it through what came after, as you see a pebble through the water moving over it.
