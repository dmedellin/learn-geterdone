@art reading-lines
When you read I think, therefore I am, whose thought is it?

@words text="One thinks, therefore one is."
Reached through words, it's anyone's. Merleau-Ponty says its true formula would be: one thinks, therefore one is.

@words text="a tacit cogito, myself experienced by myself"
Beneath it lies a tacit cogito, myself experienced by myself. It isn't words yet. It speaks as we sing when we are happy.
