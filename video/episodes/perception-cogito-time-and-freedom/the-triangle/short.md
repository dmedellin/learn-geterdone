title: The proof that isn't in the definition of a triangle

@art parallel-apex
No definition of a triangle tells you its angles make two right angles.

@math items="60 + 50 + 70 = 180°@sixty plus fifty"
You have to draw a parallel through one corner. Then, with angles of sixty and seventy, you see sixty plus fifty plus seventy lying along one line.

@words text="The construction is a gesture"
Merleau-Ponty says the construction is a gesture. The subject of geometry is a motor subject.
