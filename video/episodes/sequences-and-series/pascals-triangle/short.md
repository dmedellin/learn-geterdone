@art delegations
Three people. How many ways to pick a team of two?

@words text="{A,B}   {A,C}   {B,C}" sub="3 ways"
A and B, A and C, B and C. Three ways. Teams of one: three. Nobody: one. Everybody: one.

@math items="1   3   3   1@one, three, three, one"
One, three, three, one. That's row three of Pascal's triangle.

@words text="C(n, k)  =  ways to choose k from n"
And it isn't a coincidence. Every entry counts the ways to choose k things from n.
