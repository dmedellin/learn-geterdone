@art fence-posts
From four to eleven: how many terms? It isn't seven.

@words text="k = 4 to k = 11:  8 terms" sub="n − m + 1"
It's eight. Both ends are included, so the count is the top limit minus the bottom limit, plus one.

@words text="11 − 4 = 7 gaps" sub="subtracting counts the gaps, not the posts"
Subtracting the limits counts the gaps between the terms, like counting fence panels instead of posts. And the slip is silent: everything after it looks fine.
