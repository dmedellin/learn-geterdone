Four plus two plus one plus a half, for ever. It never reaches eight.

@math items="8 − Sₙ = 8(1/2)ⁿ@the gap;;n = 13:   gap 1/1024@thirteen terms"
The gap to eight after n terms is exactly eight times a half to the n. After thirteen terms it's one over a thousand and twenty four. Never zero.

@words text="S∞ = 8" sub="the number approached, not a number reached"
And yet the sum is exactly eight, because the sum of an infinite series is defined as the number the running totals approach.
