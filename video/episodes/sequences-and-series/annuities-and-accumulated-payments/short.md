@art growing-stacks
Five payments of a thousand, all different values when the account closes.

@math items="1464.10   1331.00   1210.00   1100.00   1000@fourteen sixty four"
At ten percent, the first payment grows for four years, to fourteen sixty four point one. The last earns nothing. In between: thirteen thirty one, twelve ten, eleven hundred.

@words text="1000 × (11/10) × (11/10) × …" sub="a geometric series, r = 1 + i"
Read them from the last payment backwards, and each is the one before times eleven tenths. It's a geometric series.

@math items="total = 6105.10@six thousand"
So the geometric sum formula gives the total: six thousand one hundred and five point one.
