title: A whole geometric sum in one subtraction

@art shift-cancel
Add ten terms of a doubling sequence with a single subtraction.

@math items="S = 3 + 6 + … + 1536@three plus six;;2S = 6 + 12 + … + 3072@two S"
Take three plus six, all the way to fifteen hundred and thirty six. Now double it. In two S, every term has slid one place along.

@math items="2S − S = 3072 − 3@subtract;;S = 3069@three thousand"
Subtract, and everything in the middle cancels. Three thousand and seventy two minus three: the sum is three thousand and sixty nine.
