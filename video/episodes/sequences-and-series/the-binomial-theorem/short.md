@art missing-middle
A plus b, squared, is not a squared plus b squared.

@math items="(a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²@two a b"
There's a middle term, two a b. Raising a sum to a power doesn't just raise each piece.

@words text="a = b = 1:   (1 + 1)ⁿ = 2ⁿ,   1ⁿ + 1ⁿ = 2" sub="the shortcut fails"
The same goes for any power above one. Put a and b equal to one: a plus b to the n gives two to the n. The shortcut gives only two.
