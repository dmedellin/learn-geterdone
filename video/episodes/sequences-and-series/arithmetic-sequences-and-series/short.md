@art gauss-pairs
One plus two plus three, all the way to a hundred. There's a shortcut.

@words text="1 + 100,  2 + 99,  3 + 98, …" sub="every pair makes 101"
Pair them from the outside in. One and a hundred, two and ninety nine, three and ninety eight. Every pair makes a hundred and one.

@math items="50 pairs × 101 = 5050@fifty pairs"
There are fifty pairs, so the total is fifty times a hundred and one, five thousand and fifty.

@words text="Sₙ = n(a₁ + aₙ) / 2"
It works for any arithmetic sum: n times the first plus the last, over two.
