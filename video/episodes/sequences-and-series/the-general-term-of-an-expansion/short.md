Does x squared plus one over x, to the eighth, have a constant term?

@math items="C(8, k) x¹⁶⁻³ᵏ@sixteen minus three k;;16 − 3k = 0  →  k = 16/3@sixteen thirds"
Each term holds x to the sixteen minus three k. Set that to zero, and k is sixteen thirds.

@words text="16, 13, 10, 7, 4, 1, −2, −5, −8" sub="the powers step over 0"
But k counts brackets, so it must be a whole number. The powers jump from one to minus two. There's no constant term, and that's the answer.
