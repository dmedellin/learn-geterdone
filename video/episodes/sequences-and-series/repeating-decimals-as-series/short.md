@art nines-approach
Zero point nine recurring is exactly one. Not nearly. Exactly.

@math items="9/10 + 9/100 + 9/1000 + …@nine tenths;;a₁ = 9/10,   r = 1/10@a one;;S∞ = (9/10)/(1 − 1/10) = 1@equals one"
The decimal is a series: nine tenths, plus nine hundredths, plus nine thousandths. It's geometric, with a one nine tenths and r one tenth. So its sum equals one.

@words text="every partial sum < 1" sub="the sum is the number they approach"
Yes, every partial sum is less than one. But the sum isn't any of them. It's the number they approach.
