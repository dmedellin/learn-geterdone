@art cancel-pairs
Add a whole list of fractions, and almost all of them vanish.

@math items="(1 − 1/2) + (1/2 − 1/3) + … + (1/n − 1/(n + 1))@one minus a half"
One over k times k plus one splits into one over k minus one over k plus one. Write the sum out: one minus a half, plus a half minus a third, and so on.

@math items="= 1 − 1/(n + 1)@only the ends"
Every middle fraction appears once with each sign and cancels. Only the ends survive: one minus one over n plus one.
