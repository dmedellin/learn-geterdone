title: Twelve alerts, nine of them useless

@art duplicate-alerts
Three setups, twenty minutes. Your scanner sends twelve alerts. Nine say nothing new.

@math items="4 scans × 3 candidates = 12@four times three;;1 transition × 3 = 3@one per candidate"
Scanning every five minutes, it sees the condition four times. Four times three is twelve alerts. Alert on the transition instead, one per candidate, and it's three.

@words text="Alert on the change"
Fewer, better alerts are the ones that actually get read, and acted on in time.
