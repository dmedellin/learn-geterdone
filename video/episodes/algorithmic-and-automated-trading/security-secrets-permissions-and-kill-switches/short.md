title: Ninety bad orders or two

@art kill-switch
A leaked key sends six bad orders a minute. How many before you stop it?

@math items="untested switch: 6 × 15 = 90@six times fifteen;;tested: 6 × 2 = 12@Tested;;scoped key: 1 × 2 = 2@one a minute"
With an untested switch that takes fifteen minutes, six times fifteen is ninety. Tested, two minutes: twelve. And with a key scoped to one a minute, just two.

@words text="Test it · scope it"
Same incident, very different damage, and none of it depends on the market.
