title: Why ten men made 240 times as many pins

@art wire-to-pin
Alone, a man might make twenty pins a day. In Smith's factory, four thousand eight hundred.

@math items="12 · 4000 = 48000 a day@twelve pounds;;48000 / 10 = 4800 each@ten men;;4800 / 20 = 240@twenty alone"
Ten men made twelve pounds of pins, upwards of four thousand to the pound. That's forty eight thousand, four thousand eight hundred each. Against twenty alone, that's two hundred and forty times as many.

@art three-causes
Smith's reasons: dexterity, time saved between tasks, and machines.
