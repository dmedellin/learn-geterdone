@art wagon-and-ship
In Adam Smith's day, one ship did the work of fifty wagons.

@math items="wagon: 2 men · 8 horses · 4 tons@two men;;ship: 6 to 8 men · 200 tons@six or eight;;200 / 4 = 50 wagons@fifty wagons"
A wagon with two men and eight horses carried about four tons between London and Edinburgh. A ship with six or eight men often carried two hundred. That's fifty wagons, a hundred men and four hundred horses.

@words text="Water widens the market"
So Smith thought industry began on coasts and rivers, where trade could reach furthest.
