@art hunters-and-civilized
How can a nation with fewer people working be four times better supplied?

@math items="hunters:  60 · 2 = 120 → 6/5 a head@sixty at work;;civilized:  40 · 12 = 480 → 24/5 a head@forty at work"
Take two nations of a hundred people, figures the lesson wrote. Sixty at work making two each gives six fifths a head. Forty at work making twelve each gives twenty four fifths a head.

@words text="Skill first, the share at work second"
That's Adam Smith's point. What each worker can make matters more than how many work.
