@art missing-term
Your partial fractions have no solution. You probably skipped a power.

@math items="1/(s·(s + 1)²) ≟ A/s + C/(s + 1)²@A over s;;1 = A·(s + 1)² + C·s@clear;;A = 0  and  A = 1  ✗@A must be zero"
Try one over s times s plus one squared, as A over s plus C over s plus one squared. Clear the denominators. The s squared term says A must be zero, but s equals zero says A is one.

@words text="add B/(s + 1)"
A repeated factor needs every power, so add B over s plus one.
