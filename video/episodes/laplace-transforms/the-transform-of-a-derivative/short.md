title: The Laplace slip that only shows when y of 0 isn't zero
---

@art starts-at-zero
This mistake gives the right answer, right up until it doesn't.

@math items="ℒ[y′] = s·Y − y(0)@minus y of zero;;y = t²:  y(0) = 0@t squared"
The transform of y prime is s Y minus y of zero. Forget that last term, and for t squared, which starts at zero, nothing changes.

@words text="y = e^(2t):  off by exactly 1"
But for e to the two t, which starts at one, the slip is wrong by exactly one, at every s.
