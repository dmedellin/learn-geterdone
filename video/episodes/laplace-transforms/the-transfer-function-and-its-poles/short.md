Complex poles. Does that i mean the response blows up?

@math items="y″ + 2y′ + 5y = 5@y double prime;;poles −1 ± 2i@minus one plus or minus two i"
For y double prime plus two y prime plus five y equals five, the poles are minus one plus or minus two i.

@words text="real part −1: it dies out"
The i only gives oscillation. The real part, minus one, is negative, so the wobble shrinks like e to the minus t, and the response settles at one.
