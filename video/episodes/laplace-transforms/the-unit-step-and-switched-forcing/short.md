@art pulse-response
A pulse switches off at three. Does that change anything before three?

@math items="y′ + y = u(t − 1) − u(t − 3)@y prime plus y;;1 ≤ t < 3:  y = 1 − e^(−(t − 1))@from one to three"
For y prime plus y equals a pulse on from one to three, the solution from one to three is one minus e to the minus t minus one.

@words text="Same curve if it ended at 30"
It's the same curve whether the pulse ends at three or at thirty. The response can't feel a switch that hasn't happened yet.
