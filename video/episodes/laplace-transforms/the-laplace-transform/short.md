title: The Laplace transform that gives a negative area

@art floor-line
This formula says the area under a positive curve is minus one.

@math items="ℒ[e^(2t)] = 1/(s − 2)@one over s minus two;;s = 1:  1/(1 − 2) = −1@s equals one"
The transform of e to the two t is one over s minus two. Put in s equals one, and you get minus one.

@words text="Only for s > 2"
But at s equals one the integral is infinite. The fraction is the transform only above its floor, s bigger than two.
