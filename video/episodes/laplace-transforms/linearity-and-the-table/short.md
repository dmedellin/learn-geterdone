title: The Laplace transform has no product rule
---

@art product-trap
Transform a product by multiplying the transforms? Try it on one times one.

@math items="ℒ[1·1] = ℒ[1] = 1/s@one over s;;ℒ[1]·ℒ[1] = 1/s²@one over s squared"
One times one is one, and its transform is one over s. But the transform of one, times itself, is one over s squared.

@words text="Sums and constants only"
Linearity splits sums and pulls out constants. Products are a different, harder operation.
