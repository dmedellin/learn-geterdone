@art uranus-neptune
Uranus wasn't where Newton's laws said it should be. Astronomers didn't drop Newton.

@words text="Drop the auxiliary: no eighth planet"
They dropped a different assumption: that there was no eighth planet. Then they computed where one would have to be.

@words text="Neptune, 1846"
Neptune was found close to that place in eighteen forty six. A failed prediction refutes the whole set of assumptions, not the theory alone.
