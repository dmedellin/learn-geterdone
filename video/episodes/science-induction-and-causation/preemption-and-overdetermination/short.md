@art backup-ready
Suzy's rock shatters the bottle. Billy's rock was right behind it.

@words text="Without Suzy, it breaks anyway."
Had Suzy not thrown, Billy's rock would have hit instead. So the bottle shatters either way, and Suzy fails the but for test.

@words text="Hold Billy's rock at what it did: nothing."
The fix: hold Billy's rock at what it actually did, which was nothing at all. Now flip Suzy's throw, and the bottle survives.
