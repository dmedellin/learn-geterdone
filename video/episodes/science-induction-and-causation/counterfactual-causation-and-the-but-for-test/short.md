@art match-and-oxygen
Did the match cause the fire, or the oxygen? By the but for test, both.

@math items="F = S ∧ O@F equals S and O;;flip either:  F = 0@Flip either"
The fire needs a struck match and oxygen: F equals S and O. Flip either one to zero, and the fire doesn't happen.

@words text="Cause or condition? The test can't tell."
Calling the match the cause and the oxygen a condition is a judgement made outside the model.
