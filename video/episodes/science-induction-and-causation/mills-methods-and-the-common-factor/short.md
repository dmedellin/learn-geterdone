@art dinner-table
Two diners fell ill. Both ate the oysters and the bread. Which one was it?

@words text="Cy ate bread and stayed well."
Look at the well diners. Cy ate bread and didn't fall ill, so bread is out. No well diner ate oysters.

@words text="Oysters: a candidate"
Oysters survive. But that's a candidate, not a proven cause. The table can't eliminate a factor it doesn't list.
