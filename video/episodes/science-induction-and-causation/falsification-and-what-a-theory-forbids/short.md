title: Ninety nine percent sure, and one observation takes it to zero

@art forbidden-outcome
You're ninety nine percent sure of a theory. One observation can take that to zero.

@math items="99/100 · 0 = 0@times zero"
If the theory gives an outcome probability zero, and you see it, the posterior is the prior times zero. Zero, whatever the prior was.

@words text="Refutation is final. Confirmation isn't."
But no run of allowed outcomes ever takes it all the way to one.
