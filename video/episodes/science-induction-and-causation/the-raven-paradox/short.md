title: A white shoe is evidence that ravens are black

@art shoe-instance
A white shoe is evidence that all ravens are black. Really.

@words text="non-black → non-raven"
All ravens are black means every non black thing is a non raven. A white shoe fits that, so it confirms.

@math items="white shoe:  BF 901/900@nine hundred and one;;black raven:  BF 10/9@ten ninths"
But measure it. In the lesson's world, the shoe's factor is nine hundred and one over nine hundred. A black raven's is ten ninths, a hundred times the boost.
