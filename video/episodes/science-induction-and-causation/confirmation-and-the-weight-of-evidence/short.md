@art sunrise-everyone
Your theory predicted that the sun would rise. It did. Does that confirm your theory?

@words text="Every rival predicted it too."
Not against its rivals. Every serious theory of the heavens predicted it too. The evidence was certain either way.

@math items="BF = P(E | H) / P(E | ¬H) = 1 / 1 = 1@one over one"
The weight of evidence is the Bayes factor. Here it's one over one, which is one. Neutral.
