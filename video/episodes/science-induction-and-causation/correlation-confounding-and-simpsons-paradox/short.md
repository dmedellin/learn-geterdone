@art two-treatments
A treatment wins for small kidney stones, wins for large ones, and loses overall.

@words text="It got the harder cases."
How? It was mostly given to patients with large stones, the harder cases. That drags its pooled rate down.

@words text="Same mix for both → it wins again"
Give both treatments the same mix of patients, and it comes out ahead again. That's Simpson's paradox.
