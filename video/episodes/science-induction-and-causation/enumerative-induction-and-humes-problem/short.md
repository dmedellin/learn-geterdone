title: Ten thousand sunrises still don't prove the next one

@art sunrise-row
The sun has risen every day you've lived. Logically, that proves nothing about tomorrow.

@words text="premises true · conclusion false"
There's a row where every past sunrise happened, and tomorrow's doesn't. Nothing in the premises mentions tomorrow, however many there are.

@words text="The missing premise is a prior."
So induction needs a premise the data can't supply: that the future resembles the past. That's Hume's problem.
