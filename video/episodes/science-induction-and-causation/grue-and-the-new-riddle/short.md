@art green-and-grue
Every emerald you've ever seen was green. Every one was also grue.

@words text="grue" sub="green if examined before t, blue if not"
Grue means green if examined before some future time t, and blue if not. Every emerald so far fits both.

@words text="Bayes factor = 1"
So a hundred green emeralds give green and grue exactly the same support. Preferring green comes from your prior, not the data.
