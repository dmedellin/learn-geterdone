title: Why a weaver costs more as a soldier than a shepherd
---

@art herds-to-war
A shepherd's army costs its chief nothing. The flocks go to war and feed it.

@art empty-workshop
But for a weaver, Nature does nothing. The day he leaves his loom, his income stops, so the public must pay him.

@art one-in-a-hundred
That's why, Smith says, a rich European country can spare not more than one in a hundred of its people as soldiers.
