title: Adam Smith on why Oxford's professors stopped teaching
---

@art empty-lecture-hall
Adam Smith said most Oxford professors had given up even pretending to teach.

@words text="Effort follows necessity"
His reason: in every profession, effort follows the necessity of making it. A professor on a fixed salary gets paid whether anyone attends or not.

@art fee-and-salary
So Smith wanted part of a teacher's pay to come from his pupils' fees. Then one more pupil means more pay.
