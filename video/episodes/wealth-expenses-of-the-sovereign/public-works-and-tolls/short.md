@art weighbridge
Should a heavy waggon pay more to use a road than a light coach?

@words text="Pay in proportion to weight"
Adam Smith says yes, by weight. Each carriage then pays exactly for the wear and tear it causes.

@math items="waggon: 3 · 100 · 1/20 = £15@waggon;;coach: 1 · 200 · 1/20 = £10@coach"
At a shilling a ton a trip, a three ton waggon making a hundred trips pays fifteen pounds. A one ton coach making two hundred pays ten.
