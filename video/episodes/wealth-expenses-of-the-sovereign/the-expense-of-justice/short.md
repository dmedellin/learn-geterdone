For centuries, the courts made the king money. Then justice was called free.

@words text="Justice, however, never was in reality administered gratis in any country."
Smith wasn't fooled. Justice, however, never was in reality administered gratis in any country. Salaries stopped the bribes, but the parties still paid lawyers far more.

@art cashier-and-court
His fix: fees fixed by law, paid to a cashier, and shared among the judges only after the case is decided.
