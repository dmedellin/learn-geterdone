title: Adam Smith's case for a thousand small sects
---

@art many-small-sects
Adam Smith thought religion was safest with not one church, but thousands.

@art one-great-sect
Zeal is dangerous, he said, only where one sect rules, or two or three great ones divide the country.

@words text="Candour and moderation"
Split society into hundreds of small sects, and each preacher finds more adversaries than friends. He has to learn candour and moderation, as long as the government leaves them all alone.
