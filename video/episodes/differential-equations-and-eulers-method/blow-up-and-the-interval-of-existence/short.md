@art vertical-asymptote
This equation is as smooth as they come. Its solution still stops existing at t equals one.

@math items="y′ = y²,   y(0) = 1@y squared;;y = 1/(1 − t)@one over one minus t"
Y prime equals y squared, starting from one. The solution is one over one minus t, which grows without limit and has no value at one.

@art step-past
And Euler's method steps straight past the end, printing finite numbers, because it only ever looks one step ahead.
