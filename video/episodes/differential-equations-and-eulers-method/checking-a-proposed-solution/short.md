@art two-exponentials
Two exponentials, almost the same. This equation accepts one and rejects the other.

@math items="e^(2t):   4 − 2 − 2 = 0@e to the two t;;eᵗ:   1 − 1 − 2 = −2@e to the t gives"
For y double prime minus y prime minus two y equals zero, e to the two t leaves four minus two minus two, times itself. Zero. But e to the t gives one minus one minus two. Minus two.

@words text="Only the exponent changed"
The only difference is the number in the exponent, and it decides everything.
