@art euler-polygon
Every number this method gives is an exact fraction. And every one is off.

@math items="y′ = y,  h = 1/4@y prime equals y;;y₄ = (5/4)⁴ = 625/256 ≈ 2.44@six hundred and twenty five;;e ≈ 2.718@The true value"
Take y prime equals y from one, with steps of a quarter. Each step multiplies by five quarters, so four steps give six hundred and twenty five over two hundred and fifty six, about two point four four. The true value is e, about two point seven one eight.

@words text="The error belongs to the method"
The arithmetic is perfect. Each step just assumes the slope stays put.
