@art slope-field-grid
You can see the solutions of a differential equation without solving it.

@math items="(2, 0):   slope 2@two, zero;;(1, 1):   slope 0@one, one;;(0, 2):   slope −2@zero, two"
For y prime equals t minus y, just substitute. At two, zero, the slope is two. At one, one, it's zero. At zero, two, it's minus two.

@art nullcline-line
Draw a short segment at every point, and the picture appears. Along the line y equals t, every segment is flat.
