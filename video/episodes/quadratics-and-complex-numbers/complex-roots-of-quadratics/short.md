@art floating-parabola
This parabola never touches the axis. It still has two solutions.

@math items="x² − 6x + 13 = 0@x squared minus six x;;D = 36 − 52 = −16@minus sixteen;;x = 3 ± 2i@three plus or minus two i"
x squared minus six x plus thirteen has discriminant minus sixteen. No real solutions. But the formula still works, and gives three plus or minus two i.

@art mirror-pair
A conjugate pair: same real part, opposite imaginary parts. Negative discriminant means no real solution, not no solution.
