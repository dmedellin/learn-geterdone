@art signed-tiles
If b is minus seven, what's b squared? Not minus forty nine.

@math items="b = −7@b equals minus seven;;b² = (−7)² = 49@forty nine;;−b = 7@minus b"
Write b equals minus seven on its own line first. Then b squared, with brackets, is forty nine, and minus b is seven.

@words text="−7² = −49 turns 25 into −73"
Drop the brackets and, in two x squared minus seven x plus three, twenty five becomes minus seventy three. Two solutions become none.
