title: Why you can't divide by x
---

@art lost-solution
Divide both sides by x, and you can lose an answer.

@math items="x² = 5x@x squared equals;;x = 5  ✗ loses x = 0@you get five;;x(x − 5) = 0 → x = 0 or 5@factor instead"
Take x squared equals five x. Divide by x and you get five. But zero works too. Move everything to one side and factor instead: x times x minus five. So x is zero or five.

@words text="Never divide by an unknown"
You can't divide by something that might be zero.
