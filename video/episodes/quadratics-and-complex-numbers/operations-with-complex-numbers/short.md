title: Root minus four times root minus nine is not six

@art expand-grid
Root minus four, times root minus nine. Is it six?

@math items="√−4 · √−9 = 2i · 3i@two i times three i;;= 6i² = −6@minus six"
It isn't root thirty six. Convert to i first. That's two i times three i, which is six i squared, so minus six.

@words text="√a · √b = √(ab) needs a, b ≥ 0"
The root law only holds for non-negative numbers. Down here it gives you the wrong sign.
