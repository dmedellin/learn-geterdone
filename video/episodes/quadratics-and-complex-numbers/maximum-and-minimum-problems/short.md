@art rectangle-family
Eighty metres of fence. What's the biggest rectangle it can enclose?

@math items="A(x) = x(40 − x) = −x² + 40x@forty minus x;;x = 20  →  A = 400@twenty"
Call one side x. The other side is forty minus x, so the area is minus x squared plus forty x. The vertex is at twenty, giving four hundred.

@words text="20 m × 20 m = 400 m²"
So the answer is a twenty by twenty square, four hundred square metres.
