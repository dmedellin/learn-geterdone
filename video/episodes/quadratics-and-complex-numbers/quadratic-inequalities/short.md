title: Don't divide an inequality by x
---

@art two-separate
Cancel an x in x squared greater than three x, and you lose answers.

@math items="x² − 3x > 0@move everything;;x(x − 3) > 0@factor;;x < 0  or  x > 3@less than zero"
Instead, move everything to one side and factor: x times x minus three. That's positive when x is less than zero, or greater than three.

@words text="x = −1:  1 > −3  ✓"
Cancelling only gives greater than three. But try minus one: one is greater than minus three.
