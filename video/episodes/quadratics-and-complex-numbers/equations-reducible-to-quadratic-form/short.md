title: A quartic that's secretly a quadratic

x to the fourth minus five x squared plus four. It's secretly a quadratic.

@math items="u = x²:  u² − 5u + 4 = 0@call x squared u;;u = 1 or u = 4@one or four;;x = ±1,  ±2@plus or minus one"
Call x squared u. Then it's u squared minus five u plus four, so u is one or four. Go back to x, and you get plus or minus one, and plus or minus two.

@words text="four solutions: −2, −1, 1, 2"
Four solutions, from a quadratic you already know how to solve.
