Converting to vertex form? One step puts the vertex nine units too high.

@math items="2(x² − 6x + 9 − 9) + 13@two times;;= 2(x − 3)² − 18 + 13@minus eighteen;;= 2(x − 3)² − 5@minus five"
In two times x squared minus six x plus nine minus nine, plus thirteen, the minus nine is inside a bracket that's doubled. So it comes out as minus eighteen, and the answer is two times x minus three, squared, minus five.

@words text="f(3) = 18 − 36 + 13 = −5" sub="vertex (3, −5)"
And it checks instantly: f of three is minus five, which is k.
