@art square-gap
x squared plus six x is a square with one corner missing.

@words text="halve b, then square it" sub="x² + 6x:  6 → 3 → 9"
To fill the corner, halve the coefficient of x, then square it. For x squared plus six x, half of six is three, and three squared is nine.

@math items="x² + 6x + 9 = (x + 3)²@x plus three"
So x squared plus six x plus nine is x plus three, squared. Just add that nine to both sides.
