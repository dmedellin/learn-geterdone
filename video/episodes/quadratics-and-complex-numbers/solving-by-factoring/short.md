@art factor-blocks
x squared plus three x equals ten factors neatly. And gets you nowhere.

@math items="x(x + 3) = 10  ✗@x times x plus three;;x² + 3x − 10 = 0@subtract ten;;(x + 5)(x − 2) = 0@five and minus two;;x = −5  or  x = 2@minus five or two"
x times x plus three equals ten tells you nothing. Subtract ten first. Then it factors with five and minus two: x plus five, times x minus two. So x is minus five or two.

@words text="Zero on one side, then factor"
The zero comes first, every time.
