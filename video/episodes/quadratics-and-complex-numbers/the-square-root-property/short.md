title: Is the square root of nine plus or minus three?

@art square-tile
Is the square root of nine plus or minus three? No. It's three.

@words text="√9 = 3" sub="one number, the non-negative one"
The root sign names one number, the non-negative one.

@math items="x² = 9@x squared equals nine;;x = ±√9 = ±3@you write"
But x squared equals nine still has two solutions. The plus or minus comes from the property, and you write it yourself, at the moment you take the root.
