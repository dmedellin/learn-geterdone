Imaginary numbers got their name as an insult.

@list items="negatives@negatives|fractions@fractions|irrationals@irrationals|i@i"
Every time numbers were extended, the new ones were mocked. Negatives, fractions, irrationals, and then i, the number whose square is minus one.

@words text="i² = −1" sub="no less legitimate than the others"
Imaginary is a seventeenth century insult that stuck as a name. The objection didn't. Nothing in the arithmetic treats i as second class.
