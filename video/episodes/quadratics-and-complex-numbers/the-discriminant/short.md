@art three-parabolas
One line of arithmetic tells you how many solutions a quadratic has.

@math items="D = b² − 4ac@b squared minus"
Work out b squared minus four a c. That's the discriminant.

@list items="D > 0: two real solutions@positive|D = 0: exactly one@zero|D < 0: none that are real@negative"
If it's positive, there are two real solutions. If it's zero, exactly one. If it's negative, none that are real. So compute it first, before you pick a method.
