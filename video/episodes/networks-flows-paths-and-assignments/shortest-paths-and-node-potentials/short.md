@art float-potentials
Add seven to every shortest path label. Is anything wrong now?

@words text="0, 3, 2, 6  →  7, 10, 9, 13"
The labels zero, three, two and six become seven, ten, nine and thirteen. Each arc's condition compares two labels, so the seven cancels.

@art one-potential-moved
But add five to just one label, and arcs break. The whole vector floats together. That's how prices behave, not distances.
