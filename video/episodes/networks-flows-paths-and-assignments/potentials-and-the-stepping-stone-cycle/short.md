@art false-rectangle
The stepping stone cycle is a rectangle, right? On the very first pivot, it isn't.

@art staircase-cycle
Cell C, D one enters the three plant table. The rectangle would turn at B, D one, and that cell is empty. The real cycle has six corners, a staircase.

@words text="Turn only at occupied cells"
A cycle may cross an empty cell, but it may only turn where something is allocated. Trace it, don't guess it.
