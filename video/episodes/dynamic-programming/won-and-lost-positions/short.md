A table can show you a pattern, and still not prove it.

@words text="losing: 0, 2, 7, 9, 14, 16" sub="moves 1, 3 or 4"
In a game where you take one, three or four stones, the losing piles up to eighteen are zero, two, seven, nine, fourteen and sixteen. It repeats every seven.

@words text="proved: period ≤ 16"
But nineteen positions are a measurement. What's proved is that the labels eventually repeat, with period at most sixteen.
