title: The best order quantity is where two roots collide
---

@art closing-interval
You can find the cheapest order quantity without any calculus at all.

@words text="h·Q²/2 − T·Q + K·D ≤ 0"
Ask whether any quantity costs at most T. That's a quadratic inequality, true between its two roots.

@art double-root
Lower T, and the roots move together. When the discriminant hits zero, they collide, and that single point is the economic order quantity.
