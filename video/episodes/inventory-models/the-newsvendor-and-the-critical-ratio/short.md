title: Why ordering the average demand is usually wrong

@art below-and-above-mean
Average demand is ten. The right order is nine.

@words text="ratio = cᵤ/(cᵤ + cₒ) = 2/7"
A bakery loses two on every cake short, and five on every cake left over. The critical ratio is two over two plus five, two sevenths.

@art cdf-staircase
That's a probability, not a quantity. Order the smallest amount whose chance of covering demand reaches two sevenths. Here, that's nine.
