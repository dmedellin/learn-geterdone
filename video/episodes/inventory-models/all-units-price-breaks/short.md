@art purchase-bill
Choose the cheapest ordering and holding, and you choose the dearest plan.

@math items="219:  cycle 438 · total 12438@two nineteen;;1000:  cycle 848 · total 10448@a thousand"
Ordering two nineteen at a time has the smallest cycle cost, four thirty eight. But ordering a thousand drops every unit's price, and the total falls to ten thousand four hundred forty eight.

@words text="Count the purchase bill"
The discount lives in the purchase bill. Leave it out and you'll always choose wrong.
