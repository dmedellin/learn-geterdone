title: Why planning to run out can be cheaper
---

@art backorder-dip
Planning to run out of stock can cut your cost by a third.

@math items="no shortage:  900@nine hundred;;owe 100 units:  600@six hundred"
At a run of four hundred, refusing every shortage costs nine hundred per unit time. Planning to owe a hundred units costs six hundred.

@words text="A unit owed takes no shelf"
A backordered unit isn't sitting in a warehouse. As long as a broken promise has a real price, owing can be cheaper than holding.
