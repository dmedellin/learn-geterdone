title: The stocking mistake that turns 90% into a third

@art lead-only-window
You aimed for ninety percent service and got barely over a third.

@words text="Window = R + L"
If you only review every R periods, today's stock must last until the next order lands: R plus L periods, not just the lead time.

@math items="cover L only:  S = 2@two;;over R + L:  11/32 = 34.38%@thirty four"
Cover just the lead time, and S comes out at two. Over the real window, that delivers thirty four point three eight percent.
