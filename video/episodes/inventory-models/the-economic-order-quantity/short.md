@art half-and-double
Order half the best amount, or twice it, and you pay exactly the same.

@math items="C(t·Q*) / C* = (t + 1/t) / 2@t plus one over t;;t = ½ or 2:  5/4@a half"
The cost of ordering t times the best quantity is t plus one over t, over two, times the minimum. For a half, or for two, that's five quarters.

@words text="No K, no D, no h"
Twenty five percent extra, in every warehouse, whatever the numbers.
