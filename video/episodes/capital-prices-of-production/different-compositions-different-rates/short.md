Exploit workers four times as hard, and earn a lower rate of profit. Here's how.

@math items="Europe:  84c + 16v,  s′ 100% → 16%@sixteen v;;Asia:  16c + 84v,  s′ 25% → 21%@eighty four v"
Marx's European capital has eighty four c and sixteen v, at a hundred per cent: sixteen per cent profit. His Asiatic capital has sixteen c and eighty four v, at twenty five per cent: twenty one.

@words text="Composition beats exploitation"
The Asian capital sets more than five times the labour in motion.
