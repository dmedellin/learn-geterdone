@art rate-and-mass
The rate of profit halves. And the total profit goes up by half.

@math items="4c + 2v + 2s:  1/3,  2 million@a third;;15c + 3v + 3s:  1/6,  3 million@a sixth"
In Marx's example, in millions: four c, two v, two s is a rate of a third, and two million of profit. Fifteen c, three v, three s is a sixth, and three million.

@words text="Rate down, mass up"
The capital trebled while the rate halved.
