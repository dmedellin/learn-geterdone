@art three-directions
Wages rise by a quarter everywhere. One price rises, one stays, and one falls.

@math items="average:  120 → 120@stays;;50c + 50v:  120 → 128 4/7@rises;;92c + 8v:  120 → 116 4/7@falls"
In Marx's example, the average sphere stays at a hundred and twenty. A wage heavy sphere rises to a hundred and twenty eight and four sevenths. A machine heavy sphere falls to a hundred and sixteen and four sevenths.

@words text="Paid out of profits"
The rise comes out of profit, and only rearranges prices.
