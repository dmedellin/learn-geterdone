@art three-groups
Same goods, same price. But some producers pocket an extra hour on every piece.

@math items="market value = 4h@four hours;;3-hour group:  +1h a piece@an hour"
A trade's market value is four hours a piece. A group making each piece in three hours still sells at four, and gains an hour on every one.

@words text="Surplus profit"
Marx calls that surplus profit. It isn't a mark-up. Their own value is just below the market's.
