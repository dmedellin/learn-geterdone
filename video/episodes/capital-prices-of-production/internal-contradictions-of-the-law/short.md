title: Too much capital, not too little: Marx's crisis
---

@art swollen-capital
Marx says crises come from too much capital, not too little.

@math items="1,000 → 100:  10%@ten per cent;;1,500 → 100:  1/15@fifteen hundred"
A thousand earns a hundred, ten per cent. Add five hundred that earns nothing more, and the rate on fifteen hundred falls to one fifteenth.

@words text="Restored by destroying capital"
On his account, the rate recovers only when part of the capital is idled or written off.
