@art premium-draw
Give shipping a premium for danger. Every other trade quietly pays for it.

@math items="no premium:  22% for all@twenty two;;V +5%:  base 21%,  V gets 26%@twenty one"
Marx's five spheres share a hundred and ten of surplus value: twenty two per cent each. Allow one a five per cent premium, and everyone else drops to twenty one.

@words text="Total profit: still 110"
The premium shares out the same pool. It doesn't add to it.
