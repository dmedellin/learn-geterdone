@art held-rate
Constant capital rose by two thirds. The profit rate didn't move.

@math items="500c + 100v at 100%:  1/6@a sixth;;500c + 100v at 150%:  1/4@a quarter"
Start at a quarter. Raise c from three hundred to five hundred, and at the old exploitation the rate would fall to a sixth. Raise the rate of surplus value to a hundred and fifty per cent, and it stays at a quarter.

@words text="A tendency, not a fact"
Marx calls that a counteracting influence. It checks the law without suspending it.
