A hundred and twenty ways to order five jobs, and the last one always finishes at the same time.

@math items="6 + 4 + 5 + 3 + 7 = 25@twenty five"
On one machine that never idles, the last job finishes when the work runs out. Six plus four plus five plus three plus seven is twenty five, in every order.

@words text="Resequencing can't move the makespan"
So don't spend effort there. Every other objective does move, some by a factor of two.
