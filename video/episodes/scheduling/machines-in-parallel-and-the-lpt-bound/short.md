@art bound-gap
Seven jobs, three machines. The lower bound says eleven and a third. Nothing gets there.

@math items="bound: 34/3 ≈ 11.33@thirty four thirds;;optimum: 12@twelve;;longest first: 13@thirteen"
The work adds to thirty four, so some machine does at least thirty four thirds. But loads are whole numbers. The best possible is twelve. Longest first gets thirteen.

@words text="A bound is a proof, not a target"
Chase the bound and you're chasing a schedule that doesn't exist.
