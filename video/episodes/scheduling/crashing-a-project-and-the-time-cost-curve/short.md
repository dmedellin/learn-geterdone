@art slack-day
To finish a project one day sooner, the obvious activity is the wrong buy.

@words text="A: critical, on both paths, 20 a day"
Activity A is critical and sits on both paths. Shortening it always helps. It costs twenty a day.

@words text="C: 15 a day, and that's enough"
But the other path has one day of slack. So one day off the longer path alone is enough, and C does that for fifteen. A only becomes the right buy once both paths are critical.
