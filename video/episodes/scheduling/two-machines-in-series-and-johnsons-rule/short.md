Two machines in series. The second one does the same work in every order. So what are you optimising?

@words text="makespan = 27 hours of work + idle"
On the lesson's five jobs, machine two always does twenty seven hours of work. The only thing an order changes is how long machine two waits.

@math items="typed order: idle 7 → 34@thirty four;;Johnson's order: idle 3 → 30@thirty"
The typed order leaves it idle seven hours, finishing at thirty four. Johnson's rule cuts that to three, finishing at thirty.
