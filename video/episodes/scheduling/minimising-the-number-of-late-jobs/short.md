title: To cut late jobs, drop the job that wasn't late

@art discard-longest
A job goes late. The right move is to throw out a different job.

@math items="D joins:  finish 7,  due 6@seven;;discard B, the longest: frees 4 hours@free four;;discard D instead: frees 3@frees three"
In the lesson's example, D joins and the set finishes at seven, past D's due date of six. Throw out B, the longest, and you free four hours. Throwing out D frees three.

@words text="Same one late job, one more hour of room"
Either way one job is late. The extra hour lets two later jobs fit.
