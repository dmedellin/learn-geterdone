title: Why you can't just set h to zero
---

@art empty-fraction
Want the rate at a point? Just set the step to zero. Except that gives zero over zero.

@math items="(2th + h²) / h@expand;;= 2t + h@Cancel;;h = 0:  2t@Now"
So expand first. For t squared, the top is two t h plus h squared, and every term has an h. Cancel it, and you're left with two t plus h. Now set h to zero, and you get two t.

@words text="Cancel first, then set h = 0"
The order is the whole trick.
