title: Why e is the special base

@art three-bases
Why is e such a special number? Because its rate is exactly itself.

@math items="b^t·(b^h − 1)/h@factors;;b = 2:  → ln 2 ≈ 0.693@For two;;b = e:  → 1@For e"
The quotient of b to the t factors into b to the t, times a column that doesn't depend on t. For two, that column heads for about zero point six nine three. For e, it heads for one.

@words text="(eᵗ)′ = eᵗ"
So the rate of e to the t is e to the t.
