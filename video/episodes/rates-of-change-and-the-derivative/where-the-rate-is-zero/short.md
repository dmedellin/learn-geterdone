title: A level tangent isn't always a turn
---

@art flat-step
The derivative of t cubed is zero at zero. But the graph doesn't turn there.

@math items="p′(t) = 3t²@three t squared;;t < 0:  p′ > 0@Before zero;;t > 0:  p′ > 0@After zero"
Its derivative is three t squared. Before zero, that's positive. After zero, it's positive too. So the graph rises, flattens for an instant, and keeps on rising.

@words text="A turn needs a sign change"
A level tangent only shows where a turn could be. A change of sign is what makes it one.
