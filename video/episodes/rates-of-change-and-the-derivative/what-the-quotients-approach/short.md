@art never-reaches
No row of this table ever equals two. So why is the rate two?

@math items="3@three;;5/2@five halves;;9/4@nine quarters;;129/64@one hundred and twenty nine"
For t squared at one, halving the step gives three, five halves, nine quarters, and on down to one hundred and twenty nine sixty fourths. The gap to two is exactly the step.

@art bound-band
Approach doesn't mean reach. It means that any bound you name, however small, is beaten once the step is small enough.
