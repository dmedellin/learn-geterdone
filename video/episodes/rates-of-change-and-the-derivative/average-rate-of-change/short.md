title: Same function, different rate

@art shrinking-secants
Halve the step, and the rate changes, even though the function didn't.

@math items="h = 1:  rate 3@a step of one;;h = 1/2:  rate 5/2@A half;;h = 1/4:  rate 9/4@A quarter"
Take t squared, starting at one. A step of one gives a rate of three. A half gives five halves. A quarter gives nine quarters.

@words text="rate = 2 + h"
Every one is two plus the step, because each is the slope of a different secant line through the same point.
