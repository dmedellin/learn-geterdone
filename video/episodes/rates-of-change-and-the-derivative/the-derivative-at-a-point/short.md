@art tangent-line
A tangent line isn't just a line that touches a curve. It's a prediction.

@math items="tangent to t² at 2:  y = 4t − 4@y equals four t;;line at 21/10:  440/100@The line says;;t² at 21/10:  441/100@The truth"
The tangent to t squared at two is y equals four t minus four. A tenth to the right, the line says four hundred and forty hundredths. The truth is four hundred and forty one hundredths.

@words text="error = h²" sub="halve the step: a quarter of the error"
The error is exactly h squared. Halve the step, and the error falls to a quarter.
