@art missing-factor
Differentiate three t plus one, squared, the quick way, and you'll be off by a factor of three.

@math items="2(3t + 1) = 6t + 2@Two times;;true:  18t + 6@the true;;× 3, the slope of 3t + 1@missing"
Two times three t plus one is six t plus two. But the true derivative is eighteen t plus six. The missing three is the slope of the inner function.

@words text="outer rate × inner rate"
That's the chain rule. Take the outer rate at the inner value, then multiply by the inner rate.
