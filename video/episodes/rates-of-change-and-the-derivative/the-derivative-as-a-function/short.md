title: Sketch a cubic without plotting a point
---

@art cubic-shape
You can sketch this cubic without plotting a single point.

@math items="p(t) = t³ − 6t² + 9t@t cubed;;p′(t) = 3(t − 1)(t − 3)@Its derivative"
Take t cubed minus six t squared plus nine t. Its derivative, by the power rule, is three times t minus one, times t minus three.

@art sign-strip
Test the sign between the zeros. It's positive before one, negative between one and three, and positive after. So the graph rises, falls, and rises again.
