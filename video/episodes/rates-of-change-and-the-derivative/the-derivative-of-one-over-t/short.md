title: The derivative of 1/t isn't 1
---

@art hyperbola
The derivative of one over t isn't one. It isn't even positive.

@math items="1/(a + h) − 1/a = −h/(a(a + h))@Combine;;quotient:  −1/(a(a + h))@Cancel the h;;f′(a) = −1/a²@So the derivative"
Combine the two fractions, and the top becomes minus h. Cancel the h, and the quotient is minus one over a times a plus h. As h shrinks, that heads for minus one over a squared. So the derivative is minus one over a squared.

@words text="1/t falls, so its slope is negative"
Which makes sense. One over t falls as t grows.
