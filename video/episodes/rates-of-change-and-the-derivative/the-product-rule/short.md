@art degree-towers
The derivative of a product is not the product of the derivatives.

@math items="t²·(t + 1) = t³ + t²@T squared;;true:  3t² + 2t@the true;;✗  2t·1 = 2t@multiply"
T squared times t plus one is t cubed plus t squared, so the true derivative is three t squared plus two t. Differentiate each factor and multiply, and you get two t. Wrong degree.

@art growing-rectangle
Think of a rectangle. It grows in two directions, so the rule has two terms: f prime g, plus f g prime.
