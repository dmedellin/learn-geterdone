title: Getting faster at eye exercises won't prove you read faster
---

@art practice-curve
You'll get faster at this eye exercise in three tries. Does that make you read faster?

@math items="62 s → 41 s@sixty two"
One reader's Schulte table went from sixty two seconds to forty one, about a third faster, in three tries.

@words text="Practice improves the practised task"
That's what practice does to any new task. Whether it carries over to reading is the claim to test, and the evidence for it is weak.
