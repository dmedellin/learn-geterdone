Can a trained eye take in a whole line at once? Here's what eye tracking found.

@list items="3 to 4 letters left@three to four|14 to 15 letters right@fourteen to fifteen|words identified within 7 to 8 right@seven to eight"
In reading, one fixation reaches about three to four letters to the left, and fourteen to fifteen to the right. Words are identified only within about seven to eight letters to the right.

@words text="No evidence it widens to a line"
A line of sixty five characters is about eight of those spans long.
