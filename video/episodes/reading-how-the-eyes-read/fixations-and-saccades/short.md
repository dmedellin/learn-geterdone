title: Your eyes stop four times a second when you read
---

@art stops-and-jumps
Your eyes don't glide along a line. They jump.

@words text="Stop · jump · stop" sub="fixations of about 200 to 250 ms"
They stop for about a quarter of a second, jump a few letters, and stop again. Vision is suppressed during each jump.

@art four-hundred-stops
So nearly all the reading happens in the stops, about four of them a second. A four hundred and fifty three word passage, at about two fifty a minute, is about four hundred stops.
