@art bookshelf
Twenty five thousand words a minute? Here's what each glance would need.

@math items="25,000 × 6 × 255 / 60,000 = 637.5@six hundred and thirty seven point five"
At ordinary fixations, each stop of the eyes would have to take in six hundred and thirty seven point five new characters. That's about ten lines of text at every glance.

@words text="Words are identified within about 8 characters"
One fixation identifies words within about eight characters. It sees about fifteen to the right.
