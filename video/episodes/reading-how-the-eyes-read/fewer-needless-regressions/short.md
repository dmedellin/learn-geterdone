title: Not all rereading is wasted
---

@art repair-vs-drift
Some of your rereading is wasted. Some of it is how you understand.

@words text="Repair · or drift"
A repair goes back because the sense didn't hold: a word misread, a sentence that turned. A lapse goes back because your mind drifted off the line.

@words text="Cut the drift, keep the repair"
Training away every look back trains away the repairs too. A pacer may help with drift. It can't make a repair unnecessary.
