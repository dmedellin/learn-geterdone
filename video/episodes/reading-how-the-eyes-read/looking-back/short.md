What if every word you read turned to x's the moment you left it?

@words text="Comprehension fell" sub="Schotter, Tran and Rayner (2014)"
Researchers did exactly that, with an eye tracker. Readers could still look back, but found nothing. Comprehension fell, for ordinary sentences as well as ambiguous ones.

@words text="Regressions came when they were needed"
In normal reading, the backward glances came when they were needed. They're not just wasted time.
