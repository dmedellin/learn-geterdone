@art crown-grab
What if breaking your word wins you a kingdom?

@words text="There is no such thing as Justice"
That's the Fool's case. Keeping covenants is only reasonable when it pays, he says, and sometimes it doesn't.

@art confederates
Hobbes's answer: nobody survives without allies. A man who breaks faith stays among them only by their errors, and errors are something no one can reasonably reckon on.
