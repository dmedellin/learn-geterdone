@art gift-box
Two sentences that sound alike. Only one of them gives anything away.

@math items="I will that this be thine tomorrow → transfers@I will that this be thine;;I will give it thee tomorrow → nothing yet@I will give it thee"
For Hobbes, I will that this be thine tomorrow gives away tomorrow's right today. But I will give it thee tomorrow is only a promise, a sign you haven't given yet.

@words text="Present words transfer · future words promise"
In a free gift, the tense decides whether the right has passed.
