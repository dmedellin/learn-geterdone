title: Why Hobbes says don't disarm alone
---

@art disarm-alone
Hobbes said lay down your weapons, but only when others do too.

@words text="When others are so too"
His second law of nature says a man should be willing to lay down his right to all things, when others are so too.

@words text="That were to expose himselfe to Prey"
The condition matters. If you disarmed alone, you'd only make yourself prey, and nobody is bound to do that.
