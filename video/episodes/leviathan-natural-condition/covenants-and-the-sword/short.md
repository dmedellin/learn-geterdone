title: Why a big penalty that rarely lands doesn't work
---

@art sword-over-two
A huge penalty can still fail to make anyone keep his word.

@math items="certain: least sanction 8@certain;;half the time: least sanction 16@half the time"
In the lesson's sale, the buyer saves eight by breaking. A certain penalty of eight is enough. But if it falls only half the time, you need sixteen.

@words text="Force sufficient to compel performance"
That's why Hobbes asks for a power with force sufficient to compel, not just a big sword that usually isn't there.
