@art foul-weather
You can be at war, Hobbes says, while asleep in your own bed.

@words text="Not a shower or two, but an inclination"
Foul weather isn't a shower or two of rain. It's an inclination to rain over many days. War is the same: not battle, but a known will to fight, with no assurance against it.

@art locked-door
And if you doubt it, ask why you lock your door at night.
