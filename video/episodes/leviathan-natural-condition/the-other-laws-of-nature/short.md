@art two-rows-of-choices
Forgiving one offence can leave you better off than getting even.

@math items="echo: 25 each@twenty five;;pardon: pardoner 27, offender 32@twenty seven"
In the lesson's game, answer a refusal with a refusal, and over ten rounds you both get twenty five. Let one refusal pass, and you settle into helping. You get twenty seven.

@words text="Pardon, on caution of the future"
That's Hobbes's sixth law: pardon those who repent and give assurance for the future.
