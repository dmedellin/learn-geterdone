title: The skim that "won" and lost the facts

@art scale-tipped
This skim beat a full reading, and lost two of three facts.

@math items="skim: 615 × 3/6 = 307.5@three hundred and seven point five;;read: 258 × 6/6 = 258@two fifty eight"
The skim's effective rate came to three hundred and seven point five. The full reading's was two fifty eight. But the skim kept one detail in three.

@words text="Gist suffers less than detail"
A skim doesn't lose evenly. Gist suffers less than detail. So choose by what you need from the text, not by the rate.
