title: Slowing down on a hard text isn't always a choice

@art shift-vs-collapse
You slowed down on the hard part. Did you choose to, or did it beat you?

@words text="Slower, and the facts kept"
A shift of gear is a slowdown that keeps the facts. If your rate fell and your questions on that section fell too, the text slowed you, and still won.

@art constant-speed
Carver found most readers seldom shift at all. They hold one rate, and when a text gets harder, their understanding gives way, not their speed.
