@art clock-late
Start the clock after your preview, and you look faster for the wrong reason.

@math items="433 × 60 / 150 = 173.2@Counted from the preview;;433 × 60 / 120 = 216.5@Leave the preview out"
A thirty second preview, then a hundred and twenty seconds of reading four thirty three words. Counted from the preview, a hundred and fifty seconds, that's one seventy three point two. Leave the preview out, and it jumps to two sixteen point five.

@words text="Always count the preview's time"
A preview pays only if you understand more, or it lets you skip a part.
