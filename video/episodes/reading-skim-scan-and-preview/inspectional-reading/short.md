@art preface-signpost
The page most readers skip is the one that tells them where to start.

@words text="The preface"
In the drill's imaginary book about apples, the preface says who it's for, and that a reader who wants only practical advice can begin with the fourth and fifth chapters and the calendar.

@words text="A reason not to start on page one"
That's what an inspection is for. Five minutes on the title, contents, preface and index, and you decide: read it all, read part, or put it back.
