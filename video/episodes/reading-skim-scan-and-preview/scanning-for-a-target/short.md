@art timetable-search
Nine hundred words a minute went past your eyes. You took in five.

@math items="613 × 60 / 40 = 919.5 words a minute@six hundred and thirteen"
A reader scanned a six hundred and thirteen word ferry timetable and found all five targets in forty seconds. That's nine hundred and nineteen point five words a minute passed over.

@words text="A search, not a reading"
But a scan hunts for a known form, like a time or a fare. It isn't reading for meaning, so its speed says nothing about your reading rate.
