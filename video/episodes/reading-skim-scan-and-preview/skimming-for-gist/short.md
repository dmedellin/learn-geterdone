Your skim score came from under a quarter of the text.

@math items="141 / 594 ≈ 23.7%@one hundred and forty one"
A forty second skim of a glacier passage showed one hundred and forty one of its five hundred and ninety four words. About twenty three point seven percent.

@words text="Score the gist, per minute"
So don't divide the whole passage by the time and call it a reading rate. Score a skim on gist answers per minute: three right in forty seconds is four point five.
