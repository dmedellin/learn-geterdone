@art flip-gate
Not of p and q is not the same as not p and not q.

@math items="¬(p ∧ q) ≡ ¬p ∨ ¬q@flips;;¬(p ∨ q) ≡ ¬p ∧ ¬q@or becomes and"
When a negation moves inside, the connective flips. Not of p and q is not p or not q. And the other way, or becomes and.

@words text="Forget the flip: wrong in 2 of 4 rows"
Forget the flip, and your formula is wrong in two of the four rows. That's De Morgan's law.
