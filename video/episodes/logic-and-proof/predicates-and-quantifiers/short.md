@art domain-fence
Is x squared always at least x? It depends where x lives.

@math items="∀x (x² ≥ x) over ℤ:  true@over the integers;;∀x (x² ≥ x) over ℝ:  false@over the reals;;x = ½:  ¼ < ½@one half"
Over the integers, it's true. Over the reals, it's false. Take x equals one half. Its square, one quarter, is less than one half.

@words text="The domain is part of the statement"
So the domain isn't background, it's part of the statement. Without it, there's no claim at all.
