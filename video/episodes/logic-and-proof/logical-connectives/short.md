Soup or salad? In logic, the answer can be both.

@math items="p ∨ q: true when at least one is true@at least one;;T and T  →  p ∨ q is T@both are true"
p or q is true when at least one of them is true. So when both are true, p or q is still true.

@words text="Exactly one? That's p ⊕ q"
If you mean exactly one, that's a different connective, exclusive or. The two differ in only that one row, both true.
