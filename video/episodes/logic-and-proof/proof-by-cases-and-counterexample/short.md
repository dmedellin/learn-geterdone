This formula gives a prime forty times in a row. Then it doesn't.

@math items="n² + n + 41:  prime for n = 0 to 39@zero to thirty nine;;n = 40:  1681 = 41²@at forty"
N squared plus n plus forty one is prime for every n from zero to thirty nine. At forty, it's one thousand six hundred and eighty one: forty one squared.

@words text="Checking is not proving"
Forty checks prove forty cases, and nothing more. One counterexample settles it.
