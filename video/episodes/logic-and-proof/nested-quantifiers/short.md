@art own-keys
Swap two words in logic, and a true statement becomes false.

@math items="∀x ∃y (y > x)  TRUE@every integer;;∃y ∀x (y > x)  FALSE@no single integer"
Over the integers, every integer has something larger. True. But no single integer is larger than every integer. False.

@words text="∀x ∃y: each x gets its own y" sub="∃y ∀x: one y for every x"
In the first, y can depend on x. In the second, one y has to work for all of them. Order decides the meaning.
