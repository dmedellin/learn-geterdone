@art odd-one-out
What's the opposite of all swans are white? It isn't no swan is white.

@math items="¬∀x (S(x) → W(x))@not every swan;;≡ ∃x (S(x) ∧ ¬W(x))@there exists a swan"
Negate it properly. Not every swan is white becomes there exists a swan that is not white. The for all flips to there exists, and the arrow becomes and.

@words text="Not all = some fail" sub="None = all fail"
No swan is white is a far stronger claim. Not all just means some fail.
