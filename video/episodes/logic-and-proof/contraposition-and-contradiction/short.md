title: Why the square root of two can't be a fraction
---

@art halved-bars
Can the square root of two be written as a fraction? Assume it can.

@math items="√2 = a/b, lowest terms@lowest terms;;a² = 2b²  ⟹  a even@a is even;;b² = 2c²  ⟹  b even@b is even"
Write it as a over b, in lowest terms. Squaring gives a squared equals two b squared, so a is even. Write a as two c, and you find b is even too.

@words text="Both even? Not lowest terms." sub="contradiction"
But then the fraction wasn't in lowest terms. The assumption fails, so the square root of two is irrational.
