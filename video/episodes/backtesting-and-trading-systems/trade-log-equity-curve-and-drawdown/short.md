@art peak-trough-recovery
Your account falls four point nine percent. You need more than that to recover.

@math items="1 − 49,427 ÷ 51,974 = 4.9%@one minus;;51,974 ÷ 49,427 − 1 = 5.2%@to recover"
From a peak of fifty one thousand, nine hundred and seventy four to a trough of forty nine thousand, four hundred and twenty seven: one minus trough over peak is four point nine percent. Peak over trough, minus one, to recover: five point two.

@words text="The climb starts lower"
The recovery is measured from the smaller trough, so it's always bigger.
