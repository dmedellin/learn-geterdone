@art compounding-steps
A trade makes seven point five percent, the next loses one point one. Net?

@math items="✗  7.5 − 1.1 = 6.4%@not six point four;;1.075 × 0.989 = 1.0632@one point zero seven five;;≈ +6.3%@six point three"
It's not six point four. Multiply the growth factors: one point zero seven five times zero point nine eight nine is one point zero six three two, about six point three percent.

@words text="Returns compound, they don't add"
The loss was taken on the bigger account the first trade left.
