title: Why buy strong stocks is not a trading rule
---

@art vague-cloud
Buy strong stocks. Sounds like a rule. A computer can't run it.

@words text="Strong compared with what?"
Strong has no threshold, no data field and no timing. Two people reading the same chart would make different trades.

@words text="Buy next open when close > prior 20-bar high"
Compare that with: buy at the next open when the close exceeds the prior twenty bar high. Data, threshold and timing, all stated.
