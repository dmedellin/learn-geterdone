@art narrow-peak
Your best setting returns eight percent. Its neighbours manage one point five.

@math items="1.5 ÷ 8.0 = 0.19@one point five divided by eight;;19% survives@nineteen percent"
One point five divided by eight is zero point one nine. Only nineteen percent of the result survives a small change in the parameter.

@art broad-plateau
A real effect shows up across nearby settings. A broad plateau beats a narrow peak.
