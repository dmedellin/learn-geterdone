@art peeking-leak
Your final test set stops being a test the moment you peek.

@words text="Look after every change → it becomes training data"
Check out of sample results after every change, and that data starts shaping your rules. It's no longer unseen.

@art three-segments
So split in time order, develop, select, then freeze the system completely. Only then look at the final test, once.
