@art gross-vs-net
Your strategy earns fourteen basis points a trade. It still loses money.

@math items="1 + 8 + 2 × 5 + 2.2 = 21.2 bps@twenty one point two;;14 − 21.2 = −7.2 bps@minus seven point two"
Commission one, spread eight, slippage five on each side, and impact two point two. That's twenty one point two basis points a round trip. Fourteen minus that is minus seven point two.

@words text="Small edges, many trades, big costs"
High turnover with a small edge is the most cost sensitive strategy there is.
