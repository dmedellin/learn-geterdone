title: Risk 1% without betting the account

@art risk-box
Fifty thousand dollar account, one percent risk. How many shares?

@math items="$50,000 × 1% = $500@five hundred;;$500 ÷ $2.00 = 250 shares@two hundred and fifty;;250 × $50 = $12,500@twelve thousand"
With a fifty dollar entry and a stop two dollars away: one percent is five hundred dollars. Divided by two dollars, that's two hundred and fifty shares, or twelve thousand five hundred dollars.

@words text="Size from the stop, not the account"
A quarter of the account, but only one percent at risk.
