@art five-in-six-out
Capitalists put five hundred in, and take six hundred out. Where's the extra hundred from?

@words text="They spend it in themselves"
Marx's answer sounds paradoxical. The capitalists throw it in themselves, not as capital, but as consumers, paying for their own households.

@math items="£500 + £100 spent = £600 returned@six hundred"
Five hundred advanced, plus a hundred spent on living, buys back a product worth six hundred.
