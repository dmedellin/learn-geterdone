Part of this capital turns over five times a year. The whole, barely once.

@math items="£8,000 + 5 · £20,000 = £108,000@a hundred and eight;;108,000 / 100,000 = 27/25@twenty seven"
Marx's machine of eighty thousand returns eight thousand a year. Twenty thousand circulating goes round five times. That's a hundred and eight thousand on a hundred thousand: twenty seven twenty fifths turnovers.

@words text="The machine sets the pace"
Four fifths of the capital is in a machine, so the whole moves slowly.
