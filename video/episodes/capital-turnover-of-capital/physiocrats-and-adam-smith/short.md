@art two-sorts
Two capitals, split exactly the same way. Different surplus value.

@math items="farm:  £200 seed + £200 wages → s = £200@the farm;;mine:  £150 coal + £250 wages → s = £250@the mine"
A farm and a mine both have six hundred fixed and four hundred circulating. But the farm pays two hundred in wages, and the mine two hundred and fifty. At a hundred per cent, that's two hundred of surplus against two hundred and fifty.

@words text="Only wages create value"
Smith put wages beside cotton. Marx says that hides the part that makes the surplus.
