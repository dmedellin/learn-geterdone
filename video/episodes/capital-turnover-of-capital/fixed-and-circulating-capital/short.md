title: The ox that stops being capital when you eat it

@art ox-two-ways
An ox is fixed capital, until you eat it.

@words text="Fixed: value leaves piece by piece"
Marx's test isn't what a thing is, or whether it moves. It's how its value goes into the product. A ploughing ox passes on its value bit by bit, while it keeps working.

@art coal-and-cotton
Eaten, it's no longer an instrument of production at all. Coal and cotton pass on their whole value at once, so they're circulating capital.
