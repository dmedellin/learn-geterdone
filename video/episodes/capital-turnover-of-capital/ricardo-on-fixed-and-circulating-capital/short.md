@art twin-capitals
Two businesses, identical machinery, identical size. One makes twice the surplus.

@math items="A:  £250 wages → s = £250@two hundred and fifty;;B:  £500 wages → s = £500@five hundred"
Both are a thousand pounds, a quarter fixed. A pays two hundred and fifty in wages, B five hundred. At a hundred per cent, that's two hundred and fifty of surplus value against five hundred.

@words text="The wage bill decides, not durability"
Ricardo's fixed and circulating split can't tell them apart.
