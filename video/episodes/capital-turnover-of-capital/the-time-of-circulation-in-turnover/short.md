@art india-voyage
Nothing changed in the factory. Its capital now turns over half as often.

@math items="near market:  26 weeks → twice a year@twice;;India:  52 weeks → once a year@once"
Four months of production, and a market a month away each way: the capital turns over twice a year. Ship to India, four months out and four back, and it's once.

@words text="Distance is measured in weeks"
The voyage ties up the producer's own capital, as cloth on a ship and money on its way home.
