title: Four hundred pounds that turns over sixteen hundred

@art same-coins
Sixteen hundred pounds goes through this business. Its capital is four hundred.

@math items="n = 52 / 13 = 4@fifty two;;4 · £400 = £1,600@sixteen hundred"
A thirteen week turnover fits into a fifty two week year four times. So four hundred pounds advanced turns over sixteen hundred pounds of value.

@words text="Same £400, four times round"
But it's the same four hundred going round. Turnovers multiply the value passing through, not the capital.
