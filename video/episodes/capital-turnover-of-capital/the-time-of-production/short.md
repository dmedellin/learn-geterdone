title: The tannery that sped up without saving an hour

@art tanning-pits
A new machine cut tanning from a year to eight weeks. It saved no labour.

@math items="old:  52 + 4 weeks → 13/14 a year@thirteen fourteenths;;air-pump:  8 + 4 weeks → 13/3 a year@thirteen thirds"
In the old pits, the acid took a year to soak in. With the air pump, eight weeks. Four weeks of work and four of selling either way. The turnovers go from thirteen fourteenths a year to thirteen thirds.

@words text="The capital waited less"
The work didn't change. Only nature's share of the time did.
