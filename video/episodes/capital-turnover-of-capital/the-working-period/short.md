@art yarn-vs-loco
A spinner and a locomotive builder spend the same each week. One needs twelve times the capital.

@math items="spinnery:  £100 · 1 = £100@the spinner;;locomotive:  £100 · 12 = £1,200@the locomotive"
At a hundred pounds a week, the spinner sells every week and starts again. The locomotive takes twelve weeks, so twelve hundred is tied up before anything comes back.

@words text="Twelve times the capital"
Marx calls those joined days a working period.
