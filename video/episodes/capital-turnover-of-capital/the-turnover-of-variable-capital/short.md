title: Same exploitation, a thousand per cent versus a hundred
---

@art same-workers
Two firms, same workers, same exploitation. One shows a thousand per cent, the other a hundred.

@math items="A:  £500 · 10 turnovers → 1,000%@ten times;;B:  £5,000 · 1 turnover → 100%@once"
Both pay a hundred a week and make five thousand of surplus value a year. A advances five hundred and turns it over ten times. B advances five thousand, once.

@words text="S′ = s′ · n"
The annual rate is the real rate times the turnovers. It measures the money advanced, not the work.
