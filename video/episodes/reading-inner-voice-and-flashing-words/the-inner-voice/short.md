@art voice-brake
Speed reading courses say your inner voice holds you to speaking pace. Check the numbers.

@list items="Silent: about 238@two hundred and thirty eight|Aloud: about 183@a hundred and eighty three"
Brysbaert's review puts silent reading of nonfiction at about two hundred and thirty eight words a minute, and reading aloud at about a hundred and eighty three.

@words text="Silent ≈ 1.3 × aloud"
So most people already read silently about one point three times as fast as they speak. The voice isn't a brake set at speaking pace.
