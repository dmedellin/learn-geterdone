Someone says they read fifteen hundred words a minute. Here's how to check.

@list items="A passage they haven't seen@haven't seen|Questions someone else wrote@someone else wrote|A comparison reader@comparison"
Give them a passage they haven't seen, with questions someone else wrote, and a comparison reader on the same test.

@words text="None beyond about 600 wpm" sub="Carver (1985)"
When Carver tested some of the world's best readers that way, none showed comprehension beyond about six hundred words a minute.
