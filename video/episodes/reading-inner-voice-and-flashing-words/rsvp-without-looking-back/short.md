@words text="The dog walked past the gate barked."
The dog walked past the gate barked. Read it again. You just did something a word stream won't let you do.

@words text="the dog that was walked past the gate barked"
It means the dog that was walked past the gate barked. On a page, one glance back repairs it.

@art garden-path
In a stream, the next words are already coming. And looking back helps with ordinary sentences too.
