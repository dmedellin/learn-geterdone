Speed reading courses say you can see a whole phrase in one glance. Can you?

@words text="The eyes still fixate most content words"
In normal reading, the eyes still land on most content words. And words are identified only about seven to eight letters to the right of a fixation.

@words text="Chunking groups by meaning, not by glance"
Grouping words by meaning might help. It doesn't widen what one glance takes in.
