@art word-box
Speed reading apps remove your eye movements. That saves you about a tenth.

@math items="saccade 30 ms of 255 ms ≈ 11.8%@thirty of every"
In the eye model, the jumps between stops take thirty of every two hundred and fifty five milliseconds. About eleven point eight percent.

@art fixation-time
Nearly all reading time is spent in the stops, identifying words. And a flashed word still needs that time.
