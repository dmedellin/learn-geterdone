title: The spread that leaves you holding stock
---

@art split-legs
A defined-risk spread can still leave you holding stock on Monday.

@words text="Stock between the strikes"
It happens when the stock finishes between the two strikes, so one leg is in the money and one is out. One leg can be exercised or assigned, while the other just expires.

@art auto-exercise
In the money options may be exercised automatically, but broker policies and instructions can change that. So review the legs before the firm's deadline.
