title: Time decay is not a straight line
---

@art decay-curve
An option doesn't lose the same amount every day. It speeds up.

@words text="Extrinsic value → 0 at expiration"
Hold the stock and volatility still. The option's extrinsic value still runs down to zero at expiration.

@art steepening-days
But the path isn't straight. It's gentle with lots of time left, and steepens as expiry nears. So the daily decay, theta, grows toward the end.
