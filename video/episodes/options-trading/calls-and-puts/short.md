@art short-call-cliff
One of the four basic option positions can lose without any limit.

@math items="Short 100 call, $4.00 premium@one hundred call;;Breakeven = $104@one hundred and four;;At $110: (4 − 10) × 100 = −$600@one hundred and ten"
Sell a one hundred call for four dollars. Your breakeven is one hundred and four. At one hundred and ten, you're down six hundred dollars.

@words text="Short call: loss uncapped"
Above the breakeven, every dollar the stock rises is the writer's loss, with no ceiling. That's the short call.
