@art event-spike
The stock went your way, and your call still lost money.

@words text="IV prices size, not direction"
Implied volatility prices how big a move might be, not which way. Before a scheduled event it can rise. After the event, it can fall fast.

@math items="Change = stock part + IV part@two parts;;Stock up: +;;IV crush: −, and bigger@bigger"
So a premium change has two parts. The stock rising adds value. But if the IV crush is bigger, the call still loses.
