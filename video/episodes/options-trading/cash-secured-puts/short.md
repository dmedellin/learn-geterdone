@art cash-reserve
You get paid four hundred dollars, and ten thousand dollars gets locked up.

@math items="Short 100 put at $4.00@one hundred put;;Cash reserved: $10,000@ten thousand;;Max profit: $400@four hundred;;Stock to zero: −$9,600@ninety six hundred"
Sell a one hundred put for four dollars. You reserve ten thousand dollars to buy the shares. The most you can make is four hundred. If the stock goes to zero, you lose ninety six hundred.

@words text="Effective price: 100 − 4 = $96"
Your effective purchase price is ninety six.
