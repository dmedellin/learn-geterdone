@art credit-payoff
This trade makes two hundred and fifty dollars at most. It can lose seven fifty.

@math items="Short 100 put $4.00 · long 90 put $1.50@one fifty;;Credit = $2.50 → max profit $250@two fifty;;Max loss = (10 − 2.50) × 100 = $750@seven fifty"
Sell the one hundred put at four, buy the ninety put at one fifty. The credit is two fifty, the most you keep. The width is ten, so the most you lose is seven fifty.

@words text="Weigh the credit against the worst case"
A high win rate means little without that comparison.
