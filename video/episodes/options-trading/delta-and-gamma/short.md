@art tangent-gap
Delta said this call would make two sixty seven. It made three twenty two.

@math items="Delta: 0.534 × $5 × 100 = +$267@zero point five three four;;Full repricing: +$322@three twenty two"
The stock went from one hundred to one hundred and five. Delta, zero point five three four, predicted two sixty seven. The full repricing gave three twenty two.

@words text="Gamma: the slope changes as you go"
Delta is a slope, and the slope rose along the way, to zero point seven four three. Gamma is the correction.
