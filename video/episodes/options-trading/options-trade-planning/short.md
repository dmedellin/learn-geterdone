title: Don't start an options trade with the option chain
---

@art thesis-first
Starting an options trade from the option chain gets the order backwards.

@words text="Thesis → structure → contract → risk → exit"
Start with the thesis: direction, target, invalidation and duration. Then the structure, then the contract, then the risk, then the exit.

@art risk-scale
And write the risk limits before entry. If you change them later just to avoid a loss, they're worth nothing.
