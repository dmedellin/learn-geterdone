title: A covered call is not downside protection
---

@art downside-kept
Selling a covered call doesn't protect your shares from a fall.

@math items="Stock falls $20: shares −$20@twenty dollars;;Call premium kept: + premium@premium;;P/L = (premium − 20) × 100@times one hundred"
If the stock drops twenty dollars, the shares lose twenty a share. The call expires and you keep the premium. That's the premium minus twenty, times one hundred.

@words text="Upside capped · downside kept"
The premium offsets only its own amount. You've capped your upside and kept the downside.
