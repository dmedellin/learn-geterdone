title: The debit spread breakeven most people get wrong
---

@art spread-payoff
Your call spread's breakeven isn't the long strike plus the long premium.

@math items="Long 100 call $5.50 · short 110 call $2.00@five fifty;;Net debit = $3.50@three fifty;;Breakeven = 100 + 3.50 = $103.50@one hundred and three fifty"
Buy the one hundred call at five fifty, sell the one ten at two. The net debit is three fifty, so the breakeven is one hundred and three fifty.

@words text="Long strike + net debit"
The short leg lowered the debit, so it lowered the breakeven too.
