@art round-trip
You can lose fifteen dollars on an option before it moves at all.

@math items="Bid $3.31 · ask $3.46@three thirty one;;Buy at the ask: $346@three hundred and forty six;;Sell at the bid: $331@three hundred and thirty one"
With a bid of three thirty one and an ask of three forty six, a market buy pays three hundred and forty six dollars. Sell straight back and you get three hundred and thirty one.

@words text="Round trip = the whole spread"
That fifteen dollar gap is the spread, paid on the way in and again on the way out.
