title: That option doesn't cost four dollars
---

@art quote-to-dollars
That option quoted at four dollars? It costs four hundred.

@math items="$4.00 per share@quoted per share;;$4.00 × 100 = $400 per contract@one hundred shares"
Equity option premiums are normally quoted per share. A standard contract commonly represents one hundred shares, so four dollars becomes four hundred dollars a contract.

@words text="Premium × multiplier × quantity"
So multiply the quote by the multiplier and by the number of contracts. And check for adjusted contracts, where the deliverable can be different.
