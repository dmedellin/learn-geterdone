title: What a $9.20 option is really made of
---

@art split-bars
This option costs nine dollars twenty. Only eight dollars of it is intrinsic value.

@math items="100 call, stock $108@stock at one hundred and eight;;Intrinsic = $8.00@eight dollars;;Extrinsic = 9.20 − 8.00 = $1.20@one dollar twenty"
A one hundred call with the stock at one hundred and eight has eight dollars of intrinsic value. The rest, one dollar twenty, is extrinsic.

@words text="Expiration removes the $1.20"
If the stock is still at one hundred and eight at expiration, the call is worth eight. The clock takes the one twenty.
