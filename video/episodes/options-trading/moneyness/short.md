@art atm-nearest
Your chain says at the money. It might be two dollars in the money.

@math items="Stock $102 · chain “ATM” strike: 100@one hundred and two;;Call intrinsic: 102 − 100 = $2.00@two dollars"
With the stock at one hundred and two, a chain calls the nearest strike, one hundred, at the money. But that call's intrinsic value is one hundred and two minus one hundred: two dollars.

@words text="Strictly ATM: strike = stock"
Strictly, at the money means the strike equals the stock. The chain's label is only a convention.
