title: Below breakeven doesn't mean losing
---

@art curve-over-kink
Your call is below breakeven, weeks before expiry. Is it losing money?

@words text="Breakeven = strike + premium" sub="at expiration"
Not necessarily. Strike plus premium is the breakeven at expiration, and only then.

@art exit-model
Weeks earlier, time and implied volatility are still in the price. So the option can be worth more, or less, than the expiration line says. An early exit is priced by time and volatility.
