@art pulled-to-middle
One survey question worded backwards can make an unhappy team look average.

@math items="reversed = 8 − answer@eight minus the answer;;r6: 2.0 → 3.5 if you forget@forget"
If agreeing with a statement is bad news, reverse it first: on a scale of seven, eight minus the answer. Forget, and a respondent who scored two point oh shows up as three point five.

@words text="Reverse before you average"
Everyone gets pulled toward the middle, and the people who differ most disappear.
