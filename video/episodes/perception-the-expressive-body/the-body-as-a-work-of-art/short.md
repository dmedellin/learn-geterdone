@art same-scribble
Sign your name with your fingers, your whole arm, or your foot in sand. What stays the same?

@math items="limbs: all vary@all vary;;shapes, stroke order, rhythm: all stay@stay"
Not a single muscle. The limbs all vary. The shapes, the stroke order and the rhythm stay.

@words text="Not a machine, but a work of art"
That's Merleau-Ponty's point. Your body hangs together like a painting's colours carry its meaning: a nexus of living meanings, not a machine.
