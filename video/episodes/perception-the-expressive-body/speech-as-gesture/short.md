@art brush-dusk
In the half light you say: it's a brush. Did you recognise it first, then name it?

@words text="Naming is itself recognition"
Merleau-Ponty says no. There's no concept of a brush you consult first. The denomination of objects does not follow upon recognition. It is itself recognition.

@quote text="Thus speech, in the speaker, does not translate ready-made thought, but accomplishes it." cite="Merleau-Ponty, Phenomenology of Perception, Part One, Chapter 6, p. 207"
So speech doesn't translate a finished thought. It accomplishes it.
