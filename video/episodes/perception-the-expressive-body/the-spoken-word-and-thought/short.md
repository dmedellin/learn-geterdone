title: Is there a universal sign of anger?
---

@art three-gestures
Is there one bodily sign of anger everyone shares?

@quote text="The angry Japanese smiles, the westerner goes red and stamps his foot or else goes pale and hisses his words." cite="Merleau-Ponty, Phenomenology of Perception, Part One, Chapter 6, p. 219"
Merleau-Ponty's answer is this. A smile, a flush and a stamp, pallor and a hiss. No sign is in all three.

@words text="In man there is no natural sign"
So in man there is no natural sign. Feelings are invented like words, and anger is still read in every one of them.
