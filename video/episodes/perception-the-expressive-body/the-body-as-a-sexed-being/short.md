title: She lost her voice. She wasn't keeping silent.
---

@art flat-wave
Forbidden to see the man she loved, a girl lost her voice.

@words text="Not a paralysis · not a silence"
It wasn't a paralysis: left free to see him, she spoke again. And it wasn't a choice. One keeps silence only when one can speak.

@art collapsed-field
Merleau-Ponty says she lost her voice as one loses a memory. Not one option refused, but the whole field of possibilities collapsing at once.
