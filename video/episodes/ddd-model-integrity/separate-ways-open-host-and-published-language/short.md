title: Six systems, fifteen translators. Or six.

@art square-growth
Six systems all talking to each other need fifteen translators.

@math items="6 × 5 / 2 = 15@fifteen;;one each: 6@one translator"
Point to point, every pair needs its own: six times five, over two, is fifteen. Give them one shared, documented language instead, and each needs one translator. Six.

@words text="Published Language"
And when one system changes, you fix one translator, not five. That's Evans's Published Language.
