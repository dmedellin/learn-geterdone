@art two-concessions
Half your team says concession and means discounts. The other half means ice cream.

@words text="concession: discounted price · concession: snack sale"
In a theatre, both meanings are real. Two halves of one team stopped talking for a month, and each took one.

@art two-levels-merge
A green build won't catch that. Evans's Continuous Integration works at two levels: merge the code often, and hammer out the language just as often.
