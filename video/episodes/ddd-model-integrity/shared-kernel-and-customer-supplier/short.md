title: Why a Shared Kernel should stay small
---

@art kernel-overlap
Sharing code between two teams saves translation. So why does Evans keep it small?

@art kernel-two-way
A Shared Kernel has two owners, and neither is upstream. So a change by either team reaches the other, and everything the other supplies.

@words text="Changed only by agreement"
That's why every kernel change needs consultation, and both teams' tests. Share a carefully chosen part. Reduce duplication, don't end it.
