title: A breakout on huge volume can still fail
---

@art failed-break
Price breaks resistance on huge volume. Then it closes back inside the range.

@words text="Failed breakout"
That's a failed breakout. Buyers were active, but they couldn't maintain higher prices. And because the volume was high and the level still held, it's more informative than a weak break.

@words text="Volume supports · acceptance validates"
Volume supports a break. Acceptance validates it: closes, follow through, or a retest outside the level.
