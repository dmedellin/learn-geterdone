@art reject-above
Price pokes above the value area high. Is that a breakout?

@words text="It depends on what happens next"
It depends on what happens next. If price stays up there and trade keeps building, that's acceptance, led by buyers.

@art accept-above
If it slips straight back inside, that's rejection. The higher prices attracted little trade. Acceptance needs time, or follow through, outside value.
