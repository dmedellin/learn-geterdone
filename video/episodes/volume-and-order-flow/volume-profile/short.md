title: Volume profile can't tell you who won
---

@art both-sides
A volume profile shows where trading happened. Can it show who was buying?

@words text="By itself, no"
By itself, no. A profile sums all the volume at each price, and every trade has a buyer and a seller. Both sides land in the same bin.

@art poc-bar
So the biggest bin, the Point of Control, shows where both sides were most willing to trade. It doesn't show who won.
