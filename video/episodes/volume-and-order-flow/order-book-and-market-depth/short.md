title: That huge ask in the order book just vanished
---

@art vanishing-ask
A huge ask sits above the market. Price climbs toward it, and it vanishes.

@words text="Cancelled, never tested"
It was cancelled or moved. It never executed, so it never provided any resistance, and it never became volume.

@words text="Intentions, not control"
The order book shows intentions, not completed control. A wall matters only if it stays, and absorbs the orders that hit it.
