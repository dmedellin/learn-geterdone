title: Strong volume is not a trading rule

@art vague-vs-measurable
Strong volume. Sounds like a rule. It isn't. Strong compared with what?

@words text="RVOL ≥ 1.5 over the prior 20 comparable bars"
Without a baseline and a threshold, two people reading the same bar would disagree. A measurable condition looks like this: relative volume of at least one and a half, over the prior twenty comparable bars.

@words text="Quantity · operator · baseline"
A quantity, an operator, and a baseline. Then anyone can check it.
