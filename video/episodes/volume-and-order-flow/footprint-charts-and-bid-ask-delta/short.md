@art split-levels
This candle's delta is zero. Did nobody take control inside it?

@math items="(500 − 200) + (300 − 300) + (100 − 400)@five hundred minus two hundred;;= 300 + 0 − 300 = 0@cancel out"
Look level by level. At the top, five hundred minus two hundred is plus three hundred. In the middle, zero. At the bottom, minus three hundred. They cancel out.

@words text="Buyers at the top · sellers at the bottom"
So the aggression was split: buyers at the top, sellers at the bottom. The total hid it.
