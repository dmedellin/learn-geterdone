Two candles. One closes near its high, one near its low. Same story?

@math items="((108 − 100) − (110 − 108)) ÷ 10 = 0.6@close minus low;;0.6 × 1,000 = +600@six hundred"
Not for money flow. High one ten, low one hundred, close one oh eight. Close minus low, minus high minus close, over the range, is point six. Times a thousand contracts, that's plus six hundred.

@words text="Inferred from the bar, not the trades"
Remember it's an inference from the bar, not a count of real buyers.
