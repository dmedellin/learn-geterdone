@art obv-steps
Four bars, and you can work out On Balance Volume in your head.

@math items="101 > 100 → +800@add eight hundred;;100 < 101 → −300@subtract three hundred;;102 > 100 → +400@add four hundred;;OBV = +900@nine hundred"
Closes of one hundred, one oh one, one hundred, one oh two, on volumes of five, eight, three and four hundred. The first bar has no prior close. Then add eight hundred, subtract three hundred, add four hundred. That's nine hundred.
