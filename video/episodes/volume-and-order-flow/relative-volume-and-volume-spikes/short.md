@art spike-stall
Volume just exploded, and price barely moved. What does that tell you?

@words text="A spike says activity changed"
A spike only says activity changed. Price response says what it meant.

@art spike-progress
If price makes clear progress, that supports the aggressive side. If it stalls or reverses, it suggests absorption, or a temporary climax. The orders are active, but they aren't moving price far.
