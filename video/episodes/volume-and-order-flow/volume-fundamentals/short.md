title: How much of this volume was buying?
---

@art one-trade-two-sides
A bar prints five thousand contracts. How many were bought?

@math items="bought = 5,000@five thousand bought;;sold = 5,000@five thousand sold"
All of them. Five thousand bought, and five thousand sold. Every contract that trades has a buyer and a seller, so volume counts participation, not direction.

@art rising-expanding
Direction comes from price. Buyers are in control only when they keep accepting higher prices.
