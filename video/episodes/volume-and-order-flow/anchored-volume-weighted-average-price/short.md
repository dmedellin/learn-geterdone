@art session-vs-anchor
Anchored VWAP uses exactly the same formula as VWAP. So what's different?

@words text="Only the starting point"
Only the starting point. Instead of the session open, the sums begin at an event you choose, like a major low, a gap, or a breakout.

@art many-anchors
And that choice has to come first. Move the anchor until the chart agrees, and you've turned a measurement into a drawing.
