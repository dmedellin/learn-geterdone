title: Rising price, falling volume: is it about to reverse?
---

@art up-contracting
Price keeps rising, but volume keeps shrinking. Is a reversal coming?

@words text="Upward progress, weakening participation"
Not necessarily. Buyers are still moving price higher. There are simply fewer participants behind the move.

@words text="Weaker evidence ≠ reversal"
Thin participation lowers the evidence for the trend. It doesn't reverse it, and trends on falling volume can last a long time. Direction comes from price.
