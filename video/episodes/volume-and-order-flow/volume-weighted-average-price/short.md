@art weighting
Prices of one hundred and one oh two. Why isn't VWAP one oh one?

@math items="(100 × 100 + 102 × 300) ÷ 400@one hundred contracts;;= 40,600 ÷ 400 = 101.50@one oh one point five"
Because volume counts. One hundred contracts traded at one hundred, but three hundred at one oh two. Weight each price by its volume, divide by four hundred, and you get one oh one point five, closer to where most of the trading happened.
