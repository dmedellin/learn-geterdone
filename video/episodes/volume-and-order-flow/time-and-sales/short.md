@art absorption
Trade after trade at the ask, and price won't budge. What's going on?

@words text="Possible sell-side absorption"
Possibly absorption. Every one of those prints had a seller on the other side. Passive sellers may be replenishing enough liquidity to stop the move.

@art prints-rising
Fast buying matters only when it moves or holds price higher. Aggression is the effort. Price is the result.
