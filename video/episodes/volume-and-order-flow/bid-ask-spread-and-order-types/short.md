title: What a limit order actually guarantees
---

@art limit-resting
You place a limit buy. What does it actually guarantee you?

@words text="A maximum price, not execution"
A maximum purchase price. Not a fill. It rests until a seller trades at or below your price, and if nobody does, it never fills.

@words text="Market: fill first · Limit: price first"
A market order is the opposite trade off. It prioritises execution, but it doesn't guarantee the price you saw.
