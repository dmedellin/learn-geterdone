@art fermat-blind
Five sixty one fools the Fermat primality test on every base coprime to it.

@words text="561 = 3 · 11 · 17"
It's a Carmichael number. a to the five sixty, mod five sixty one, is one for all three hundred and eighteen coprime bases.

@math items="strong test:  550 of 558 bases are witnesses@five hundred and fifty"
The strong test also watches the squaring chain, and it catches five sixty one with five hundred and fifty of its five hundred and fifty eight bases.
