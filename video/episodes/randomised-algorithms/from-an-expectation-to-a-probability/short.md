title: A probability bound of 1.018 is true and useless

@art bound-vs-truth
Markov's inequality says this probability is at most one point zero one eight.

@math items="P(C ≥ 24) = 332/675 ≈ 0.492@the true chance;;Markov: E/a = 30791/30240 ≈ 1.018@Markov allows"
For quicksort on ten keys, the true chance of twenty four or more comparisons is about point four nine two. Markov allows one point zero one eight.

@words text="Compare every bound with one"
Every probability is at most one anyway, so that bound says nothing. Check before you quote it.
