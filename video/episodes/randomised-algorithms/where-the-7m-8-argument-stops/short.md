@art repeated-literal
x one or x one or x two looks like a normal clause. It breaks the formula.

@math items="x₁ ∨ x₁ ∨ x₂:  3/4, not 7/8@three quarters;;mean 27/8  ≠  7m/8 = 7/2@twenty seven eighths"
It has only two distinct variables, so a random assignment satisfies it three quarters of the time, not seven eighths. On four clauses the true mean is twenty seven eighths, not seven halves.

@words text="Keep the sum, per clause"
Add each clause's own share. That's right for every formula.
