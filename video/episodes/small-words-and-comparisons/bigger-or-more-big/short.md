@art beat-dots
Bigger, but more beautiful. Count one thing, and you'll always know which.

@math items="big  ·  1 part  →  bigger@Big has one part;;happy  ·  2 parts, -y  →  happier@Happy has two"
Count the parts, the beats of the word. Big has one part, so bigger. Happy has two and ends in y, so the y becomes i: happier.

@words text="beautiful  →  more beautiful" sub="3 parts: more, every time"
Beautiful has three parts, so it takes more. In the novel the lab counts, not one word of three parts takes an ending.
