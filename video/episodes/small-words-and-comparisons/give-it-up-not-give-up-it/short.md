@art pronoun-middle
Give it up, but look at it. Why does the it move?

@words text="give it up" sub="up is a particle: the pronoun goes between"
Up is a particle. It joins the verb to make one action, and the pronoun goes in the middle. Give it up. Find it out.

@words text="look at it" sub="at is a preposition: the pronoun goes after"
At is a preposition. The pronoun comes after it. Look at it. Think of it. So it's give it up, and never give up it.
