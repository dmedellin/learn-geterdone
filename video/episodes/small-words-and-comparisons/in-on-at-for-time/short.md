@art named-day
In the morning, but on Monday morning. Why does the small word change?

@words text="in the morning" sub="a part of the day"
A morning on its own takes in. In the morning. In the evening.

@words text="on Monday morning" sub="the named day wins"
But name the day, and the day decides. A day takes on, so it's on Monday morning, and on the following morning.

@words text="on Sunday night" sub="the same step reaches night"
The same step works for night. At night, but on Sunday night.
