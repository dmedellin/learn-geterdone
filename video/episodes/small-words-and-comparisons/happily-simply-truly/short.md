@art four-stacks
Angryly, capablely, dramaticly. Just adding l-y gets twenty-eight words wrong.

@words text="98 of 126" sub="plain -ly, right 77.8%"
In the lab, plain l-y is right on ninety-eight of a hundred and twenty-six adjectives. The misses group by their ending.

@math items="angry  →  angrily@Angry becomes;;capable  →  capably@Capable becomes;;dramatic  →  dramatically@Dramatic becomes"
Angry becomes angrily. Capable becomes capably. Dramatic becomes dramatically. Look at the ending first, and the rule is right on all hundred and twenty-six.
