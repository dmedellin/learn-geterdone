title: Falling profits are a sign of prosperity, said Smith
---

@art dutch-canal
When profits fall, is trade decaying? Adam Smith said the opposite.

@words text="The Dutch trade upon lower profits than any people in Europe"
The Dutch, he wrote, trade upon lower profits than any people in Europe, and their government borrowed at two per cent.

@art competing-stocks
Low profit there wasn't decline. More and more stock was competing in the same trades. The diminution of profit is the natural effect of prosperity.
