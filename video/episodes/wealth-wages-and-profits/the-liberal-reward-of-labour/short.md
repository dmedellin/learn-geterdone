title: Why Adam Smith blamed overwork for idle days

@art four-and-three
Why do workers take three days off? Adam Smith blamed the other four.

@words text="Excessive application, during four days of the week"
Paid well by the piece, he said, workmen overwork. And excessive application during four days of the week is frequently the real cause of the idleness of the other three.

@art piece-work
The man who works moderately enough to work constantly keeps his health longest, and does the most work in a year.
