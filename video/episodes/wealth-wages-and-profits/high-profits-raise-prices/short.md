@art simple-vs-compound
Adam Smith said high profits push prices up faster than high wages. Here's why.

@words text="Wages: simple interest · Profit: compound interest"
A wage rise adds to the price once, for each worker and each day. But a profit rise is charged at every stage, on everything advanced, including the extra profits of the stages before.

@art stacking-profit
So wages work like simple interest, and profit like compound interest. And the merchants, he noted, blamed only wages.
