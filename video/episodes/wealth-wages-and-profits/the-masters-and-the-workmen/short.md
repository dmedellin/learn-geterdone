title: Why Adam Smith thought the masters usually win
---

@art waiting-glasses
In a wage dispute, Adam Smith said, the side that can wait longest wins.

@words text="A week · a month · a year"
Many workmen couldn't live a week without work, few a month, and scarce any a year. The masters could hold out far longer. In the long run each side needs the other, but the workman's need is more immediate.

@art bargaining-scale
The masters are also fewer, so they combine more easily, and the law left their combinations alone while it forbade the workmen's.
