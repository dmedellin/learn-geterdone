A posting rule moves five per cent of each ticket entry to a restoration fund. Twenty point six two five rounds up. So does four point eight one five.

@words text="Rounding left: −0.01"
Two half cents, both rounded the same way. A rule that rounds each entry on its own drifts, a little. Your experts decide whether that matters.
