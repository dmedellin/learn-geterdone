Same lever, same deaths, same five saved. So why do the cases feel different?

@math items="switch:  F = E@In the switch;;loop:  F = E and H@In the loop"
In the switch, the five survive if the trolley turns. In the loop, they survive only if it turns and hits the man.

@art flip-loop
Flip the harm. In the switch, the five still live, so his death is a side effect. In the loop, they die, so it's the means.
