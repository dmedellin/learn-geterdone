title: Why facts alone never prove a duty
---

@art counterexample-row
Jones promised to repay Smith. Does it follow that he ought to?

@math items="p: Jones promised@he promised;;q: Smith isn't repaid@hasn't been repaid;;p = T,  q = T,  o = F@both facts are true"
Not by logic alone. He promised, and Smith hasn't been repaid. But there's a row where both facts are true and the duty is false.

@art gap-bridge
To close the gap you need a bridge: if he promised and hasn't repaid, he ought to. And that premise is where the real argument lives.
