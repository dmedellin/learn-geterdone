title: Is the slippery slope really a fallacy?

@art staircase
The slippery slope isn't a fallacy. It's a perfectly valid argument.

@math items="F(40)@Forty weeks;;F(k) → F(k − 1)@one week less;;∴ F(0)@So none"
Forty weeks' notice is adequate. One week less never makes a difference. So none is adequate. Every step follows.

@art fading-bars
So the real question is which premise fails. Maybe each step costs just a fortieth. Too little to notice, and enough to lose it all.
