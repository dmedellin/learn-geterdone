title: Is more equal always better?
---

@art levelling-down
Can making things more equal help absolutely no one?

@math items="A = 10, 2:  Gini 1/3@ten and two;;B = 2, 2:  Gini 0@two and two"
Take ten and two, and bring the ten down: two and two. Inequality falls from one third to zero.

@words text="Better for no one"
But nobody gains. The person at two stays at two. That's levelling down, and it's why priority for the worse off isn't the same as equality.
