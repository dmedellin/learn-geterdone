title: Why a lie can't be a universal law
---

@art collapsed-practice
A lie pays only because almost everyone else tells the truth.

@math items="alone:  liar 5, honest 2@Among nine honest;;everyone lies:  liar 0@if everyone lies"
Among nine honest people, a false promise earns five where honesty earns two. But if everyone lies, no promise is believed, and the liar gets nothing.

@words text="The lie destroys what it feeds on"
That's Kant's test. It isn't asking whether the world gets worse. Willed as a universal law, the maxim defeats its own aim.
