@art two-doors
Two promises, both at six, on opposite sides of town. Is that even possible?

@list items="You ought to keep each@ought to keep each|Oughts combine@combine|Ought implies can@ought implies can|You can't keep both@can't keep both"
You ought to keep each. Oughts combine. Ought implies can. And you can't keep both. Those four claims together can't all be true.

@art drop-one
So something has to give. Each way out drops exactly one sentence, and every one of them has a price someone won't pay.
