Pulling the lever and pushing the man have exactly the same numbers.

@math items="divert −1,  do nothing −5@divert;;push −1,  do nothing −5@push"
Divert: one dies, against five. Push: one dies, against five. Any rule that reads only the numbers gives the same answer.

@art struck-row
So the difference has to come from something else: a side constraint that strikes pushing from the table before anything is weighed.
