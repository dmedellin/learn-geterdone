Aristotle's function argument, as usually told, isn't valid.

@math items="if f then g,  r  ∴  g@If reasoning;;f = F,  g = F,  r = T@a row"
If reasoning is our function, our good is reasoning well. Reasoning sets us apart. So our good is reasoning well. But there's a row where reasoning sets us apart, isn't our function, and the conclusion fails.

@art laughing-test
The fix is, whatever sets a kind apart is its function. But laughing sets us apart too.
