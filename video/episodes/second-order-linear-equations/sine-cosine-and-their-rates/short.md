The rate of cosine isn't sine. One minus sign proves it.

@math items="h = 1:  cos 2 − cos 1 ≈ −0.956449@cosine of two minus cosine of one;;limit:  −sin 1 ≈ −0.841471@minus sine of one"
At one, cosine is falling. The first quotient is cosine of two minus cosine of one, about minus zero point nine five six. The column heads for minus sine of one, not plus.

@words text="cos′ = −sin" sub="so cos″ = −cos"
Keep the minus, and cosine's second rate is minus cosine. Drop it, and the next equation fails.
