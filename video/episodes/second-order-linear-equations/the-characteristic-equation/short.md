One guess turns a differential equation into a quadratic.

@math items="y = e^(rt),  y′ = r·e^(rt),  y″ = r²·e^(rt)@e to the r t;;(a·r² + b·r + c)·e^(rt) = 0@factor it out"
Try e to the r t. Its rates are just r and r squared times itself. Substitute, and factor it out.

@words text="a·r² + b·r + c = 0" sub="e^(rt) is never zero"
E to the r t is never zero, so the bracket must be. Solve that quadratic for r, and every root you find gives you one exponential solution.
