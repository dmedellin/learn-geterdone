This wave looks like it solves the equation. It doesn't.

@math items="y = t·cos t@t times cosine;;y″ + y = −2·sin t@the residual is"
Take t times cosine of t, in y double prime plus y equals zero. Work out its rates, and the residual is minus two sine of t.

@art residual-crossings
That's zero at every multiple of pi, but not for every t. At t equals one it's about minus one point six eight.

@words text="Zero for every t" sub="or it isn't a solution"
A solution's residual is zero for every t. The residual decides, not the picture.
