Every number here is an exact fraction. The answer is still wrong.

@math items="x² + v² = 1@at one;;Euler:  × (1 + h²) per step@one plus h squared"
The oscillator keeps x squared plus v squared at one, on a circle. But each Euler step multiplies it by exactly one plus h squared.

@words text="h = 1/4:  17/16 per step" sub="after 24 steps ≈ 4.28444"
With h equals a quarter, that's seventeen sixteenths a step. After twenty four steps, about four point two eight. Not rounding. The recipe.
