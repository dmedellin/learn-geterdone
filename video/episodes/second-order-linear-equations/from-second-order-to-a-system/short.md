Forget one division, and your matrix has the wrong roots.

@math items="2x″ + 8x′ + 6x = 0@two x double prime;;bottom row −3, −4@minus three, minus four;;✗  −6, −8:  λ = −4 ± √10@minus six, minus eight"
For two x double prime plus eight x prime plus six x, divide by two, and the bottom row is minus three, minus four. Skip it, use minus six, minus eight, and the roots come out as minus four plus or minus root ten.

@words text="roots −1 and −3"
The true roots are minus one and minus three. Check against the characteristic equation.
