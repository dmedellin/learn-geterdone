@art no-real-root-parabola
No real roots. Yet the solution is perfectly real, and it swings.

@math items="roots −1 ± 2i@minus one plus or minus two i;;y = e^(−t)·sin(2t)@e to the minus t"
For y double prime plus two y prime plus five y, the roots are minus one plus or minus two i. And e to the minus t times sine of two t solves it exactly.

@art decaying-oscillation
The real part, minus one, shrinks the swings. The imaginary part, two, sets their speed.
