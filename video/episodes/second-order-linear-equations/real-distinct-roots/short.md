@art knife-edge
This solution decays. Nudge its start by one percent, and it explodes.

@math items="y′(0) = −1:  y = e^(−t)@minus one;;y′(0) = −99/100:  C₁ = 1/200@minus ninety nine"
In y double prime minus y equals zero, start at one with rate minus one, and you get e to the minus t. Change the rate to minus ninety nine over a hundred, and the growing term gets a constant of one over two hundred.

@words text="A positive root means growth"
Tiny, but e to the t grows past everything. So a positive root means growth.
