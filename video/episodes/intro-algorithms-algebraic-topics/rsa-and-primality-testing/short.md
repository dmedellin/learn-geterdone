Two thousand forty seven passes the base two prime test. It isn't prime.

@math items="2047 = 23 · 89@twenty three;;2¹⁰²³ ≡ 1 (mod 2047)@two to the"
It's twenty three times eighty nine. But two to the one thousand twenty three is one, mod twenty forty seven, so base two gives no testimony.

@words text="Composite = proof · prime = no proof yet"
Base three exposes it. A failed test proves composite. A pass only means this base had nothing to say.
