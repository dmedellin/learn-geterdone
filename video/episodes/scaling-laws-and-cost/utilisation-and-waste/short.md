Sixty nine percent of your fleet sits idle. Cut it to the average?

@math items="mean 5 250 rps,  peak 12 000 rps@twelve thousand;;ρ at the peak = 16/7 > 1@sixteen sevenths"
You use five thousand two hundred and fifty requests a second on average, but the evening peak is twelve thousand. Cut to the average, and at the peak, utilisation is sixteen sevenths. The backlog just grows.

@words text="The waste is a mean. The failure is at the max."
Flatten the profile instead, or fill the quiet hours.
