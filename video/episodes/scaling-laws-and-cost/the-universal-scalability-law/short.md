title: Twice the machines, less throughput

@art descending-curve
Double your fleet, and get less throughput. Here's how.

@math items="peak at 99 machines:  25.19×@ninety nine;;198 machines:  22.397×@a hundred and ninety eight"
When every machine has to agree with every other, that cost grows with the square of the fleet. With these coefficients, throughput peaks at ninety nine machines, about twenty five times one. At a hundred and ninety eight, it's down to twenty two point four.

@words text="The fix is fewer agreements, not more machines"
More machines only make it worse.
