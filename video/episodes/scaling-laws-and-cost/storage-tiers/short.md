@art too-early
Move fresh data to cold storage, and pay nearly four times as much.

@math items="a* = (0.023 − 0.004) / 0.010 = 1.9 reads/GB-month@one point nine;;8 reads:  hot $23.00,  cold $84.00@eight times"
Cold saves money each month, but every read back costs. The break even is one point nine reads per gigabyte a month. A bucket read eight times costs twenty three dollars hot, and eighty four cold.
