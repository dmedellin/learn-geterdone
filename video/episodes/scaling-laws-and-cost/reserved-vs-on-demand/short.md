@art reserve-the-peak
Reserve enough instances for your peak, and you can pay more than reserving none.

@math items="reserve nothing:  $51.84 a day@fifty one;;reserve the peak:  $60.48 a day@sixty;;reserve 12:  $37.44 a day@thirty seven"
At seven cents reserved and twelve on demand, reserving the peak costs sixty dollars forty eight a day. Reserving nothing costs fifty one eighty four. Reserving the bottom twelve costs thirty seven forty four.

@words text="Reserve only what runs more than 7/12 of the time"
The peak instances run one hour a day. Don't commit to them.
