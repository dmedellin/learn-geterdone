title: Why a readability score can call a hard text easy
---

@art blind-formula
A text about an unfamiliar idea can score as easy as a nursery rhyme.

@words text="Words per sentence · syllables per word"
Readability formulas see just two things: how long the sentences are, and how many syllables the words have. That's all.

@art linked-sentences
They can't tell a common word from a rare one, or whether you know the subject, or whether each sentence leans on the last. The score is a count, not a judgement.
