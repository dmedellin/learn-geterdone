@art two-patterns
Your speed barely dropped on the hard text. That might be bad news.

@math items="easy: 250 wpm, 6 of 6@two fifty;;hard: 240 wpm, 3 of 6@two forty;;effective: 250 → 120@one twenty"
One reader went two fifty on an easy passage with six of six, and two forty on a hard one with three of six. The effective rate fell from two fifty to one twenty.

@words text="A rate is a rate on a kind of text"
The pace held, and half the meaning went. So always check the score beside the speed.
