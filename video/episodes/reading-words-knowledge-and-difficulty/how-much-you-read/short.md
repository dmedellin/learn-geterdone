@art two-levers
Ten more minutes a day equals reading half again as fast.

@math items="30 × 250 × 365 = 2,737,500@thirty minutes;;20 × 375 × 365 = 2,737,500@three seventy five"
Twenty minutes a day at two fifty words a minute. Make it thirty minutes, and you read as much in a year as if your rate jumped to three seventy five.

@words text="Time is the easier factor"
No technique reliably delivers a rate gain like that. Ten minutes is a choice you can make tomorrow.
