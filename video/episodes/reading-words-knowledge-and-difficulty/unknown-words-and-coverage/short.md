@art density-rows
Knowing ninety five percent of the words sounds plenty. It often isn't.

@math items="95% → one unknown in every 20@one unknown in every twenty;;98% → one unknown in every 50@one in every fifty"
Ninety five percent coverage means one unknown in every twenty. Ninety eight percent means one in every fifty.

@words text="Nearly every sentence, or every two or three"
That's an unknown word in nearly every sentence, against one in every two or three. In Hu and Nation's study of learners of English, most didn't understand the story adequately at ninety five percent.
