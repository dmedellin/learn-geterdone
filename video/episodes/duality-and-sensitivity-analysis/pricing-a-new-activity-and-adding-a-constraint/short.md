@art profit-trap
This product earns four a unit. Making it loses you money.

@math items="uses 1, 2, 3 hours at prices 0, 3/2, 1@one hour of cutting;;worth 6,  earns 4@six;;4 − 6 = −2@six to make four"
It takes one hour of cutting, two of glazing, three of assembly. At the plan's prices, those hours are worth six. It earns four. So every unit displaces six to make four.

@words text="Profit beyond what it displaces"
Price a new product against what its ingredients are already worth.
