title: A guessed price that proves a bound

@art crude-bound
You can prove an upper bound on a profit without solving anything.

@math items="max 5x + 4y,  6x + 4y ≤ 24, …@five x plus four y;;guess y = (1, 0, 0):  6 ≥ 5,  4 ≥ 4@six at least five;;no plan earns more than 24@twenty four"
A bakery maximises five x plus four y. Guess prices one, zero, zero, and check each column: six at least five, four at least four. Valid. So no plan can earn more than twenty four.

@words text="Feasible prices are a certificate"
The optimum is twenty one. The guess wasn't sharp, but it was proved.
