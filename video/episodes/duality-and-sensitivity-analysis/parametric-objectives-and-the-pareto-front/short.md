@art grid-miss
Slide the weight in hundredths, and you'll never hit the moment the answer changes.

@math items="λ = 0.08:  (7, 0)@point oh eight;;λ = 0.09:  (6, 3)@point oh nine;;breakpoint:  λ = 1/12@one twelfth"
Weight two objectives with lambda. At point oh eight, the best corner is seven, zero. At point oh nine, it's six, three. The switch happens at exactly one twelfth, strictly between them.

@words text="Compute breakpoints, don't sample them"
So compute the breakpoints exactly. A grid can miss a whole corner.
