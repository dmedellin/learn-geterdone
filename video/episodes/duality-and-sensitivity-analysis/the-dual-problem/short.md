@art transpose-trap
Transpose the matrix, swap b and c, done? That dual bounds nothing.

@math items="multiply each row by a price y ≥ 0@a price;;add them:  3x₁ + 5x₂ ≤ 4y₁ + 12y₂ + 18y₃@add them up;;minimise that bound@smallest"
The dual comes from one idea. Multiply each row by a price, add them up, and get a bound on the objective. The dual asks for the smallest such bound.

@words text="Every sign is forced by the bound"
Every direction and every sign comes from making that sum a real bound.
