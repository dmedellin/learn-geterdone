title: A shadow price is wrong outside its range

@art outside-range
An assembly hour is worth one pound. Until it isn't.

@math items="price 1, for 12 ≤ b₃ ≤ 24@twelve to twenty four;;at 26:  predict 44@forty four;;truth 42@forty two"
The workshop's assembly price is one, but only from twelve to twenty four hours. At eighteen hours the optimum is thirty six. Extend the price to twenty six hours and you predict forty four. The truth is forty two.

@words text="Always quote the range"
A price without its range isn't approximately right. It's a different number.
