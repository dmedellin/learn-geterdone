title: What mixing your strategy actually buys

@art exploit-pure
Your best single move guarantees minus one. Mixing guarantees more.

@math items="rows (3, −1) and (−2, 1)@this game;;best pure guarantee:  −1@minus one;;mix 3/7, 4/7:  guarantee 1/7@one seventh"
In this game, row one's worst case is minus one, and row two's is minus two. But mix them three sevenths to four sevenths, and no opponent can hold you below one seventh.

@words text="Mixing buys 8/7"
That gap, eight sevenths, is what mixing buys.
