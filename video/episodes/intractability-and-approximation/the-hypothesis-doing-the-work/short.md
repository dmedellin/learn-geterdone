@art shortcut
Double a spanning tree, shortcut it, and get within twice the best tour. Usually.

@words text="D(a, b) ≤ D(a, c) + D(c, b)"
The shortcut only works if a direct hop is never longer than going round. That's the triangle inequality.

@math items="one distance raised to 50@fifty;;tour 53  vs  optimum 4@fifty three"
Raise one distance to fifty with the rest at one, and the tour comes out at fifty three against an optimum of four.
