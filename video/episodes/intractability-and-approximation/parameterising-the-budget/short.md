@art edge-branch
Every edge has to be covered by one of its two ends. That's a two way branch.

@words text="depth = the budget k"
Each branch spends one unit of budget, so the search tree is only k deep, however big the graph.

@art star
A star on eight vertices with budget one settles in three nodes. Forty leaves would take the same three.
