@art fibre-route
New York to London, seventy six milliseconds. Most of it is physics.

@math items="floor = 3d/c = 55.889 ms@three d over c;;remainder = 20.111 ms@twenty point one one one"
Light in fibre goes about two thirds of c, so the round trip floor is three d over c. For five thousand five hundred and eighty five kilometres, that's fifty five point eight eight nine milliseconds. Only the remaining twenty point one one one is yours to fix.

@words text="A CDN shortens d. Nothing shortens 3/c."
So the only way to lower the floor is to serve from somewhere nearer.
