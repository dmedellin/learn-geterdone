@art mean-in-gap
The average request took fifty three milliseconds. Not one request did.

@math items="mean = 53.2 ms@fifty three point two;;neighbours: 52 and 96@fifty two and ninety six;;17 of 20 faster@seventeen of the twenty"
In this sample of twenty, the mean is fifty three point two. The values on either side are fifty two and ninety six, and seventeen of the twenty were faster than the mean.

@words text="A percentile is a time someone actually got"
A percentile is picked by rank, so it's always a real measurement.
