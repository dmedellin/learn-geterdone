@art god-and-meaning
If God exists, life has meaning. Life has meaning. So God exists. Is that valid?

@math items="p F, q T:  premises T  ·  conclusion F@no God"
Look for one row. No God, and life has meaning anyway. Both premises are true, and the conclusion is false.

@words text="Invalid ≠ false"
That's affirming the consequent. It's invalid. But it doesn't show there's no God. Only that these premises don't settle it.
