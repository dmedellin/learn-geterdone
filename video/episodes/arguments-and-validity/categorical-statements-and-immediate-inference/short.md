title: In logic, some doesn't mean some but not all

@art two-circles
Some S are P. Does that mean some S are not P?

@words text="Some = at least one"
In logic, no. Some means at least one. It occupies the overlap of the two circles, and says nothing about the rest.

@words text="Some S are P and All S are P" sub="can both be true"
So some S are P is compatible with every S being a P. Both statements can be true at once.
