@art wrapped-ring
Everyone loves someone. Does it follow that someone is loved by everyone?

@words text="A ring of four people"
No. Put four people in a ring, each loving the next. Everyone loves someone. But nobody is loved by all four.

@words text="∀x ∃y  ≠  ∃y ∀x"
For all x, there exists a y, isn't the same as, there exists a y, for all x. Swapping the order is the quantifier shift.
