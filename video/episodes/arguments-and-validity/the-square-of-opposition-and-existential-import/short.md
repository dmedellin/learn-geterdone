@art empty-circle
All unicorns are white. And no unicorns are white. In modern logic, both are true.

@words text="Nothing to be an exception"
On the Boolean reading, an all statement only says a region is empty. There are no unicorns at all, so there's nothing that could be an exception either way.

@words text="Existential import" sub="assume every term has members"
Assume every term names something, and the old square returns. That assumption is called existential import.
