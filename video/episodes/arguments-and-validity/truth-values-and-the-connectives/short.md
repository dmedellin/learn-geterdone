@art soup-and-salad
In logic, soup or salad means you're allowed both.

@math items="T T:   ∨ T    ⊕ F@both are true;;all other rows agree@other three rows"
Logic's or is inclusive. It's true when both parts are true. The exclusive or is a separate connective, and it says false when both are true. In the other three rows, they agree.

@words text="One row apart"
So the two kinds of or differ in exactly one row.
