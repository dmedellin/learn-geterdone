@art banned-combination
Here's a perfectly valid argument that whales can fly.

@list items="All whales are fish.@All whales are fish|All fish fly.@All fish fly|So all whales fly.@So all whales fly"
All whales are fish. All fish fly. So all whales fly. If those premises were true, the conclusion would have to be.

@words text="Valid ≠ sound"
That's validity. It's about the link, not the facts. Valid isn't sound: the premises are false, so the argument is unsound.
