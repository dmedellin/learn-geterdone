@art cats-dogs-fish
No cats are fish. No dogs are fish. So no dogs are cats. All true. Still invalid.

@words text="The form allows the overlap"
The premises say nothing about cats and dogs outside the fish. So the form allows them to overlap there, and then the conclusion fails.

@words text="True parts ≠ valid form"
A true conclusion is a coincidence of subject matter. It's no evidence of form.
