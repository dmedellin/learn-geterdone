@art conclusion-first
The conclusion of an argument is often the very first thing you read.

@words text="“The bus will be late, since the road is closed.”"
The bus will be late, since the road is closed. The conclusion comes first, and the premise comes last.

@words text="What does it want you to accept?"
So don't hunt at the end. Ask what the passage wants you to accept. That's the conclusion, wherever it stands.
