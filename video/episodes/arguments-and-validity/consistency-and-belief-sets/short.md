@art no-bad-pair
Three beliefs. Any two are fine together. All three are impossible.

@list items="If p then q@If p then q|p@then p|not q@and not q"
If p then q. Then p. And not q. Each pair has a row that makes both true. But no row of the table makes all three true at once.

@words text="Something must go." sub="Logic won't say which."
So something has to go. Drop any one, and the rest can all be true. The logic won't tell you which.
