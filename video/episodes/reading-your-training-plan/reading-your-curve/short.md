@art peak-pick
Your best reading session isn't your new level. Here's why.

@words text="Above the range ≈ 1 time in 4"
With nothing changed, a single test lands above your baseline range about one time in four, just by chance.

@art median-of-three
So look at the median of your last three tests instead. One lucky day can't move it. If the median is above your baseline range, that's evidence of a change.
