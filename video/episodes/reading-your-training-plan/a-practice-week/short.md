@art all-sprints
Seven practice sessions, a hundred and five minutes, and nothing learned about your reading.

@words text="No test in the week"
A week of seven fifteen minute sprint sessions has no test reading in it. And the verdict on your reading uses test readings only.

@words text="A test at least every fifth session"
So keep sessions short, on most days, and make at least every fifth session a test at natural pace, with no pacer.
