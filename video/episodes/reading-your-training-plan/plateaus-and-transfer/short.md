@art rate-kept
Three hundred words a minute on a hard argument. And it's the worse reading.

@math items="300 × 4/6 = 200@four of six;;240 × 6/6 = 240@all six"
One reader kept their practised pace, three hundred a minute, but got four of six right: an effective rate of two hundred. Another slowed to two forty, and got all six: two forty.

@words text="The rate carried over. The understanding didn't."
On a new kind of text, check the effective rate, not the speed.
