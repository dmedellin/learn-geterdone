title: A most agreeable man is not the most agreeable

@words text="a most agreeable man"
A most agreeable man. Is he the most agreeable man of all? No.

@art two-jobs-most
The most beautiful names the one, so it takes the. But after a, most just means very. He's a very agreeable man.

@math items="the most  ·  37@the most;;a most  ·  45@a most"
In Pride and Prejudice, the most before an adjective is written thirty-seven times. A most, meaning very, is written forty-five times.
