title: The plural rule that gains one word and loses three
---

@words text="potato → potatoes"
Potato takes e-s. So should every noun ending in a consonant and o?

@math items="potato → potatoes ✓@gains potato;;photo, piano, pro ✗@loses photo"
Add that rule to the plain plural rule, and it gains potato. But it loses photo, piano and pro. One word gained, three lost.

@art gain-one-lose-three
The spelling can't tell potato from photo. So learn potato as one word, and keep the rule simple.
