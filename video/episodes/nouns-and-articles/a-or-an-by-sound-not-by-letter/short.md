title: Why it's an hour but a University

@words text="an hour · a University"
An hour, but a University. The letter rule gets both of these wrong.

@art letter-vs-sound
A or an isn't about the first letter. It's about the first sound.

@math items="hour  →  vowel sound  →  an@Hour begins;;University  →  sounds like you  →  a@University begins"
Hour begins with a vowel sound, because the h is silent. University begins like you, a consonant sound. On four hundred and ninety-three lines, the sound rule is never wrong.
