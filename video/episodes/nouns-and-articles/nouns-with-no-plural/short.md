@words text="informations ✗  advices ✗"
Informations and advices are two of the commonest mistakes in written English.

@art mass-vs-count
Information and advice are things you don't count. You have a lot of it or a little of it, not one and two.

@math items="some advice@some advice;;a piece of advice@a piece of advice;;much information@much information"
So they never take s, and never take a or an. Say some advice, a piece of advice, and much information.
