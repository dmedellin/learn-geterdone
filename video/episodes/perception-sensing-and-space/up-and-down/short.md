title: A tilted room that suddenly stands up straight

@art tilted-mirror
Show someone their room in a tilted mirror. Then wait a few minutes.

@words text="a sudden change occurs"
In Wertheimer's experiment the room first looks slantwise. Then the walls, the man walking, the falling cardboard, all suddenly become vertical. The image never changed.

@words text="a certain gearing of my body to the world"
Merleau-Ponty says up and down come from a spatial level: the body's hold on a room it could live in.
