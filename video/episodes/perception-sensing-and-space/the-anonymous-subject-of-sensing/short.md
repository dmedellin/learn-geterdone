@art sky-gaze
Did you decide to see the sky blue? Then who did?

@words text="one perceives in me, and not that I perceive"
Not your personal self, the one that decides to take a job. Merleau-Ponty says one perceives in me, and not that I perceive.

@words text="another self which has already sided with the world"
But it isn't nobody. It's the life of your eyes, hands and ears: another self which has already sided with the world.
