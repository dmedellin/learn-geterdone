title: You can see movement where nothing moved
---

@art two-lights
Two lights flash in turn. Nothing lights the gap. You see movement.

@words text="not a passage of the line, but a pure passage"
That's Wertheimer's experiment. Put a rod in the gap, and the moving line never fills it in. It's not a passage of the line, but a pure passage.

@words text="identical in them, not beneath them"
Merleau-Ponty's answer: what moves isn't one thing beneath its positions. It's identical in its moving.
