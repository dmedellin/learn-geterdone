title: Nobody has ever seen a cube with six equal faces

@art die-turning
Turn a die in your hand. When do you see its six equal faces?

@math items="corner view 1 2 2: right 1/3, above 2/3, front 2/3@one third"
Never. From a corner, projection draws three faces, at one third, two thirds and two thirds of their area. No face is ever drawn whole.

@quote text="The cube with six equal sides is not only invisible, but inconceivable" cite="Merleau-Ponty, Phenomenology of Perception, Part Two, introduction, pp. 235–239"
For Merleau-Ponty, the six equal sides are a limiting idea. It's a body perceiving from somewhere that makes a cube in perspective a cube.
