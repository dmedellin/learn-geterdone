@art night-enwraps
Where is the night? In front of you, like a wall?

@words text="Night is not an object before me; it enwraps me"
Merleau-Ponty, after Minkowski, says no. Night is not an object before me. It enwraps me, pure depth, with no distance between us.

@words text="existence is spatial"
Night, dreams and myth have their own spaces. Not distortions of geometry, but ways of existing. Space is existential, and existence is spatial.
