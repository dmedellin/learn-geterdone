@art eye-triangle
Does your brain work out distance from the angle of your eyes?

@math items="6.5 / 50 − 6.5 / 60 = 13/600 rad@thirteen over six hundred"
The classical theory says yes. Two points at fifty and sixty centimetres give a disparity of thirteen over six hundred radians.

@words text="neither signs nor causes of depth"
But nobody seeing depth has that number. Merleau-Ponty says convergence is neither a sign nor a cause of depth. It's a motive, like a reason in a decision.
