title: Why a breaking glass sounds like the glass

@art glass-falling
You can see that glass is brittle. Before it even breaks.

@words text="the sound is conveyed by the visible glass"
When it shatters, the tinkle doesn't arrive as a second thing beside it. It comes out of the glass you're watching.

@art double-finger
For Merleau-Ponty the senses join like your two eyes on one finger, by focusing, not by a judgement. The senses translate each other without any need of an interpreter.
