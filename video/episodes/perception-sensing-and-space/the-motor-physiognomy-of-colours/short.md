@art arm-red-green
Could a colour move your arm before you even see it?

@list items="Red, yellow: outward@Red and yellow|Blue, green: inward@blue and green"
In Goldstein's patients, red and yellow drew the arm outwards, blue and green drew it in.

@art faint-light
And in Werner's experiments, one subject felt a slipping downwards and knew it was blue, though he saw no blue.

@words text="already the amplification of our motor being"
So the movement isn't an effect added to a colour. Merleau-Ponty says it's part of how the colour appears.
