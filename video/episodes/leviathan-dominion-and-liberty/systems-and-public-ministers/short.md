@art assembly-votes
Your council borrows money you voted against. Hobbes says you owe nothing.

@words text="Only those who voted for it"
When an assembly borrows from a stranger, all they, and only they, who voted for the borrowing are responsible.

@math items="4 yes, 2 no: passes@four vote yes;;each of the 4 owes the whole 120@whole"
Four vote yes and two no on a loan of a hundred and twenty. The two owe nothing. Each of the four owes the whole, until one pays.
