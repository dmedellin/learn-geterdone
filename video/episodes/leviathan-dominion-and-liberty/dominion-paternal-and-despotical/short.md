title: Hobbes: winning a war gives you no right to rule
---

@art captive-in-bonds
Hobbes says beating a man in war gives you no right over him.

@words text="Not the victory, but his own covenant"
The right comes from the beaten man's own covenant. He comes in and submits, and is trusted with his liberty. Only then is he a servant.

@art trusted-servant
Until then he's a captive in bonds, and he owes nothing at all. He may break out, and even kill his master, justly.
