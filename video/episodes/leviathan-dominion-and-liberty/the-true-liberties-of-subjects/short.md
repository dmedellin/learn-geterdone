title: The one order Hobbes says you may refuse

@art self-defence-shield
Even under Hobbes's absolute sovereign, there are orders you may refuse.

@words text="What no covenant could transfer"
A covenant not to defend your own body is void. So no one ever gave that right away.

@quote text="yet hath that man the Liberty to disobey." cite="Hobbes, Leviathan, Chapter 21"
Ordered to kill himself, even if justly condemned, yet hath that man the liberty to disobey. The sovereign may still punish him. But the covenant never reached that far.
