title: Hobbes: acting out of fear is still acting freely

@art goods-overboard
Throwing your cargo overboard in a storm, Hobbes says, is a free act.

@words text="Fear and liberty are consistent"
Nothing outside stopped you, and you could have refused. For Hobbes, liberty is only the absence of external impediments.

@art artificial-chains
So under a sovereign, your liberty lies wherever the laws are silent: what you buy, where you live, how you raise your children.
