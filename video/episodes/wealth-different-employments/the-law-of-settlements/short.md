title: The parish boundary that was harder to cross than the sea
---

@art parish-boundary
In Adam Smith's England, a parish boundary could be harder to cross than the sea.

@words text="Forty days · a £10 tenement · or security"
A newcomer could be sent back within forty days unless he rented a ten pound tenement or gave security, which scarce any working man could give.

@art two-wage-bars
So wages in places a short way apart could differ sharply. Smith called removing a blameless man an evident violation of natural liberty and justice.
