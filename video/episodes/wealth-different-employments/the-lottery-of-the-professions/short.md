title: Why the one who succeeds should be paid for twenty who fail

@art twenty-to-one
Send your son to study law, said Adam Smith, and it's twenty to one he never lives by it.

@math items="tickets:  21 · 200 = 4200@twenty one;;fair prize:  4200 / 20 years = 210 a year@two hundred and ten"
In the lesson's figures, twenty one enter at two hundred pounds each. The one who succeeds must earn two hundred and ten a year over common labour, for twenty years, to repay them all.

@words text="Never equal to this"
Smith says the law's real reward never reaches that.
