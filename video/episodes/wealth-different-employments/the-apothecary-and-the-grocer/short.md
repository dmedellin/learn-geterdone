@art apothecary-jars
How can a thousand per cent profit be perfectly reasonable?

@words text="Real wages disguised in the garb of profit"
Adam Smith's apothecary bought perhaps thirty or forty pounds of drugs a year, and sold them for three or four hundred. But most of that, Smith said, was real wages disguised in the garb of profit.

@art share-pies
His skill and trust had to be paid for somehow, and the only place to charge it was the price of his drugs.
