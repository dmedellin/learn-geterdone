@art grouped-parameters
Nine parameters, three ints in a row. Which one did you swap?

@words text="About seven, at most"
Steve McConnell's advice is to keep a routine's parameters to about seven, after Miller's work on how much people can keep track of.

@art nine-to-three
But don't just bundle them. Group what travels together, by what the routine is about. Seven invoice values became an invoice line and a tax rule.
