title: How deep should an inheritance tree go?

@art deep-chain
How many levels of inheritance can you really keep in your head?

@words text="Two or three"
Riel suggested a limit of six. Steve McConnell calls that grossly optimistic. Most people, he says, have trouble juggling more than two or three.

@art inheritance-tree
Because to know what one call does, you may have to read every level above it. So keep hierarchies shallow, and contain before you inherit.
