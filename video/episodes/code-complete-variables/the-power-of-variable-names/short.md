@art name-lengths
How long should a variable name be? One study actually measured it.

@words text="Best: 10 to 16 characters" sub="almost as good: 8 to 20"
Debugging was easiest when names averaged ten to sixteen characters, and almost as easy at eight to twenty.

@art loop-crosstalk
But it's an average, not a rule. The shorter the scope, the shorter a name can be. In a three line loop, i is fine.
