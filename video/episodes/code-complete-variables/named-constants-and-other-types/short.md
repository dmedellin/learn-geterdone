@art safe-bet
A loop to twelve. It's probably over months. Would you bet on it?

@art single-dial
Steve McConnell's advice is to name even the safe literals. A named constant says what the value means, and gives you one place to change it.

@words text="Only 0 and 1 in the body"
His rule of thumb: the only literals in a program's body should be zero and one.
