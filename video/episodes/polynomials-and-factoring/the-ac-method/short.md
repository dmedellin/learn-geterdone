@art split-middle
The AC method looks like a trick. Its key step is one line of arithmetic.

@math items="6x² + 11x + 3@six x squared;;6x² + 9x + 2x + 3@nine x plus two x"
To factor six x squared plus eleven x plus three, you rewrite eleven x as nine x plus two x.

@math items="9x + 2x = (9 + 2)x = 11x@nine plus two"
That's allowed because nine x plus two x is nine plus two, times x, which is eleven x. It's equal for every x. Nothing has changed.
