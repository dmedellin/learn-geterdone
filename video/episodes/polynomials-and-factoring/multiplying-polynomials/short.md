@art foil-runs-out
FOIL only works for one kind of product. Here's the rule that always works.

@art product-grid
Multiply every term of the first polynomial by every term of the second. m terms times n terms makes m times n products.

@math items="(2x − 3)(x² + 4x − 5)@two x minus three;;2 × 3 = 6 products@six products;;2x³ + 5x² − 22x + 15@collect"
So two x minus three, times x squared plus four x minus five, owes two times three, six products. Write all six, collect, and you get two x cubed plus five x squared minus twenty-two x plus fifteen.
