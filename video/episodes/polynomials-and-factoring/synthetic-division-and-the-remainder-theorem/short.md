title: The remainder is secretly the function's value

@art value-on-curve
Divide a polynomial by x minus c, and the remainder tells you something surprising.

@math items="(2x³ − 5x² + 3x − 7) ÷ (x − 3)@divide;;remainder 11@remainder is eleven"
Divide two x cubed minus five x squared plus three x minus seven by x minus three. The remainder is eleven.

@math items="f(3) = 54 − 45 + 9 − 7 = 11@f of three"
Now plug in three. f of three is fifty-four minus forty-five plus nine minus seven, which is eleven. The remainder is always f of c.
