title: The missing zero that wrecks polynomial division
---

@art missing-column
Leave out one zero, and your whole division goes wrong.

@words text="2x⁴ − 3x³ + 0x² + 5x − 1"
Dividing two x to the fourth minus three x cubed plus five x minus one? There's no x squared term. So write zero x squared in its place.

@art leading-terms
Skip it, and every later coefficient slides one column over. You get a quotient of the right degree, with all the wrong numbers.
