title: The highest power you see isn't always the degree
---

@art cancelling-bars
The biggest power on the page isn't always the degree. Here's why.

@math items="x³ + 5x − x³@x cubed plus five x;;= 5x@leaves five x;;degree 1@degree one"
Take x cubed plus five x minus x cubed. The highest power written is three. But the x cubed terms cancel, which leaves five x, and that has degree one.

@words text="Combine first, then read the degree"
Degree belongs to the polynomial, not to how it's written. So combine like terms first, then read it.
