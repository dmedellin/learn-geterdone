@art missing-piece
The most persistent error in school algebra fits in one line.

@words text="(a + b)² ≠ a² + b²" sub="a = 1, b = 3:  16 vs 10"
a plus b, squared, is not a squared plus b squared. Try a equals one and b equals three. The left side is sixteen. The right side is ten.

@math items="(a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²@two a b"
The missing piece is the middle term, two a b. Squaring never distributes over addition.
