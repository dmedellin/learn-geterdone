@art sign-flip-row
One missed sign flip causes more wrong answers than any other slip.

@math items="−(x² − 5x − 2)@minus, x squared;;= −x² + 5x + 2@every sign flips;;✗  −x² − 5x − 2@not"
Subtracting x squared minus five x minus two means every sign flips: minus x squared plus five x plus two. Not minus x squared minus five x minus two.

@words text="Write the negation on its own line"
So write the negated polynomial out on its own line, then add. It costs one line.
