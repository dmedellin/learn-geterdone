@art mismatched-brackets
Your grouping didn't fail. You probably just picked the wrong sign.

@math items="x³ + 3x² − 4x − 12@x cubed plus three x squared;;x²(x + 3) + 4(−x − 3)  ✗@plus four;;x²(x + 3) − 4(x + 3)  ✓@minus four"
In x cubed plus three x squared minus four x minus twelve, take plus four out of the last pair and the brackets don't match. Take out minus four, and both brackets are x plus three.

@words text="Make the second bracket match the first"
So when the third term is negative, take the negative out. Make the second bracket match the first.
