@art cross-and-touch
Why do some graphs cross the axis at a root, and others just bounce off?

@math items="(x − c)  odd power: crosses@odd number of times;;(x − c)²  even power: touches@even number of times"
It's the multiplicity. If x minus c appears an odd number of times, its sign changes at c, so the curve crosses. An even number of times, no sign change, so it touches and turns back.

@art the-sketch
In x plus two, times x minus one squared, times x minus three, the curve crosses at minus two and three, but touches at one.
