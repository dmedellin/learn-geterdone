title: x squared plus four does not factor, and here's why

@art always-above
x squared plus four looks like it should factor. It doesn't.

@words text="(x + 2)(x + 2) = x² + 4x + 4"
The usual guess is x plus two times x plus two. Expand it, and you get x squared plus four x plus four. There's an extra four x.

@art pattern-twice
In fact x squared plus four is greater than zero for every real x. No real root means no real linear factor. A sum of squares stops there.
