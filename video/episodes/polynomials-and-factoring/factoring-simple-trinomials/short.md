@art empty-list
Sometimes the correct answer to factor this is: it doesn't.

@math items="x² + x + 1@x squared plus x plus one;;(1)(1) sum 2 · (−1)(−1) sum −2@sums two and minus two"
Take x squared plus x plus one. You need two integers with product one and sum one. The only pairs are one and one, and minus one and minus one, with sums two and minus two.

@words text="Exhausted list = proof"
Neither is one, and the list is finished. So it doesn't factor over the rationals. That's a proof, not a give up.
