@art branching-futures
Necessarily, a battle tomorrow or not. Does that make the outcome necessary?

@math items="□(p ∨ ¬p):  true@box around the whole;;□p ∨ □¬p:  false@a box on each side"
Put the box around the whole, and it's true at every world. Put a box on each side, and it's false wherever the seen futures disagree.

@art box-split
The fatalist moves the box across or. Necessity distributes over and, but not over or. So the argument for fatalism is invalid.
