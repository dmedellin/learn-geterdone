title: Reid's puzzle: the general who forgot the boy

@art boy-officer-general
The officer remembers the boy. The general remembers the officer. But the general has forgotten the boy.

@list items="officer is boy@the officer is the boy|general is officer@the general is the officer|general is not boy@not the boy"
If memory decides identity, the officer is the boy, the general is the officer, and the general is not the boy. That's a contradiction.

@art filled-closure
The fix: identity follows the chain of overlapping memories, not each single memory. The general is the boy, through the officer.
