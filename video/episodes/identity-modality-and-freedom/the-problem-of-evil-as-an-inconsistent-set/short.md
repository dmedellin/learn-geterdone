@art clash
All powerful, all knowing, wholly good, and evil exists. Can all four be true?

@words text="(o ∧ k ∧ g) → ¬e"
Not if such a being eliminates evil. That bridge makes the five sentences inconsistent. Something has to give.

@art possible-story
The free will defence weakens the bridge. Such a being eliminates only unnecessary evil. Now there's a model, and the contradiction isn't forced. That doesn't prove the story true.
