@art distant-past
You have no power over the distant past, or the laws of nature.

@math items="N(h ∧ l)@the past and laws;;N((h ∧ l) → a)@they entail;;∴  N a@So"
No power over the past and laws. No power over the fact that they entail your act. So no power over your act.

@words text="Valid: the step is the K axiom"
The step is valid. So to resist, you must deny a premise, or deny that no power over works like necessity.
