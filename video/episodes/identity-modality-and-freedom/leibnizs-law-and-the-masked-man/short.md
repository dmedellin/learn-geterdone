The masked man is your father. You know your father is here, but not that the masked man is.

@math items="f ↔ m:  true@the same man;;□f:  true,  □m:  false@what you know"
At the actual world, both sentences are true, the same man. But what you know looks at other worlds too, and there they come apart.
