@art no-self-loop
Does necessarily p imply p? It depends on one thing: whether each world sees itself.

@math items="T:  □p → p@if necessarily p;;holds  ⟺  every world sees itself@exactly when"
The axiom T says, if necessarily p, then p. It holds exactly when every world has an arrow to itself.

@art all-loops
Leave one loop out, and you can make p necessary at a world where it's false. Each axiom is a shape of the arrows.
