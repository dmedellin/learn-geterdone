@art jones-and-black
Jones couldn't have done otherwise. Is he still responsible?

@math items="B = ¬D:  Black steps in only if Jones doesn't decide@Black steps in;;A = D ∨ B:  the act happens either way@either way"
Black steps in only if Jones doesn't decide. So the act happens either way. But Jones decided on his own, and Black did nothing.

@art spare-tyre
Black was a backup, never used. The act came from Jones's decision. So maybe responsibility never needed an alternative, just the right source.
