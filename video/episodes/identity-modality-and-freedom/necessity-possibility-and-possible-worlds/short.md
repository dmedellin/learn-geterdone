title: Something can be necessary and false
---

@art one-way-arc
In modal logic, p can be necessary at a world where p is false.

@math items="w1 sees only w2@sees only;;p true at w2:  □p true at w1@true there;;p false at w1@false at"
World one sees only world two, and p is true there. So box p, necessarily p, is true at world one. But p itself is false at world one.

@art self-loop
Add one arrow, from world one to itself, and the necessity disappears. Necessity depends on what each world can see.
