@art three-way-clash
Every position on free will is just a choice of which sentence to drop.

@list items="Determinism is true@Determinism|Some people act freely@act freely|If determinism, no one is free@no one is free"
Determinism is true. Some people act freely. If determinism is true, no one is free. All three can't hold.

@art three-costs
Drop the second, and you're a hard determinist. Drop the first, a libertarian. Drop the third, a compatibilist. Each position pays a price somewhere.
