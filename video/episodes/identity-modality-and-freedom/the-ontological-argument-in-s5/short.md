Grant that God possibly exists necessarily, and in S5 you've granted that God exists necessarily.

@math items="◇□G → □G@if possibly;;valid in S5@valid"
If possibly necessarily G, then necessarily G. That step is valid in the logic S five, for any sentence at all.

@art two-exits
So the fight is elsewhere. Either deny the modest sounding premise, or deny that S five is the right logic for necessity.
