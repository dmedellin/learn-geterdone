title: Is it still the same ship?
---

@art ship-planks
Replace every plank of a ship, one at a time. Is it the same ship?

@math items="F(k) → F(k − 1)@one plank;;1000 steps:  F(1000) to F(0)@a thousand"
One plank never seems to matter. But a thousand steps of that take you to a ship with no original wood at all.

@art two-ships
Now rebuild the old planks into a second ship. Both have a claim, and only one can be the original.
