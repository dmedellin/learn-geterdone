@art negative-slack
Pick the wrong ratio, and your answer looks better than the true optimum.

@math items="ratios:  12 ÷ 2 = 6,  18 ÷ 2 = 9@six and nine;;take 9:  s₂ = −6,  z = 45@forty five"
Two rows limit the entering variable, at six and nine. Take the smallest, six, and you reach the next corner. Take nine, and a slack goes to minus six, and z reads forty five, above the real thirty six.

@words text="Smallest ratio, positive entries only"
So always take the smallest ratio, and only over rows with a positive entry.
