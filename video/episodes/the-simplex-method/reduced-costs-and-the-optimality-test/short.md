@art steep-vs-long
A rate of nine sounds better than a rate of three. Not here.

@math items="x₂:  rate 9 × step 4 = 36@nine per unit;;x₃:  rate 3 × step 20 = 60@three per unit"
At the origin, x two improves z at nine per unit, but the ratio test stops it after four: thirty six. x three improves at only three per unit, but it can run to twenty: sixty, the whole optimum.

@words text="Improvement = rate × step"
The improvement is rate times step, not the rate alone.
