title: Why this induction proof needs three base cases
---

@art stamps
Every amount from eight cents on can be made with threes and fives.

@words text="k + 1 = (k − 2) + 3"
The step is easy: to make k plus one, make k minus two, then add a three cent stamp. It reaches back three places.

@art three-pillars
So you need three base cases: eight, nine and ten. Check only eight, and the step at ten leans on seven, which can't be made at all.
