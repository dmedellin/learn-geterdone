@art halving-gap
What's the smallest positive fraction? There isn't one.

@words text="q  →  q/2" sub="smaller, and still positive"
Name any positive rational q. Then q over two is smaller, and still positive. So that set has no least element.

@art first-in-line
But any nonempty set of natural numbers always has a smallest member. That's the well-ordering principle, and it's a property of the naturals, not of numbers in general.

@words text="No smallest counterexample, no counterexample"
It's what makes induction work: if a claim failed anywhere, there'd be a smallest failure to rule out.
