@art peel-last
Every induction proof of a sum formula has the same first move.

@words text="Σᵢ≤ₖ₊₁ f(i) = Σᵢ≤ₖ f(i) + f(k+1)"
Peel off the last term. The sum up to k plus one is the sum up to k, plus one more term.

@art square-pyramid
Now the hypothesis covers the sum up to k. Substitute it, and what's left is plain algebra. Write the claim at k plus one first, so you know which factor to aim for.
