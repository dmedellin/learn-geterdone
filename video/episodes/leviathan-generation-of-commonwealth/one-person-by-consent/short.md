What does a crowd want? Hobbes says: nothing, because a crowd isn't one thing.

@quote text="For it is the Unity of the Representer, not the Unity of the Represented, that maketh the Person One." cite="Hobbes, Leviathan, Chapter 16"
A multitude is many. It becomes one person only when someone represents it, with each member's consent. For it is the unity of the representer, not the unity of the represented, that maketh the person one.
