Hobbes said tyranny isn't a kind of government. It's a mood.

@list items="Monarchy misliked: tyranny@tyranny|Aristocracy misliked: oligarchy@oligarchy|Democracy misliked: anarchy@anarchy"
There are only three kinds: one rules, a part rules, or all rule. Call monarchy tyranny, aristocracy oligarchy, or democracy anarchy, and you haven't named a new form. You've only said you dislike it. The power is the same either way.
