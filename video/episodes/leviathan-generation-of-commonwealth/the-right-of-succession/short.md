@art family-tree
A king with a son names his nephew heir. Who gets the throne?

@quote text="the word Heire does not of it selfe imply the Children, or nearest Kindred of a man; but whomsoever a man shall any way declare" cite="Hobbes, Leviathan, Chapter 19"
For Hobbes, the nephew. The word heir does not of itself imply the children, or nearest kindred of a man, but whomsoever a man shall any way declare.

@words text="The present monarch disposes of the succession"
The son's claim is only a guess at the king's will, when he's left no words.
