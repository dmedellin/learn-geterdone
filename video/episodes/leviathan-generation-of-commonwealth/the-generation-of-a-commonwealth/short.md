@art tug-of-war
Ten people all want peace. Each still does better to break it.

@math items="keep: 1 + k · break: 5 + k@one plus the number;;nobody keeps: 5 each · all keep: 10 each@five each"
In the lesson's table, a keeper gets one plus the number of others keeping. A breaker gets five plus that number. So everyone breaks, and gets five each, instead of ten.

@words text="Covenants, without the Sword, are but Words"
That's Hobbes's point. Add a penalty of five on breaking, and the peace finally holds.
