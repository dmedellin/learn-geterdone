@art stage-mask
For Hobbes, a person isn't a human being. It's a mask.

@words text="Persona: a disguise on the stage"
The Latin persona meant the mask an actor wore on stage. So a person is anyone whose words or actions count, either as his own, or as someone else's.

@art owner-and-agent
That's how one man can act for another. The actor speaks, the author owns the act, and the author is bound.
