@art reserve-army
A company triples its capital. It hires nobody new. How?

@math items="£6,000 at 1:1 → £3,000 v@six thousand;;£18,000 at 5:1 → £3,000 v@eighteen thousand"
At half and half, six thousand pounds puts three thousand into wages. As machinery grows to five parts in six, eighteen thousand pounds still puts only three thousand into wages.

@words text="An industrial reserve army"
Capital triples. Employment stands still. The workers it doesn't take on, Marx says, form a reserve.
