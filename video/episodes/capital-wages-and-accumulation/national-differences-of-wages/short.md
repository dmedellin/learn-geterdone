title: The country with higher wages had cheaper labour

@art two-countries
England paid higher wages than the Continent. Its labour was still cheaper.

@math items="England: 6d / 2 lb = 3d a pound@threepence;;Continent: 4d / 1 lb = 4d a pound@fourpence"
An English spinner earns sixpence an hour and spins two pounds of yarn: threepence a pound. A Continental spinner earns fourpence and spins one: fourpence a pound.

@words text="Compare per unit of output"
Dearer by the day, cheaper by the piece. That's why Marx compares wages per unit of output.
