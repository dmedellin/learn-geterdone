@art rate-cut
You double your output on piece rates. Why does your pay stay the same?

@math items="24 · 1½d = 3s@twenty four pieces;;48 · ¾d = 3s@forty eight"
The rate was set from a normal day: three shillings over twenty four pieces, a penny ha'penny. When forty eight becomes normal, the rate falls to three farthings. Forty eight pieces still pay three shillings.

@words text="A converted form of time-wages"
The piece rate follows the normal day, not your effort.
