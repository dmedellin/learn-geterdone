@art golden-chain
When capital grows faster than the workforce, wages rise. Marx says that rise has a ceiling.

@math items="400 wanted · 380 available@three eighty;;s: £3,600, not £4,000@surplus value"
Capital wants four hundred workers. Only three eighty are there. They're hired at a higher wage, and the extra comes out of surplus value.

@words text="Accumulation leads, wages follow"
Less surplus means slower accumulation, and the pressure on wages eases. The chain is relaxed, not removed.
