title: A pay cut that doesn't show on your payslip
---

@art same-pay-longer-day
Your weekly pay stays the same. Your boss just cut the price of your labour by a third.

@math items="36d / 10 h = 3⅗d an hour@ten hours;;36d / 15 h = 2⅖d an hour@fifteen hours"
Three shillings over ten hours is three and three fifths pence an hour. Stretch the day to fifteen hours for the same three shillings, and it's two and two fifths.

@words text="Same wage, cheaper labour"
Nothing on the payslip records it. That's why Marx divides by the hours.
