title: In five years, the capitalist eats his capital

@art debtor
A capitalist spends every penny of profit and keeps his capital intact. Or does he?

@math items="£1,000 / £200 a year = 5 years@five years"
A thousand pound capital brings in two hundred a year, all spent. In five years, he's consumed a thousand pounds, exactly what he advanced.

@words text="Capitalised surplus value"
He still holds a thousand pound capital. But on Marx's reckoning, its value is now the surplus he took from others.
