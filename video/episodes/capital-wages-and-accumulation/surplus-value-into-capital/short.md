title: Five years later, the original capital is 40%

@art vanishing-original
Start a business with ten thousand pounds. In five years, most of its capital won't be yours.

@math items="£10,000 → £12,000@adds two thousand;;× 6/5 each year → £24,883 4s@twenty four thousand"
At Marx's figures, a year's surplus value adds two thousand. Reinvest it all, and the capital grows by a fifth each year, to twenty four thousand, eight hundred and eighty three pounds in five.

@words text="Original: about 40%"
The original ten thousand is now only about forty percent. The rest, on Marx's definitions, is capitalised surplus value.
