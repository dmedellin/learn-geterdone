title: Did Marx say wages must fall?
---

@art two-poles
Marx's general law is often read as wages must fall. Read his words.

@quote text="in proportion as capital accumulates, the lot of the labourer, be his payment high or low, must grow worse." cite="Marx, Capital, Volume I, Chapter 25, §4"
In proportion as capital accumulates, the lot of the labourer, be his payment high or low, must grow worse.

@math items="wage: £10 → £12@ten pounds to twelve;;reserve ratio: 0 → 1/19@one nineteenth"
In the lab's boom, the wage rises from ten pounds to twelve, and a reserve still forms, one nineteenth of those employed. The law ties accumulation to the reserve, not to the wage.
