title: Paid threepence an hour for work worth sixpence

@art half-paid
Your wage looks like the price of every hour you worked. Marx says it isn't.

@math items="3s / 12 h = 3d an hour paid@threepence;;each hour creates 6d@sixpence"
A day's labour-power is worth three shillings. Over twelve hours, that looks like threepence an hour. But each hour creates sixpence of value.

@words text="Even unpaid labour appears as paid"
Half of every hour is unpaid. The wage form makes the whole day look paid.
