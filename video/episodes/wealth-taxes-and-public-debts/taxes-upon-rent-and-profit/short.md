title: The tithe that looked equal and wasn't

@art tithe-rich-and-poor
A tax of one tenth sounds equal for everyone. Adam Smith showed it isn't.

@math items="rich land: rent 5 → 4@rich land;;poor land: rent 2 → 1@poor land"
Take two lands that each produce ten. On rich land, the rent is five, and a tithe of one takes a fifth of it. On poor land, the rent is two, and the same tithe takes half.

@words text="Under the appearance of perfect equality"
The tithe, he says, is very unequal under the appearance of perfect equality.
