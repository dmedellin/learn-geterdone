title: Tax wages by a fifth, and they rise by a fourth

@art rise-outruns-rate
Tax wages by a fifth, and Adam Smith says they rise by a fourth.

@math items="10 / (4/5) = 12s 6d@twelve and sixpence;;12s 6d − 2s 6d = 10s@ten"
A labourer must keep his ten shillings of subsistence. So his wage must rise to twelve and sixpence. A fifth of that is two and sixpence, which leaves ten.

@words text="The employer advances it"
And the labourer doesn't really pay it. His employer does, then the consumer or the landlord.
