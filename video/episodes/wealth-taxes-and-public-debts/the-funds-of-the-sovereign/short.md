title: Why the crown's free rent cost the country three times over
---

@art rent-and-produce
The crown's rent looks free. Adam Smith says it costs the country three times over.

@words text="Rent: about a third of the produce"
The people live on what land produces, not on its rent. And rent is scarce ever more than a third of the produce.

@math items="rent 10 → 20 · produce 30 → 60@ten million"
So land that pays ten million but could pay twenty has a produce of thirty that could be sixty. The owners lose ten. The country loses thirty.
