@art debt-staircase
Fifty four years of steady repayment, undone by one war.

@math items="129 · 3% = 3.87 interest@a hundred and twenty nine;;+100 war → interest ≈ 6.68@a war"
A debt of a hundred and twenty nine million at three per cent costs three point eight seven a year. Pay a million more than that, and it's gone in fifty four years. But add a war loan of a hundred, and the interest outruns the whole payment.

@words text="Easy borrowing, no saving"
Smith's point: a state that can borrow easily doesn't save.
