title: The tax where two and two make one

@art revenue-hump
Double a customs duty, and you might halve the revenue.

@math items="6d · 100,000 = 600,000@six pence;;12d · 25,000 = 300,000@twelve pence"
At six pence a pound, say a hundred thousand pounds weight pay the duty. That's six hundred thousand pence. At twelve pence, smuggling and lost sales cut it to twenty five thousand. Three hundred thousand pence.

@words text="Two and two make sometimes only one"
As Swift said, in the arithmetic of the customs, two and two make sometimes only one.
