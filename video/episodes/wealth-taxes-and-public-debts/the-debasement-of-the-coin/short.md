@art sixpence-shilling
Call a sixpence a shilling, and pay a national debt with half the silver.

@math items="owed: 128 · 4 = 512 million oz@owed;;paid: 128 · 2 = 256 million oz@paid"
A debt of a hundred and twenty eight million pounds was owed in five hundred and twelve million ounces of silver. It's paid back with two hundred and fifty six.

@words text="A pretended payment"
The sum is the same. The silver is half. Adam Smith calls it a real bankruptcy disguised as a pretended payment.
