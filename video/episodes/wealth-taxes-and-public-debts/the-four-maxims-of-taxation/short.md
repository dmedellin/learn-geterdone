title: Why Adam Smith feared an uncertain tax more than an unfair one

@art tax-gatherer-door
Adam Smith thought an unfair tax less dangerous than an unpredictable one.

@words text="Inequality is not near so great an evil as uncertainty"
A very considerable degree of inequality, he says, is not near so great an evil as a very small degree of uncertainty.

@art leaky-collection
Why? If nobody knows what's owed, everyone is in the tax gatherer's power. He can lean on anyone he dislikes, or extort a present by threatening to.
