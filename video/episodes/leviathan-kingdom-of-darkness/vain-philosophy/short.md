title: Hobbes: why falling things don't fall because of heaviness

@art falling-stone
Why does a stone fall? Because of its heaviness, said the schools.

@words text="Bodies descend because they do"
And heaviness, they said, is an endeavour to go to the centre of the earth. Hobbes says that's as much as to say bodies descend because they do.

@art empty-doublet
Empty names like that, he warns, frighten men from the laws, as an empty doublet and a hat frighten birds from the corn.
