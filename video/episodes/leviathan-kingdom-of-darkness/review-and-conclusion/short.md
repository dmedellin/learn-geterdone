@art shield-and-crown
At the very end of Leviathan, Hobbes adds one more law of nature.

@words text="Protect in war what protects you in peace"
Every man is bound to protect, in war, the authority that protects him in time of peace.

@words text="Protection and obedience"
You can't claim a right to preserve yourself and a right to destroy the one whose strength preserves you. That, he says, is what the whole book was for: the mutual relation between protection and obedience.
