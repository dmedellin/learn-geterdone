@art ghost-in-glass
Hobbes thought ghosts were real, but not where people thought they were.

@words text="Inhabitants of their own brain"
Press your eye and you see a light that isn't out there. Dreams of the dead are the same kind of image.

@art seeming-image
The ancients didn't know that, so they took the images in their own brains for beings in the air, and named them demons.
