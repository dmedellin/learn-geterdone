@art ghost-on-grave
Hobbes saved his sharpest joke in the whole book for the Pope.

@quote text="the Ghost of the deceased Romane Empire, sitting crowned upon the grave thereof" cite="Hobbes, Leviathan, Chapter 47"
The papacy, he says, is no other than the ghost of the deceased Roman Empire, sitting crowned upon the grave thereof.

@words text="Cui bono? Who profits?"
It comes from a judge's question: who profits? For Hobbes, who gains by a doctrine is the best presumption of who made it.
