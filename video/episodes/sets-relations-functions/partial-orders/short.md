title: Minimal is not the same as least
---

@art two-minimal
Can a set have two smallest elements? It can have two minimal ones.

@words text="{2, 3, 4, 6} under divides"
Take two, three, four and six, ordered by divides. Nothing in the set divides two, so two is minimal. Nothing divides three either, so three is minimal too.

@words text="No least element"
But a least element must be below everything, and neither two nor three divides the other. So this set has two minimal elements, and no least one.
