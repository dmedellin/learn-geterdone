@art bool-matrix
How many different relations are there on just three elements? Five hundred and twelve.

@words text="3 × 3 = 9 pairs"
A relation is any set of pairs. Three elements give three times three, nine possible pairs.

@math items="2⁹ = 512@two to the nine"
Each pair is either in the relation or out. Nine independent choices gives two to the nine, which is five hundred and twelve. On five elements it's over thirty three million.
