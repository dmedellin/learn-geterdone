@art pairing-lines
There are exactly as many even numbers as natural numbers. Here's the proof.

@math items="0 1 2 3 4 …@zero, one;;0 2 4 6 8 …@one with two"
Pair each number with its double. Zero with zero, one with two, two with four. Every natural number gets exactly one partner, and every even number is used exactly once.

@words text="Same size = a perfect pairing"
For infinite sets, a perfect pairing is what same size means. And an infinite set can match a part of itself.
