@art calendar
In a room of three hundred and sixty seven people, two must share a birthday. Not probably. Certainly.

@words text="367 people · 366 dates"
There are only three hundred and sixty six possible dates, counting February the twenty ninth. Put each person in the box for their birthday.

@words text="More objects than boxes"
More people than boxes means some box holds two. No luck involved. That's the pigeonhole principle.
