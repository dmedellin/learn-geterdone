@art socks-shoes
Socks then shoes. To undo it, shoes come off first. Functions work the same way.

@math items="(g ∘ f)⁻¹ = f⁻¹ ∘ g⁻¹@g after f"
The inverse of g after f is f inverse after g inverse. The last thing done is the first undone.

@math items="f(x) = 2x,  g(x) = x + 3@doubles;;(g ∘ f)⁻¹(y) = (y − 3)/2@subtract three"
Say f doubles and g adds three. To undo both, subtract three first, then halve: y minus three, over two.
