What's outside A union B? Most people get the flip wrong.

@math items="(A ∪ B)‾ = Ā ∩ B̄   ✓@is the complement;;(A ∪ B)‾ = Ā ∪ B̄   ✗@not the complement"
The complement of A union B is the complement of A, intersect the complement of B. It's not the complement of A union the complement of B.

@words text="Not in either = missing both"
The complement moves inside, and the operation flips. Not being in either set means missing both.
