title: Same formula, different function

@art parabola
x squared from the reals to the reals. Is every real an output? No.

@words text="image [0, ∞)  ≠  codomain ℝ"
Squares are never negative, so the outputs only fill zero to infinity. That's the image. The codomain, the reals, is just where outputs are declared to live.

@words text="The codomain is part of the function"
Declare the codomain as zero to infinity instead, and it's a different function, one whose image fills its codomain.
