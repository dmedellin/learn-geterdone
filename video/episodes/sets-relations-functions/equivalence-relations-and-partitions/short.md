title: Why equivalence classes never half overlap
---

@art overlapping-blobs
Two equivalence classes can never half overlap. They're identical, or they share nothing.

@words text="x ∼ a  and  x ∼ b  ⟹  a ∼ b"
Suppose some x is in the class of a and in the class of b. Then by symmetry and transitivity, a is related to b.

@art merging-loops
And once a is related to b, every member of one class is a member of the other. So the two classes are the same class.
