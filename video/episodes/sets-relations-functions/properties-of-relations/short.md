title: Antisymmetric does not mean not symmetric
---

@art one-way-arrows
Antisymmetric sounds like the opposite of symmetric. It isn't.

@words text="Equality is both"
Antisymmetric says distinct elements never relate both ways. Equality never relates distinct elements at all, so it's antisymmetric. And it's symmetric too.

@math items="{(1,2), (2,1), (1,3)}@one, two"
Now take one, two, then two, one, then one, three. The two way pair breaks antisymmetry, and one, three has no reverse, so it isn't symmetric either. It's neither.
