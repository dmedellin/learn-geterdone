@art payback-curve
Spend eight hours automating a job, and you might still lose time.

@math items="30 min → 2 min, once every 4 weeks@once every four weeks;;18 runs → 72 weeks to pay back@seventy two weeks"
The job takes thirty minutes, and the script cuts it to two. But it comes round once every four weeks. Paying back eight hours takes eighteen runs, which is seventy two weeks.

@words text="Lazy, provided the tool saves time"
Writing a tool is the best kind of laziness, McConnell says, provided it ends up saving time. So do the sum first.
