Only a hundred and twenty eight of five hundred pounds of yarn is anyone's income.

@math items="£500 = £372 + £50 + £78@three hundred and seventy two;;income:  £50 + £78 = £128@wages and surplus"
Marx's spinner's yarn is worth five hundred pounds. Three hundred and seventy two must buy back cotton, coal and wear. Only wages and surplus value, one hundred and twenty eight, is new value.

@words text="Most of the product is nobody's income"
So most of what a capital sells has to go straight back into production.
