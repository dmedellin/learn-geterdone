@art partial-sale
Sell most of your yarn at full value, and you still have no surplus.

@math items="M = £422;  product = £500@four hundred and twenty two;;8,440 lb → £422;  M′ − M = £0@eight thousand four hundred and forty"
Marx's spinner lays out four hundred and twenty two pounds and makes yarn worth five hundred. Sell eight thousand four hundred and forty of ten thousand pounds weight, and he's only got his four hundred and twenty two back.

@words text="Produced in the mill, realised in the sale"
But the seventy eight pounds of surplus is still there, in the unsold yarn. It was made in production. The sale only turns it into money.
