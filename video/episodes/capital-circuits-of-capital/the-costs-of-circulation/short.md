@art surplus-shrinks
Pay a clerk ten pounds or a carter ten pounds. Marx says they're different.

@math items="book-keeper:  £100 − £10 = £90@ninety;;carter:  £510 − £410 = £100@a hundred"
Goods worth five hundred cost four hundred, a surplus of a hundred. The book-keeper only changes the form of value, so his pay comes out of surplus: ninety left. The carter moves the goods, so his pay comes back in their value: still a hundred.

@words text="Necessary is not productive"

Both are necessary. Only one adds value.
