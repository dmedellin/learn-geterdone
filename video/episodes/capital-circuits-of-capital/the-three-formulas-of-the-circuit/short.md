title: Slow sales tie up half your capital
---

@art slow-sale
Make selling take longer, and half your capital ends up sitting unsold.

@math items="4 + 4 + 4:  commodity 4/12 = 1/3@four weeks;;4 + 4 + 8:  commodity 8/16 = 1/2@eight weeks"
Four weeks buying, four producing, four selling: a third of the capital is waiting to be sold. Let selling take eight weeks, and it's eight sixteenths, half.

@words text="Only a quarter is at work"
And just four sixteenths, a quarter, is still producing. The share in each form follows the time spent there.
