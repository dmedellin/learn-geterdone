Marx's spinner saves seventy eight pounds a week. Take the lesson's minimum of two hundred pounds for a useful addition. The safe holds seventy eight, then one hundred and fifty six, then two hundred and thirty four, which clears it.

@words text="Latent capital"
Until then, the money produces nothing. Marx calls it latent capital: it grows only because more is added.
