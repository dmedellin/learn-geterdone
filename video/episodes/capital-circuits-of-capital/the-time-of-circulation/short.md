title: Waiting on the market makes nothing, said Marx

@art distant-ship
Your goods sail for twenty weeks. Marx says they earn nothing on the way.

@math items="turnover:  6 + 20 = 26 weeks@twenty six;;circulating:  20/26 = 10/13@ten thirteenths"
Six weeks to make, twenty to reach market and sell. The turnover is twenty six weeks, and ten thirteenths of it is circulation.

@words text="Neither commodities nor surplus value"
During that time, Marx argues, the capital produces neither commodities nor surplus value. Circulation time is a limit, not a source.
