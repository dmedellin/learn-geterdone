title: Every society needs a surplus. Not every society has profit.

@art common-fund
Every society that produces sets aside a surplus. Only one calls it profit.

@math items="capitalist: wages 100 + rent 30 + interest 20 + profit 50@capitalist;;association: 150 consumed + 50 common fund@association"
In the lesson's illustration, a capitalist firm splits two hundred pounds into wages, rent, interest and profit. An association of producers splits it into a hundred and fifty consumed, and fifty for insurance, expansion and those who can't work.

@words text="Same foundations, different forms"
Marx's point: distribution is the other face of how things are produced.
