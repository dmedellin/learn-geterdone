title: Rent, interest, wages: one pie, not three trees

@art three-trees
Wages, interest and rent look like fruit from three different trees. Marx says it's one harvest.

@math items="v + s = £200@two hundred;;wages 100 · rent 30 · interest 20 · enterprise 50@a hundred"
In the lesson's illustration, a year's labour adds two hundred pounds of value. Wages take a hundred. Rent takes thirty, interest twenty, and profit of enterprise fifty.

@words text="No landlord: rent £0, enterprise £80"
Remove the landlord, and rent paid falls to nothing while profit of enterprise rises to eighty. The total stays two hundred.
