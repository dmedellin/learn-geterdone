@art income-gap
Society's income in Marx's scheme is three thousand. Its product is worth nine thousand.

@math items="product: 6000 + 3000 = 9000@nine thousand;;revenue: 3000@three thousand;;Ic = 4000, never anyone's income@four thousand"
The product is nine thousand. Wages, profit and rent come to three thousand. And four thousand of machines and iron are sold between the makers of machines and iron. No consumer ever buys them.

@words text="Income is part of the product"
Adam Smith missed this. Income isn't the whole product. It's one part of it.
