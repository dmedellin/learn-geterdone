@art working-day
Wages rise by fifty. In Marx's example, the price of the product doesn't change at all.

@math items="before: 400c + 100v + 150s = 650@before;;after: 400c + 150v + 100s = 650@after"
Before, a hundred of wages and a hundred and fifty of surplus value. After, a hundred and fifty of wages and a hundred of surplus value. The same labour, the same six hundred and fifty.

@words text="The value comes first"
Wages aren't added to profit and rent to make the price. They divide a value that already exists.
