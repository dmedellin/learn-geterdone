title: One huge red candle is not a reversal
---

@art big-candle-only
One huge red candle after a long rally. Is that the top? Not on its own.

@words text="Size ≠ structure change"
Size alone doesn't show that structure has changed. A reversal needs the prior sequence of highs and lows to break.

@art retest-swing
Then a retest, and a swing on the other side, which gives you a place to be wrong.
