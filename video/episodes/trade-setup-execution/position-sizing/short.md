title: How many shares? Let the stop decide
---

@art stop-widens
How many shares should you buy? Not as many as you feel like.

@math items="shares = budget ÷ per-share risk@divided by;;round down@rounded down"
Set the risk budget first. Shares are the budget divided by the per share risk, entry to stop plus costs, rounded down.

@words text="Wider stop → fewer shares"
So a wider stop means fewer shares. The quantity is an output of your risk rules, not your conviction.
