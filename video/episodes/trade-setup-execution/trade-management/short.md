@art process-vs-outcome
A losing trade can score higher than a winning one. Here's why.

@words text="Discipline ≠ outcome"
A poor decision can make money, and a correct decision can lose. So the lesson scores whether you followed the written plan, not the result.

@art stop-ratchet
A stopped out trade managed to the plan beats a winner managed by feel. That ordering is the whole point of the lesson.
