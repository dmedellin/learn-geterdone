title: How far is too far for a stop?
---

@art too-wide
A tight stop can be wrong. So can a stop that's much too far away.

@art atr-span
The lesson's yardstick is ATR, the average distance a candle travels, high to low, on your timeframe.

@words text="> ~2 ATR beyond invalidation = too wide"
More than about two ATR beyond invalidation, and you're no longer buying room for noise. You're paying for a thesis that already failed, with fewer units of size.
