title: How deep can a pullback go?
---

@art shallow-vs-deep
How deep can a pullback go before it stops being a pullback? There's no magic percentage.

@art failed-swing
What decides it is the protected swing. While price holds above it, a deep pullback is still a pullback.

@words text="Below the protected swing → failed"
Once price breaks it, the pullback has failed, and the original idea has to be reassessed.
