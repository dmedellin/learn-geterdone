@art bias-arrow
I'm bullish. That's not a plan. It's a view.

@art thesis-card
A plan has five lines: the decision, the setup, the trigger, the invalidation, and the target.

@words text="Written before · checkable after"
Each line is written before the trade, so after it anyone can check whether you followed it. And not one line changes once the later candles appear.
