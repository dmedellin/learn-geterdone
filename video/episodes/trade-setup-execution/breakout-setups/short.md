title: A clean breakout can still be no trade
---

@art crowded-room
A perfect breakout. Close outside, accepted, holding. And still no trade. Why?

@words text="Room to the next level"
Because a valid breakout is not automatically a good trade. You need room to the next opposing level.

@art close-and-hold
If that level sits just above, there's nowhere for the trade to go. The break is real. The trade isn't worth it.
