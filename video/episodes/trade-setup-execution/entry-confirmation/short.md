@art timing-line
Enter early, and you get a better price. Enter late, and you get more evidence. You can't have both.

@list items="Reaction close: earlier@Reaction close|Structure shift: middle@structure shift|Retest: later@retest"
A reaction close comes earliest. A structure shift sits in the middle. A retest comes latest, with the most evidence, and sometimes a missed trade.

@words text="Choose before the setup"
So choose your model before the setup, and take the first candle that meets it.
