@art invented-targets
Your target is two R. But what if resistance sits well before it? Which one wins?

@art opposing-levels
The structure does. Targets come from prior highs, lows, range edges and other opposing levels.

@words text="Structure picks · R measures"
Pick the target from structure, then measure it in R. Never let the ratio you'd like invent the target.
