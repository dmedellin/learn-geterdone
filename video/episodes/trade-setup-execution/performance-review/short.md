title: Your overall profit is hiding something
---

@art aggregate-hides
Your journal shows a small overall profit. That number may be hiding a losing setup.

@art segment-split
Strong setups can mask weak ones. So segment first: by setup, direction, plan adherence and execution quality.

@words text="One weakness → one rule"
Then pick the one weakness the data supports, and write one measurable rule for the next sample. Change one thing at a time.
