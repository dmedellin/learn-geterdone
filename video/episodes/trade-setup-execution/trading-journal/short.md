@art plan-vs-actual
Write your trade thesis after the outcome, and you've already changed it.

@words text="Hindsight changes what seemed knowable"
Hindsight changes what appeared knowable before entry. And a thesis rewritten after the fact can't be reviewed honestly.

@art journal-card
So record the thesis before entry, the execution while it's factual, and the result in R after the exit.
