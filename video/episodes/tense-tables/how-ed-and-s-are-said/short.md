@math items="walked /t/ · played /d/ · wanted /ɪd/@Walked, played and wanted"
Walked, played and wanted. Same spelling, three different sounds.

@art throat-wave
Put a finger on your throat. After a sound with no shake, like the k in walk, e-d is t. After a sound with a shake, like a vowel, it's d.

@words text="want → wanted" sub="after t or d: an extra part"
And after t or d, it gets a part of its own: wanted.
