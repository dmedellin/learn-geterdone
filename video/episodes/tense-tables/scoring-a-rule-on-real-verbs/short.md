@words text="leaf → leaves" sub="-f becomes -ves"
For nouns, f often becomes v-e-s: one leaf, two leaves. So the rule was added for verbs too.

@math items="1209 of 1210 → 1205 of 1210@fell from"
The score fell from twelve hundred and nine to twelve hundred and five, because brief, golf, proof and roof are verbs that simply take s.
