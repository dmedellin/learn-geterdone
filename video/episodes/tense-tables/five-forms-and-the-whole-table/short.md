@art twelve-grid
Twelve tense boxes in English. You only need five verb forms to fill them.

@math items="walk · walks · walking · walked · walked@walk, walks"
For walk, the five forms are walk, walks, walking, walked, and the form after have, walked again.

@math items="she has walked@she has walked;;she is walking@she is walking;;she will walk@she will walk"
Every other box is one of those forms with a small word in front. She has walked. She is walking. She will walk.
