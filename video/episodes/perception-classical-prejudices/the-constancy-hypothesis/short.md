@art muller-lyer
These two lines are the same length. But are they equal?

@math items="predicted: 10 − 10 = 0 cm@ten minus ten;;reported: 2 cm longer@two centimetres"
Measure them: ten minus ten, no difference. People still report one, say two centimetres. The textbook calls that a mistake.

@words text="Neither equal nor unequal"
Merleau-Ponty says equal and unequal belong to a measured world. In the figure you're looking at, one line becomes different, not unequal.
