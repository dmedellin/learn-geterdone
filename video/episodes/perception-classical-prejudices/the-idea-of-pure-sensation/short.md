@art dots-shift
This dot never moved, and it lost its partner.

@math items="0, 1, 4, 5, 8, 9 → three pairs@three pairs;;move 5 to 6 → {0, 1} {4} {6} {8, 9}@move"
Dots at nought, one, four, five, eight and nine group into three pairs. Move the five to six, and the four stands alone.

@words text="The group is in no single dot"
Its group was never in the dot. For Merleau-Ponty, the simplest thing you perceive is already a figure on a ground.
