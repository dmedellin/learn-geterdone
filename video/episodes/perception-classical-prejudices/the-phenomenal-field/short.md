@art window-on-things
What if science is a kind of perception that forgot where it came from?

@words text="Not an inner world"
Merleau-Ponty's point: sensation and judgement looked clear only while the world was taken as finished. Set that aside and you find the phenomenal field. Not a private inner world, but the world as lived.

@art circle-look
There, a circle isn't its mathematical law. It's its physiognomy, the look it has, the way a face has one.
