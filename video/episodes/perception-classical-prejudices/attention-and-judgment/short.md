@art ship-in-forest
Masts in a forest. Or trees. When do you see the ship?

@math items="cues 1 and 2: forest or ship, count 2@two;;cue 3: only the ship, count 1@three"
A judgement model keeps both readings for two cues. At cue three, the ship's colour and fit, it concludes: ship.

@art ship-revealed
But Merleau-Ponty describes it the other way round. Suddenly the details become part of the ship. The reasons are found afterwards.
