@art word-blanks
You misread a word, and memory filled the gap. Or did it?

@math items="ho_se → house, horse@two words;;in a sentence → one@sentence"
Leave a letter out of h, o, blank, s, e, and memory offers two words, house and horse. It can't choose. A sentence around the word chooses.

@words text="To perceive is not to remember"
For Merleau-Ponty, to call up the right memory, what you see must already have its shape. Then memory has nothing left to do.
