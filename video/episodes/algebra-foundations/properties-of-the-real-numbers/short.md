@words text="−(x − 3) = ?"
Minus, x minus three. Is it minus x minus three? No.

@art distribute-arrows
Write that minus sign as minus one, times the bracket. Now it's just distribution, and the minus one reaches every term inside.

@math items="(−1)(x) = −x@times x;;(−1)(−3) = +3@times minus three;;−(x − 3) = −x + 3@so it's minus x plus three"
Minus one times x is minus x. Minus one times minus three is plus three. So it's minus x plus three.

@words text="Compute the signs"
Don't remember signs. Compute them.
