@words text="12 × 10⁴"
Twelve times ten to the fourth isn't scientific notation. Can you fix it?

@art seesaw
Twelve is too big for the front, so move the point one place left. That divides the mantissa by ten.

@math items="12 × 10⁴ = 1.2 × 10⁵@ten to the fifth"
So the power of ten has to grow by ten to pay for it. That's one point two times ten to the fifth.

@words text="not 1.2 × 10⁴"
Leave the exponent alone, and you've changed the number by a factor of ten.
