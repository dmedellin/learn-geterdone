title: x squared at minus three

@words text="x² at x = −3"
x squared, at x equals minus three. Is it nine, or minus nine?

@art escaped-sign
Write it without brackets, and you've got minus three squared. The exponent grabs the three, the sign escapes, and you get minus nine.

@math items="(−3)² = 9@in brackets"
Put the minus three in brackets, and the sign stays in the base. Then it's nine.

@words text="Brackets, every time"
So when you substitute, write the value in brackets. Every time.
