@words text="8^(2/3) = ?"
Eight to the two thirds. Is it eight times two thirds? No.

@art root-first
A fraction exponent means a root and a power. The bottom number is the root. The top number is the power.

@math items="∛8 = 2@cube root of eight;;2² = 4@two squared"
The cube root of eight is two. Two squared is four. So eight to the two thirds is four.

@words text="Root first"
Take the root first, and the numbers stay small.
