@words text="five less than n"
Five less than n. Most people write five minus n, and it's backwards.

@art order-reverse
Less than names the second quantity first. You start with n, and take five away.

@math items="n − 5@n minus five;;test n = 8:   8 − 5 = 3@at eight"
So it's n minus five. Test it at eight: five less than eight is three, and eight minus five is three.

@words text="Test with a number"
One test number catches the wrong order.
