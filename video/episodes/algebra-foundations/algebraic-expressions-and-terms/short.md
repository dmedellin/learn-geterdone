@words text="5(x + 2)"
How many terms are in five times, x plus two?

@art bracket-one-term
As written, just one. Terms split only at plus and minus signs outside every bracket, and this plus sign is inside one.

@math items="5(x + 2):   one term@just one term;;5x + 10:   two terms@two terms"
So it's just one term. Multiply it out, and five x plus ten has two terms. Same value for every x, different count.

@words text="Name the form"
How many terms is always a question about one way of writing it.
