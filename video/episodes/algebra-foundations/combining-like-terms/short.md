@words text="3x² + 5x² = ?"
Three x squared plus five x squared. Is it eight x to the fourth?

@art common-factor
Both terms share the same variable part, x squared. Pull it out, and you're just adding three and five.

@math items="3x² + 5x² = (3 + 5)x² = 8x²@three plus five"
Three plus five, times x squared, is eight x squared.

@words text="The exponent never changes"
Only the coefficients add. Exponents add when you multiply powers, not when you add terms.
