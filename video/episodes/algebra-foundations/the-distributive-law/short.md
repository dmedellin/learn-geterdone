@words text="−2(x − 5)"
Minus two times, x minus five. Most people get the second term wrong.

@art reach-every-term
The minus two outside multiplies every term inside. Not just the first one.

@math items="(−2)(x) = −2x@times x;;(−2)(−5) = +10@times minus five;;= −2x + 10@so it's minus two x"
Minus two times x is minus two x. Minus two times minus five is plus ten. So it's minus two x, plus ten.

@words text="not −2x − 5"
Not minus two x minus five. That's what happens when the eye moves on after the first term.
