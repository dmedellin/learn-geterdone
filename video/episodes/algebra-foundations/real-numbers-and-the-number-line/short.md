@words text="1/3 = 0.333 ?"
Is one third the same as zero point three three three? No, and the gap matters.

@math items="0.333 = 333/1000@three hundred thirty three thousandths;;1/3 = 0.333…@repeats forever"
Zero point three three three, stopped, is three hundred thirty three thousandths. One third is zero point three that repeats forever. The stopped one is smaller.

@art one-point
A number is a place on the line. The fraction names that place exactly. A decimal you can see is only a report on it.

@words text="Compare exactly" sub="common denominator, not rounding"
So when two numbers are close, don't decide with a rounded decimal. Use a common denominator.
