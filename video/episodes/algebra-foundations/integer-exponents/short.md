title: Is two to the minus three negative?

@words text="2⁻³ = ?"
Two to the minus three. Is that a negative number?

@art cross-bar
No. A negative exponent tells you which side of the fraction bar the power goes on. It says nothing about the sign.

@math items="2⁻³ = 1 / 2³ = 1/8@one over two cubed"
So two to the minus three is one over two cubed. That's one eighth, a positive number.

@words text="(−2)³ = −8" sub="the base decides the sign"
The sign of the value comes from the base. Minus two, cubed, is minus eight.
