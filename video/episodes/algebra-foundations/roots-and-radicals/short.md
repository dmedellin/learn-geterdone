The square root of nine. Is it three, or plus or minus three?

@art principal-root
A radical sign names one number: by convention, the one that isn't negative. So the root of nine is three.

@math items="x² = 9   →   x = ±3@x squared equals nine"
The plus or minus belongs to an equation. x squared equals nine has two solutions, so you write the plus or minus yourself.

@words text="The symbol names one"
The equation has two answers. The symbol names one.
