@art mirror-echo
Hobbes argued that the red you see isn't in the thing at all.

@words text="If it were in the object, it couldn't be severed"
If colours and sounds were in objects, they couldn't be pulled away from them. But a mirror shows the image in one place and the thing in another, and an echo does the same for sound.

@words text="Motion produces nothing but motion"
So in the object, colour is just motion of matter. What you see is how that motion seems to you.
