Get one definition wrong, and the mistake doesn't stay put.

@math items="man = living-creature, forgetting rational@forget;;man, Peter, just: rational? false@three"
In the lesson's lab, forget that man is rational. Man is rational turns false. So do Peter is rational and just is rational, because both are defined from man. That's three wrong verdicts from one slip.
