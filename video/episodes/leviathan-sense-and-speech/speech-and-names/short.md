@art one-and-many
Is there such a thing as man, or only men?

@words text="Nothing in the world universal but names"
Hobbes's answer is blunt. There's nothing in the world universal but names. Peter is one man, and every other thing named is individual and singular too.

@art nested-names
A common name like man is just one name put on many things, for some likeness they share. And for Hobbes, the likeness isn't one more thing.
