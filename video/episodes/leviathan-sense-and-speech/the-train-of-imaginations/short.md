title: Why being sure of the rule doesn't make you sure of the next case
---

@art two-benches
You can be almost certain about a court and still not know the verdict.

@math items="severe bench: 4096/4097@four thousand;;next hanged: 3277/4097@three thousand"
In the lesson's model, after six hangings the man is as good as sure the bench is severe. But it hangs only four in five, so the next offender hangs with chance three thousand two hundred seventy seven over four thousand ninety seven.

@words text="It is but presumption"
That's Hobbes's point: prudence is presumption, never certainty.
