@art giant-of-many
Hobbes said the state is a man, and we built him.

@list items="Sovereignty: the soul@the soul|Laws: reason and will@reason and will|Civil war: death@death"
In Leviathan, the sovereignty is the soul, the laws are its reason and will, and civil war is its death.

@words text="Artificial, not grown"
The key word is artificial. This man was put together by covenants, not grown like an organism. And anything people made, they can ask how to make well.
