This insurance costs more than the loss it covers, on average. It's still worth buying.

@words text="cover 19 · expected loss 18.75"
Cover costs nineteen. A fire, with a one in four chance, would cost seventy five, so the expected loss is eighteen point seven five.

@words text="insure: utility 9 · risk: 8.75"
But that seventy five would come out of a smaller pot, where each unit is worth more. In utility, insuring wins.
