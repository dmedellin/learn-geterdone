title: Safe or bold? Two sensible rules disagree

@art two-temperaments
Safe pays three either way. Bold pays two or ten. Which would you pick?

@words text="Maximin: safe"
Plan for the worst, and you pick safe. Its worst is three. Bold's is two.

@words text="Minimax regret: bold"
Plan to avoid regret, and you pick bold. Safe could leave you seven short of what bold would pay. Bold is never more than one short.
