@art not-a-payoff
Taking the umbrella has an expected utility of four thirds. It never pays four thirds.

@math items="2 · 1/3 + 1 · 2/3 = 4/3@two times a third"
It pays two in rain and one when dry. With rain at a third, two times a third plus one times two thirds is four thirds.

@words text="A score, not a forecast"
That's an average used to rank acts, not a prediction of what happens.
