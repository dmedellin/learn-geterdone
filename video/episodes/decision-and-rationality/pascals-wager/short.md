@art two-gods
Pascal's wager says: bet on God, because the reward is infinite. Here's the catch.

@words text="p · ∞ = ∞"
With an infinite reward, any probability above zero wins. So the argument doesn't need God to be likely, only possible.

@words text="Every rival god gets the same argument."
But then any rival god offering an infinite reward wins too, and you can't wager on all of them.
