@art best-cell
Leaving the umbrella has the best possible outcome. That doesn't make it the best choice.

@math items="leave:  rain −3,  dry 3@minus three"
Leave it at home, and you score three if it's dry. But the same act scores minus three if it rains.

@words text="Compare column by column."
One cell doesn't rank an act. To beat another act, it has to be at least as good in every state.
