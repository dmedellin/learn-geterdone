@art trader-swap
You prefer C to A, B to C, and A to B. A trader is about to get rich.

@math items="A → C → B → A@He swaps you;;−3 cents a lap@three cents"
You hold A. He swaps you C for a cent, then B for a cent, then A for a cent. Each swap you wanted. You're back to A, three cents poorer.

@words text="Transitivity, or the pump"
And he can run that lap forever. That's the money pump.
