@art ninety-balls
Thirty red balls. Sixty black or yellow, mix unknown. Bet on red, or on black?

@words text="Most pick red. Then black or yellow."
Most people bet on red. But offered red or yellow against black or yellow, they pick black or yellow. Known odds, both times.

@words text="p(black) < 1/3 and > 1/3"
The first choice needs black below a third. The second needs it above a third. No single guess fits both.
