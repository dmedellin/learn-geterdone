@art sure-vs-shot
A sure million, or a gamble that might pay five? Most people take the sure million.

@words text="10% of 5M, or 11% of 1M?"
Now, ten percent of five million, or eleven percent of one million? Most people take the five million.

@words text="Same gap, opposite signs"
But expected utility scores both choices by exactly the same expression. Whatever your utilities, those two preferences contradict it. That's the Allais paradox.
