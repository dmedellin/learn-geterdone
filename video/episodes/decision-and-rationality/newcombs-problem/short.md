A thousand dollars in one box. A million, or nothing, in the other. Take both, or just one?

@words text="Both: always a thousand more"
The boxes are already filled, so taking both always gets you a thousand more. That's dominance.

@words text="One: 900,000 against 101,000"
But a predictor that's right nine times in ten filled the box only if it foresaw you taking one. Score by its record, and one box wins.
