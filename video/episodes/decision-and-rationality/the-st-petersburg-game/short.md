@art coin-run
Toss a coin until heads. The prize doubles every toss. What's it worth?

@math items="2 · 1/2 + 4 · 1/4 + 8 · 1/8 + … = 1 + 1 + 1 + …@one plus one"
Every term of the expected value is the prize times its chance, and every one comes to one. One plus one plus one, forever. Infinite.

@words text="Bank of 1024 → worth 11"
But a bank with only a thousand and twenty four dollars caps the prizes. Then the game is worth eleven.
