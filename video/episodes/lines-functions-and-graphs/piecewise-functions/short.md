@words text="8 − 2(−1) = 10" sub="so f(−1) = 10?"
Eight minus two times minus one is ten. So f of minus one is ten, right?

@words text="8 − 2x  if x ≥ 2"
Not here. That formula only applies when x is at least two, and minus one isn't.

@math items="−1 ≤ −1 < 2  →  x²@middle piece;;f(−1) = (−1)² = 1@f of minus one is one"
Minus one falls in the middle piece, x squared. So f of minus one is one. Read the condition first, then use its formula.
