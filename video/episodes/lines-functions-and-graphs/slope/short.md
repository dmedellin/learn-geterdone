@art sign-flip
One subtraction in the wrong order, and your rising line starts falling.

@math items="(10 − 1) / (4 − (−2)) = 3/2@ten minus one;;✗  (10 − 1) / (−2 − 4) = −3/2@mix the order"
From minus two, one to four, ten: ten minus one, over four minus minus two, is three halves. Now mix the order on the bottom, and you get minus three halves.

@words text="First point first, top and bottom"
So pick which point comes first, and use it first in both differences.
