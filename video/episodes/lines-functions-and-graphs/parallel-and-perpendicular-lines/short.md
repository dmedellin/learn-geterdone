@art right-angle-cross
Perpendicular to a slope of three quarters? It isn't four thirds.

@words text="flip, then negate"
You need both moves. Flip the fraction, and change the sign. Flipping swaps rise and run. The minus turns a rising line into a falling one.

@math items="3/4  →  −4/3@minus four thirds;;3/4 · (−4/3) = −1@multiply"
So three quarters becomes minus four thirds. Multiply them, and you get minus one. That's the test.
