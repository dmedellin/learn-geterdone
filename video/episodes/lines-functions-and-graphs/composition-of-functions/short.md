@words text="f ∘ g" sub="which one goes first?"
In f circle g, f is written first. But g goes first.

@art two-machines
f circle g of x means f of g of x. g works on x, then f works on the answer. Read it as f after g.

@art order-swap
With f as two x plus three and g as x minus four, f after g gives minus one at two. g after f gives three. Order matters.
