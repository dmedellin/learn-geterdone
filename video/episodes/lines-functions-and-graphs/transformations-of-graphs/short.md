title: Why f(x − 3) moves right
---

@art shift-right
f of x minus three has a minus in it. So why does the graph move right?

@words text="x − 3 = t  →  x = t + 3"
Ask where the new graph takes the old value at t. You need x minus three to equal t. So x is t plus three.

@art point-map
Same height, three further right. Every point moves that way, so the whole graph moves right by three.
