@art swapped-points
Is three, five the same point as five, three? Not even close.

@art point-plotted
The first number always goes across. The second always goes up. So three, five is three right and five up.

@words text="(3, 5) ≠ (5, 3)" sub="both in quadrant I"
Five, three is five right and three up. Both land in the same quadrant, so a swap slips past any check that only looks at signs.
