Is root x the inverse of x squared? Only with one extra sentence.

@words text="2 → 4   and   −2 → 4"
x squared sends two and minus two both to four. Going backwards, four would need two answers. So x squared has no inverse.

@art restricted-half
Cut it down to x at least zero. Now no output repeats, and the inverse is root x.
