title: Can two inputs share an output?
---

@art many-to-one
Three and minus three both square to nine. Is x squared still a function?

@words text="Outputs may repeat. Inputs may not."
Yes. A function gives each input exactly one output. Sharing an output is fine.

@art circle-vertical
A circle is different. At x equals three, y is four and minus four. One input, two outputs, so it's not a function. A vertical line crossing twice shows it.
