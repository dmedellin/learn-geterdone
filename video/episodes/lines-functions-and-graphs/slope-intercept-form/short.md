@words text="2x + 3y = 12" sub="slope = 2?"
What's the slope of two x plus three y equals twelve? It isn't two.

@math items="3y = −2x + 12@subtract two x;;y = −(2/3)x + 4@divide every term"
First get y alone. Subtract two x, then divide every term by three. Now it reads y equals minus two thirds x plus four.

@art intercept-start
Only now can you read it. The slope is minus two thirds, and the line crosses the y axis at four.
