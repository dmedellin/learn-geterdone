title: Why plot a third point?
---

@art third-point-check
Two points always look right. That's exactly the problem.

@words text="Any two points lie on a line"
Any two points lie on some straight line, so two points can never show you a mistake.

@math items="(6, 0)   (0, 4)   (3, 2)@plot a third"
So plot a third. For two x plus three y equals twelve, the intercepts are six, zero and zero, four. The third, three, two, lands right on the line.

@end text="Full lesson on Learn"
If the third point misses, one of them is wrong. The full lesson is on Learn.
