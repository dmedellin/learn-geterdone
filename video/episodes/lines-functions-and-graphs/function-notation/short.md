@words text="f(x) = x² − 4x + 5" sub="f(−2) = ?"
One pair of missing brackets turns seventeen into minus seven.

@math items="(−2)² − 4(−2) + 5 = 17@in brackets;;✗  −2² − 4·2 + 5 = −7@Without them"
Evaluate f of minus two. Put minus two in brackets, and you get four plus eight plus five, seventeen. Without them, you get minus four minus eight plus five, minus seven.

@art expression-in-slot
So every input goes into the slot in brackets, whatever it is.
