title: Greater than doesn't always mean above

@art below-line
Greater than means shade above the line, right? Not always.

@math items="4x − 2y > 8@greater than eight;;y < 2x − 4@flips the sign"
Four x minus two y is greater than eight. Solve for y and you divide by minus two, which flips the sign. It's really y less than two x minus four.

@art test-point
So don't guess. Test zero, zero: zero is greater than eight is false, so shade the other side.
