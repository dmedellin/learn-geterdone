Take three out of the domain. Which number disappears from the range?

@words text="q(x) = 2x + 1,   x ≠ 3"
Here's q of x equals two x plus one, for every x except three.

@math items="q(3) = 7@seven;;range misses 7, not 3@not three"
Put three through the formula: two times three plus one is seven. So the point three, seven is gone, and the range misses seven, not three.
