title: Write a line's equation without solving anything

@art general-point
One point and a slope. That's all you need to write a line down.

@words text="y − y₁ = m(x − x₁)"
Point slope form: y minus y one equals m times x minus x one. Drop in the point and the slope, and you're done.

@math items="through (−2, 1), m = 3/2;;y − 1 = (3/2)(x + 2)@x plus two"
Through minus two, one with slope three halves, that's y minus one equals three halves times x plus two. Watch that double negative turn into x plus two.
