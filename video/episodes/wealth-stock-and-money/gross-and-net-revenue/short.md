Is a country's money part of its income? Adam Smith said no.

@quote text="The great wheel of circulation is altogether different from the goods which are circulated by means of it." cite="Adam Smith, The Wealth of Nations, Book II, Chapter 2"

@art one-guinea
The same guinea pays one man's pension today and another's tomorrow. What each man really gets is the guinea's worth, the goods it buys.
