title: Why Adam Smith called seed fixed capital
---

@art seed-granary
A farmer's seed goes into the ground and back every year. Adam Smith still called it fixed capital.

@words text="Does it change masters?"
His test isn't whether a thing moves. It's whether the owner profits by keeping it, or by parting with it. The seed goes back and forth between ground and granary, but it never changes masters.

@art farm-sort
So the seed is fixed, like the plough. The cattle bought to fatten and sell are circulating.
