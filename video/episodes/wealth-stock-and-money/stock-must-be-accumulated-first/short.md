title: Why a weaver needs food before he can weave
---

@art weaver-loom
A weaver can't eat his cloth until he's sold it. So who feeds him while he weaves?

@words text="A stock stored up beforehand"
Adam Smith said someone must store up a stock beforehand: food to keep him, yarn to work up, and a loom to work with, until his web is not only finished but sold.

@art three-stocks
It needn't be his. A master or lender can hold it. But it has to exist first, or he can't give himself to one trade.
