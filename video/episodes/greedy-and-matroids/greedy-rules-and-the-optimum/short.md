@art short-blocks
A greedy rule can hit the optimum and still be wrong.

@words text="Shortest first: 4 of 4" sub="on the lab's eleven intervals"
On the lab's eleven intervals, shortest first picks four, and the best possible is four.

@words text="0–5 · 4–6 · 5–10" sub="shortest first: 1 · optimum: 2"
Now try zero to five, four to six and five to ten. It grabs the short middle one, which blocks both others. One, when two was possible.
