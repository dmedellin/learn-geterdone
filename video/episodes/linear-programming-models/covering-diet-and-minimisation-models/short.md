@art open-region
This feasible region goes on forever. The problem still has an answer.

@math items="minimise 2G + 3P@requirement;;every step out costs money@costs money;;cheapest at (3, 2):  12@twelve"
A feed ration must meet a protein and a fibre requirement. Feed more of everything and it still qualifies, forever. But every step outwards costs money, so the cheapest ration is at a corner: three and two, costing twelve.

@words text="Unbounded is about the objective, not the region"
What matters is whether some direction lowers the cost.
