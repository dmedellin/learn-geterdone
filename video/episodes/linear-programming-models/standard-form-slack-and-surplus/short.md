title: Why a surplus is subtracted, not added
---

@art sign-trap
Add a surplus instead of subtracting it, and your model quietly changes.

@math items="F + G ≥ 4  →  F + G − e₁ = 4@At least four;;F + G + e₁ = 4  at (0, 10):  e₁ = −6@minus six"
At least four should become F plus G minus e one equals four, so e one is the over delivery. Add it instead, and at zero, ten you'd need e one equals minus six.

@words text="Only exact deliveries survive"
Every feasible point that over delivers is gone, and nothing warns you.
