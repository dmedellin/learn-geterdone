@art target-row
Here's the most common broken model: it makes profit a variable.

@words text="P = 3C + 5T" sub="one more letter, no information"
Someone adds P for profit, writes P equals three C plus five T, and maximises P. Nothing is gained. P is fixed once C and T are, so the model has one more variable and exactly the same answer.

@words text="Could you hand it over as an instruction?"
Here's the test. Make two chairs is an instruction. Earn thirty six pounds isn't.
