@art two-manufactories
Is profit just the boss's pay for managing? Adam Smith says no.

@math items="same wages:  20 · £15 = £300@twenty workmen;;capitals:  £1000 · £7300@capitals of;;profits at 10%:  £100 · £730@a hundred"
Two manufactories each pay twenty workmen fifteen pounds a year. One spends seven hundred on materials, the other seven thousand, so capitals of a thousand pounds and seven thousand three hundred. At ten per cent, profits of a hundred and seven hundred and thirty.

@words text="Profit follows the stock, not the toil"
The overseeing can be the same in both, yet one owner expects more than seven times the profit.
