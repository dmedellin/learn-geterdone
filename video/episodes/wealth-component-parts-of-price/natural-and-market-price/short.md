title: Why a glut of oranges is worse than a glut of iron

@art glut-oranges
Why is a glut of oranges worse than the same weight of old iron?

@words text="More brought than buyers will take at the natural price"
When more is brought to market than buyers will take at the natural price, some must sell to people who'll pay less. And that low price drags down the whole.

@art gravitation-line
How far it falls depends on the sellers' hurry. Oranges perish, so their sellers can't wait. Iron can sit.
