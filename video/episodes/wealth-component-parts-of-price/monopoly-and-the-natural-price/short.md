@art withhold-bars
Hold back more goods, get a higher price. But not always a bigger gain.

@math items="500 yards at 20s:  10 · 500 = 5000s@five hundred;;400 yards at 22s:  12 · 400 = 4800s@four hundred"
On the lesson's schedule, with a natural price of ten shillings, five hundred yards sell at twenty, five thousand shillings over. Four hundred sell at twenty two, but only four thousand eight hundred over.

@words text="The highest which can be got"
Smith's price of monopoly is the highest for the quantity brought.
