@art chartres-road
Could everything you see be an illusion? Watch what happens when one breaks.

@math items="poplars → church → Chartres@poplars"
Driving towards a town, you take it for poplars, then a church, then: it is Chartres. Each reading was replaced by another thing on the horizon. Never by nothing.

@words text="the horizon of all horizons"
That's Merleau-Ponty's reply to the sceptic. Every perception is cancelled only by another. The world is the horizon of all horizons.
