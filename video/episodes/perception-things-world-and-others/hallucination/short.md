@art two-powders
Can someone who hallucinates tell it from the real thing? Mostly, yes.

@words text="this powder was damp, the other was dry"
One patient complained of powder in her bed. Shown real powder, she said this powder was damp. The other was dry.

@words text="no definite path leading from it to all the remaining experiences"
For Merleau-Ponty, a hallucination is no part of the world. There's no path from it to the rest of experience, or to anyone else's.
