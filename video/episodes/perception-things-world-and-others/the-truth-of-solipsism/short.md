@art paul-grief
You can see your friend's grief. Can you ever live it as he does?

@words text="For him these situations are lived through, for me they are displayed."
Merleau-Ponty says no. For him these situations are lived through. For me they're displayed. That's a solipsism rooted in living experience.

@art woven-dialogue
And yet in a conversation, two thoughts weave into one fabric. Solitude and communication are two moments of one phenomenon.
