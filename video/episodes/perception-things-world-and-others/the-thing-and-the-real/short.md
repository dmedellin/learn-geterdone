title: You can't describe a carpet's colour without its wool
---

@art woolly-carpet
Try describing the blue of a carpet without mentioning wool.

@words text="it is impossible completely to describe the colour of the carpet without saying that it is a carpet, made of wool"
Merleau-Ponty says you can't. The colour as you see it is already woolly, already heavy and soft.

@words text="that unique manner of existing"
So a thing isn't a pile of qualities, or an idea that joins them. It's one manner of existing, found in each of them.
