@art paper-in-beam
A black disc can send your eye exactly as much light as white paper.

@math items="1/20 × 16 = 4/5@one twentieth"
In Gelb's experiment, a hidden beam of sixteen on a disc reflecting one twentieth gives four fifths, white paper's luminance. It looks faintly lit, like the room.

@words text="It is not seen itself, but causes us to see the rest."
Slip white paper into the beam, and the disc is suddenly black and strongly lit. Lighting isn't seen. It's what we see by.
