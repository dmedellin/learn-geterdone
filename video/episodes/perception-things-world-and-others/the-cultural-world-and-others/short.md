title: A baby understands biting without any analogy

@art baby-bite
Pretend to bite a baby's finger, and it opens its mouth. Why?

@words text="reasoning by analogy presupposes what it is called on to explain"
It's hardly seen its own face, and its teeth aren't like yours. So it can't be reasoning by analogy. Following Scheler, Merleau-Ponty says that reasoning presupposes what it's called on to explain.

@words text="the perception of others is anterior"
We don't infer other people. The perception of others comes first.
