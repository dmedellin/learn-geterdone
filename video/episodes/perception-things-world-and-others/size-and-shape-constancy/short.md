@art corridor-wave
A friend walks away down a corridor. Why don't they shrink?

@math items="170 × 2/8 = 85/2 cm@eighty five over two"
At eight metres, the retinal image says a hundred and seventy centimetres becomes eighty five over two. A quarter. You don't see that.

@art gallery-picture
Merleau-Ponty says each thing, like a picture in a gallery, has an optimum distance. Its real size is the size it shows there.
