@art kings-walls
Hobbes said we all want power after power. But not because we're greedy.

@words text="He cannot assure what he has without more"
The reason, he says, is that a man can't secure the power he already has without getting more. Standing still isn't safe.

@art two-houses
So even two moderate neighbours, who'd both rather hold, may both arm. Each fears being the one who holds while the other adds.
