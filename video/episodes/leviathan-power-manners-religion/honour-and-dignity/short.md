title: The same robe can honour you or mock you

@art kings-robe
A king's robe can make you honoured, or make you a fool.

@art robe-of-fool
One Persian king paraded Mordecai in the royal garment, and it was honour. Another let a man wear a royal robe, but as the king's fool, and it was dishonour.

@words text="The fountain of honour is the sovereign's will"
Same robe. The difference was what the sovereign meant by it. That's Hobbes's point: civil honour flows from the sovereign's will.
