@art one-person-pillar
Every religion, Hobbes says, rests at first on trust in one person.

@list items="Wisdom@wisdom|Sincerity@sincerity|Love@love|Miracles@miracles"
People trust that founder's wisdom, sincerity and love, and look for miracles as the sign of his calling.

@art golden-calf
Lose any of those, and faith goes with it. When Moses was gone forty days, Israel made a golden calf. Miracles failing, faith also failed.
