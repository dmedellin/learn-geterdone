@art crow-on-roof
A crow on the roof, and then bad news. Is the crow a sign?

@words text="After one or two encounters, ever after"
Hobbes said men take chance things for signs after one or two encounters, and call them signs ever after.

@math items="1/10 → 1/6@one in six;;two encounters → 9/34@nine thirty fourths"
On the lesson's numbers, one encounter moves a sensible man from one in ten to one in six. Two get him to nine thirty fourths. Hardly ever after.
