@words text="x < 4 or x > 1" sub="which numbers work?"
x less than four, or x greater than one. Which numbers work?

@art all-covered
All of them. Take any number. If it's below four, the first part holds.

@words text="not below 4 → at least 4 → above 1"
If it isn't below four, it's at least four, so it's above one, and the second part holds.

@art union
Or keeps every number that satisfies at least one. Nothing is left out.
