@words text="x/2 + x/3 = 5" sub="multiply by 6"
Clearing fractions? Here's the step that's easiest to forget.

@art clear-fractions
Multiply x over two plus x over three equals five by six. The fractions vanish: three x plus two x.

@art every-term
But the five gets multiplied too. Forget it and you get x equals one, which fails the check.

@math items="3x + 2x = 30@three x plus two x equals thirty;;x = 6@x equals six"
Done right, three x plus two x equals thirty, so x equals six.
