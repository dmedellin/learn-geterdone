@words text="|x| < 3  vs  |x| > 3" sub="and, or or?"
Absolute value inequalities: and, or or? You don't need to memorise it.

@art band
Less than three means within three of zero. That's one band, between minus three and three. Both conditions at once: an and.

@art two-rays
Greater than three means further than three from zero. That's two separate rays: below minus three, or above three. An or.
