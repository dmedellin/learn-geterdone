@words text="3x = 1" sub="is x = 0.333 a solution?"
Is zero point three three three a solution of three x equals one?

@art substitute
There's only one way to know. Substitute it. Three times three hundred and thirty three thousandths is nine hundred and ninety nine thousandths.

@art check-fail
That's not one. So it's false. Zero point three three three is not a solution, and rounding won't make it one.

@art check-pass
One third is. Three times one third is exactly one. A solution is a value that makes the statement true.
