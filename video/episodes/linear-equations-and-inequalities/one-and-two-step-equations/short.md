title: Why you add 4 before dividing by 3

@words text="3x − 4 = 11" sub="what do you undo first?"
Three x minus four equals eleven. Which step do you undo first?

@art recipe-forward
Read it as a recipe. Take x, multiply by three, then subtract four.

@art recipe-backward
Solving runs the recipe backwards. The subtraction came last, so undo it first. Add four to both sides, then divide by three.

@math items="3x = 15@three x equals fifteen;;x = 5@x equals five"
Three x equals fifteen, so x equals five.
