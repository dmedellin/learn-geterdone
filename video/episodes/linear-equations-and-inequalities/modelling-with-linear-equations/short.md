@words text="3 consecutive whole numbers, sum 50" sub="what are they?"
Three consecutive whole numbers add up to fifty. What are they?

@art consecutive
Call the first n. Then they're n, n plus one, and n plus two, so three n plus three equals fifty.

@art reject-answer
That gives n equals forty seven thirds. Not a whole number.

@words text="The fraction is the answer" sub="no such numbers exist"
The equation is solved, but the model isn't. No three consecutive whole numbers add to fifty, and the fraction is how the algebra tells you.
