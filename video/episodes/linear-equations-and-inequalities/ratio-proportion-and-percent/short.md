@words text="100 → +20% → −20% → ?" sub="back to 100?"
Up twenty percent, then down twenty percent. Are you back where you started?

@art base-shift
No. A hundred rises twenty percent to a hundred and twenty. The fall is twenty percent of a hundred and twenty, which is twenty four.

@words text="96, not 100"
So you land at ninety six. The two percents were taken on different bases.

@art part-whole
A percent means nothing until you say what it's a percent of.
