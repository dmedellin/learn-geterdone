title: Why multiplying by −1 flips an inequality
---

@words text="3 < 5" sub="multiply both sides by −1"
Three is less than five. Multiply both sides by minus one. What happens?

@art flip-negative
You get minus three and minus five. On the number line, minus three is to the right of minus five.

@words text="−3 > −5" sub="the relation turned round"
So minus three is greater than minus five. The relation had to turn round, or the statement would be false.

@words text="x + 5 < 2  →  x < −3" sub="no flip: you only subtracted"
But a minus sign on the page isn't enough. Only multiplying or dividing by a negative flips it.
