title: Why infinity always gets a round bracket
---

@words text="[3, ∞]  or  [3, ∞) ?"
Square bracket or round bracket next to infinity?

@art brackets-dots
A square bracket includes its endpoint. A round bracket leaves it out.

@art infinity-end
But infinity isn't a number. It's a direction, an end that never arrives. There's nothing there to include.

@words text="[3, ∞)" sub="always round at infinity"
So the bracket beside infinity is always round. Three to infinity gets a square bracket at three, and a round one at infinity. Every interval, every time.
