@words text="5x + 2 = 3x − 8" sub="x is on both sides"
x is on both sides. How do you get rid of one?

@art remove-term
Subtract three x from both sides. Once x has a value, three x is just a number, so the usual rule applies.

@math items="2x + 2 = −8@two x plus two;;2x = −10@two x equals minus ten;;x = −5@x equals minus five"
That leaves two x plus two equals minus eight. Then two x equals minus ten, and x equals minus five.

@art two-routes
Collect on the right instead and you still get minus five.
