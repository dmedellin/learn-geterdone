@art phase-line
You can see where every solution goes, without solving anything.

@math items="y′ = 2 − y@two minus y;;f(1) = 1 > 0   ↑@f of one;;f(3) = −1 < 0   ↓@f of three"
Take y prime equals two minus y. The equilibrium is two. Test either side: f of one is positive, so solutions below rise. f of three is negative, so solutions above fall.

@words text="Both arrows point at 2"
Every solution heads for two. That's a phase line.
