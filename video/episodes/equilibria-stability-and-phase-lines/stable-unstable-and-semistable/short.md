title: Semistable isn't "a bit stable"
---

@art one-sided
Semistable isn't halfway between stable and unstable. It's its own case.

@math items="f(y) = y²·(1 − y)@y squared;;f(−1) = 2 > 0,  f(1/2) = 1/8 > 0@both positive"
Take y squared times one minus y. Just below zero and just above, f is positive. Both positive, so both arrows point up.

@words text="drawn in from below, pushed away above"
So a solution below zero rises towards it, and one above rises away. Same sign on both sides means semistable, every time.
