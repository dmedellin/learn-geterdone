Zero slope at an equilibrium? That tells you nothing at all.

@math items="y′ = y³:  unstable@y cubed;;y′ = −y³:  stable@minus y cubed;;y′ = y²·(1 − y):  semistable@y squared times"
y cubed, minus y cubed, and y squared times one minus y all have f prime of zero equal to zero. Yet the first is unstable, the second stable, and the third semistable.

@words text="f′ = 0?  Use the signs of f"
When the slope is zero, fall back on the signs of f on either side.
