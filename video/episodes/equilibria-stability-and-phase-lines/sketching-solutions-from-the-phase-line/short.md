title: Where does the logistic curve change its bend?
---

@art logistic-sketch
A logistic curve changes its bend at a height, not at a time.

@math items="y″ = f′(y)·f(y)@f prime of y;;f′(y) = 1 − y/2 = 0  at y = 2@zero at two"
Differentiate y prime equals f of y, and y double prime is f prime of y times f of y. For y times one minus y over four, f prime is zero at two.

@words text="every solution bends at y = 2"
So every rising solution switches from bending up to bending down as it crosses two, whenever it gets there.
