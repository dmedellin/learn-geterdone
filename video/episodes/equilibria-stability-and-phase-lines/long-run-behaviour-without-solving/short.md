title: Can a solution bounce between two equilibria?
---

@art stays-in-gap
Can a solution wobble back and forth between two equilibria? Never.

@words text="can't cross an equilibrium"
Each equilibrium is itself a solution, and solutions can't cross. So a solution that starts between two of them stays between them.

@words text="one sign of f  →  one direction"
Inside that gap, f has one sign, so the solution only rises or only falls. It heads for the equilibrium its arrow points to.
