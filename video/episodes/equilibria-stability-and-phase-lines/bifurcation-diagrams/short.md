@art pitchfork
Turn one dial, and one equilibrium splits into three.

@math items="y′ = a·y − y³@a y minus y cubed;;a < 0:   0 stable@negative;;a = 4:   −2 stable,  0 unstable,  2 stable@At four"
Take y prime equals a y minus y cubed. For negative a, zero is the only equilibrium, and it's stable. At four, zero has turned unstable, and two new stable ones sit at minus two and two.

@words text="solid = stable,  dashed = unstable"
Drawn against a, that's a fork opening to the right.
