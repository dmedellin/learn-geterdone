@art amplifier
The best-rated leaders weren't more likely to lead high performing teams.

@words text="Top 10% of leaders" sub="no more likely, possibly less, to be high performers"
Accelerate looked at teams with the top ten percent of reported transformational leadership. They were no more likely, and possibly less likely, to be high performers than teams overall.

@words text="Leaders amplify practices"
The authors' reading: a leader can't reach goals alone. Teams deliver, through architecture, technical practices and Lean principles. Leadership amplifies those.
