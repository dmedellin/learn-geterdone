@art two-funnels
A group can be twenty percent of a field and eleven percent of a survey.

@math items="Group B: 200 of 1,000 = 20% of the field@two hundred;;30 of 270 answers = 11.1% of the sample@eleven point one"
In the lesson's invented region, Group B is two hundred of a thousand people. But only fifteen percent of them answer, against thirty percent of Group A, so they're eleven point one percent of the answers.

@art reach-out
Reach them through a list they use, and the share matches again. Who answers depends on how you ask.
