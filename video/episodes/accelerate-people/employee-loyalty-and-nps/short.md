@art edge-step
One person changes an eight to a nine. The score jumps ten points.

@words text="9–10 promote · 7–8 passive · 0–6 detract"
The employee Net Promoter Score is the percentage of promoters minus the percentage of detractors.

@math items="before: 40% − 30% = 10@forty;;8 → 9: 50% − 30% = 20@fifty"
On a team of ten, forty minus thirty is ten. When one eight becomes a nine, it's fifty minus thirty, twenty. But an eight that becomes a seven changes nothing.
