@art two-groups
Teams with a training budget were nearly twice as likely to hit their goal. Or were they?

@math items="budget: 13/20 = 65% · none: 7/20 = 35%@sixty five;;ratio = 1.86@one point eight six"
On forty invented teams, sixty five percent with a budget met the goal, against thirty five percent without: a ratio of one point eight six.

@art split-groups
Split them by whether the manager removes obstacles, and within each group the ratio is one. A third factor stood behind it.
