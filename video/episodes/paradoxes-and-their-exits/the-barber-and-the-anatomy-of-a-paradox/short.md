title: Who shaves the barber?
---

@art village-barber
The barber shaves everyone who doesn't shave themselves. So who shaves the barber?

@math items="b shaves b  ↔  b doesn't shave b@exactly when"
Apply the rule to him. He shaves himself exactly when he doesn't. That's false whether he does or not.

@art empty-chair
The logic is fine. So the premise goes: there's no such barber. The rule reads clearly, and nobody could ever meet it.
