It's raining, but I don't know it. That could be true. So why can't anyone say it?

@math items="p ∧ ¬□p:  can be true@can be true;;□(p ∧ ¬□p):  true at no reflexive world@know it"
The sentence can be true. But to know it, you'd have to know it's raining, and know you don't know. With knowledge factive, that can't happen.

@words text="True, but unknowable by you"
Some sentences are true, and impossible for their own subject to know. Not from ignorance, but from what they say.
