@art jar-label
This sentence is not true. Is it true?

@math items="p ↔ ¬p@exactly when;;both rows:  false@both rows"
If it's true, then what it says holds, so it's not true. If it's not true, that's what it says, so it's true. It's true exactly when it's not. And that's false in both rows.

@art language-ladder
Tarski's way out: no language can contain its own truth predicate.
