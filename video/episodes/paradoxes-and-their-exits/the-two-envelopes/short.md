@art two-envelopes
You find eight. The other holds four or sixteen. Switching is worth ten. So always switch?

@math items="8 in the middle:  switch 10@fifty fifty;;8 at the top:  switch 4@largest amount"
That needs a fifty fifty chance at every amount. But if eight is the largest amount there is, the other must hold four.

@words text="The one half can't hold everywhere"
The amount you see is evidence. With a bounded prior, the chance of double changes with the amount.
