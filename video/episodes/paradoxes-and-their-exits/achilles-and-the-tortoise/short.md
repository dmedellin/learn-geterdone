title: Can Achilles ever catch the tortoise?
---

@art race-track
Every time Achilles reaches where the tortoise was, it's moved on. So he never catches it?

@math items="100 + 10 + 1 + …@a hundred;;= 1000/9@a thousand ninths"
The gaps are a hundred, ten, one, each a tenth of the last. They add up to a thousand ninths of a metre.

@art meeting-point
That's where he draws level, a little over a hundred and eleven metres. Every one of Zeno's stages happens before it.
