@art three-doors
Two doors left. Isn't it fifty fifty? Not if the host knew where the car was.

@math items="car at 1:  host had a choice, 1/2@a choice;;car at 2:  host was forced, 1@forced"
If the car's behind your door, the host had a choice. If it's behind door two, he was forced to open three. That makes switching twice as likely to win.

@words text="Switch: 2/3"
Count the host's choices, not the doors.
