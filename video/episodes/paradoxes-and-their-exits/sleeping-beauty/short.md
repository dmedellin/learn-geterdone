@art wakings
Heads, you're woken once. Tails, twice, with no memory. Woken now, what's the chance of heads?

@math items="halfer:  factor 1  →  1/2@Halfers;;thirder:  factor 1/2  →  1/3@Thirders"
Halfers say being awake was certain either way, so one half. Thirders say tails makes twice the wakings, so one third.

@words text="Same rule, different evidence"
Both use Bayes' rule correctly, from the same fair prior. They disagree only about what the evidence is.
