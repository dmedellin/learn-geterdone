title: Is Thomson's lamp on or off?
---

@art lamp-flicker
A lamp is switched on and off infinitely often in one minute. Is it on or off at the end?

@math items="times:  1/2, 3/4, 7/8, …  → 1@The times;;states:  on, off, on, …  no limit@the states"
The times add up to exactly one minute. But the states just alternate, on, off, on, and approach nothing at all.

@art no-last-switch
There's no last switch. So the story fixes when it ends, but not how the lamp ends up.
