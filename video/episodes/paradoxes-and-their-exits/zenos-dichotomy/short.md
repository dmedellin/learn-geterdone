@art halving-room
To cross a room, you must first go halfway, then half the rest, forever. So you never arrive?

@math items="1/2 + 1/4 + 1/8 + …@a half;;after n steps:  1 − 1/2ⁿ@after n steps;;limit:  1@one"
Add them up: a half, a quarter, an eighth. After n steps you've covered one minus one over two to the n. The limit is exactly one.

@words text="Infinitely many steps, finite total"
The steps shrink, so infinitely many of them add up to the whole crossing, and no more.
