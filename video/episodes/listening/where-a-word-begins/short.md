@art no-gaps
Speech has no spaces. So how do you hear where words begin?

@words text="FA·ther  ·  AN·swer  ·  HAP·pi·ness" sub="the strong part comes first"
Listen for the strong part. Most English words that carry meaning start on it: father, answer, happiness.

@words text="390 of 474  =  82.3%" sub="start on their strong part"
On the page in the lab, three hundred and ninety of four hundred and seventy-four words start strong. About four in five. So a strong part usually means a new word.
