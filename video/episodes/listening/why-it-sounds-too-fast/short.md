@art squash
English isn't spoken too fast. Two words in five are squashed.

@words text="387 of 936 words" sub="41.3%, from just 43 different words"
On a real page of a novel, three hundred and eighty-seven of nine hundred and thirty-six words come from one short list.

@math items="to → tə@like to;;of → əv@of and;;and → ən@and and"
Small words like to, of and and. Said in a sentence, they're short and flat: tuh, uv, un. Learn that list, and the gaps stop being gaps.
