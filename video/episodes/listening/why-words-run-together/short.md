@art hop-consonant
An apple sounds like a napple. That's not a mistake. It's normal.

@words text="an apple  →  a napple" sub="the consonant joins the next word"
When a word ends in a consonant and the next begins with a vowel, the consonant is said with the next word. Take it sounds like takit.

@words text="proud of  →  proudov" sub="listen for the strong part"
So proud of can arrive as one word. Listen for the strong part, proud, and what follows it is of.
