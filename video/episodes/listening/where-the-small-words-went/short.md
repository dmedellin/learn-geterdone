@art share-bars
In an old play, most nots are short. In the novel, almost none.

@math items="the play, 1895  ·  22 of 36 short@In the play;;the novel, 1813  ·  1 of 13 short@In the novel"
In the play, twenty-two of thirty-six nots are written short, like don't and can't. In the novel, just one of thirteen.

@words text="made to be said" sub="talk contracts · careful text for readers does not"
The play was written to be said, and text made to be said contracts. So when people talk, expect the short forms, and listen hard for the short not.
