title: How a logic formula becomes a subset sum problem
---

@art digit-columns
A logic formula can become a list of numbers with a target sum.

@words text="1 per variable · 4 per clause"
Give each variable two numbers, one for true and one for false, with a digit for every clause it satisfies. The target needs a one per variable and a four per clause.

@art failing-clause
Slack can add at most three to a clause. So a subset hits the target exactly when every clause has a true literal.
