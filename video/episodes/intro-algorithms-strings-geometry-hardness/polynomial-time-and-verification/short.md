@art class-diagram
NP doesn't stand for not polynomial. And every easy problem is in it.

@words text="NP: a short certificate, checked fast"
NP is defined by checking. A problem is in NP if every yes has a short certificate that a polynomial time algorithm can verify.

@art hamiltonian-check
Hand someone an ordering of the vertices, and they can check it's a hamiltonian cycle quickly. Finding one is the part nobody knows how to do fast.
