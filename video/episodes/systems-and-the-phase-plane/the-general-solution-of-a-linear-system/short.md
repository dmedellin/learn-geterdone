title: The nearest eigenline is not your solution

@art neither-line
Your start is near an eigenline. So the solution is that line's solution, right?

@words text="(3, 0) is not on the diagonal"
For rows one, two and three, zero, the start three, zero is closest to the diagonal. But the diagonal solution passes through one, one, not three, zero.

@math items="C₁ = 3/5,   C₂ = 9/5@three fifths"
Fit the constants properly and C one is three fifths, C two is nine fifths. Both terms are there, and the orbit curves between the lines.
