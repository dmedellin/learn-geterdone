There's a balance point for foxes and rabbits. They never reach it.

@math items="V = x − ln x + y − 2·ln y@V equals;;V′ = 0@V prime is zero"
For x prime equals two x minus x y, and y prime equals minus y plus x y, the quantity V equals x minus log x, plus y minus two log y, never changes. V prime is zero.

@words text="start: V = 2  ·  balance: V ≈ 1.61"
Start at one, one and V is two. At the balance it's about one point six one. So you can never get there. You go round.
