@art cubic-escape
The Jacobian says this origin is a centre. Every orbit leaves it anyway.

@math items="x′ = −y + x³,   y′ = x + y³@x prime equals;;J: τ = 0,  Δ = 1,  λ = ±i@a centre;;D′ = 2x⁴ + 2y⁴ > 0@two x to the fourth"
Take x prime equals minus y plus x cubed, and y prime equals x plus y cubed. At the origin, the Jacobian is a centre. But the squared distance has derivative two x to the fourth plus two y to the fourth.

@words text="Centre: inconclusive"
That's positive, so orbits spiral out. At a centre, the curved terms decide.
