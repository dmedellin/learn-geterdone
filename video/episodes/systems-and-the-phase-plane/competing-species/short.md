Both species are alive at this balance point. It still won't last.

@math items="x′ = x·(3 − x − 2y),   y′ = y·(2 − x − y)@x prime;;at (1, 1):  Δ = −1@minus one"
For x prime equals x times three minus x minus two y, and y prime equals y times two minus x minus y, the mixed point is one, one. Its Jacobian has determinant minus one.

@words text="a saddle: one species ends alone"
That's a saddle. Nudge it, and one species ends alone.
