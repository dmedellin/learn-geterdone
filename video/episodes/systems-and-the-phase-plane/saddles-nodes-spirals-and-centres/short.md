title: Complex eigenvalues don't always mean a centre
---

@art spiral
Complex eigenvalues, so the orbits go round in closed loops? Not so fast.

@math items="rows 1 2 and −2 1@rows one, two;;λ = 1 ± 2i@one plus or minus two i"
For rows one, two and minus two, one, the roots are one plus or minus two i. They're complex, so orbits turn.

@words text="turning: imaginary part · in or out: real part"
But the real part is one, so they move steadily out. A centre needs the trace to be zero.
