title: An epidemic ends before everyone catches it

@art s-levels-off
An epidemic doesn't end because everyone caught it. Here's the proof.

@math items="W = S + I − (γ/β)·ln S@the quantity S plus I;;W′ = 0@never changes"
In the SIR model, the quantity S plus I, minus gamma over beta times log S, never changes along a solution.

@words text="S → 0 would send W to ∞"
If the susceptibles ran out, that log term would grow without limit. So S stays above zero. The infected run out first.
