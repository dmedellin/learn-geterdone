title: The sum of two dice can be independent of the first die

@art dice-columns
The sum of two dice depends on the first die. Yet these two events are independent.

@math items="P(first even) = 1/2,  P(sum 7) = 1/6@one half;;both: (2,5), (4,3), (6,1) = 3/36 = 1/12@three outcomes"
The first die is even has probability one half. The sum is seven has probability one sixth. Both together is three outcomes, three over thirty-six, one twelfth.

@words text="1/12 = 1/2 × 1/6"
One twelfth is exactly one half times one sixth. Independence is that equation, not a story.
