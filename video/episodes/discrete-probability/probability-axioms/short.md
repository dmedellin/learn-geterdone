@words text="Three rules"
Every rule of probability you've ever used comes from just three axioms.

@math items="P(E) ≥ 0@never negative;;P(S) = 1@the whole space;;P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B)  if A ∩ B = ∅@disjoint"
Probabilities are never negative. The whole space has probability one. And for disjoint events, probabilities add.

@art three-pieces
From those, you can prove the complement rule, inclusion and exclusion, and the union bound. None of them is an extra assumption. They're all theorems.
