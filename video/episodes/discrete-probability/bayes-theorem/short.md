A ninety-nine percent sensitive test says you're positive. Your chance of disease? About two percent.

@math items="100 have it → 99 positive@ninety-nine;;99 900 don't → 4 995 positive@four thousand nine hundred ninety-five;;99 / 5 094 ≈ 2%@about two percent"
Picture a hundred thousand people, with one in a thousand ill. A hundred have it, and ninety-nine test positive. Of the rest, a five percent false positive rate gives four thousand nine hundred ninety-five more. So only about two percent of positives are real.
