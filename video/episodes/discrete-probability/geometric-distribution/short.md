title: Waiting for a six: average six, most likely one
---

@art falling-bars
On average, a six takes six rolls. But the most likely wait is one roll.

@math items="P(X = 1) = 1/6 ≈ 0.167@the first roll;;P(X = 6) ≈ 0.067@exactly six"
The chance the first roll is a six is one sixth, about zero point one six seven. Needing exactly six rolls is only about zero point zero six seven.

@art tail-bars
The average balances a peak at one against a long tail. One time in nine, you wait more than twelve rolls.
