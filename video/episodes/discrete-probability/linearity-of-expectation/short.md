@art hats-pegs
Shuffle everyone's hats. On average, how many get their own back? Exactly one.

@math items="P(person i gets their own hat) = 1/n@one over n;;E[X] = n · (1/n) = 1@n times"
Each person gets their own hat with probability one over n. Expectation adds, so the expected number is n times one over n, which is one.

@words text="Same for 5 or 5 million"
That's true for five people or five million, and it doesn't need independence at all.
