title: The expected roll of a die is impossible
---

@art die-bars
The expected roll of a fair die is three point five. No die can show that.

@math items="E[X] = (1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 6) / 6 = 3.5@one plus two"
Add one plus two plus three plus four plus five plus six, and divide by six. You get three point five.

@words text="A long-run average, not a roll"
That's not a mistake. Expectation is the long-run average of many rolls. It summarises the distribution, not any single outcome.
