In a room of just twenty-three people, a shared birthday is more likely than not.

@words text="P ≈ 0.507" sub="23 people, 365 birthdays"
With twenty-three people and three hundred sixty-five equally likely birthdays, the chance of a shared birthday is about zero point five zero seven.

@art pairs-web zoom=in
Why? The question is about pairs. Twenty-three people make two hundred fifty-three pairs, and each pair might match.
