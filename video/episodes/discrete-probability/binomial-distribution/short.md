title: Five heads in ten flips is less likely than you think

@art coin-sequence
Flip ten coins. Five heads is the most likely result. But how likely?

@math items="C(10, 5) / 2¹⁰ = 252 / 1024 ≈ 0.246@ten choose five"
Count the ways: ten choose five over two to the ten. That's two hundred fifty-two over ten twenty-four, about zero point two four six. Slightly under a quarter.

@words text="The mean isn't the likely value"
The most likely value isn't actually likely. At a hundred flips, exactly fifty heads is only about eight percent.
