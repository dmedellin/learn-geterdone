title: Why two dice don't give eleven equal sums

@art sums-bars
Two dice give eleven sums. So is each sum one chance in eleven? No.

@art two-dice-grid
The outcomes that really are equally likely are the thirty-six ordered pairs. A sum of seven arises six ways. A sum of two arises only one way.

@words text="Keep outcomes fine" sub="pairs, not sums"
So keep your outcomes fine enough that symmetry makes them equally likely. Pairs, not sums.
