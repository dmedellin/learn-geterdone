title: Monty Hall: why switching wins two thirds
---

@art three-doors
You pick a door. The host opens an empty one. Should you switch? Yes.

@math items="prize at 1: switching loses@door one;;prize at 2: switching wins@door two;;prize at 3: switching wins@door three"
If the prize is behind door one, switching loses. Behind door two or door three, switching wins. Each is one third, so switching wins two thirds of the time.

@art host-opens
The key is that the host knows, and must open a losing door. That's information.
