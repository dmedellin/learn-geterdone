title: Expectations always add. Variances don't.
---

@words text="Var(X + X) ≠ 2 Var(X)"
Double a die, and its variance doesn't double. It goes up four times.

@math items="two separate dice: 35/12 + 35/12 = 35/6@two separate;;one die twice: 4 · 35/12 = 35/3@the same die"
For two separate independent dice, the variances add: thirty-five twelfths plus thirty-five twelfths. Count the same die twice, and you get four times thirty-five twelfths. That's twice as much.

@art two-dice-sums
Independent dice can cancel each other out. One die counted twice never can.
