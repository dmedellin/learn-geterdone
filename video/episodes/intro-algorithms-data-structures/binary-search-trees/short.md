@art chain-vs-bushy
Ten keys, one binary search tree. Height three, or height nine. You choose.

@words text="Insertion order decides the height"
The keys don't decide the shape. The order you insert them does. Put them in increasing order, and the tree becomes a chain.

@math items="mixed order: height 3, 19 comparisons@mixed order;;increasing: height 9, 45 comparisons@increasing"
In a mixed order, height three and nineteen comparisons. In increasing order, height nine and forty five. Every operation costs the height.
