@art grave-unknown
Deuteronomy reports Moses's death, and then adds that nobody knows his grave.

@quote text="no man knoweth of his sepulcher to this day" cite="Hobbes, Leviathan, Chapter 33"
No man knoweth of his sepulchre to this day. Hobbes says to this day means the day those words were written.

@words text="A book can't predate the latest thing it mentions"
So those words were written after the burial, by a later hand. A book can't predate the latest thing it mentions.
