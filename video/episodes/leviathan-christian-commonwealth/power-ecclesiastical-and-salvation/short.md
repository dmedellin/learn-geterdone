Can you obey your king and still be saved? Hobbes says always.

@quote text="All that is NECESSARY to Salvation, is contained in two Vertues, Faith in Christ, and Obedience to Laws." cite="Hobbes, Leviathan, Chapter 43"
All that is necessary to salvation is contained in two virtues, faith in Christ, and obedience to laws.

@words text="Belief can't be commanded"
No sovereign can command your belief, because faith is a gift of God. And an outward act done at his command, he says, is his act, not yours.
