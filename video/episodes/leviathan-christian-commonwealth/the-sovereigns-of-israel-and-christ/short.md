@art mountain-and-people
Why did Israel obey Moses? Hobbes says it wasn't because God told them to.

@words text="God spake not to them immediately"
God spoke only to Moses. And Moses's own word about himself couldn't bind them, any more than any man's.

@words text="Speak thou with us, and we will hear"
So it was their own promise at Sinai: speak thou with us, and we will hear. His authority, like every prince's, rested on the people's consent.
