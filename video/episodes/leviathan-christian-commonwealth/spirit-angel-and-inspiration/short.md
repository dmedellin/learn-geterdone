@art body-fills-room
Hobbes thought one famous phrase from the philosophy schools was a flat contradiction.

@math items="substance = body = extended + real@substance;;incorporeal = unextended@incorporeal;;extended + unextended: cancel@cancel"
A substance is a body, something that fills some room. Incorporeal means not filling room. Put the two names together, and they cancel each other out.

@quote text="as if a man should say, an Incorporeall Body." cite="Hobbes, Leviathan, Chapter 34"
Saying incorporeal substance, he says, is as if a man should say an incorporeal body.
