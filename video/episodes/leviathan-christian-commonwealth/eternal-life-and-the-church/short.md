title: Hobbes: church and state are one thing
---

@art one-sovereign-two-names
Hobbes says a church that can act and a Christian state are one thing.

@words text="Christians, united in one sovereign"
A church that can command or judge must be one person. So it's a company of Christians united in one sovereign, who calls it together.

@words text="Temporal and spiritual: two words to make men see double"
So spiritual and temporal government, he says, are two words brought in to make men see double, and mistake their lawful sovereign.
