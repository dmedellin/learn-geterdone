Pharaoh's magicians did real miracles. Hobbes says that proves nothing.

@quote text="there be two marks, by which together, not asunder, a true Prophet is to be known." cite="Hobbes, Leviathan, Chapter 32"
There be two marks, together, not asunder: a miracle, and teaching only the religion already established.

@art empty-pulpit
A wonder that teaches a strange god is only a trial of your constancy. And since miracles have ceased, he says, no private man's claim to revelation can meet the test now.
