@art many-prophets
A prophet's prediction comes true. He's probably still a false prophet.

@words text="400 false, only one Micaiah"
Hobbes counts Ahab's four hundred prophets, all false but one. Take that as a base rate: one in four hundred and one.

@math items="true: always comes to pass@always;;false: 1 in 10 by chance@one time in ten;;after one hit: 1 in 41@forty one"
A true prophet's word always comes to pass, a false one's one time in ten. After one hit, the chance he's true is only one in forty one.
