@art witness-scale
An eyewitness to a miracle tells you almost nothing about whether it happened.

@words text="A trick is made to be witnessed"
A trick produces witnesses as readily as a miracle does. Hobbes says confederates make nothing impossible to be believed.

@math items="miracle: 1/100 before@starts at;;after one witness: 9/500@after"
In the lesson's sum, the miracle's share starts at one in a hundred. After one witness, it's nine in five hundred, barely higher.
