title: When is a crowd wiser than its members?
---

@art rising-curve
Bigger juries are more often right. But only if each juror beats a coin flip.

@math items="3 jurors at 3/5:  81/125@three fifths;;3 jurors at 2/5:  44/125@two fifths"
Three jurors, each right three fifths of the time, form a majority right eighty one times in a hundred and twenty five. At two fifths each, just forty four.

@words text="Size magnifies competence"
Above one half, crowds get wiser. Below it, they get worse. That's the Condorcet jury theorem.
