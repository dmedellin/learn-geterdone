@art veil
Equal at five, or unequal at two, eight and twenty? You don't know your place.

@math items="maximin:  worst 5 vs 2 → equal@Look only at the worst;;average:  5 vs 10 → unequal@average the places"
Look only at the worst place, and the equal society wins, five against two. Average the places, and the unequal one wins, ten against five.

@words text="Same table, two rules, two societies"
So the real dispute is about the rule, and that's exactly what Rawls and Harsanyi argued over.
