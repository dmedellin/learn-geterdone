@art same-ballots-four-rules
The same nine ballots can elect three different candidates. It depends on the counting rule.

@list items="Plurality: A@Plurality|Runoff: B@runoff|Borda: C@Borda"
Four rank A C B, three B C A, two C B A. Plurality elects A. A runoff elects B. And Borda elects C.

@words text="The winner belongs to the rule"
Nothing in the ballots changed. Each rule just reads a different part of them.
