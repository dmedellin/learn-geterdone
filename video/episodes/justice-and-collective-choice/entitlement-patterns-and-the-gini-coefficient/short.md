@art basketball-payments
Four people with ten each. Three pay one to watch Wilt Chamberlain play.

@math items="10, 10, 10, 10:  Gini 0@ten each;;9, 9, 9, 13:  Gini 3/40@nine, nine, nine"
Ten each has a Gini of zero. Afterwards it's nine, nine, nine and thirteen, a Gini of three fortieths. And nobody was wronged.

@words text="Liberty upsets patterns"
That's Nozick's point. Every payment was free, and the pattern broke anyway. To keep the pattern, you'd have to stop people paying, or take it back.
