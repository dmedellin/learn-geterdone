@art level-vs-raised
Rawls's principle of justice can prefer an unequal society to an equal one.

@math items="5, 5, 5@five, five, five;;6, 9, 20@six, nine, twenty"
Compare five, five, five with six, nine, twenty. Look at the worst off first. Six beats five, so the unequal one wins.

@words text="Inequality is allowed if it raises the floor"
The top numbers don't count for or against. What matters is whether the worst off are better off. That's the difference principle.
