@art struck-ballot
Remove a candidate who was never going to win, and the winner changes.

@math items="A 4,  B 3,  C 2:  A wins@A wins;;strike C:  A 4,  B 5:  B wins@Strike C"
Nine voters, plurality. A wins with four first places. Strike C from every ballot, and its voters' first places pass to B. B wins.

@words text="Nobody changed their mind about A and B"
Same four prefer A, same five prefer B. That's a failure of independence, and Arrow showed every ranking rule gives something up.
