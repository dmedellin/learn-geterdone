@art bury-rival
Sometimes the way to help your favourite is to lie about your second choice.

@math items="sincere:  B 9,  C 8@Honestly;;two bury B:  B 7,  C 8@bury B"
Honestly, B beats C, nine to eight, under Borda. Now two C voters bury B at the bottom of their ballots. B falls to seven, and C wins.

@words text="Every reasonable rule can be gamed somewhere"
That's not a flaw in Borda alone. With three or more candidates, the theorem says every reasonable rule can be gamed.
