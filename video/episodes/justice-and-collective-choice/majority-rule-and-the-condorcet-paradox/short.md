@art three-rankings
Three voters, each perfectly consistent. The majority goes round in a circle.

@math items="A beats B, 2–1@A beats B;;B beats C, 2–1@B beats C;;C beats A, 2–1@C beats A"
Rankings A B C, B C A, and C A B. Count each contest. A beats B. B beats C. And C beats A. Each by two to one.

@art cycle-arrows
That's the Condorcet paradox. Every voter is in order, and the majority still has no best candidate.
