@words text="She has seen the letter. · She has a letter."
She has seen the letter. She has a letter. Same word, two jobs.

@art two-jobs
To tell them apart, you don't need the meaning. Just look at the next word.

@math items="has + seen  →  helping verb@a participle;;has + a letter  →  main verb@a noun phrase"
If a participle follows, like seen, have is a helping verb. If a noun phrase follows, like a letter, have is the main verb, and it means to hold.
