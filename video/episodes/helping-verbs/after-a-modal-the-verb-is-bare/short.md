title: Why she can goes is wrong

@words text="She can goes ✗"
She can goes. It sounds close, but it's wrong, because it shows the tense twice.

@art helper-takes
Can, could, will, must and the other modals take the tense. The verb after them takes nothing.

@math items="she can go ✓@she can go;;he could go ✓@he could go"
So it's she can go, and he could go. In a whole novel, with two thousand seven hundred and thirty-eight modals, no true exception.
