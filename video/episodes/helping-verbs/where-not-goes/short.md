@words text="I know not?"
Why doesn't English say I know not? It used to.

@art do-arrives
Today, not follows a helping verb. If the sentence has none, do arrives to be one.

@math items="She liked it.@She liked it;;She did not like it.@did not like it"
She liked it has no helping verb. So it becomes she did not like it. Did takes the tense, and like goes back to its bare form.
