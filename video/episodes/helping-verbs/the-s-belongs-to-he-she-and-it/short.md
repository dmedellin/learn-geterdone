title: On a verb, -s means exactly one
---

@art noun-verb-mirror
On a noun, s means more than one. On a verb, it means the opposite.

@math items="books  →  more than one@book, books;;she walks  →  exactly one@she walks"
Book, books: more than one. But she walks: one person walking.

@words text="they walks ✗   they walk ✓"
So never write they walks. The s on a verb belongs to he, she and it, and to any one thing named in their place.
