@words text="she is walking"
Courses start the verb be with she is walking. On a real page, that's rare.

@math items="137 forms of be@one hundred and thirty-seven;;6 followed by an -ing word@just six"
In one chapter of a novel, there are one hundred and thirty-seven forms of be. Just six are followed by an ing word.

@art five-jobs
Most of the time, be is the verb itself: her brother is the cause, I am not afraid.
