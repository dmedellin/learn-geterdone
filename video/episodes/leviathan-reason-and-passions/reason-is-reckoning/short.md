@art arbitrator
Reason is never wrong, Hobbes says. But you might be.

@words text="Reason is infallible, reckoners are not"
Arithmetic is infallible, and so is reason. But a man adding up a bill can still cast it up wrong, and so can a crowd of men who all approve it.

@art trump-suit
So when two parties dispute, they need a judge they both accept. The man who insists his own reason is right reason is calling trump the suit he holds most of.
