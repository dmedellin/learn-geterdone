@art endeavour-arrows
Hobbes thought anger contains fear. Here's why.

@math items="fear = aversion + opinion of hurt@fear is;;courage = fear + hope of avoiding@courage is;;anger = courage + sudden@anger is"
Fear is aversion, with an opinion of hurt. Courage is fear, with the hope of avoiding that hurt by resisting. And anger is courage that comes suddenly.

@words text="Anger is fear: true"
So every angry man is, by definition, afraid and brave at once. The names aren't separate feelings. Each one is built on the last.
