title: Your will is just your last appetite

@art seesaw
According to Hobbes, your will is just whatever you wanted last.

@words text="The last appetite, or aversion"
You go back and forth: lend the money, don't, lend it, don't. Hobbes says the will isn't a judge standing over that. It's the last appetite or aversion before you act.

@art reversed-order
Take the same five feelings in another order, and the will flips. Nothing about the friend changed. Only the order did.
