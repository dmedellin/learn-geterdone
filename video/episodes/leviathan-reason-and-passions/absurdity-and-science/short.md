@art round-quadrangle
Some sentences aren't false. They don't say anything at all.

@words text="Faith is poured"
Take faith is poured. Faith is an accident, a quality. Only a body can be poured. Add the two names and they clash, so the sentence fails before you even check the facts.

@words text="Science starts from definitions"
That's why Hobbes says reasoning must begin from definitions, as geometry does.
