@art passion-dial
For Hobbes, madness isn't a strange illness. It's ordinary passion, turned up.

@list items="No desire: dead@dead|Weak passions: dullness@dullness|Passions for everything: giddiness@giddiness|Vehement passions: madness@madness"
To have no desire is to be dead. Weak passions are dullness. Passions for everything are giddiness. And passions more vehement than usual are what men call madness.

@words text="The same passions that make wit"
The same desires that sharpen a mind can, in excess, unhinge it.
