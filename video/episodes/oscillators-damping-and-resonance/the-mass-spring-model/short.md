Does a heavier mass on a spring swing faster? It's the other way round.

@math items="ω₀² = k/m@k over m;;k = 4, m = 1:  T = π@a period of pi;;k = 4, m = 2:  T = 2π/√2 ≈ 4.44288@double the mass"
Omega nought squared is k over m. With k equals four and m equals one, you get a period of pi. Double the mass, and the period is two pi over root two, about four point four four.

@words text="Heavier is slower"
The same pull has more mass to move. Heavier is slower.
