title: A damped spring's biggest response is not at its natural frequency
---

@art amplitude-curve-peak
Push a damped spring at its natural frequency. That's not where it responds most.

@math items="ω₀² = 5:  A² = 9/20@at omega nought;;ω_r² = 3:  A² = 9/16@omega squared equals three"
With m one, c two, k five, and a push of three, the amplitude squared at omega nought squared equals five is nine twentieths. But at omega squared equals three, it's nine sixteenths. Bigger.

@words text="ω_r² = k/m − c²/(2m²)"
The damper moves the peak down, below the natural frequency.
