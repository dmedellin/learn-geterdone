@art slower-swing
Add a damper, and the spring doesn't just fade. It swings slower.

@math items="ω₀ = √5,  T = 2π/√5 ≈ 2.80993@root five;;ω_d² = 5 − 1 = 4,  T_d = π ≈ 3.14159@five minus one"
With m one and k five, the natural frequency is root five, period about two point eight one. Add a damper of two, and omega d squared is five minus one, four. The pseudo-period is pi, about three point one four.

@words text="ω_d < ω₀"
Every damper lowers the frequency.
