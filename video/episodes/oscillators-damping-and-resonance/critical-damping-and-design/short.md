Critically damped means no overshoot, right? Not quite.

@math items="start (1, −5):  x = (1 − 3t)·e^(−2t)@one minus three t;;zero at t = 1/3@a third"
Give the mass a hard push inward, from one at speed five. With m, c and k at one, four, four, the motion is one minus three t, times e to the minus two t. It crosses zero at t equals a third.

@words text="At most once"
It's a straight line times a decay, and a line meets the axis at most once.
