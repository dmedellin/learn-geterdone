@art against-push-waves
Push a spring too fast, and it moves against you.

@math items="A = F₀/(m·(ω₀² − ω²))@F nought over;;ω = 3:  A = 3/(4 − 9) = −3/5@omega equals three"
The amplitude is F nought over m times omega nought squared minus omega squared. With m one, k four and a push of three at omega equals three, that's three over four minus nine. Minus three fifths.

@words text="A < 0: against the push"
Negative. The mass is too sluggish to keep up, so it's answered the wrong way round.
