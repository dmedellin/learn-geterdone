@art turning-point
The mass stops dead at the end of its swing. Where did its energy go?

@math items="E = ½m·v² + ½k·x²@a half m v squared;;x = 5, v = 0:  ½·4·25 = 50@a half of four"
Energy is a half m v squared plus a half k x squared. Start at three, eight, with m one and k four, and it's fifty. At the turning point, x is five and v is zero, so it's a half of four times twenty five. Still fifty.

@words text="all in the spring"
It's all in the spring.
