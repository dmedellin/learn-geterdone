title: A stronger damper can make it slower
---

@art creeping-return
Double the damper, and the spring takes longer to settle.

@math items="c = 5:  slow root −1@a damper of five;;c = 10:  slow root −5 + √21@A damper of ten"
With m one and k four, a damper of five gives a slow root of minus one. A damper of ten gives minus five plus root twenty one, which is between minus one and zero. Slower.

@words text="Critical is quickest" sub="c_crit = 2√(mk) = 4"
The damper holds the mass so firmly it creeps. Critical damping, four here, is the quickest of the non-oscillating cases.
