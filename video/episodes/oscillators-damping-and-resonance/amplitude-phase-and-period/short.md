title: It starts at 3, so why does it swing to 5?
---

@art overshoot-start
A mass starts at three. It swings out to five. Here's why.

@math items="ω₀ = 2,  x₀ = 3,  v₀ = 8@velocity eight;;A² = 3² + (8/2)² = 9 + 16 = 25@A squared"
On a spring with omega nought two, it starts at three with velocity eight. A squared is three squared plus eight over two, squared. Nine plus sixteen, twenty five.

@words text="A = 5" sub="not 3, and not √73"
So the amplitude is five. It was already moving outward when released, so it overshoots its start.
