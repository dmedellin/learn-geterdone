title: Is show an irregular verb?
---

@words text="show · showed · ?"
Showed looks perfectly regular. So is show a regular verb? Here's a quick test you can use on any verb.

@math items="guess:  showed, showed@your guess;;written:  showed, shown@people write"
Build your guess with e-d: showed, showed. Now look at what people write: showed, shown.

@art two-spellings
The form after have breaks the rule. One broken form is enough, so show is irregular, and you learn shown as its own word.
