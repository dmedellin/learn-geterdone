@words text="1 word in 6"
One word in six on a Pride and Prejudice page is an irregular verb form.

@art three-big
But three verbs, be, have and do, are ninety-one of those one hundred and forty-nine forms.

@math items="with be, have, do: 15.9%@With them;;without them: 6.2%@Without them"
With them, the share is fifteen point nine percent. Without them, it falls to six point two. So always ask what's in the count.
