title: Why know and go are in different classes
---

@words text="know · knew · known  /  go · went · gone"
Know and go look alike. A rule puts them in different classes.

@art near-miss
Both have three different forms. So the next question is the last letter. Known ends in n. Gone ends in e.

@math items="known → class of 37@class of thirty-seven;;gone → class of 9@class of nine"
That one letter sends know to the class of thirty-seven, and go to the class of nine, with do and undergo.

@end text="Full lesson on Learn"
All six classes are on Learn.
