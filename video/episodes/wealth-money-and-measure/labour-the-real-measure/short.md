@art two-rulers
A price can double in money, and not get any dearer at all.

@math items="before:  10s / 1s a day = 10 days@ten shillings;;after:  20s / 2s a day = 10 days@twenty"
A good costs ten shillings when a day's labour costs one. Later it's twenty, but a day costs two. Divide, and it's ten days of labour both times.

@words text="Labour is the real price; money the nominal"
That's Adam Smith's point. When the change is in the money, it cancels out.
