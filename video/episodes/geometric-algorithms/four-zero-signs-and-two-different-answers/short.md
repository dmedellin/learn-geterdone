@art overlap-vs-gap
These two segment pairs give identical signs. One meets, one doesn't.

@math items="(0, 0)–(6, 0)  vs  (4, 0)–(10, 0):  meet@six, zero;;(0, 0)–(4, 0)  vs  (6, 0)–(10, 0):  miss@four, zero"
Zero, zero to six, zero overlaps four, zero to ten, zero. Zero, zero to four, zero leaves a gap before six, zero. All four orientation signs are zero in both.

@words text="Finish it along the line"
So no rule on the signs can work. You have to compare positions along the line.
