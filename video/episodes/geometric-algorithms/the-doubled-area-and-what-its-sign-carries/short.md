title: Why the shoelace formula gave you a negative area
---

@art square-both-ways
Your polygon's area came out negative. That's not a bug.

@math items="(0, 0), (5, 0), (5, 5), (0, 5):  2A = 50@fifty;;same corners, reversed:  2A = −50@minus fifty"
Type a five by five square counter clockwise, and the shoelace sum is fifty, twice the area of twenty five. Type the same corners in reverse, and it's minus fifty.

@words text="The sign is the winding"
The magnitude is the area. The sign tells you which way round you listed the vertices.
