title: Why traders care about the close, not the wick
---

@art candle-anatomy
Price stabbed through your level. So is the level broken? Not yet.

@words text="Wick = where price went" sub="Close = where it was accepted"
A wick shows where price went during the candle. The close shows where price was accepted when the span ended.

@art bullish-staircase
So in a bullish trend, the higher low buyers defend is broken by a close below it. A wick through it that closes back above is a level tested, not a level broken.
