title: The breakout that was really a sweep

@art wick-sweep
Price broke the high, then fell straight back. Was that ever a breakout?

@art resting-orders
Just beyond an obvious level sit resting orders: stops from traders inside the range, and entries waiting for the break.

@words text="Through the level, then a close back inside"
A sweep trades through far enough to fill them, finds nothing behind, and closes back inside. The failed break is the evidence.
