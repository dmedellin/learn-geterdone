@art normalized-lines
Your stock is up. The market is up. Is your stock actually leading?

@words text="Start both at 100"
Normalize them. Start both series at one hundred, so their paths show percentage performance, not raw prices.

@art ratio-line
Then watch the ratio, asset over benchmark. If it rises and finishes above where it started, the asset is leading.
