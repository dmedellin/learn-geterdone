@art time-decay
Right about direction, and the option still disappoints. How?

@list items="Elapsed time@elapsed time|Lower implied volatility@implied volatility|Bid and ask friction@friction|Too small a move@too small"
Elapsed time eats premium. A drop in implied volatility lowers the value. Bid and ask friction costs you both ways. Or the move was too small.

@words text="Expiration must outlast the thesis"
So the option is a wrapper around your thesis, and its expiration must outlast it.
