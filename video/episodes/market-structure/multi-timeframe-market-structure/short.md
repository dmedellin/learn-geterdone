title: A falling hourly chart doesn't mean short
---

@art pullback-inside-trend
The hourly chart is falling. That doesn't mean you should be short.

@art timeframe-stack
The higher timeframe tells you the environment. The lower timeframe tells you whether the pullback is still unfolding.

@words text="Lower TF bearish ≠ short"
A lower timeframe downtrend can be just a pullback inside a larger uptrend. So ask that first, before you call it bearish.
