@art three-entries
Right direction, wrong entry. It happens all the time.

@art aggressive-touch
Enter early at the reaction zone, and you get a better price, but more false starts.

@art continuation-break
Wait for price to resume through a continuation level, and you get stronger proof, but a later price.

@words text="Choose the model before the setup"
Neither is best. So pick your model before the setup, and judge it by its own rule.
