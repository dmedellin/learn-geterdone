@art crowd-dilution
One liar can rewrite a log. In a survey, one liar is diluted by everyone else.

@math items="shift = (s − m) ÷ n@score minus the mean;;6 respondents: 0.108@nought point one oh eight;;11 respondents: 0.059@nought point oh five nine"
Adding one respondent moves a mean by their score minus the mean, over n. In the lesson's invented team, that's nought point one oh eight with six people, and nought point oh five nine with eleven.

@words text="23,000+ respondents"
Accelerate pooled over twenty three thousand. The authors estimate it would take several hundred people lying together to make a noticeable change.
