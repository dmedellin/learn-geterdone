title: One unpaid bill, and suddenly everyone needs cash

@art broken-chain
One unpaid bill can create a demand for cash out of nothing.

@math items="A → B → C → A: cash needed £0@cancel;;A fails: cash needed £100@cannot pay"
Three firms owe each other a hundred pounds each in a circle. The bills cancel, and nobody needs cash. Now A cannot pay. B still owes C, and the chain needs a hundred pounds.

@words text="Same goods, same prices"
Nothing real changed. That's Marx's point: in a crash, firms borrow to pay, not to buy.
