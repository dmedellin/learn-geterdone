@art same-stack
A thousand pounds of wages and a thousand of business payments need exactly the same notes.

@math items="wages: £1000 / 10 = £100@wages;;between capitalists: £1000 / 10 = £100@between capitalists"
In the lesson's illustration, wages spent in shops at ten turns need a hundred pounds of notes. Payments between capitalists, at ten turns, also need a hundred.

@words text="Velocity matters. The label doesn't."
Marx's point against Tooke: calling money capital changes nothing about how much is needed. Only prices and velocity do.
