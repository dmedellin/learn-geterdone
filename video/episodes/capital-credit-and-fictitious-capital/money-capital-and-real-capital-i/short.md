In Volume Two's scheme, when every accumulating capitalist hoards, they sell five hundred of product and nobody buys. The money that would have bought it sits in the bank.

@words text="Plenty of loan capital, no real accumulation"
So cheap, plentiful money can mean the opposite of growth. Industry has stopped, and that's why the money is idle.
