title: The gold drain that came before the crash
---

@art feather-scale
In eighteen forty seven, the gold stopped leaving in April. The crash came in October.

@words text="A feather on the scales"
The gold England sent abroad was tiny beside its production. Marx calls it a vanishing magnitude. But it came out of the Bank's reserve, the pivot of all credit, like a feather on swaying scales.

@words text="A warning, not the cause"
So the drain is a symptom of trouble in foreign trade. It warns of the crisis. It doesn't cause it.
