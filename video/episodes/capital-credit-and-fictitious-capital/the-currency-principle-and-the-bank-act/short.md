title: The 1844 law that made panics worse

@art gold-leaves
In eighteen forty four, England passed a law that shrank the money supply just before every panic.

@math items="cap: 14 + 14 = 28 million@twenty eight;;6 million of gold leaves: cap 22@six millions"
The Bank could issue fourteen millions of notes plus its gold: twenty eight. When six millions of gold left, the cap fell to twenty two, just as panic made people hoard notes.

@words text="Suspended 1847 and 1857"
Twice the government had to suspend the law, and both times that broke the crisis.
