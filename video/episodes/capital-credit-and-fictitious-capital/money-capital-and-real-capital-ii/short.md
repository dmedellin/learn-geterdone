@art released-to-bank
A business that halves its output can add six hundred pounds to the banks.

@math items="before: 12 weeks · £100 = £1,200@twelve weeks;;after: 12 weeks · £50 = £600@fifty;;released: £600@released"
In Volume Two's figures, each week's outlay is tied up for twelve weeks: twelve hundred pounds. Halve the scale to fifty a week, and six hundred is released.

@words text="Loan capital nobody saved"
It goes to the bank as a deposit. Loan capital grows while production shrinks, and nobody saved a penny.
