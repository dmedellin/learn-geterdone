title: When interest eats the whole profit

@art profit-bar
In November eighteen forty seven, interest could swallow a manufacturer's entire profit.

@math items="profit on £100 at 10%: £10@ten pounds;;at 3%: £7 left@three percent;;at 10%: £0 left@ten percent"
Take ten pounds of profit on a borrowed hundred, an illustrative rate. At the Bank's three percent of January, seven pounds is left. At its ten percent of November, nothing is.

@words text="Same profit, different split"
The rate of profit never moved. Marx's point: a high rate of interest isn't a sign of high profits. It's a bigger share of the same one.
