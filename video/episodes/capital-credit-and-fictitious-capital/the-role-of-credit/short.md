title: In a stock company, who gets the profit?
---

@art owner-and-manager
In a stock company, the person running the business doesn't get the profit.

@math items="£220 profit − £20 salary = £200 dividend@two hundred;;profit of enterprise: £0@nothing"
In the lesson's illustration, a company makes two hundred and twenty pounds. The manager is paid a salary of twenty. The shareholders get two hundred. Nothing is left over.

@words text="Owners paid like lenders, managers paid like workers"
That's Marx's point. The old capitalist splits in two: owners paid as if with interest, managers paid a wage.
