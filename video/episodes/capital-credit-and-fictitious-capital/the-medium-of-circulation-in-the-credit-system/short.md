@art reflux
A bank can print extra notes. Marx says it can't keep them out.

@math items="needed: £600 / 4 = £150@a hundred and fifty;;issued £200: £50 flows back@fifty"
In the lesson's illustration, six hundred pounds of goods, with each note serving four times, need a hundred and fifty. Issue two hundred, and fifty comes straight back as deposits.

@words text="In a panic: £600 / 2 = £300"
But in a panic, notes are hoarded and serve only twice. The same goods suddenly need three hundred.
