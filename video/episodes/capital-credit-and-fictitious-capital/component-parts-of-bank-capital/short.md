@art seesaw-rate
The same hundred pounds a year can be worth one thousand pounds or four thousand.

@math items="at 10%: £100 / (1/10) = £1000@ten percent;;at 5%: £2000@five percent;;at 2½%: £4000@two and a half"
Divide the income by the rate of interest. At ten percent, a thousand. At five percent, two thousand. At two and a half percent, four thousand.

@words text="Fictitious capital"
Nothing that pays the income changed. Only the rate did. That's why Marx calls the price fictitious capital.
