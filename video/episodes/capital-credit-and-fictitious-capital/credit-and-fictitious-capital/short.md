@art bill-circle
Three hundred pounds of goods can change hands without a single coin.

@math items="A owes B £100 · B owes C £100 · C owes A £100@A owes;;every balance: 0 · cash: £0@every balance"
A owes B a hundred, B owes C a hundred, and C owes A a hundred, all on bills due the same day. Set them off, and every balance is zero.

@words text="Commercial money"
That's why Marx calls bills of exchange commercial money. When they cancel, they do the work of money with no money at all.
