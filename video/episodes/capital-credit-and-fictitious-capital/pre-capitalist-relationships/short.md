@art growing-debt
A peasant who pays the usurer everything he can spare still loses his land.

@math items="year 1: 50 + 15 − 10 = 55@fifty five;;year 6: about 113.78, past the £100 holding@year six"
In the lesson's illustration, he owes fifty pounds at thirty percent and can spare ten a year. The interest is fifteen, so the debt grows to fifty five. By year six it passes the hundred pound value of his holding.

@words text="Interest bigger than the surplus"
Once the interest is more than the whole surplus, Marx says, the debt can only grow.
