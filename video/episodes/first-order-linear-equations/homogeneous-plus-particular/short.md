title: The constant-fitting mistake in linear equations

@art two-parts-sum
Fit the constant too early, and your solution starts in the wrong place.

@math items="y = (2/3)·t − 2/9 + C·e^(−3t)@two thirds t;;−2/9 + C = 1@at zero;;C = 11/9@eleven ninths"
For y prime plus three y equals two t, the solution is two thirds t minus two ninths, plus C e to the minus three t. At zero, minus two ninths plus C must equal one. So C is eleven ninths.

@words text="Add the parts first, then fit C"
Fit C before adding, and you'd start at seven ninths.
