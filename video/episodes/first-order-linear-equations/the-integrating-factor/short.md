title: The integrating factor is just the product rule backwards

@art product-rule
The integrating factor isn't magic. It's the product rule, read backwards.

@math items="(μ·y)′ = μ·y′ + μ′·y@mu times y;;μ′ = p·μ  ⟹  = μ·(y′ + p·y)@choose mu"
The derivative of mu times y is mu y prime plus mu prime y. Choose mu so that mu prime equals p times mu, and that's mu times y prime plus p y.

@words text="μ = e^(∫p dt)"
So mu is e to the integral of p. Not e to the p.
