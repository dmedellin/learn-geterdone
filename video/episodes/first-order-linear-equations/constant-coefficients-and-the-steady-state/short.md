title: The steady state isn't the number on the right
---

@art gap-never-zero
y prime plus two y equals six. Does y settle at six? No.

@math items="6 − 2y = 0@six minus two y;;y = 3@three"
The steady state is where the rate is zero. Six minus two y is zero at y equals three. Try six: two times six is twelve, not six.

@words text="steady state = b/a"
For y prime plus a y equals b, it's b over a. The right side and the steady state match only when a is one.
