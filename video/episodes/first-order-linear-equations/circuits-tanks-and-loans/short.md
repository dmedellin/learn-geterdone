Owe a hundred, pay six a year. Paid off in about seventeen years? Not even close.

@math items="B′ = B/20 − 6@B over twenty;;B = 120 − 20·e^(t/20)@a hundred twenty;;t = 20·ln 6 ≈ 35.8352@twenty ln six"
With five percent interest, B prime equals B over twenty minus six. The balance is a hundred twenty minus twenty e to the t over twenty, which hits zero at twenty ln six, about thirty five point eight years.

@words text="interest all the way down"
The balance keeps earning interest while you pay it.
