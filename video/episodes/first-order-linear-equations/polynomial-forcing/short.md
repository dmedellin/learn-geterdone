Forcing is t squared, so guess t squared? It's off by exactly two t.

@math items="(t²)′ + t² = 2t + t²@adds a two t;;y_p = t² − 2t + 2@minus two t plus two"
For y prime plus y equals t squared, the derivative adds a two t you don't want. So the guess needs every lower power. The answer is t squared minus two t plus two.

@words text="Every lower power, down to the constant"
Each derivative lands one power down, and something there has to cancel it.
