title: Cosine in, sine out: why the guess needs both

@art lagging-wave
Force an equation with a cosine, and a sine shows up in the answer.

@math items="y′ + 2y = cos(t)@cosine t;;y_p = (2/5)·cos(t) + (1/5)·sin(t)@two fifths"
For y prime plus two y equals cosine t, the particular solution is two fifths cosine plus a fifth sine.

@words text="each is the other's derivative"
The derivative of a cosine is a sine, so a guess with only a cosine leaves a sine nothing can cancel. The response lags the forcing.
