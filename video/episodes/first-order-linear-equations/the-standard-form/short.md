@art linear-pieces
y prime equals two y plus t squared y. Linear or not?

@math items="y′ + (−2 − t²)·y = 0@minus two minus t squared"
It's linear. The t squared is a coefficient of y, not a power of y. Collect it, and it's y prime plus minus two minus t squared, times y, equals zero.

@words text="Linear is about y, never t"
y and y prime appear alone, to the first power. What multiplies them can be anything in t.
