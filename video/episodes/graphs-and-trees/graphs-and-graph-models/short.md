@art same-graph-two-drawings
These two drawings look nothing alike. They're the same graph.

@words text="A graph = vertices + edges" sub="which pairs are joined"
A graph is just a set of vertices, and which pairs of them are joined by edges. That's all it records.

@words text="The drawing is not the graph"
Where you place the dots, and how you bend the lines, carries no information. So two pictures that look completely different can be exactly the same graph.
