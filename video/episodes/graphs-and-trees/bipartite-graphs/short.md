@art odd-cycle-clash
Try colouring a five cycle with two colours. You'll always get stuck.

@words text="Bipartite ⟺ no odd cycle"
A graph can be split into two sides, with every edge crossing, exactly when it has no odd cycle.

@art even-cycle-colours
Each edge switches sides. After an odd number of steps you're on the wrong side, so an odd cycle can never close up. Even cycles always can.
