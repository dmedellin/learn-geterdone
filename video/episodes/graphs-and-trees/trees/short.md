title: Five edges on six vertices isn't always a tree

@art path-star-cycle
Six vertices, five edges. Is it a tree? Not necessarily.

@words text="A tree has n − 1 edges"
Every tree on n vertices has n minus one edges. But the count alone proves nothing.

@art tree-forest
A triangle plus a separate path also has six vertices and five edges. It's disconnected, and it has a cycle. So pair the count with connected, or with no cycles, and only then do you know it's a tree.
