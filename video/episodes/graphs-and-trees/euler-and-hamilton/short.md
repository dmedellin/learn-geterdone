title: Why Königsberg's bridge walk was impossible

@art konigsberg-abstract
Seven bridges, four land masses. Can you cross every bridge exactly once?

@math items="degrees: 5 · 3 · 3 · 3@five, three, three and three"
Euler counted the bridges at each land mass: five, three, three and three. All four are odd.

@words text="A walk allows at most 2 odd vertices"
Each time you pass through, you use one bridge in and one out. Only the start and the end can be odd. Four odd vertices, so the walk is impossible.
