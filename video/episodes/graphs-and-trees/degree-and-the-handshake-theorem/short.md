title: Why five people can't each shake three hands

@words text="5 people, 3 handshakes each?"
Can five people each shake exactly three hands? No, and here's the one line proof.

@art edge-two-ends
Every handshake has two ends. So if you add up everyone's handshakes, you get twice the number of handshakes. That total is always even.

@math items="3 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 15@three plus three;;15 is odd  ✗@fifteen is odd"
But three plus three plus three plus three plus three is fifteen. Fifteen is odd, so the party can't exist.
