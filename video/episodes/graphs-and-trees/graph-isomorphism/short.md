@art triangles-vs-hexagon
Same vertices, same edges, same degrees. Still not the same graph.

@words text="Two triangles vs a 6-cycle" sub="6 vertices · 6 edges · all degree 2"
Two separate triangles, and a six cycle. Each has six vertices, six edges, and every degree is two.

@words text="Components: 2 vs 1"
But the triangles are in two pieces, and the cycle is one. That single difference proves they're not isomorphic. Matching invariants never prove they are.
