French economists called every manufacturer and merchant barren. Adam Smith had an answer.

@words text="Barren: replaces its costs, leaves no surplus"
They meant that a workshop only replaces what it consumes, and leaves no surplus. Only land yields a rent.

@quote text="We should not call a marriage barren or unproductive, though it produced only a son and a daughter, to replace the father and mother." cite="Adam Smith, The Wealth of Nations, Book IV, Chapter 9"
