@art two-payments
Under a bounty, you pay twice: once in taxes, once at the shop.

@quote text="Consumption is the sole end and purpose of all production" cite="Adam Smith, The Wealth of Nations, Book IV, Chapter 8"
Adam Smith's maxim is that consumption is the sole end and purpose of all production. The producer matters only as far as he serves the consumer.

@words text="The consumer sacrificed to the producer"
The mercantile system, he says, turned that round, and sacrificed the consumer to the producer.
