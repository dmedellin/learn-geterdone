title: The lottery where the winners got rich and the whole lost

@art lottery-tickets
Smith called hunting for gold mines the most disadvantageous lottery in the world.

@math items="prizes: 10 · 2,000 · 20 = 400,000@ten;;tickets: 1,000 · 500 = 500,000@thousand"
Say ten adventurers each strike a vein worth two thousand a year for twenty years. That's four hundred thousand. But a thousand set out, at five hundred each. That's five hundred thousand in tickets.

@words text="Rich winners · a losing lottery"
The winners are rich. The lottery as a whole is a hundred thousand short.
