@art more-land
In the American colonies, the workers kept leaving to become landlords.

@art labourer-to-landlord
A colonist had more land than he could farm, so he paid the most liberal wages to get labourers. But high wages and cheap land soon let those labourers buy land of their own.

@math items="100 / 25 = 4 doublings@four doublings;;3,000,000 · 16 = 48,000,000@forty eight"
