@art returns-a-year
A merchant can grow rich on a trade that does his country little good.

@math items="once a year: 1,000 · 1 = 1,000@once a year;;once in five years: 1,000 · 1/5 = 200@five years"
Smith's rule: a thousand pounds that comes home once a year keeps a thousand pounds' worth of labour at work at home. Back once in five years, it keeps only two hundred.

@words text="Profit to a merchant isn't gain to a nation"
The merchant's profit may be just as great. The country's is not.
