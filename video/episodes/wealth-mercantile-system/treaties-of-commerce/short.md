title: How a cheaper wine cost Britain nine thousand pounds
---

@art two-barrels
Britain's buyers saved money on Portuguese wine, and Britain still lost.

@math items="French: 20 + 30 = 50@French;;Portuguese: 29 + 20 = 49@Portuguese"
Say French wine is twenty pounds a tun plus thirty duty. Portuguese wine pays two thirds of the duty, so its merchants can charge twenty nine and still beat France by a pound.

@words text="Buyers save 1,000 · treasury loses 10,000"
On a thousand tuns, buyers save a thousand, the treasury loses ten thousand, and nine thousand more goes to Portugal.
