@art refund-on-export
Adam Smith disliked most export help. One kind he called reasonable.

@words text="A drawback refunds the duty, and no more"
A drawback gives back the duty paid on goods that are exported again. It can never cause more exports than there'd have been with no duty at all.

@art three-ledgers
So it doesn't tilt trade. It only undoes the tilt the duty made. Refund any more, and it's a bounty.
