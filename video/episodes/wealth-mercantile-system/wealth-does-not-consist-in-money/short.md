Does a family dine better because it owns too many pots and pans?

@words text="Gold and silver are utensils"
Adam Smith says gold and silver are utensils, as much as the furniture of the kitchen. You need enough to circulate your goods, and no more.

@art packet-boat
And a country that can pay for gold will always get it. One packet boat brings fifty tons, more than five million guineas.
