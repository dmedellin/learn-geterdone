@art tobacco-for-wine
England swaps tobacco for French wine, and the books call it a loss.

@math items="balance: 100,000 − 110,000 = −10,000@the books;;gain: 110,000 − 100,000 = +10,000@Smith"
Tobacco worth a hundred thousand pounds buys wine worth a hundred and ten thousand in England. On the books, imports beat exports by ten thousand. Smith says the capital of England just grew by ten thousand.

@words text="Gain is value, not gold"
Gain, for Smith, is the value of what you get back, not the gold that moves.
