@art two-curves
A penny at compound interest could outgrow every product labour has ever made.

@math items="claim at 5% for 1,772 years: 37 digits@thirty seven digits;;grown with a product at 1%: 7 digits@seven"
Richard Price's penny, at five percent from year one to seventeen seventy two, has thirty seven digits in shillings. Grow it with a real product at one percent, and it has seven.

@words text="Interest is a share of surplus labour"
Marx's point: interest is paid out of surplus value, so no claim can outgrow the labour that pays it.
