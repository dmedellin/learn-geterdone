@art merchant-joins
A merchant can sell at exactly the value and still make a profit.

@math items="180 / 1,000 = 18%@eighteen percent;;900 + 162 = 1,062@sixty two;;1,062 + 18 = 1,080@eighty"
In Marx's figures, one hundred and eighty of surplus value is spread over all capital, merchant's included: eighteen percent. The manufacturers sell at one thousand and sixty two. The merchant adds his eighteen and sells at one thousand and eighty.

@words text="Bought below value, sold at value"
That's the value of the goods. The merchant didn't mark up. He bought below value.
