title: Why the grocer's margin is thin

@art furniture-and-grocer
The grocer didn't choose a thin margin. Marx says his turnover chose it.

@math items="once: 100 sells at 115@once a year;;five times: 15% / 5 = 3%, 100 sells at 103@five times"
At fifteen percent a year, a merchant who turns his hundred over once a year sells at one hundred and fifteen. Turn it over five times, and each sale carries three percent: one hundred and three.

@words text="Same 15 a year, either way"
Five sales at three make fifteen. The rate on the capital is the same. Only the mark up on each sale has changed.
