@art orchestra
If profit is the boss's wage for managing, what happens when he hires a manager?

@math items="£20 − £5 interest = £15@fifteen;;£15 − £6 manager = £9@nine pounds"
Take twenty pounds of profit on borrowed money at Marx's rates. Pay five in interest, and fifteen is left. Now pay a manager six, which is the lesson's own example. Nine pounds are still left.

@words text="Paid for no work at all"
The managing is paid for. On Marx's account, that nine is surplus value, the unpaid labour of others.
