title: The interest rate has a ceiling but no floor
---

@art ceiling-no-floor
Marx says the rate of interest has a ceiling, but no floor.

@words text="Ceiling: the rate of profit"
Interest is paid out of profit, so normally it can't be more than the profit. At the ceiling, the borrower keeps nothing.

@words text="Floor: “It may fall to any depth.”"
Below, nothing stops it. In September eighteen forty three, Marx reports, it fell to one and a half percent. In between, no law decides, only competition and custom.
