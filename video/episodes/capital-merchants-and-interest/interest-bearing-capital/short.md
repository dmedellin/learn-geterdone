@art profit-split
Marx says interest isn't the price of money. It's a slice of profit.

@math items="£100 at 20% → £20 profit@twenty pounds;;interest £5 · borrower keeps £15@five pounds"
Lend a hundred pounds to someone who uses it as capital at twenty percent. It makes twenty pounds. Pay five pounds of interest out of that, and the borrower keeps fifteen.

@words text="At 10%: £10 and £10"
Double the rate, and the split is ten and ten. The twenty never changes. The rate only decides who gets how much.
