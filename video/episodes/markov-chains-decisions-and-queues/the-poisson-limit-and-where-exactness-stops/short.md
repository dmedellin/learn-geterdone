title: Where Poisson arrivals really come from
---

@art sliced-time
Poisson arrivals are just lots of tiny coin flips.

@words text="n slots · chance λ/n each"
Cut a unit of time into n slots, each with a small chance of an arrival, lambda over n. The count is binomial, with mean lambda.

@art shrinking-gap
Add more slots, and it closes in on the Poisson distribution. With lambda three, the biggest gap is nearly six percent at eight slots, and under a thousandth at four hundred.
