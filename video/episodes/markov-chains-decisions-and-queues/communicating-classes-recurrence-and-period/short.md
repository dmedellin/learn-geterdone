title: A one in a thousand exit is still an exit
---

@art thin-arc-out
A one in a thousand chance of leaving means the chain leaves. Every time.

@words text="Recurrent ⇔ no arc leaves"
Each visit gives a fixed positive chance of taking that arc. Over infinitely many visits, never taking it has probability zero.

@art transient-escape
So any class with an arc out, and no way back, is transient. The size of the arc only changes how long it takes.
