Same customers, same work. One more server, and the chance of waiting more than halves.

@math items="2 servers:  96.3% wait@two servers;;3 servers:  44.4% wait@three servers"
With an offered load of two, two servers make ninety six point three percent of arrivals wait. Three servers, and it's forty four point four.

@words text="ρ = 1  →  ρ = 2/3"
The work didn't change. Each server's share did, from all of its time to two thirds.
