title: A steady state that is never reached

@art cycle-no-limit
This chain has one exact steady state, and it never gets there.

@words text="π = (1/3, 1/3, 1/3)"
Three states in a ring. The balance equations have exactly one solution: a third each.

@math items="P³ = I@P cubed;;distance from π: 2/3, every step@two thirds"
But P cubed is the identity, so the powers repeat forever. The distance from pi is two thirds at every step. A steady state is an equation, not a limit.
