@art stays-put
Add an ending, and the stress often stays exactly where it was.

@words text="HAP·py  →  HAP·pi·ness" sub="a·GREE  →  a·GREE·ment"
Happy is strong on its first part, and so is happiness. Agree is strong on its second part, and so is agreement.

@words text="-ly · -ness · -ment · -er · -ful · -able" sub="185 of 192 pairs"
Six endings work like this. The lab finds that the strong part stays put in a hundred and eighty-five of a hundred and ninety-two pairs.
