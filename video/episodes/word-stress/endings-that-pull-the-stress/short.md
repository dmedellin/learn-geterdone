@art pull-magnet
Educate, education. Add four letters, and the stress jumps.

@words text="ED·u·cate  →  ed·u·CA·tion" sub="-tion: the part just before it"
Educate is strong on its first part. But t-i-o-n puts the push on the part just before it. Education.

@words text="A·ble  →  a·BIL·i·ty" sub="-ity: the third part from the end"
Able becomes ability, strong on bil, the third part from the end. The ending decides, not the word you started with.
