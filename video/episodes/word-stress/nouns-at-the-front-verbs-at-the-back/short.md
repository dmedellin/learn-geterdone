@art one-spelling-two
A record, but to record. Same spelling, two different words.

@words text="a RE·cord" sub="a noun: the strong part first"
As a noun, a record is strong on the first part. A noun of two parts usually is: action, brother, country.

@words text="to re·CORD" sub="a verb: the strong part second"
As a verb, to record is strong on the second part, like decide and agree. In the lab, each rule is right about nine times in ten.
