title: The same sentence can be true and false

@art three-dots
Every planet orbits something. Is that true? It depends on the model.

@math items="Orbits: ab, bc  →  True@Orbits holds;;remove bc  →  False at b@Remove the pair"
With three individuals, where Orbits holds of a, b and of b, c, the sentence is true. Remove the pair b, c, and planet b orbits nothing. Now it's false.

@words text="A sentence has truth conditions, not a truth value"
Same words, same order, one pair different. Its value belongs to the sentence and a model together.
