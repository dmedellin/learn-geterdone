title: Is the present King of France bald?

@art empty-throne
Is the present King of France bald? There is no King of France.

@list items="There is a king@There is a king|At most one king@at most one|He is bald@he is bald"
Russell says the sentence makes three claims. There is a king, there's at most one, and he is bald. The first is false.

@words text="So the sentence is false"
So for Russell, it's simply false. Strawson disagreed. He said it presupposes a king, so it's neither true nor false.
