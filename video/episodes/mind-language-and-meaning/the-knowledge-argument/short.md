@art grey-room
Mary knows every physical fact about colour, but she's never seen red.

@math items="a → ¬n,  n  ∴  ¬a@If physicalism"
If physicalism is true, she learns nothing new on seeing red. She does learn something. So physicalism is false. And that form is valid.

@art ability-tools
So the physicalist must deny a premise. Maybe what she gains is an ability, a way of recognising red, and not a new fact.
