@art evening-morning
The evening star and the morning star are the same planet. Why was that a discovery?

@math items="h = h:  trivial@Hesperus is Hesperus;;h = p:  news@Hesperus is Phosphorus"
Hesperus is Hesperus tells you nothing. Hesperus is Phosphorus told astronomers something new. Yet both names pick out Venus.

@words text="Same reference, different sense"
Frege's answer: the names share a referent, but present it in different ways, as the evening star and the morning star. That's the sense.
