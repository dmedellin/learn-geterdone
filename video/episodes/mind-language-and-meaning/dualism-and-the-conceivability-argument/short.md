If you can imagine pain with no brain activity, does that prove the mind isn't the brain?

@math items="□(p ↔ c):  false, with the pain-only world@With a pain only world;;□(p ↔ c):  true, without it@Remove that world"
With a pain only world in the model, the identity fails as a necessity. Remove that world, and the same identity holds. No logic changed.

@words text="Conceivable is not yet possible"
So the whole argument rests on one step, that what you can imagine is genuinely possible.
