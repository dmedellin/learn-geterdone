@art sand-heap
One grain never turns a heap into a non heap. So is zero grains a heap?

@math items="each step:  9999/10000 true@almost perfectly true;;the end:  0@zero"
Under degrees of truth, each step is almost perfectly true. But ten thousand tiny losses add up, and the end is zero.

A sharp cutoff, a blurry borderline, or degrees. Each answer to the heap gives something up.
