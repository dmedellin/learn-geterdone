@art love-ring
Everyone loves someone. Does that mean there's one person everybody loves?

@math items="∀x ∃y Loves(x, y):  True@each person;;∃y ∀x Loves(x, y):  False@one person"
In a ring where a loves b, b loves c, and c loves a, each person loves someone. True. But no one person is loved by all. False.

@words text="Same words, two formulas"
The order of the quantifiers changes the claim, and English leaves it unsaid. The hearer has to choose.
