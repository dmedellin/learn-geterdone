title: The first thing ING's managers changed was their own calendar

@art time-log
Before changing anyone else, ING's managers measured themselves.

@words text="Log where the days go"
The Bells challenged them to replace long meetings and firefighting with visible work, short meetings at a board, and coaching. First, they logged their own time for several weeks.

@words text="A tenth of their time, freed"
Then they designed new ways to work, and reached their goal: a tenth of their time free for what they chose. You change the routines, and culture follows.
