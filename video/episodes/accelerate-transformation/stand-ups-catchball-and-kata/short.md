title: The stand-up rule that keeps it to fifteen minutes
---

@art standup-board
At ING's stand-ups, nobody solves problems. That's the point.

@words text="Note it · pass it on · keep it visible"
Each person says what they're on, what's in the way, and what's done. A problem goes to whoever can help, climbs a level if it must, and stays on the board until it's resolved.

@art catchball
The Bells call that passing up and across catchball. It keeps the stand-up to about fifteen minutes.
