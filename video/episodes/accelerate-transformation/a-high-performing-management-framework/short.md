Halve the work in process and lead time halves. But only if one thing holds.

@math items="lead time = WIP ÷ throughput@divided by;;4 ÷ 4/5 = 5 days@five days;;2 ÷ 4/5 = 2.5 days@two and a half"
Lead time is work in process divided by throughput. The lesson's invented squad finishes four fifths of an item a day. With four in process, that's five days. Limit it to two, and it's two and a half.

@art bet-on-throughput
That assumes throughput holds. It's the bet a limit makes, and the squad's board will show if it pays.
