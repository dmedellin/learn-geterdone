@art scattered-constructors
Miss just one constructor call, and a Motif menu turns up in your Mac app.

@art factory-family
So don't name the style's classes. Ask a factory for a scroll bar, and the Mac factory gives you a Mac one. Every widget it makes belongs to one family.

@words text="Swap the factory, swap the whole family"
Choose the factory once, at start up. Change it, and the whole look and feel changes together.
