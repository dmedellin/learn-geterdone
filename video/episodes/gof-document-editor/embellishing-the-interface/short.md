@art forward-then-add
Same three add ons, same rental. Three different prices. How?

@math items="(6 + 2) × 3 + 1.5 = 25.5@six plus two;;(6 + 1.5) × 3 + 2 = 24.5@six plus one point five;;6 × 3 + 2 + 1.5 = 21.5@six times three"
Each wrapper holds the charge inside it and works on the way out. Six plus two, times three, plus one point five, is twenty five point five. Change the order: six plus one point five, times three, plus two, is twenty four point five. Or six times three, plus two, plus one point five: twenty one point five.

@words text="Order matters when operations don't commute"
That's Decorator. Wrapping order is a design choice.
