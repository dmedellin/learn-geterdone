@art highlighter
Highlighting and summarising both rated low utility. Here's how to fix one of them.

@words text="Low utility" sub="Dunlosky et al. (2013)"
A big review rated highlighting, rereading and summarising all of low utility. Practice testing rated high.

@art closed-then-check
So close the text first. Write three sentences from memory, then check them against the page. Now your summary is retrieval practice, the kind of study the review rated high.
