title: Rereading wins at five minutes and loses at two days

@art crossover
Rereading wins at five minutes. Two days later, it loses.

@math items="5 minutes: read twice ≈ 81% · recalled ≈ 75%@five minutes;;2 days: read twice ≈ 54% · recalled ≈ 68%@two days later"
In Roediger and Karpicke's study, rereaders recalled about eighty one percent at five minutes, against seventy five. But two days later, it was fifty four against sixty eight.

@words text="Recall it, don't reread it"
So after one reading, close the page and write what you remember. It feels harder, and that's the point.
