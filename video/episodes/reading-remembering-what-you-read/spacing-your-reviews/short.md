title: Cramming feels better. Spacing keeps more.

@art massed-day
Five reviews tonight feel great. That's exactly the problem.

@words text="Same 50 minutes"
By the fifth pass in a row the text is fluent, and a self test scores high. Spread the same fifty minutes over a month, and each review starts with something forgotten.

@art expanding-gaps
It feels worse. But spaced review is the one retained better at a delay. Review by recalling, with the text closed, on days you've put in the calendar.
