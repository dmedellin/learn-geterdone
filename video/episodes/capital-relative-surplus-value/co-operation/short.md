@art masons-ladder
Twelve masons passing stones up a ladder beat twelve men climbing alone. Who's paid for the difference?

@words text="100 labour-powers bought, one at a time"
The employer makes a separate contract with each worker, for that worker's own labour-power, at its value.

@quote text="He pays them the value of 100 independent labour-powers, but he does not pay for the combined labour-power of the hundred." cite="Marx, Capital, Volume I, Chapter 13"
So, Marx says, he pays them the value of a hundred independent labour-powers, but he does not pay for the combined labour-power of the hundred.
