title: The machine that saves £6,000 and still isn't bought

@art two-tests
A machine can save society six thousand pounds of labour, and still not be worth buying.

@math items="labour saved: £6,000@six thousand;;wages saved: £3,000@three thousand;;machine: £3,000@machine"
A hundred and fifty men add six thousand pounds of value a year, but they're paid three thousand. A machine costing three thousand saves the capitalist nothing.

@words text="Not less labour. Less labour paid for."
He pays for labour-power, not labour. So cheap labour delays machines.
