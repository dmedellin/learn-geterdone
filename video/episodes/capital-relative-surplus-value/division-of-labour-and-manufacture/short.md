@art type-line
Why do the slowest jobs in a workshop get the most workers?

@math items="2,000 · 4,000 · 8,000 an hour@two thousand;;4 · 2 · 1 = 7 workers@four founders"
A founder casts two thousand type an hour, a breaker breaks four thousand, a rubber polishes eight thousand. To keep everyone busy, you need four founders, two breakers and one rubber. Seven in all.

@words text="A fixed mathematical ratio"
The plan is fixed in advance. No market sets society's proportions that way.
