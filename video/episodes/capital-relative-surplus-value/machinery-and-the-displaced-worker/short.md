title: Fifty workers sacked, nothing set free

@art carpet-factory
A factory sacks fifty workers and buys a machine. Is there now money to rehire them?

@math items="wages saved: 50 · £30 = £1,500@fifteen hundred;;machine: £1,500@the machine;;set free: £0@nothing"
The fifty earned thirty pounds a year each, fifteen hundred in all. The machine cost fifteen hundred too. So nothing is set free.

@words text="Variable has become constant capital"
The money that paid wages is now machinery. It can't hire anyone.
