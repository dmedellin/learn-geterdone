@art sago-palm
Sago grows wild. Two hours a day feeds the cutter. So why does he end up working twelve?

@math items="alone: 2 h, rate 0@rate of zero;;12-hour day: 10 / 2 = 500%@five hundred"
Working for himself, all two hours are necessary: a rate of zero. As a wage labourer on a twelve hour day, it's ten over two, five hundred percent.

@words text="Nature gives the possibility, never the reality"
Nature set the two hours. Something else made him work the other ten.
