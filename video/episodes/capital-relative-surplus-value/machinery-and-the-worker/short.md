@art pores-in-the-hour
The law cut the day from twelve hours to ten. The employers lost nothing. How?

@math items="10 h · 6/5 = 12 h@six fifths;;12 − 8 = 4 h surplus · 50%@fifty percent"
Each hour got a fifth denser. Ten hours times six fifths is twelve hours of normal labour. Eight are necessary, four are surplus: fifty percent, as before.

@words text="Fewer pores in the hour"
Fewer pauses, faster machines, more looms per weaver. Marx calls it intensity.
