title: Why "workers get a quarter" means 300%
---

@art lavergne-field
Workers get a quarter of the product, owners three quarters. Is that seventy five percent, or three hundred?

@math items="share: ¾ = 75%@seventy five percent;;rate: ¾ / ¼ = 300%@three hundred"
As a share of the whole, it's seventy five percent. But surplus over necessary labour is three quarters over one quarter: three hundred percent.

@words text="A share can never reach 100%"
Marx's point: the share hides the rate. It can never reach a hundred percent, however hard people work.
