@art fixed-pie
Double how much a worker makes in a day. Does the day create any more value?

@words text="A day of given length creates the same value"
No. On Marx's first law, a twelve hour day creates the same value whatever its productivity. Only the split changes.

@math items="labour-power: 8 h → 6 h@eight hours to six;;rate: 50% → 100%@to a hundred"
Cheaper necessaries cut labour-power's value from eight hours to six. Surplus rises from four hours to six, and the rate from fifty percent to a hundred.
