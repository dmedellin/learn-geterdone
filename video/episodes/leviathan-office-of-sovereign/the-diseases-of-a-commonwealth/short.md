@art two-masters
What happens when two powers both claim the last word over you?

@quote text="which is a Kingdome divided in it selfe, and cannot stand." cite="Hobbes, Leviathan, Chapter 29"
Hobbes says there are then two commonwealths of the same subjects, which is a kingdom divided in itself, and cannot stand.

@math items="both insist: civil war@both insist;;chance: 2/3 · 2/3 = 4/9@four times in nine"
Write it as a game of chicken. If both insist, it's civil war. And if each insists two times in three, it comes four times in nine.
