@art missing-link
Can a ruler be bound by a law he can repeal tomorrow?

@quote text="he that can bind, can release" cite="Hobbes, Leviathan, Chapter 26"
Hobbes says no. He that can bind can release. Whoever can free himself whenever he likes is already free.

@words text="Valid · but is the first premise true?"
The argument is valid. So a critic like Sir Edward Coke had to deny the first premise: that the sovereign can repeal any law at will.
