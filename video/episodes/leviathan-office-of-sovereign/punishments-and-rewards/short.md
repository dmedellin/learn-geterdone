title: Hobbes: some fines aren't punishments, they're prices
---

@art price-tag
Hobbes says a fine smaller than the gain isn't a punishment at all.

@words text="Rather the price, than the punishment"
Punishment is meant to dispose men to obey. A harm less than the benefit of the crime does the opposite. It's rather the price of the crime.

@math items="gain 100 · penalty 60@sixty;;60 < 100: a price@price"
So if a crime gains a hundred and the penalty is sixty, you haven't been punished. You've paid the price.
