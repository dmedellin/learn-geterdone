title: Hobbes: God rules by power, not by creating us

@art creator-and-creature
Hobbes says God's right to rule doesn't come from creating us.

@words text="Not as Creator, and Gracious; but as Omnipotent"
Men laid down their natural right to rule others only because they couldn't win it by force. A power no one can resist has no reason to lay it down.

@art whirlwind
So when Job asked why he suffered, God answered with his power: where wast thou when I laid the foundations of the earth?
