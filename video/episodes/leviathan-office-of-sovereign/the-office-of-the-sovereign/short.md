@art frugal-and-idle
Why should someone who saves pay more tax than someone who spends everything?

@words text="Equal debt for equal protection"
Hobbes says everyone gets the same protection, so taxes should fall on what each consumes, not on what each owns.

@math items="frugal: 25 on riches, 10 on consumption@frugal man;;idle: 5 on riches, 20 on consumption@idle man"
Raising a hundred from five people, the frugal man pays twenty five on riches, but ten on consumption. The idle man pays five, or twenty.
