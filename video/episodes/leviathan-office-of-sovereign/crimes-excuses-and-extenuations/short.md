title: Hobbes: a small fine is an invitation

@art scales-gain-penalty
Hobbes says a penalty that's too small doesn't deter a crime. It invites it.

@words text="Benefit vs harm of punishment"
Men compare what they gain by the crime with what the punishment costs them, and choose what looks best.

@math items="gain 100, penalty 150, caught 1 in 2@one time in two;;1/2 · 150 = 75 < 100@seventy five"
A fraud gains a hundred. The penalty is a hundred and fifty, but it lands one time in two. That's seventy five expected, so the crime pays.
