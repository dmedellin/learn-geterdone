@art labour-and-fence
Hobbes says that without a commonwealth, nothing you own is really yours.

@words text="Neither propriety, nor community, but uncertainty"
With no sovereign, a thing is his who gets it and keeps it by force. That's not property. It's uncertainty.

@art twelve-portions
So property is made, not found. The sovereign makes it in the laws, the way Eleazar and Joshua divided the land of Canaan into twelve portions.
