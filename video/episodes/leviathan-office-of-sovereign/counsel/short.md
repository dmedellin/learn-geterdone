@art one-voice-echo
Eleven advisers in a room can be worth exactly one.

@words text="Better counselled by hearing them apart"
Hobbes says men in an assembly vote moved by another's eloquence, or by fear of the room.

@math items="11 independent, each 3/5: about 75%@independent;;10 copy the first speaker: 3/5@copy"
Eleven independent counsellors, each right three fifths of the time, are right together about seventy five percent of the time. But if ten copy the first speaker, it's three fifths, one man's chance.
