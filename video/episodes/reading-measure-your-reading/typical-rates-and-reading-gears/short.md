title: Most readers never shift gear
---

@art five-gears
Reading has five gears, and most people only ever use one.

@list items="Scanning|Skimming|Rauding|Learning|Memorising"
Carver described five: scanning, skimming, rauding, which is ordinary reading, learning, and memorising. Each has its own purpose and its own typical rate.

@art stuck-gear
But he found most readers hold a fairly constant rauding rate, whatever they read. His advice: shift deliberately, by purpose.
