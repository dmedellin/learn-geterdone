title: Reading twice as fast can mean understanding less
---

@art speed-up-score-down
Double your reading speed, and you can still end up reading less.

@math items="240 × 6/6 = 240@six of six;;480 × 2/6 = 160@two of six"
Multiply your rate by your share of questions right. Two forty words a minute with six of six gives two forty. Four eighty with two of six gives just one sixty.

@words text="Effective rate = rate × comprehension"
That product is the effective reading rate: understood words per minute. The rate doubled, and it fell.
