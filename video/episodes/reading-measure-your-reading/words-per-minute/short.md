Brysbaert pooled the studies of adult silent reading in English. He estimated about two hundred and thirty eight words a minute for nonfiction, and about two hundred and sixty for fiction.

@words text="300 is an overestimate"
In his analysis, three hundred is an overestimate. And an average has readers on both sides, so a first reading below it is a starting point, not a verdict.
