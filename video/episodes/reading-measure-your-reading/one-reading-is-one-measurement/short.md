title: Why one fast reading proves nothing
---

@art four-runners
Change nothing, and your next reading still beats your best one time in four.

@words text="Each of four is equally likely to be fastest"
Take four readings with nothing changed between them. Each is as likely as any other to be the fastest. So the fourth lands above the range of the first three about one time in four.

@words text="Wait for the median of three tests"
That's why one high reading proves nothing. Wait until the median of your last three tests is above your baseline range.
