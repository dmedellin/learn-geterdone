Three overlapping sets. Why do you add the triple overlap back in?

@art sign-arrows
An element in all three sets gets counted three times when you add the sizes. Then the three pairwise overlaps each subtract it once.

@words text="+3 − 3 = 0" sub="so add it back: +1"
Three minus three is zero, so it has vanished from the count. Adding the triple overlap back puts it at exactly one.

@words text="add singles · subtract pairs · add triples"
Add the singles, subtract the pairs, add the triples. Every element counted once.
