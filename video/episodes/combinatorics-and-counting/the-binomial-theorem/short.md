@art choice-picks
Expand x plus y to the fifth. Where does the ten come from?

@art factor-row
x plus y to the fifth is five factors multiplied together. To expand it, you pick x or y from each one.

@math items="y from exactly 2 of 5 factors@exactly two;;C(5, 2) = 10@ten ways"
You get x cubed y squared whenever y comes from exactly two of the five factors. Choosing which two is C of five, two: ten ways.

@words text="Expanding is choosing"
So the coefficient counts choices. That's the binomial theorem.
