@art stars-bars
Five doughnuts from three kinds. Why does the picture need just two bars?

@art empty-group
Write each box as stars for the doughnuts, and bars that split them into kinds. Three groups need only two dividers, because the bars go between the groups.

@math items="5 stars + 2 bars = 7 symbols@seven symbols;;C(7, 5) = 21@twenty one"
So a box is a row of seven symbols, five of them stars. Choose where the stars go: C of seven, five, which is twenty one.

@words text="n types → n − 1 bars"
One bar fewer than the number of kinds. That's the whole trick.
