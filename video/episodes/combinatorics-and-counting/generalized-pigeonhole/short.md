@art six-points-graph
Any six people: three all know each other, or three are all strangers.

@art pigeon-boxes
Pick one person. The other five are each an acquaintance or a stranger. Two boxes, five people, so one box holds at least three.

@words text="three acquaintances of P"
Say three acquaintances. If any two of them know each other, that's a triangle with P. If none do, those three are mutual strangers.

@art five-cycle
And with five people it can fail. That's why the number is six.
