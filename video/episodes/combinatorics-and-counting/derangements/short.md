@art hat-check
Ten people or ten million, nobody gets their own hat with the same chance.

@math items="4 people: 0.375@point three seven five;;6 people: ≈ 0.3681@point three six eight one;;7 or more: 0.3679@point three six seven nine"
Hand the hats back at random. With four people, the chance nobody gets their own is point three seven five. With six, about point three six eight one. And from seven on, it's point three six seven nine to four places, whatever the number.

@words text="→ 1/e"
That's one over e. More people make more matches possible, but they also bring more ways to be mismatched.
