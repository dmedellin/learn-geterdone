Most counting mistakes aren't arithmetic. They're the right calculation of the wrong quantity.

@math items="P(5, 3) = 60@sixty;;C(5, 3) = 10@ten;;5³ = 125@a hundred and twenty five;;C(7, 3) = 35@thirty five"
Choose three from five, and the answer could be sixty, ten, a hundred and twenty five, or thirty five. It depends on whether order matters, and whether repetition is allowed.

@words text="Order? Repetition? Then compute."
So answer those two questions out loud, before you touch a formula.
