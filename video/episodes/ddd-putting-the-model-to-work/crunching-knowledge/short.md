One team, three vocabularies, and twenty places every conversation has to stop.

@art three-vocabularies
A seed library's curators, growers and developers each had their own word for most concepts. Every pair of names that differs is a translation.

@art sketch-together
Evans's answer is knowledge crunching: developers and experts building one model together, not one side for the other.

@math items="20 → 2 translations@down to two"
After some weeks of it, the synonyms are consolidated, and twenty translations are down to two.
