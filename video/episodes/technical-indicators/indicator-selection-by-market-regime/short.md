title: The same crossover means different things in different markets
---

@art range-regime
A moving average crossover that works in a trend can whipsaw you in a range.

@words text="Trend: crossovers hold · Range: they flip"
In a trend, the averages cross and stay apart. In a sideways range, they cross back and forth again and again.

@words text="Classify first, then read the signal"
So decide what kind of market you're in before you decide what a signal means.
