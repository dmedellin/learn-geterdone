title: Why the stochastic sticks at one hundred in a trend
---

@art trend-pinned-stoch
In a strong uptrend, the stochastic can sit near one hundred, bar after bar.

@math items="%K = (C − LL) ÷ (HH − LL) × 100@close minus"
It measures where the close sits in its recent range: the close minus the lowest low, over the high minus low range, times one hundred.

@words text="Trend: closes keep landing near the top"
In an uptrend, each close lands near the top of that range, so the reading stays pinned.
