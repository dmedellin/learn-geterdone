title: RSI above seventy is not a sell signal
---

@art trend-pinned
RSI above seventy doesn't mean the price is about to fall.

@words text="High RSI can persist in an uptrend"
RSI compares recent gains with recent losses. In a strong uptrend, gains keep coming, so RSI can stay high while price keeps climbing.

@words text="Extreme + price confirmation"
So treat an extreme as information about momentum. A trade still needs a defined price trigger.
