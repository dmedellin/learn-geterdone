title: Three indicators agreeing might be one opinion

@art same-input
RSI, the stochastic and rate of change all agree. That might be one vote, not three.

@words text="All three: momentum from the same prices"
They're all momentum related transformations of the same price series, so they tend to move together.

@art job-slots
So give each indicator its own job: trend, momentum, volatility or breakout. If two do the same job, drop one.
