title: A high ADX doesn't mean the price is going up
---

@art adx-both-trends
A high ADX can mean a strong downtrend.

@words text="ADX = strength · +DI vs −DI = direction"
ADX measures how persistent the directional movement is, not which way it's going. For direction, compare plus D I with minus D I.

@art di-lines
A steady fall pushes minus D I far above plus D I, and ADX rises, just as it would in a steady climb.
