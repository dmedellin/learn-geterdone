title: A five dollar move isn't always the same move
---

@art scale-compare
A five dollar move can be huge, or barely anything.

@math items="$5 ÷ $20 = 25%@twenty dollar;;$5 ÷ $500 = 1%@five hundred dollar"
On a twenty dollar instrument, five dollars is twenty five percent. On a five hundred dollar instrument, it's one percent.

@words text="Momentum: price units · ROC: percent"
Simple momentum keeps the five dollars. Rate of change turns it into a percentage, so you can compare across price scales.
