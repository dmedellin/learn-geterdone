title: Divergence is a warning, not a buy signal
---

@art bullish-divergence
Price made a lower low. RSI made a higher low. Buy? Not yet.

@words text="Same two bars, opposite swings"
That's bullish divergence, if both lows are read at the same two bars. It says price and momentum changed differently.

@art confirm-trigger
But it can persist or fail while price keeps falling. So wait for a defined price event before acting.
