@art self-test
Close above the channel's high? Written this way, it never happens.

@words text="Channel includes the current bar → it's at least that bar's high"
If the channel includes the current bar, its upper level is at least that bar's own high. And a close is never above its own high.

@art prior-channel
So test the close against the channel through the previous bar, a boundary that's already known.
