title: Why high minus low misses the gap
---

@art gap-bar
A stock gaps down, then trades quietly all day. High minus low barely notices.

@math items="TR = max(H − L, |H − Cₚ|, |L − Cₚ|)@the largest"
True range takes the largest of three: high minus low, and the distances from the high and the low to the previous close.

@words text="The gap counts"
After a gap, the distance from the previous close takes over, so the move is measured.
