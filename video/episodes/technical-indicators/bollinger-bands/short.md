title: A Bollinger squeeze doesn't tell you which way
---

@art squeeze-expand
The bands squeeze together. Which way will price break? The squeeze can't say.

@words text="Narrow bands = low recent dispersion"
The band width follows recent dispersion around the average. A squeeze only means closes have stayed close together lately.

@words text="Direction needs separate evidence"
The breakout direction, if one comes at all, needs separate evidence, and the bands might stay narrow for a while.
