title: The MACD histogram is not volume
---

@art histogram-bars
Those bars under the MACD look like volume. They aren't.

@math items="Histogram = MACD − signal@MACD minus"
The histogram is MACD minus its signal line. It shows the distance between the two lines.

@words text="Growing bars: lines separating · shrinking bars: lines converging"
When the bars grow, the lines are pulling apart. When they shrink, the lines are closing in. There's no volume anywhere in the calculation.
