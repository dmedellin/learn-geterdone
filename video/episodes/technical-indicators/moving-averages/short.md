title: The EMA doesn't lead. It just lags less.
---

@art shock-response
People call the exponential moving average leading. It isn't.

@words text="SMA: equal weights · EMA: recent weighted more"
The simple average weights every bar equally. The exponential average gives recent bars more weight, so after a shock it usually responds faster.

@words text="Both lag price"
But both are built only from past prices. Both lag. The EMA, for the same period, just lags less.
