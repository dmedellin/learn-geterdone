title: A crossover happens once, not every bar
---

@art cross-point
The fast average is above the slow one. Is that a crossover? Usually not.

@words text="Event: the order changes · State: it stays"
A crossover is the one completed bar where the order changes. Every bar after that, while it stays above, is the same state.

@art range-whipsaw
And in a sideways market, the averages can cross back and forth again and again. That's a whipsaw.
