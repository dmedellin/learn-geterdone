Price touched the upper Keltner band. Is that a breakout? Not yet.

@list items="A touch@touch|A trade beyond, intrabar@trade beyond|A completed close outside@completed close"
A touch, a trade beyond the band during the bar, and a completed close outside are three different events.

@words text="Name the exact event"
So a rule has to say which one it means. The word breakout on its own isn't a rule.
