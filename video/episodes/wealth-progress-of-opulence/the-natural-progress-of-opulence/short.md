title: Does the town live off the country? Adam Smith says no

@art town-and-country
The town grows none of its own food. So is it living off the country?

@words text="The gains of both are mutual and reciprocal."
Adam Smith says no. The gains of both are mutual and reciprocal.

@math items="alone: 300 corn, 300 cloth@alone;;specialised: 600 corn, 600 cloth@keeps to"
A farmer and a weaver working alone make three hundred corn and three hundred cloth between them. When each keeps to what he's best at, six hundred of each. Both end up with twice as much.
