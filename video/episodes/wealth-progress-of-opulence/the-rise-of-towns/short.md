title: The medieval kings who froze their own revenue
---

@art farm-of-the-town
Medieval kings froze one tax for ever, just as it was about to grow.

@words text="A rent certain, never afterwards to be augmented"
They let towns collect their own dues for a fixed sum, never afterwards to be augmented. As towns grew, that sum shrank against what they could have raised.

@art king-and-burghers
Why? The lords despised the burghers, and the king feared the lords. The fixed rent bought him allies, and the towns their liberty.
