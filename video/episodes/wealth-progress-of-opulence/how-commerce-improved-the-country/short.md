@art diamond-buckles
The feudal lords lost their power over a pair of diamond buckles.

@art retainers-in-line
A lord with ten thousand a year and nothing to buy fed perhaps a thousand families. Every one of them had to obey him.

@art spread-thin
Then commerce gave him something to buy. The same money now paid tiny shares to thousands of tradesmen, none of them wholly his. Smith says no one intended the revolution.
