@art half-share
Why would a farmer refuse an improvement that clearly pays?

@math items="farmer: 15 · 9 − 90 = 45@farmer;;metayer: (1/2) · 15 · 9 − 90 = −22.5@metayer"
Say draining a field costs ninety and adds fifteen a year, over a nine year lease. A farmer who keeps it all gains forty five. A metayer who keeps half loses twenty two fifty.

@words text="Same land · different share"
Same land, same improvement. Smith's point: the landlord laid out nothing and still took half, so the metayer never improved.
