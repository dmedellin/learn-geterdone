@art chain
Twelve elements, eleven unions, and a find that walks eleven pointers.

@words text="union(0,1), union(1,2), … union(10,11)"
Link the first root under the second every time, and each union hangs the whole tree under a single fresh node. You've built a path.

@art link-by-rank
Union by rank puts the shorter tree under the taller. Same eleven unions, and the deepest find walks one pointer.
