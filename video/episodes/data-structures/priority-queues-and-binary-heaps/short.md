@words text="1  3  2  6  8  7  4  9"
This array is a perfectly valid heap. And three comes before two.

@art heap-tree
A heap only promises that every parent is at most its children. Each path down from the root increases. Siblings aren't related at all.

@words text="Sorted is stronger than a heap"
If a heap were sorted, building one would sort. And sorting costs more than building a heap.
