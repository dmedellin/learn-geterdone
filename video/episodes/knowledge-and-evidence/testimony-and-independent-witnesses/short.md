@art shared-source
Two newspapers report the same thing. That's not always two witnesses.

@words text="Independent witnesses: factors multiply"
When witnesses are independent, their Bayes factors multiply. Two witnesses right nine times in ten give a factor of eighty one.

@words text="Same source = one witness heard twice"
But if both papers reprint one agency report, they're one witness heard twice. Multiply their factors, and you count one possible error as if it had been confirmed.
