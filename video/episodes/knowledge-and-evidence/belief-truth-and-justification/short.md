@art coin-flip
You believe it'll rain tomorrow, and it does. Did you know?

@words text="Not if you flipped a coin."
Not if you decided what to believe by flipping a coin. You were right, but only by luck.

@math items="knows  ⟺  J ∧ T ∧ B@justified, true belief"
That's why the traditional analysis adds a third condition. Knowledge is justified, true belief. Truth and belief alone let in the lucky guess.
