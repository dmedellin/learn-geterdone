A test that's ninety nine percent right can leave most positives false.

@math items="990 true positives@nine hundred and ninety;;49,950 false positives@forty nine thousand"
One in a thousand has the condition. Count a million people. You get nine hundred and ninety true positives, and forty nine thousand, nine hundred and fifty false ones from the healthy majority.

@words text="P(D | +) ≈ 1.9%"
So a positive means about a one point nine percent chance.
