@art bigger-lottery
Believe only what's ninety nine point nine percent certain? The lottery still gets you.

@words text="1,000 tickets · each loses at 999/1000"
In a fair lottery with a thousand tickets, each ticket loses with credence nine hundred and ninety nine thousandths. So you accept, of every ticket, that it loses.

@words text="But one ticket wins."
But one ticket wins. For any threshold below one, a big enough lottery reaches it.
