@art coin-two-bets
Here's how to lose money, whichever way the coin lands.

@math items="3/5 + 3/5 = 6/5@three fifths credence;;net either way:  −1/5@a fifth poorer"
Give heads three fifths credence, and tails three fifths too. Buy both bets at those prices. You pay six fifths, and exactly one bet pays one. You're a fifth poorer, either way.

@words text="heads + tails must sum to 1"
That's a Dutch book. Heads and tails exclude each other, so their credences must sum to one. Break that rule, and you can be exploited.
