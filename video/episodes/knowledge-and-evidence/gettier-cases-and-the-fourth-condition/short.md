@art stopped-clock
A stopped clock can give you a justified, true belief, and still not knowledge.

@words text="Stopped twelve hours ago"
You read the time off a clock that stopped twelve hours ago. You believe it's a quarter past two, and by chance it is.

@words text="Justified · true · believed · not known"
You had good reason to trust it, the belief is true, and you hold it. But you didn't know the time.
