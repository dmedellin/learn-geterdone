title: Three heads in a row doesn't make it a trick coin

@art two-headed-coin
Three heads in a row. Is it a two headed coin?

@words text="Bayes factor 8"
Three heads are certain on a two headed coin, and one in eight on a fair one. That's a factor of eight in its favour.

@math items="prior 1/100  →  posterior 8/107@eight over a hundred and seven"
But if the prior was one in a hundred, the posterior is only eight over a hundred and seven. Under eight percent. Fit isn't proof.
