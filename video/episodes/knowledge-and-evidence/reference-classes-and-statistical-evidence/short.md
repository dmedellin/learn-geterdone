@art nested-classes
Same man, same data. His risk is one in five, or one in twenty.

@math items="smokers:  1/5@As a smoker;;smokers who cycle:  1/20@a smoker who cycles"
As a smoker, one in five applies. As a smoker who cycles, one in twenty does. Both are true rates of groups he belongs to.

@words text="A rate belongs to a group"
The data don't say which group to use. That's the reference class problem.
