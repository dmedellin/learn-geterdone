@art chain-of-support
What justifies your belief? And what justifies that? And that?

@list items="A foundation@a foundation|A circle@a circle|An infinite chain@forever"
The chain of support can only end in a foundation, loop back in a circle, or run on forever. The skeptic says all three are unacceptable.

@words text="Something has to give."
Those claims can't all be true. Drop one, and you get a position: foundationalism, coherentism, infinitism, or skepticism.
