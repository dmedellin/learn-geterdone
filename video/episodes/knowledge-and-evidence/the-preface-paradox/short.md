A hundred claims, each ninety nine percent likely. How likely are they all true?

@math items="(99/100)¹⁰⁰ ≈ 0.37@to the power of a hundred"
If they're independent, multiply. Ninety nine hundredths to the power of a hundred is about nought point three seven.

@words text="Believe each, doubt the whole"
So an author can believe every sentence and still expect an error somewhere. That's the preface paradox, and it's no contradiction.
