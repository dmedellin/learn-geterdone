@art factor-vs-term
Can you cancel the x in x plus three, over x?

@words text="(x + 3) / x ≠ 3"
No. The x on the bottom is a factor, but on top it's only a term, added, not multiplied. Only factors cancel.

@math items="x = 1:  4/1 = 4@x equals one;;x = 3:  6/3 = 2@x equals three"
Test it. At x equals one you get four. At x equals three you get two. Never three.
