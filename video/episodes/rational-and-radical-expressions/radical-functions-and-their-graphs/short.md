title: The hidden gap in √(x² − 9)

@art two-rays
The square root of x squared minus nine hides a gap six units wide.

@math items="x² − 9 ≥ 0@at least zero;;x ≤ −3   or   x ≥ 3@at most minus three"
It needs x squared minus nine at least zero. That holds for x at most minus three, or at least three.

@words text="No function between −3 and 3" sub="at x = 0: √(−9)"
In between, the function doesn't exist. At zero, you'd need the square root of minus nine. Solve the inequality to find the domain.
