title: The excluded value that vanishes when you divide

@art hidden-zero
When you divide fractions, one forbidden value disappears from sight.

@math items="a/b ÷ c/d = a/b · d/c@times d over c;;c ≠ 0@c can't be zero"
a over b divided by c over d is a over b times d over c. So c can't be zero either, because you'd be dividing by zero.

@words text="Write it down when you flip"
After the flip, c sits on the bottom, then often cancels and vanishes. Write it down when you flip.
