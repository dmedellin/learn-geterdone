@art full-picture
Two excluded values, and the graph treats them completely differently.

@math items="f(x) = (x − 3)(x + 2) / [(x − 3)(x + 1)]@x minus three;;x = 3:  cancels  →  hole@cancels;;x = −1:  survives  →  vertical asymptote@survives"
In this function, x minus three cancels, so three is a hole. x plus one survives in the bottom, so minus one is a vertical asymptote.

@words text="Cancelled: hole. Left in the bottom: asymptote."
At the hole, the graph closes in on an ordinary number. At the asymptote, it runs off to infinity.
