@art three-levels
It simplifies to minus one over three x. So why is three forbidden?

@math items="(1/x − 1/3) / (x − 3)@one third;;= −1/(3x)@minus one over three x"
Start with one over x minus one third, all over x minus three. Simplified, it's minus one over three x, which seems to exclude only zero.

@words text="x ≠ 0 and x ≠ 3" sub="3 was the main denominator's zero"
But at three, the original divides by zero. Every level keeps its exclusion.
