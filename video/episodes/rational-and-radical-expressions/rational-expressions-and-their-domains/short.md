title: Why x² − 4 over x − 2 isn't 4 at x = 2

@art approach-arrows
What is x squared minus four, over x minus two, when x is two?

@math items="(x² − 4)/(x − 2) = x + 2,  x ≠ 2@x plus two;;at x = 2:  0/0@zero over zero"
Factor the top, and it equals x plus two everywhere else. But at two, you get zero over zero.

@words text="It has no value at 2" sub="close to 4 on both sides, a hole at 2"
Nearby values close in on four, yet at two there's nothing. The exclusion comes from the denominator you started with.
