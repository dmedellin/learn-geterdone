title: Why the cube root of 2 needs the cube root of 4

@art complete-the-power
Multiply a cube root by itself, and the radical just won't go away.

@math items="∛2 · ∛2 = ∛4@is only cube root four;;∛2 · ∛4 = ∛8 = 2@is cube root eight"
Cube root two times cube root two is only cube root four. You need cube root four instead, because cube root two times cube root four is cube root eight, which is two.

@words text="1/∛2 = ∛4/2" sub="ask what the radicand is missing"
So one over cube root two is cube root four over two.
