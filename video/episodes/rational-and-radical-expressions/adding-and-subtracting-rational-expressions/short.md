title: The minus sign that wrecks fraction subtraction

@art half-reached
The most common lost mark in fraction subtraction is one minus sign.

@math items="x − (2x − 6) = 6 − x@in brackets;;✗  x − 2x − 6 = −x − 6@without them"
Subtracting two x minus six from x, in brackets, gives six minus x. Without them, you get minus x minus six, and every later line is wrong.

@words text="Bracket the second numerator, then distribute"
The minus applies to the whole top, so bracket it first.
