@art revealed-alike
Root eight and root eighteen look unrelated. They're the same kind of radical.

@math items="√8 = 2√2@root eight;;√18 = 3√2@root eighteen;;√8 + √18 = 5√2@five root two"
Simplify each. Root eight is two root two. Root eighteen is three root two. So together they make five root two.

@words text="Simplify first, then combine"
You can't tell which radicals combine until you've simplified them. Judge too early, and you'll miss a match that was there all along.
