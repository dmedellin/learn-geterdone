title: The square root of x squared is not x

@art fold-to-positive
The square root of x squared is not x. Try minus five.

@math items="x = −5:  √((−5)²) = √25 = 5@minus five squared;;5 ≠ −5@not minus five"
Minus five squared is twenty five, and the square root of twenty five is five. Not minus five.

@words text="√(x²) = |x|"
The root sign always means the non negative root, so the square root of x squared is the absolute value of x.
