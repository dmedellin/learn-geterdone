title: Faster pacer, fewer words understood
---

@art fewer-understood
Read half again as fast, and you can take away half as much.

@math items="240 × 6/6 = 240@six of six;;360 × 2/6 = 120@two of six"
One reader got six of six at two forty words a minute, an effective rate of two forty. At three sixty, two of six: an effective rate of just one twenty.

@words text="Practise at the hold" sub="fastest pace with 4 of 6 or more"
So don't set the pacer as fast as you can follow. Practise at the fastest pace where you still get four of six.
