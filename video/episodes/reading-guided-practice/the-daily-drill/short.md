@art small-steps
One great reading shouldn't change your target. Here's the rule that stops it.

@math items="3 holds: 288 × 21/20 = 302.4@Three holds;;2 misses: 288 × 19/20 = 273.6@Two misses"
Three holds in a row, four or more right each time, raise the target by a twentieth: two eighty eight becomes three oh two point four. Two misses in a row lower it by the same, to two seventy three point six.

@words text="One good day can't move it far"
So the pace moves up only when it's been held more than once.
