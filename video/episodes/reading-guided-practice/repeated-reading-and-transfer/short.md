@art big-small-gain
Read a passage three times, and you'll gain a hundred words a minute. Mostly on that passage.

@math items="240 → 340: +100@a hundred;;new passage 248: +8@plus eight"
One reader went from two forty to three forty on the same passage, a gain of a hundred. On a new passage, two forty eight: plus eight.

@words text="The hundred belongs to the passage"
By the third reading, you know the text. Only the new passage measures you.
