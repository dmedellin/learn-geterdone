title: The pacer hit its target. So what?
---

@art line-highlight
Read behind a pacer and you'll hit its speed. That tells you almost nothing.

@words text="The highlight set the rate"
If you keep to the lit line, the reading ends when the highlight leaves the last line. So your paced rate comes out close to the target, almost by definition.

@words text="With the pace fixed, did you understand as much?"
The real question is comprehension. Compare effective rates, rate times the share of questions right.
