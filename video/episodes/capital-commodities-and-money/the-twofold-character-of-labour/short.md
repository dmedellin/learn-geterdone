@art more-coats-same-day
A tailor doubles his output. Why isn't his day worth any more?

@math items="10 h / 1 coat = 10 h a coat@one coat;;10 h / 2 coats = 5 h a coat@two coats"
His ten hour day used to make one coat, worth ten hours. Now it makes two coats, worth five hours each.

@words text="Same hours, same value"
Productivity changes how many coats an hour makes. It can't change the hour. So the day is still worth ten hours, just spread over more coats.
