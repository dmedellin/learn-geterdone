title: Why a goat is worth ten fish

@art robinson-ledger
Robinson Crusoe knows exactly why a goat is worth ten fish. The market doesn't.

@math items="goat: 10 h / 2 = 5 h@five hours;;fish: 15 h / 30 = ½ h@half an hour;;5 / ½ = 10 fish@ten fish"
His ledger says a goat takes five hours and a fish takes half an hour. So one goat equals ten fish.

@art village-trade
A village trading the same goods gets the same ratio. But there, nobody sees the hours. A goat just seems to fetch ten fish. Marx calls that fetishism.
