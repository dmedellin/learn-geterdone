@art debt-circle
Three hundred pounds of debts, and nobody needs a penny to settle them.

@math items="A owes B · B owes C · C owes A@a hundred pounds;;cash to settle: £0@nothing"
A owes B, B owes C, and C owes A, a hundred pounds each, all due today. Set them against each other and they cancel. The cash needed is nothing.

@art broken-chain
Now A fails to pay. B still owes C, with nothing coming in. Suddenly a hundred pounds must be found in hard cash.
