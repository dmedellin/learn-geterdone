@art barter-web
Six goods at a barter market need fifteen different prices.

@math items="6 · 5 / 2 = 15@fifteen;;6 − 1 = 5@five"
Every pair needs its own ratio: six times five over two is fifteen. With one commodity as everyone's equivalent, you need only six minus one, five.

@words text="By a social act"
But Marx's point is that nobody did this sum. The owners traded first, and their trading set one commodity apart as money.
