@art linen-equals-coat
Twenty yards of linen used to fetch one coat. Now they fetch two. What changed?

@math items="a coat: 20 h → 10 h@to ten;;20 h / 10 h = 2 coats@two coats"
Not the linen. Coats got cheaper to make, from twenty hours to ten. So the linen's twenty hours now buy two coats.

@words text="The ratio can't tell you which one moved"
A ratio alone can't say which side changed. Marx says that's why value isn't just a ratio.
