title: Does slow work make a thing worth more?

@art three-weavers
If value is labour time, does the slowest weaver make the most valuable cloth?

@math items="180 h / 100 yards = 9/5 h a yard@nine fifths;;C slows to 5 h: 220 / 100 = 11/5@eleven fifths"
Three weavers make a hundred yards in a hundred and eighty hours, so nine fifths of an hour a yard. Slow weaver C down to five hours a yard and the yard only rises to eleven fifths.

@words text="Socially necessary time" sub="not the hours you took"
His yards are worth the socially necessary time, the same as everyone's. Most of his extra hours count for nothing in his own cloth.
