@art four-moves
Eight pounds of goods can change hands with only two pounds in coin.

@math items="4 · £2 = £8@eight pounds;;£8 / 4 = £2@two pounds"
Wheat, linen, a Bible and brandy, two pounds each. That's eight pounds of prices. But the same two pounds makes four moves in a day.

@words text="Money needed = sum of prices / velocity"
So Marx's law: the money circulation needs is the sum of prices, divided by how often each coin moves.
