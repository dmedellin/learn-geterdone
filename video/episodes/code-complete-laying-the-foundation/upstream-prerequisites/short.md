@art cost-staircase
The same bug can cost a hundred times more, depending when you find it.

@list items="Requirements: 1@one to fix|System test: 10@ten in system test|After release: 10 to 100@ten to a hundred"
By the book's table, a requirements defect costs one to fix in requirements, ten in system test, and ten to a hundred after release.

@words text="Find it near where it was made"
The longer a defect stays, the more it touches. So catch it close to where it was made.
