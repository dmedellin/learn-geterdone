title: The one activity every software project does

@art activity-row
Rushed projects skip requirements and drop testing. What do they never skip?

@words text="Construction"
Construction. Detailed design, coding and debugging, unit testing and integration. Steve McConnell calls it the only activity guaranteed to happen on every project.

@words text="30 to 80%" sub="of a project's time"
It's also big. Depending on the size of the project, construction takes thirty to eighty percent of the total time.

@words text="Improve it, improve every project"
So if you get better at construction, every project you touch gets better too.
