title: Hume's farmers help each other with no promise and no kindness

@art two-fields
Two selfish farmers. One harvest, and both lose. Many, and both win.

@words text="One harvest: refuse, 1 each"
Hume's neighbours would each gain from the other's help. Played once, refusing pays more whatever the other does, so both refuse and each collects one.

@math items="12 × 3 = 36@thirty six"
Now play twelve seasons of tit for tat. A refusal gets answered, so both help every season, and each collects thirty six. Nobody promised, and nobody is kind. What changed is that there's a next season.
