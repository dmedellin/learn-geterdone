title: Trusting your partner makes the prisoner's dilemma worse

@art trust-bites
You're sure your partner will stay silent. In the prisoner's dilemma, that's when you should inform.

@math items="partner cooperates:  defect 5, cooperate 3@five beats three"
Against a cooperator, defecting pays five and cooperating pays three. Five beats three.

@words text="The dilemma is in the payoffs."
And against an informer, defecting pays one and cooperating zero. Defecting wins whatever you believe. So trust doesn't fix it. Only different payoffs do.
