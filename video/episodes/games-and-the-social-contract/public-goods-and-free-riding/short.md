@art pool
Give ten to help everyone, and you get back three.

@math items="10 → 30 in the pool@thirty;;30 ÷ 10 = 3 back to you@three"
Ten people, a shared pool that's tripled and split ten ways. Your ten becomes thirty, but only three returns to you.

@words text="Keeping pays 7 more, always"
So keeping your money pays seven more, whatever the others do. Everyone keeps, and each gets zero instead of twenty. The good would benefit everyone, and that isn't enough to get anyone to pay.
