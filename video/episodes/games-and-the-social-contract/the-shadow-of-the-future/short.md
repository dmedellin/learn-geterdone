title: Why a known last round kills cooperation
---

@art unravel
A hundred rounds of the prisoner's dilemma can't save cooperation.

@words text="Known last round: round 100"
If both players know round one hundred is the last, there's nothing left to punish in it. So both defect there, whatever happened before.

@art fading-rounds
Then round ninety-nine has no future either, and the argument runs back to round one. But a game that ends each round with probability one half lasts two rounds on average, and it can sustain cooperation. What counts is an uncertain end.
