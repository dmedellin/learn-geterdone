The biggest payoff in the table is in row L. That doesn't make row L your best move.

@math items="column plays R:  row L 0, row R 1@against column R"
If the other player is going right, row L pays zero against column R, and row R pays one.

@words text="Fix their move, then compare."
A best response answers one particular move. Fix what the other does, then compare your own numbers.
