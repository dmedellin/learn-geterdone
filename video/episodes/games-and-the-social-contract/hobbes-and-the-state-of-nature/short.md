title: The sovereign's fine that is never paid

@art sword
The best penalty is the one nobody ever pays.

@words text="Anarchy: attack dominates, 1 each"
In Hobbes's state of nature, attacking a peaceful neighbour pays five against three. So both attack, and each gets one.

@math items="attack on peace:  5 − 4 = 1@minus four;;peace dominates: 3 each@three each"
Now a sovereign fines every attack by four. Attacking a peaceful neighbour pays five minus four, just one. Peace dominates, both keep it, three each, and nobody is fined. A threat that works costs nothing to carry out.
