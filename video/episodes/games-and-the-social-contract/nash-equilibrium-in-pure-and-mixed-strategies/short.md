@art indifference-balance
In a mixed equilibrium, you don't randomise to be unpredictable.

@words text="q = 9/10"
In chicken, each driver swerves nine times in ten. That's exactly the mix that leaves the other driver indifferent between swerving and going straight.

@words text="Your mix comes from their payoffs."
So your probabilities are fixed by the other player's numbers, not your own. At that mix, they have no better move to make.
