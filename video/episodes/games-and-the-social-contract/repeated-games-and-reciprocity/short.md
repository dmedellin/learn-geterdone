@art never-ahead
Tit for tat never beats anyone. It still nearly wins the tournament.

@words text="Cooperate first, then copy"
It cooperates first, then copies your last move. So it can only tie or lose a match.

@math items="TFT:  184@a hundred and eighty four;;ALLD:  146@a hundred and forty six"
But cooperators score thirty against each other, and defectors only ten. Across the field, tit for tat scores a hundred and eighty four. Always defect, which beats or ties everyone, gets a hundred and forty six.
