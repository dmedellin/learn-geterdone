title: Cooperators earn more together, and still die out
---

@art two-populations
A world of cooperators earns three times as much. Defectors take it anyway.

@words text="All cooperators: 3 each · all defectors: 1 each"
In the prisoner's dilemma, a population of cooperators averages three each, and a population of defectors averages one.

@math items="from 1/2:  to 1/3@a third"
But selection compares types inside one population, and defectors out-earn cooperators at every share. Start half and half, and a generation later only a third cooperate. What spreads need not be good for the group.
