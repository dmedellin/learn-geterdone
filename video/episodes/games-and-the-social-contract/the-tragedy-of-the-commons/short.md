title: The tragedy of the commons doesn't need greedy people
---

@art pasture
Ten herders ruin a pasture, and not one of them is greedy.

@math items="Lone adder:  22 vs 12@twenty two;;Everyone adds:  4 each, not 12@four"
Each can add a second animal. Add while the nine others restrain, and you get twenty two instead of twelve. But when everyone adds, each gets four, where restraint would give twelve.

@words text="The gain is private; the loss is shared"
The gain is private, and the loss is shared. That asymmetry, not greed, is the whole tragedy.
