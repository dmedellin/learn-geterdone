@art stag-and-hare
Hunt a stag together for four each, or a hare alone for a sure three?

@math items="stag:  4q@four q;;hare:  3@hare pays three"
If q is your confidence the partner joins, the stag pays four q. The hare pays three, whatever happens.

@words text="Stag only if q ≥ 3/4"
So the better outcome needs you to be three quarters sure of your partner. On an even chance, take the hare.
