@art tournament
Finding the second largest of eight numbers takes about twice the work of the largest? No.

@words text="It must have lost to the winner"
The second largest only ever lost to the maximum. So run a knockout tournament, seven comparisons, and the winner has beaten just three others.

@math items="7 + 2 = 9@two more"
The best of those three costs two more. Nine in all, and no algorithm can do better.
