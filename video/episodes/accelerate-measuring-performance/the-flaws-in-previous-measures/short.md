title: Why a busier team gets slower

@art queue-curve
Same person, same two hour review. Why does it take fifty times longer?

@math items="50% busy → 4 h@fifty percent;;90% busy → 20 h@ninety;;99% busy → 200 h@ninety nine"
In the simplest queueing model, the review's lead time is four hours at fifty percent busy, twenty at ninety, and two hundred at ninety nine.

@words text="The work doesn't slow. It waits."
Nobody got slower. The review just arrives while another is being done, more and more often.
