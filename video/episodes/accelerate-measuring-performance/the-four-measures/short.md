Is a fast team an unstable one? Accelerate measures both, with four numbers.

@list items="Lead time@Lead time|Deployment frequency@deployment frequency|Time to restore service@time to restore|Change fail rate@change fail rate"
Lead time and deployment frequency are the tempo. Time to restore service and change fail rate are the stability.

@words text="A global outcome, not output"
All four describe what the delivery achieves as a whole, so no team wins by making another lose.
