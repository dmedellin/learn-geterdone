@art virtuous-cycle
Do good product practices drive delivery, or does delivery drive them? Accelerate found both.

@words text="2016: product → delivery · 2017: delivery → product"
One year's model had Lean product practices predict delivery performance. The next year's flipped model had delivery predict the practices.

@words text="A virtuous cycle"
Combined across the years, the authors call it a reciprocal model: a virtuous cycle, from two years of analysis. It's prediction in a survey, not proof of cause.
