@art three-together
A WIP limit on its own? Accelerate found it doesn't strongly predict delivery performance.

@list items="Limit work in progress@Limit|Show the work and quality to everyone@show|Feed production data back to the team@feed"
The strong effect came with all three: limit work in progress, show the work and its quality to everyone, and feed production monitoring back to the team.

@words text="A limit must expose obstacles you remove"
And a limit only counts when it exposes obstacles the team then removes.
