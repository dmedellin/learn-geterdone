Same twelve days of work. First feedback on day five, or day fourteen?

@math items="one batch of 12: day 14@one batch;;four batches of 3: day 5@four batches"
With a two day release, one batch of twelve is out on day fourteen. Four batches of three get the first one out on day five.

@words text="But the last batch lands on day 20"
The catch: four releases cost more, unless the release itself gets cheap.
