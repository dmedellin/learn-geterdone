@art peer-architects
Good objects have narrow jobs. Evans says good developers shouldn't.

@quote text="Make the objects into specialists; let the developers be generalists." cite="Evans, Domain-Driven Design, Chapter 17"
Make the objects into specialists; let the developers be generalists.

@words text="Architects write code" sub="developers play with frameworks"
The narrow responsibilities that make a good object are no model for a team. So architects write application code, and developers play with frameworks. And strategy grows out of application work, with feedback, not from on high.
