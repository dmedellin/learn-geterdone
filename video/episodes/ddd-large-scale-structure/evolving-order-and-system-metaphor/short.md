title: The firewall metaphor, and what it hid

@art firewall
A firewall is a great metaphor. It also hides half the threats.

@words text="A barrier at the edge"
The picture made a whole product category easy to grasp. But it suggested a wall at the edge, so it said nothing about threats from inside.

@art metaphor-misleads
That's Evans's warning about a System Metaphor. Use one only if it's compelling, keep it under review, and drop it the moment it misleads.
