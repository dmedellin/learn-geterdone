A plug in framework cuts your translators from sixty six to twelve. There's a catch.

@math items="12 apps pairwise:  66 translators@sixty six;;through one core:  12@twelve"
Twelve applications integrated pair by pair need sixty six translators. Plugged into one abstract core, twelve.

@art core-change-reaches-all
But change the core's interfaces, and every component changes at once. So the core stops evolving. Evans says it's neither the first structure to apply, nor the second.
