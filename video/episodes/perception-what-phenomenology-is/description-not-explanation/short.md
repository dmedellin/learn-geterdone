@art moon-over-roofs
By the numbers, the moon overhead is bigger than the moon at the horizon.

@math items="horizon: 3474 / 384400 ≈ 0.0090 rad@at the horizon;;overhead: 3474 / 378029 ≈ 0.0092 rad@overhead"
Size over distance gives the angle: about nought point nought nine radians at the horizon, and about nought point nought nine two overhead.

@words text="Describe, don't explain"
Those figures are right, and they're a map. The huge moon over the roofs is the countryside. Merleau-Ponty asks you to describe that first.
