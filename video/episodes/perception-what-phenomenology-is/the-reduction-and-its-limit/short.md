@art wire-cube
Which face of this cube is in front? The drawing doesn't say.

@math items="reading A: 12 edges@twelve edges;;reading B: the same 12 segments@the same twelve"
A wire cube draws twelve edges. Swap the front and back squares, and it draws the same twelve segments. Two solids, one drawing.

@art slack-threads
So the front was your contribution. Merleau-Ponty's reduction works like that: it slackens the threads tying you to the world, so you can see them.
