@art cereal-box
You've never seen a cereal box whole. Neither has the person across the table.

@words text="Front · back · the top you share"
You see the front, they see the back, you both see the top. You don't check their report against a view from nowhere. There isn't one.

@art meshing-gears
For Merleau-Ponty, that's rationality: perspectives blend, perceptions confirm each other, a meaning emerges. Views that mesh like gears, with nothing above them.
