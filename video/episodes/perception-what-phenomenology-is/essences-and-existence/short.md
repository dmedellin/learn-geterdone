title: A melody that shares no notes, and is still the same tune
---

@art melody-transposed
Change every note of a melody, and it's still the same tune.

@math items="C E G → D F♯ A@C, E, G;;core: major third up, minor third up@major third"
Play C, E, G, then D, F sharp, A. No note survives. What stays is the steps: a major third up, then a minor third up.

@art fishing-net
That's what Merleau-Ponty means by an essence. It's a relation you reach by going through the cases, a net thrown to bring the living thing back up.
