title: The waterfall rent that steam can wipe out
---

@art steam-and-water
A factory on a waterfall earns a rent. Cheaper steam can make it vanish overnight.

@math items="price set by steam: 115@a hundred and fifteen;;water user: 115 − 90 − 15 = 10@ten"
In Marx's figures, steam factories set the price at a hundred and fifteen. The waterfall factory costs only ninety, so it makes ten above the average. Its owner can take that as rent.

@words text="Steam cheapens → surplus 0"
Cut the steam users' cost to ninety, and the price falls to match. The surplus is gone, though the waterfall hasn't changed.
