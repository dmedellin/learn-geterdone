@art closed-gate
Would a landlord lend his worst field for nothing? Marx says: by no means.

@math items="agriculture's value: 125@a hundred and twenty five;;price of production: 120@a hundred and twenty;;absolute rent: up to 5@up to five"
In Marx's figures, farming employs more labour than industry, so its product is worth a hundred and twenty five, while averaging gives it a price of only a hundred and twenty. The landlord can claim up to five.

@words text="Without a price above value"
That's absolute rent: paid by the worst soil, without selling corn above its value.
