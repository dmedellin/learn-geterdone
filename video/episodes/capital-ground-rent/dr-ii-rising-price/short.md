@art worse-than-a
The worst field farmed pays no rent. Until something worse is needed.

@math items="new regulator A−2: 120s a quarter@a hundred and twenty;;A: 120 − 60 = 60s rent@sixty"
In Marx's example, the last quarter the market needs comes from a soil worse than A, at a hundred and twenty shillings. Now A's quarter, costing sixty, sells for a hundred and twenty. A pays sixty in rent.

@words text="Rent follows the price"
The price didn't rise because of rent. Rent rose because of the price.
