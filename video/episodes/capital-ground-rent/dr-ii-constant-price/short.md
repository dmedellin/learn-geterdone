title: The rent that rises even as returns diminish
---

@art smaller-second
A second dose of capital yields less than the first, and the rent still goes up.

@math items="first dose: 2 · 60 − 60 = 60s@sixty;;second dose: (3/2) · 60 − 60 = 30s@thirty;;rent per acre: 60 → 90s@ninety"
On Marx's soil B, the first dose yields two quarters at sixty shillings: sixty of surplus. A second yields only one and a half: thirty more. The rent rises from sixty to ninety.

@words text="Rent rises, not in proportion"
As long as each dose beats the worst soil, the landlord's rent per acre rises.
