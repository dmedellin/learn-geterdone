@art more-on-d
The price of wheat halves. In Marx's table, the landlords' rent stays exactly the same.

@math items="price: 60s → 30s@thirty;;total rent: 360s → 360s@three hundred and sixty"
The worst soil drops out, and the price falls from sixty shillings to thirty. But with five doses of capital on the best soil, the total rent is three hundred and sixty shillings, just as before.

@words text="Half the price, twice the surplus grain"
Half the price, twice the surplus quarters. A falling price decides nothing on its own.
