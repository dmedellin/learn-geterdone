@art four-fields
The worst field farmed decides what every field's wheat sells for.

@math items="A: 60s for 1 quarter → 60s a quarter@sixty shillings;;D: 4 · 60 − 60 = 180s rent@a hundred and eighty"
In Marx's table, the worst soil grows one quarter for sixty shillings of cost and profit. So wheat sells at sixty a quarter. The best soil grows four, and pays a hundred and eighty in rent.

@words text="Unequal soils, not worse ones"
Marx's twist on Ricardo: rent needs unequal soils. It doesn't need farming to get worse.
