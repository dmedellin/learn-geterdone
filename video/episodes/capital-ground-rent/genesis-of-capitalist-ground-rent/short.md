title: The serf could see his unpaid days

@art split-week
A serf could see his unpaid labour. It was the days he spent on the lord's field.

@math items="2 days for the lord, 4 his own: 2/4 = 50%@two days;;wage-worker: 6 of 12 hours = 100%@six hours"
Two days a week for the lord and four for himself is a rate of fifty percent. On Marx's accounting, a factory worker who replaces his wage in six hours of twelve gives a hundred percent.

@words text="Same substance, different form"
The difference is that in the factory, nothing marks which hours are which.
