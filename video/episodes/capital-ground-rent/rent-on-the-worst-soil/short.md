@art cheapest-wins
The worst field in use can pay rent, even with nothing worse being farmed.

@math items="extra quarter on B: 70s@seventy;;A's quarter: 70 − 60 = 10s rent@ten shillings"
In Marx's example, the cheapest extra quarter comes from another dose on a better soil, at seventy shillings. That sets the price. A's quarter costs sixty, so A earns ten shillings of rent.

@words text="A better field can set the price"
The price is set by the cheapest source of the last quarter, wherever it lies.
