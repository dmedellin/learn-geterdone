@art four-doses
Spread capital over four fields, or pile it on one. Marx gets the same rent.

@math items="four doses on one acre: 4, 3, 2, 1 quarters@four doses;;price 60s · rent 360s@three hundred and sixty"
Put four doses of fifty shillings on one acre, yielding four, three, two and one quarters. The last dose sets the price at sixty shillings. The rent is three hundred and sixty, exactly what four soils side by side gave.

@words text="Same sums. Different lease."
What differs is who collects it, and when the landlord can claim it.
