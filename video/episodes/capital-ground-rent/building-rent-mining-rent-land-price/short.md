@art town-reaches-field
A field at the edge of town triples in price. Its owner hasn't lifted a finger.

@math items="£50 a year at 5%: £1,000@a thousand;;£150 a year at 5%: £3,000@three thousand"
In the lesson's illustration, the plot rents for fifty pounds a year: at five percent, a thousand pounds. When the streets reach it, its rent is a hundred and fifty, and its price three thousand.

@words text="The town confers the location"
Marx's point: a site's rent comes from the town growing around it, not from the owner.
