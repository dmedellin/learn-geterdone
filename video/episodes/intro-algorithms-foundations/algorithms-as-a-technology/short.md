@art two-machines
A computer two hundred and fifty times faster still loses by forty five times.

@math items="A: 3n² → 15,000 s@fifteen thousand seconds;;B: 60n lg n → about 333.8 s@six minutes"
Sorting five million keys, the fast machine running three n squared takes fifteen thousand seconds. The slow machine running sixty n log n takes under six minutes.

@words text="Order of growth beats hardware"
Past the crossover, the better order of growth wins, and the gap only grows.
