@art many-windows
A healthy service at one percent errors. A two percent alert. A hundred and four pages a week.

@math items="P(X ≥ 5 of 200) = 5.175%@five point one seven five;;× 2 016 windows = 104.32 a week@two thousand and sixteen"
With two hundred requests a window, five errors happens by chance five point one seven five percent of the time. Over two thousand and sixteen five minute windows a week, that's a hundred and four false pages.

@words text="Widen the window before you raise the threshold"
Bigger samples vary less.
