It's just one more label. Here's what that sentence costs.

@math items="40 × 120 × 6 × 30 × 4 = 3 456 000 series@three million;;× user_id (10 000) = 34 560 000 000 series@ten thousand"
Five ordinary labels multiply to three million four hundred and fifty six thousand series. Add a user id with ten thousand values, and it's thirty four and a half billion. Three hundred and ninety eight terabytes a day.
