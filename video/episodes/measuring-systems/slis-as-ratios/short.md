@art error-bar
Ninety nine point nine five percent, against a ninety nine point nine objective. Met? You can't tell.

@math items="SE = √(p(1 − p)/n) ≈ 0.0707%@the square root;;gap = 0.05% = 0.71 SE@point seven one"
Over a thousand requests, the error bar is the square root of p times one minus p over n, about point oh seven percent. The gap to the objective is point oh five. That's point seven one standard errors, inside the noise.

@words text="7 996 requests to resolve it at 2 SE"
You'd need almost eight thousand requests to tell.
