@words text="A quarantined lot got through"
A school asked a seed bank for garden seed, and a quarantined lot got through.

@art lost-clause
The request was written as a brand new rule. It kept germination and age, and lost the quarantine clause the bank's rule always carries.

@art rule-three-forms
Evans's point: validating, selecting and building to order are one rule. Write it once, as a specification, and it can't drift.
