@art swamped-bill
Is this bill delinquent? That one true or false method can swamp the whole bill with rules.

@art spec-object
Evans and Fowler's answer is a specification: the rule as its own small value object, asked whether another object satisfies it.

@words text="Drying, not discarding"
A seed bank's rule tells it not just that a lot fails, but why. Lot two fails only on moisture. It needs drying, not discarding.
