@art remainder-bars
Does high rent make food expensive? Adam Smith said it's the other way round.

@words text="Wages and profit: causes of price · Rent: its effect"
High or low wages and profit are the causes of high or low price. High or low rent is the effect of it.

@math items="corn at £2:  total rent £140@two pounds;;corn at £3/2:  total rent £50@one pound ten"
On the lesson's three farms, corn at two pounds a quarter leaves a hundred and forty in rent. At one pound ten, only fifty. The outlay didn't move. The rent took the whole fall.
