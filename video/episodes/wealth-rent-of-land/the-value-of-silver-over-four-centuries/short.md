title: When wheat got cheaper, Adam Smith said silver got dearer
---

@art coin-and-sacks
Between 1350 and 1500, the price of wheat in England halved. Adam Smith said wheat wasn't the thing that changed.

@words text="4 oz of silver → 2 oz a quarter"
A quarter of wheat went from about four ounces of silver to two. Smith's reading: silver had grown dearer in corn, because corn, the labourer's food, is the steadier measure.

@art corn-vs-cattle
Corn is a necessary. Silver is only a superfluity.
