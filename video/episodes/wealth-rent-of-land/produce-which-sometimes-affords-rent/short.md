title: How one rich coal mine sets every mine's price

@art best-undersells
One rich coal mine can wipe out its neighbours' rent, without anyone changing a law.

@words text="The most fertile coal mine regulates the price"
Adam Smith said the most fertile mine regulates the price of coals at all the others near it. By slightly underselling, it gets more rent and profit, and the rest must follow.

@math items="price £1 a ton:  total rent £130@a pound a ton;;price £3/4 a ton:  total rent £42.50@three quarters"
On the lesson's four mines, a pound a ton gives a hundred and thirty in rent. At three quarters of a pound, forty two fifty.
