@art ox-and-lamb
More silver came into Europe than gold, by twenty two times. So why wasn't gold worth twenty two times as much?

@math items="ox:  10 guineas = 210s@ten guineas;;lamb:  3s 6d = 3.5s@three and six;;210 / 3.5 = 60@sixty"
Adam Smith's answer: an ox at ten guineas is two hundred and ten shillings, a lamb three and six. That's sixty to one. Nobody thinks there are sixty lambs in the market for every ox.

@words text="Cheap goods sell in greater quantity"
More buyers can afford the cheaper thing.
