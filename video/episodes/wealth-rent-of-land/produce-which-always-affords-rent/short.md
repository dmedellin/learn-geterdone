@art new-road
Adam Smith called good roads the greatest of all improvements. Here's the arithmetic.

@math items="distant farm:  200 − 1.1 · (70 + 30) = £90@distant farm;;after the road:  200 − 1.1 · (55 + 30) = £106.50@after the road"
In the lesson's figures, a distant farm pays twenty pounds a year to carriers, and its rent is ninety. After the road, carriage falls to five, and the rent rises to a hundred and six fifty.

@words text="Remote land, nearer the market"
Roads put remote land more nearly level with land near the town.
