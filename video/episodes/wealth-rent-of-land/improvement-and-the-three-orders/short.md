@art three-orders
Adam Smith had a warning about proposals from business.

@quote text="To widen the market, and to narrow the competition, is always the interest of the dealers." cite="Adam Smith, The Wealth of Nations, Book I, Chapter 11, Conclusion"
To widen the market, and to narrow the competition, is always the interest of the dealers.

@words text="With the most suspicious attention"
Widening the market may suit the public. Narrowing competition only lets dealers raise profits, an absurd tax on everyone else. So any new law they propose, Smith says, should be examined with the most suspicious attention.
