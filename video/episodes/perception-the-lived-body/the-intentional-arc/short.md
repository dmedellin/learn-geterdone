@art mosquito-ruler
A mosquito on your skin, or a ruler on the same spot. Almost the same stimulus.

@words text="Scratch: yes · Point: no"
Schneider could scratch the bite at once, but not point to the spot the doctor touched. Reflexes can't explain that, and neither can a lost power of thought.

@art arc-limp
Merleau-Ponty's answer is an intentional arc, a body aimed at its world, and in illness it goes limp. Consciousness is first not I think that, but I can.
