@art pipe
How do you know where your hand is when you hold a pipe?

@words text="Not by adding up angles"
Not by adding up angles, hand to forearm, forearm to arm, arm to trunk. You know where the pipe is, and from that, where your hand is.

@art desk-comet
Merleau-Ponty calls it a spatiality of situation, not position. Your body's space is organised by the task, like hands leaning on a desk with the rest trailing behind.
