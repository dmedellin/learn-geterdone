@art cane-tip
Where does a blind man feel the kerb? In his hand, or at the tip of his stick?

@words text="The tip of the stick"
Merleau-Ponty says the stick has stopped being an object. Its tip has become an area of sensitivity. Its length isn't a middle term he calculates with.

@art feather-hat
Habit works like that. A woman keeps her feathered hat clear of a doorway without any calculation. She feels where the feather is, as she feels her hand.
