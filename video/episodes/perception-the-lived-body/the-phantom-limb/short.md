title: Is a phantom limb in the nerves or in the mind?
---

@art phantom-outline
Cut the nerves, and a phantom limb disappears. So it's in the nerves?

@words text="An emotion can bring one back"
But an emotion, or a reminder of the wound, can make a phantom appear. So it's in the mind?

@art no-common-ground
Merleau-Ponty says you can't just add the two. Nerves are in space, and feelings are nowhere. They have no ground to combine on. The phantom needs a third term: being in the world.
