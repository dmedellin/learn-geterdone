@art house-views
Where do you have to stand to see a house as it really is?

@words text="Is not to see always to see from somewhere?"
Objective thought says nowhere: the house is all its perspectives at once. Merleau-Ponty asks: isn't to see always to see from somewhere?

@math items="four views → 5 of 6 faces@four views"
Even four views round the house reach five of six faces, and never the pipes. More views give a better set of views, never the house in its plenitude.
