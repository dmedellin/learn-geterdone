@art piano-and-cup
He knows his arm is gone. So why does a cup still call to it?

@words text="Habit-body · body at this moment"
Merleau-Ponty says the body has two layers. The habit body still holds the movements you've always made. The body at this moment can only do what it now can.

@art reach-arcs
A cup within the old reach and outside the new one appeals to a hand which I no longer have. That's where the absence is felt.
