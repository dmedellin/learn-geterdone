title: You can walk round a box. Not round your body.
---

@art box-walked-round
Walk round a box, and you can see all six faces.

@math items="box walked round: 6 of 6@six;;your own body: 3 of 6@three"
Try it with your own body. From your own eyes, you get the front and the sides, three of six. And walking doesn't help, because the eyes go with you.

@words text="Never in front of me, but with me"
For Merleau-Ponty, that's because your body isn't an object at all. It's the place from which there are objects.
