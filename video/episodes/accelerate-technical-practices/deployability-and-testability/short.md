Can a mainframe team be high performing? The system type isn't the point.

@words text="Testability · Deployability"
What went with high performance was testing most work without an integrated environment, and releasing without waiting for other services.

@words text="Loosely coupled, on any platform"
Those come from loose coupling, which the authors say you can get on packaged software and old mainframes, and miss on the newest services in containers.
