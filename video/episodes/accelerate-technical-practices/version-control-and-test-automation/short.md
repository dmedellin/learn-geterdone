title: Who writes your tests matters more than you think

@art developer-tests
Tests written by a separate QA team didn't go with better delivery. Surprised?

@words text="Developers own the tests → did well"
In Accelerate's survey, teams whose developers chiefly create and maintain the acceptance tests did well. Tests made mainly by QA or an outsourcer weren't correlated with performance.

@words text="Testers still matter"
That's not a case against testers. They explore, and help build the suites alongside developers.
