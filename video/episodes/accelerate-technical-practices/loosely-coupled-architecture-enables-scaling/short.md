title: Does adding developers slow everyone down?
---

@art scaling-trends
Add developers and each one slows down, says the orthodox view. Not always.

@list items="Low performers: fewer per developer@Low performers|Medium: the same@medium|High: significantly more@high performers"
As teams grew, low performers deployed less often per developer, medium performers just as often, and high performers significantly more often.

@words text="Loosely coupled teams and systems"
The authors credit a modular, loosely coupled architecture, a generative culture, continuous delivery and good leadership. It's a pattern in a survey, not a law.
