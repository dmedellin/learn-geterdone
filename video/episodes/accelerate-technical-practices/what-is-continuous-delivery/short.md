@art small-batches
Smaller batches can finish later. Here's why, and the fix.

@math items="1 batch of 24: out at hour 26@one batch;;4 batches of 6: last at hour 32@four batches"
Twenty four changes, an hour each, and a two hour release. One batch is out at hour twenty six. Four batches of six bring feedback sooner, but the last leaves at hour thirty two, because you pay for four releases.

@words text="15-minute release: last at hour 25"
Automate the release down to a quarter of an hour, and the last change is out at hour twenty five.
