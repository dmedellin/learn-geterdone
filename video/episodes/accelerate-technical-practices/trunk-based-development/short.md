How long should a branch live? Accelerate's answer is under a day.

@list items="Fewer than three active branches@fewer than three|Merged within a day@within a day|No code freezes@code freeze"
Teams that did well had fewer than three active branches, merged each one into trunk within a day, and never had a code freeze.

@words text="Any size, any industry"
The authors say that held regardless of team size, organization size or industry. It's a correlation in their survey, not a guarantee.
