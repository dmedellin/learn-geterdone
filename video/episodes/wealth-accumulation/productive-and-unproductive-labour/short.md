@art crowned-and-masked
Adam Smith put kings, judges and opera singers in the same box.

@art workbench-and-tray
His test wasn't usefulness. A workman's labour fixes itself in a thing that can be sold, and its price can later set an equal quantity of labour to work. A servant's service perishes the instant it's done.

@words text="Does something remain to be exchanged later?"
The sovereign, the army, the physician and the player all leave nothing behind to exchange. So they're unproductive, however honourable the work.
