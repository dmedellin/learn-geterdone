title: How a thousand pounds makes three thousand of loans

@art three-loans
A thousand pounds of coin can carry three thousand pounds of loans.

@words text="A → W · B → X · C → Y"
A lends a thousand to W, who buys from B. B lends the same pieces to X, who buys from C. And C lends them to Y.

@art deed-and-goods
What's lent each time isn't the coin, Smith says. It's the money's worth, the goods it buys. The money is only the deed of assignment.
