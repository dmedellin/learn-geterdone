@art two-tables
What you save gets eaten, just as surely as what you spend.

@words text="The consumption is the same, but the consumers are different."
The spender feeds idle guests and servants, who leave nothing behind. The saver employs labourers, who reproduce what they consume, with a profit.

@art diverging-paths
So saving isn't hoarding. It's capital put to work, and the difference compounds, year after year.
