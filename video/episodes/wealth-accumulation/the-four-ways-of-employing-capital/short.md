title: Why Adam Smith ranked the farm above the factory

@art nature-works
Adam Smith thought a farmer's capital had a partner no factory has: Nature.

@words text="Nature's share: the landlord's rent"
In farming, Nature labours along with man. Her work is the rent, seldom less than a fourth, and often more than a third, of the whole produce.

@art hands-bars
In manufactures, man does all. So Smith ranks farm, factory, wholesale, then shop, by the labour an equal capital sets going.
