@art fast-and-slow
Adam Smith said home trade supports twenty four times more industry. Here's the sum.

@math items="4 / (1/3) = 12@four returns;;12 · 2 = 24@Twelve times two"
Home capital can make four returns a year. Foreign capital, once in three years. That's twelve. And a home trade replaces two home capitals each time, a foreign trade only one. Twelve times two is twenty four.

@words text="Foreign returns once a year → 8"
Change the returns, and the ratio changes. It's an assumption, not a law.
