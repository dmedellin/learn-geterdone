title: Why Fare plus Fare beats long plus long
---

@art closed-chain
Add two whole numbers and you get a whole number. Your design can do that too.

@math items="real × real → real@multiplying;;Fare.plus(Fare) → Fare@a plus that takes"
Multiplying two real numbers never leaves the set. That's closure. So give Fare a plus that takes a Fare and returns a Fare.

@words text="A rich interface, no new concept"
Fare plus Fare says more than long plus long, and it costs a reader nothing more. It brings in no other concept, and it chains.
