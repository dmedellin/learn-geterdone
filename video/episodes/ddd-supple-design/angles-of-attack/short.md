@art carve-module
You can't make a whole system supple. Evans says don't try.

@words text="One area, really supple"
A big impact on one area is worth more than effort spread thin. So pick a target, like the arithmetic hiding in your model, and carve it into a module of its own.

@art dependencies-inward
In the lesson's bakery, that carve removes a cycle. Everything else uses the new module, and it depends on nothing.
