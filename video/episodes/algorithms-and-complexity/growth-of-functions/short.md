@art wall
Make your computer a thousand times faster. Your exponential algorithm gains ten items.

@math items="linear:  1000 × more data@linear;;quadratic:  about 32 × more@quadratic;;exponential:  10 more items@exponential"
With that speed up, a linear algorithm handles a thousand times more data. A quadratic one, about thirty two times more. An exponential one, just ten more items.

@words text="The wall moves. It doesn't disappear."
Hardware moves the wall. Only a better algorithm removes it.
