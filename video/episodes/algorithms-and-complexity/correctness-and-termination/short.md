@words text="Stop at 1"
Start anywhere, repeat, and stop when you reach one. It has stopped for every starting value ever tested, beyond ten to the twenty.

@art two-locks
But nobody has proved it always will. If it stops, the answer's right, that part is trivial. Whether it stops is an open problem. That's why correctness and termination are two separate proofs.
