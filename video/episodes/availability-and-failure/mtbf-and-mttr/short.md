@art rare-vs-frequent
A machine that fails eighty seven times a year beats one that fails once.

@math items="yearly, 1 day to fix:  365/366 = 99.73%@once a year;;every 100 h, 6 min to fix:  1000/1001 = 99.90%@every hundred hours"
Fail once a year and take a day to fix it, and you get two nines. Fail every hundred hours but fix it in six minutes, and you get three.

@words text="Availability counts seconds, not failures"
Availability counts seconds, not failures. Faster repair is the cheaper lever.
