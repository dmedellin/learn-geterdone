Two regions, each three nines. On paper, that's six nines. In reality, it's four.

@math items="ideal:  31.54 s down a year@on paper;;4 failovers × 90 s = 6 min a year@ninety seconds"
On paper, you're down thirty one and a half seconds a year. But four regional failovers at ninety seconds each add six minutes a year.

@words text="The failover is 92% of the outage"
The switching is ninety two percent of the outage. Cut the cutover before you buy another region.
