Ten services, each at ninety nine point nine percent. Together they're not three nines.

@math items="(999/1000)¹⁰ = 99.00449%@to the tenth"
A request needs all ten, so the availabilities multiply. Nine nine nine over a thousand, to the tenth, is ninety nine point oh oh four percent. Two nines.

@words text="9.96× the downtime of any one link"
That's nearly ten times the downtime of any single link. A chain is worse than its weakest link.
