@art one-in-eighteen
Know two thousand eight hundred words? You may still miss one word in eighteen.

@words text="94.3%" sub="a page of the novel, with names"
On a page of Pride and Prejudice, the list covers ninety-four point three percent, names included.

@math items="100 − 94.3 = 5.7@A hundred minus;;100 ÷ 5.7 ≈ 18@divided by"
A hundred minus ninety-four point three is five point seven. A hundred divided by five point seven is about eighteen. An easy read needs one in fifty.
