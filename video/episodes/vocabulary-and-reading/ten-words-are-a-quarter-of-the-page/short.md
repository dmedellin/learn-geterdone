@art quarter-page
Ten words fill a quarter of a page. None of them names a thing.

@words text="I · she · you · and · the · to · of · was · it · that" sub="25.6% of the page"
On a page of Pride and Prejudice, the top ten are I, she, you, and, the, to, of, was, it and that.

@words text="the frame, not the subject"
They hold the sentence together. The words that say what the page is about are in the other three quarters.
