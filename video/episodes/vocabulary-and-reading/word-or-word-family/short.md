Is delightful a new word, or just delight with an ending?

@words text="a flemma: walk · walks · walked · walking" sub="the word list counts these"
A flemma is a word with its endings: walk, walks, walked, walking. The word list counts flemmas.

@words text="a family: delight · delightful" sub="94.3% → 95.6% on the passage"
A family adds the words made from it, like delight and delightful. Nation's ninety-eight percent counts families. On the passage, that lifts the share from ninety-four point three to ninety-five point six percent.
