@art same-shape
Three classes. Each holds an object and forwards to it. Which pattern is each?

@words text="Adapter · Proxy · Decorator"
An adapter, a proxy, and a decorator. Nothing in the code tells them apart.

@quote text="The Adapter pattern makes things work after they're designed; Bridge makes them work before they are." cite="Design Patterns, Chapter 4"
The book puts it like this. The Adapter pattern makes things work after they're designed. Bridge makes them work before they are.

@end text="Full lesson on Learn"
It's the intent that differs. The full lesson is on Learn.
