title: This risk plan has a limit that never works
---

@art unreachable-cap
One percent per trade. Four positions at most. A five percent open risk cap. Spot the problem?

@math items="4 × 1% = 4% < 5%@four positions"
Four positions at one percent can only ever commit four percent. So the five percent cap is never reached. It isn't wrong, but it isn't doing anything.

@words text="Lower the cap, or change the count"
Resolve it before trading: lower the cap to four percent, or raise the position count.
