@art magnify
Price moves just five percent against you. Your account drops twenty percent. How?

@math items="4 × 5% = 20%@four times five"
Leverage. Four times five percent is twenty. At four times leverage, you hold four times your equity in the position. So a five percent move in the underlying is a twenty percent move in your account.

@words text="Buying power is not a risk limit"
That's why buying power is a broker constraint, not a risk target.
