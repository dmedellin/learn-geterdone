@art partial
Half your position exits at plus two R. The other half is stopped at minus one R. What did you make?

@math items="0.5 × 2R − 0.5 × 1R = +0.5R@half of plus two"
Not plus one R. Each half carries half the risk. So it's half of plus two R, minus half of one R. Plus point five R.

@words text="Average by size, don't add"
Partial exits are averaged by size, not added.
