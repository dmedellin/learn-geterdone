title: High ATR doesn't mean the market is going up
---

@art up-down-same
Average true range just doubled. Is the market about to go up, or down?

@words text="ATR has no direction"
Neither. A T R measures how far price is travelling, not which way. An advance and a decline with the same bar sizes give the same A T R.

@art wider-fewer
What it does change is size. A wider stop for the same dollar risk means fewer shares.
