@art steep-vs-shallow
How many losses in a row would cut your account in half?

@math items="5%: 0.95¹⁴ = 0.488 → 14 losses@five percent;;1%: 0.99⁶⁹ = 0.50 → 69 losses@one percent"
At five percent of current equity per trade, fourteen. At one percent, sixty nine.

@words text="Risk % sets how ordinary a streak can end you"
Fourteen losses in a row can happen to a perfectly good strategy. Your risk percentage sets how ordinary a streak has to be to end the account.
