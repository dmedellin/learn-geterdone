@art recovery
Your account is down twenty percent. Isn't a twenty percent gain enough to get back?

@math items="$80,000 × 1.20 = $96,000@ninety six thousand;;0.20 ÷ 0.80 = 25%@twenty five percent"
No. From a hundred thousand down to eighty thousand, a twenty percent gain only gets you to ninety six thousand. The recovery is measured from the smaller balance: point two over point eight, twenty five percent.
