@art recovery
You lose half your account. What gain gets you back to where you started?

@words text="Not 50%"
Fifty percent? No. Fifty percent of the half that's left is only a quarter of the original.

@math items="½ × 2 = 1@double;;gain needed = 100%@a hundred percent"
The remaining half has to double. That's a hundred percent gain. A percentage loss always needs a bigger percentage gain to recover, which is why small risk per trade matters.
