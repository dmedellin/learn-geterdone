@art budget
Forty thousand dollar account, one percent risk. How many shares?

@math items="$40,000 × 1% = $400@four hundred;;(52.00 − 50.00) + 0.10 = $2.10@two ten;;$400 ÷ $2.10 = 190.5 → 190@one ninety"
The budget is four hundred dollars. Entry fifty two, stop fifty, plus a ten cent slippage reserve, is two ten a share. Four hundred over two ten is one ninety point five.

@words text="Always round down"
Round down, to one ninety. Rounding up would put you over budget.
