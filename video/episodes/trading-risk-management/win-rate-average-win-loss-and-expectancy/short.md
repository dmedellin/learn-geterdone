@art two-piles
This strategy loses six trades out of ten. It still makes money.

@math items="0.40 × 2R = 0.80R@point four times two;;0.60 × 1R = 0.60R@point six times one;;expectancy = +0.20R@point two R"
It wins forty percent of the time, at plus two R, and loses sixty percent, at minus one R. Point four times two is point eight. Point six times one is point six. That leaves plus point two R per trade, before costs.
