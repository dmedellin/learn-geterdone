---

@art fan
Your best setup just lost. Was it a bad setup?

@words text="Losses happen inside a valid process"
Not necessarily. A setup is a probability, not a promise. One loss is a single draw, and says almost nothing about whether the setup was valid.

@art control-split
You can't control the next move. You can control position size, your exit, and whether you trade at all. So size every loss to be survivable.
