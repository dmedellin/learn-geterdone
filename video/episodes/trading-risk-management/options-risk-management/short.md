@art short-put
You sell a put and collect a hundred and twenty dollars. Is that your risk?

@math items="(50 − 1.20) × 100 = $4,880@fifty minus one twenty"
No, that's your maximum profit. A fifty strike put obliges you to buy a hundred shares at fifty if you're assigned. If the stock goes to zero, fifty minus one twenty, times a hundred, is a four thousand eight hundred and eighty dollar loss.
