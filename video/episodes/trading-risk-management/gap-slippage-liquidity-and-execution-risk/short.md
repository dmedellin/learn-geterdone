@art gap-through
Your stop is at forty dollars. You got filled at thirty nine seventy.

@math items="40.00 − 39.70 = 0.30@thirty cents;;0.30 × 500 = $150@a hundred and fifty"
That's thirty cents a share of slippage. On five hundred shares, a hundred and fifty dollars more than you planned to lose.

@words text="A stop is a trigger, not a price"
A stop price is a trigger, not a guaranteed fill. So build a slippage reserve into your sizing.
