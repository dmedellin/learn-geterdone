@art budget-bar
Daily limit, one thousand dollars. Does a three hundred and fifty dollar trade fit?

@math items="$1,000 − $300 − $400 = $300@minus three hundred;;$350 > $300@three hundred and fifty"
Not if you've already lost three hundred today and have four hundred of open risk. One thousand minus three hundred minus four hundred leaves three hundred. The new trade needs three hundred and fifty.

@words text="Shrink it to $300, or pass"
So reduce its size until the planned loss is three hundred or less, or pass.
