---

@art daily-gate
You've hit your daily loss limit. The next setup looks perfect. One more?

@words text="Stop adding risk"
No. That final trade is exactly the one the limit was written to refuse. It's a decision made while losing, which is when it can't be trusted.

@art winner-reset
And a winner later doesn't reset it. If luck can reset a limit, it isn't a limit.
