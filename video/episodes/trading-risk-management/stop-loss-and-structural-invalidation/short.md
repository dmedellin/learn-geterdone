@art wide-small
The right stop is further away than you'd like. Do you tighten it?

@words text="Change the size, not the stop"
No. The stop belongs where the trade idea becomes wrong, beyond the protected low. That's a fact of the chart.

@math items="$500 ÷ $5.45 = 91.7 → 91 shares@ninety one"
What changes is size. With five hundred dollars of risk and a five forty five stop distance, that's ninety one point seven, rounded down to ninety one shares.
