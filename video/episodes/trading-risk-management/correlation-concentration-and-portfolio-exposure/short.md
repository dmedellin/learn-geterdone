@art correlated-lines
Three long tech stocks. Three separate risks? Probably not.

@math items="3 × $400 = $1,200 heat@twelve hundred;;gap: 3 × 2.5 × $400 = $3,000@three thousand"
Each risks four hundred to its stop, so portfolio heat is twelve hundred. Then a sector gap opens all three two and a half stop distances lower. Together, they lose three thousand.

@words text="Count drivers, not tickers"
Positions that share a driver can behave like one oversized trade.
