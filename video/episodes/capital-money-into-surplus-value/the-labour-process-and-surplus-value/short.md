@art two-magnitudes
Nobody cheats, every price is fair, and the capitalist still ends up ahead. How?

@math items="advanced: 20 + 4 + 3 = 27s@twenty seven;;yarn: 20 + 4 + 6 = 30s@thirty"
He pays three shillings for a day's labour-power, plus twenty four for cotton and wear: twenty seven. Twelve hours of spinning add six shillings, so the yarn is worth thirty.

@words text="Six hours pay the wage. Six more are surplus."
Half a day's labour keeps the worker for a day. The other half adds value that costs the buyer nothing extra.
