@art seesaw
Can a boss sack half his workers and keep the same surplus? Up to a point.

@math items="100 workers · 100% = 300s@a hundred workers;;50 workers · 200% = 300s · 18 h@fifty;;25 workers · 400% = a 30-hour day@twenty five"
A hundred workers at a hundred percent give three hundred shillings. Fifty can match it at two hundred percent, an eighteen hour day. Twenty five would need a thirty hour day.

@words text="A day is always less than 24 hours"
That's where the compensation stops.
