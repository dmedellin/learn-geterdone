title: Why a longer day doubles the surplus
---

@art day-line
Make a twelve hour day half as long again, and the surplus doubles. Here's why.

@math items="12 h: 6 / 6 = 100%@a hundred percent;;18 h: 12 / 6 = 200%@two hundred"
Six hours pay for the worker's labour-power. In twelve hours, six are surplus: a hundred percent. In eighteen, twelve are surplus: two hundred.

@words text="Only the surplus part grows"
The necessary part is fixed. Every hour you add falls on the surplus side. That's why the length of the day is fought over.
