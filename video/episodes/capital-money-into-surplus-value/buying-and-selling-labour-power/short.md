@art free-twice
When you take a job, Marx says, you don't sell your labour.

@words text="Labour-power: the capacity to work"
You sell your capacity to work, for a period. Its value is fixed before any work is done, by what it costs to keep you working.

@math items="4 + 1 + 1 = 6 h a day@six hours;;6 h = 3s@three shillings"
Food, clothes and furniture, spread over their lives, come to six hours a day. That's three shillings. How long you then work is settled afterwards.
