@art two-hours-off
The Ten Hours Act took two hours off a twelve hour day. Why did that cut the surplus by a third?

@math items="12 h: 6 / 6 = 100%@a hundred percent;;10 h: 4 / 6 = ⅔@two thirds"
With six hours necessary, twelve hours leave six of surplus, a hundred percent. Ten hours leave four, two thirds.

@words text="Every hour cut was a surplus hour"
The necessary part didn't move. Both hours removed were surplus labour. That's why the employers fought the law.
