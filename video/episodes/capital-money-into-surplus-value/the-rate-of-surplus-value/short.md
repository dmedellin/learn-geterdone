@art whole-capital
A firm makes ninety pounds on five hundred. Eighteen percent. Marx says the real figure is a hundred.

@math items="s / C = 90 / 500 = 18%@eighteen percent;;s / v = 90 / 90 = 100%@a hundred percent"
Ninety over five hundred is eighteen percent. But four hundred and ten of that only reappears. Divide by the ninety spent on wages, and you get a hundred percent.

@words text="Half the day for himself, half for the capitalist"
That's the rate of surplus value. It says how the working day is divided.
