@art last-hour-clock
In eighteen thirty six, an Oxford professor said a mill's whole profit is made in its last hour.

@words text="Cut one hour, profit destroyed?"
So cutting the day by an hour would wipe it out. Marx separated the wages from the cotton and machinery, and redid the sum.

@math items="11½ h: 5¾ / 5¾ = 100%@a hundred percent;;an hour off: 4¾ / 5¾ = 19/23@nineteen twenty thirds"
Half the day reproduces wages, half is surplus: a hundred percent. Cut an hour, and the rate is nineteen twenty thirds, about eighty three percent. Not zero.
