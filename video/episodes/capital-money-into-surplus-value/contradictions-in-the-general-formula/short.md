title: If every seller marks up 10%, who gains?

@art ring-of-traders
What if every seller in the economy marked up ten percent?

@math items="+£10 as seller@as a seller;;−£10 as buyer@as a buyer;;£300 before · £300 after@three hundred"
Each trader gains ten pounds as a seller, and loses ten pounds as a buyer. A ring that held three hundred pounds of value still holds three hundred.

@words text="Every seller is a buyer"
That's Marx's point. A mark-up everyone pockets is a mark-up everyone pays. Profit for the whole class can't come from selling dear.
