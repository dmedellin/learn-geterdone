@art two-circuits
Why would anyone buy cotton for a hundred pounds just to sell it again?

@words text="£100 → cotton → £100" sub="the same for the same"
If it came back as a hundred pounds, the whole trip would be pointless. Money at both ends differs only in amount.

@math items="£100 → cotton → £110@a hundred and ten;;ΔM = +£10@ten pounds"
So buying to sell only makes sense if more comes back: a hundred and ten. Marx calls the extra ten pounds surplus value.
