@art two-bounds
Your solver stops at a two percent gap. Is the plan probably optimal?

@words text="Nothing beats the plan by more than 2%"
That's all a two percent gap proves. Nothing beats the plan in hand by more than two percent. There's no probability in it.

@math items="node 5:  plan 21,  gap 5/2@twenty one;;answer: 23@twenty three"
In the lesson's example, the plan was twenty one with a gap of five halves. The answer turned out to be twenty three. The gap allowed it, and couldn't tell you.
