@art fraction-open
Make big M larger to be safe, and your relaxation opens a twentieth of a warehouse.

@math items="M = 30:  bound 240@thirty;;M = 600:  bound 70@six hundred"
On the lesson's two facilities the answer is two forty. With M at thirty, total demand, the bound is two forty too. With M at six hundred, the bound falls to seventy.

@words text="Size M from a reason"
Same answer, much weaker proof. Use the smallest M you can justify.
