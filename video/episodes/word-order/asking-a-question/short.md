@words text="She went → ?"
She went. How do you make it a question? You can't simply move went to the front.

@art verb-jumps
There's no helping verb in the sentence, so English adds one: do, in the past form.

@math items="she went  →  did she go?@did she go"
Did takes the tense, and went goes back to go. Did she go? The same do appears whenever there's no helping verb to move.
