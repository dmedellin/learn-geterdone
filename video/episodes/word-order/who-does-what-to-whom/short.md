@words text="The dog bit the man. · The man bit the dog."
Same words, opposite meaning. In English, the order tells you who did it.

@art pronoun-pairs
Only pronouns still show their job in the word itself. He and him. They and them.

@math items="he saw them@he saw them;;subject pronoun → its verb: 72 of 80@seventy-two times"
So he saw them can't mean the reverse. And in a passage from a novel, a subject pronoun is followed by its verb seventy-two times out of eighty.
