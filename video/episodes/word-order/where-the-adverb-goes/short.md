@words text="I drink always tea ✗"
I drink always tea. People understand it, but a speaker wouldn't say it.

@art middle-slot
Words like always and never have a favourite home: the middle of the verb phrase.

@math items="she always knew@she always knew;;she has always known@has always known;;she is always late@is always late"
Before the main verb: she always knew. After the first helping verb: she has always known. And after be: she is always late.
