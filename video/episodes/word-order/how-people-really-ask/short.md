@words text="89 of 256"
In a play, only about a third of the questions follow the textbook rule.

@math items="Gwendolen, will you marry me?@Gwendolen, will;;You don't mean to say Gwendolen refused you?@you don't mean;;Your guardian?@your guardian"
Some start with a name: Gwendolen, will you marry me? Some are statements with a question mark: you don't mean to say Gwendolen refused you? And some are just two words: your guardian?

@art repeated-back
They're not mistakes. They're how people talk. Keep the rule, and learn to hear the rest.
