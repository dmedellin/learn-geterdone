Dividing by a hundred thousand seconds instead of a real day? Your rate is low.

@math items="10⁵ / 86 400 = 125/108@the shortcut day;;day: +15.74%@fifteen point seven four;;rate: −13.6%@thirteen point six"
The shortcut day is a hundred and twenty five over a hundred and eight of a real one, fifteen point seven four percent longer. But the rate isn't fifteen point seven four percent low. It's thirteen point six percent low.

@words text="17/108 ≠ 17/125"
Same numerator, different denominators. A reciprocal doesn't keep a percentage.
