@words text="0 = 0"
You eliminated, and got zero equals zero. That doesn't mean x is zero.

@math items="2x − 6y = 4@two x minus six y;;−2x + 6y = −4@minus two x;;0 = 0@Add them"
Two x minus six y equals four. Minus two x plus six y equals minus four. Add them, and both variables vanish.

@art same-line-dots
A true statement with no variable in it means both equations are one line. Infinitely many solutions, like two, zero and five, one. And zero, zero isn't even one of them.
