@words text="[ 6  −7 ; −2  4 ]" sub="not the inverse"
This looks like the inverse of four, seven, two, six. It's ten times too big.

@math items="ad − bc = 24 − 14 = 10@twenty four minus fourteen;;A⁻¹ = (1/10) · [ 6  −7 ; −2  4 ]@divide every entry"
Swapping the diagonal and negating the other two is only half the formula. The determinant is twenty four minus fourteen, ten, so you divide every entry by ten.

@art swap-negate
Skip that, and the product with A comes out as ten I instead of I. Every solution you compute is then ten times too large.
