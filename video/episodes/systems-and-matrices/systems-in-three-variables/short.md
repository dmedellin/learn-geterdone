@art same-variable-wrong
Eliminate x from one pair, then y from another, and you've made no progress at all.

@math items="E2 − 2E1:   −3y + z = −3@E two minus two E one;;2E2 + E3:   3x + 10z = 33@two E two plus E three"
From the same system, E two minus two E one gives minus three y plus z equals minus three. Two E two plus E three gives three x plus ten z equals thirty three. Both are true.

@art same-variable
But between them they still hold x, y and z. Both eliminations have to remove the same variable.
