@words text="9 = 9" sub="true, and useless"
You substituted, and got nine equals nine. That proves absolutely nothing.

@math items="3x + y = 9  →  y = 9 − 3x@y equals nine minus three x;;3x + (9 − 3x) = 9  →  9 = 9@back into"
From three x plus y equals nine, you got y equals nine minus three x. Put that back into the same equation, and of course it fits. It was built from it.

@art spent-equation
So the expression always goes into the other equation, the one you haven't used yet. That's where the information is.
