@words text="AB ≠ BA" sub="usually"
Swap the order of two matrices, and the product usually changes.

@math items="AB = [ 2  1 ]  [ 4  3 ]@A B has rows;;BA = [ 3  4 ]  [ 1  2 ]@B A has rows"
Let A be one, two, three, four, and B be zero, one, one, zero. A B has rows two, one, and four, three. B A has rows three, four, and one, two.

@art swapper
On the right, B swaps the columns of A. On the left, it swaps the rows. Same two matrices, two different products, so the order is part of the question.
