title: Rows yes, columns never

@art row-swap
You can swap two rows of a matrix freely. Swap two columns, and your answer is quietly wrong.

@words text="rows are equations" sub="reordering them changes nothing"
A row is an equation. Writing equations in a different order changes nothing about what's being asked.

@art column-swap
But a column is a variable. Swap two columns and you've renamed two unknowns, so the numbers come out attached to the wrong letters, with no record of it anywhere on the page.
