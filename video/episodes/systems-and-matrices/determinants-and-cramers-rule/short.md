Two systems, the same determinant, and completely different answers.

@math items="x + y = 1,    2x + 2y = 5@equals five;;x + y = 1,    2x + 2y = 2@to a two"
x plus y equals one, with two x plus two y equals five, has no solution. Change the five to a two, and it's one line written twice, with infinitely many. Both have D equal to zero.

@words text="D = 0" sub="not unique, and that's all it says"
So D equals zero rules out exactly one solution, and says nothing else. To find out which case you have, you need elimination.
