@art fake-corner
Two boundary lines crossing isn't always a corner. And here, the fake one wins.

@math items="(2, 6):    P = 34@thirty four;;(8, 0):    P = 40,   but 2x + y = 16 > 10@gives forty"
Maximising five x plus four y, the real best corner is two, six, with P equal to thirty four. The crossing at eight, zero gives forty, but there two x plus y is sixteen, breaking the limit of ten.

@words text="Test every crossing" sub="against every constraint"
So test every crossing against every constraint before you compare values. Otherwise the winner is a point that isn't allowed.
