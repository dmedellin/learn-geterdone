title: The fishing harvest that flips to collapse

@art collapse
Raise a harvest a little, and a healthy population can vanish completely.

@math items="H = 3/4:  stable at 3@three quarters;;H = 1:  one fragile level at 2@one;;H = 5/4:  nothing, every start falls@five quarters"
For a logistic population with capacity four, a harvest of three quarters leaves a stable level at three. A harvest of one leaves a single fragile level at two. At five quarters there's nothing left to settle on.

@words text="threshold H = r·K/4"
The threshold is the peak of the natural growth.
