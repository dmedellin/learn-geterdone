@art tank
Salt water pours into a tank of fresh water. Does the tank ever get as salty?

@math items="y′ = −(1/20)·(y − 200)@minus one twentieth;;gap = 200·e^(−t/20)@two hundred times"
Here, y prime equals minus one twentieth times y minus two hundred. So the gap to two hundred grams is two hundred times e to the minus t over twenty.

@words text="Always positive, never zero"
That gap shrinks fast, halving about every fourteen minutes. But it's never zero, so the tank gets as close as you like and never arrives.
