title: Where does a logistic curve grow fastest?
---

@art s-curve
A logistic population grows fastest at the halfway point, not near the top.

@math items="f(y) = y·(1 − y/4)@y times;;f(2) = 1,  f(3) = 3/4,  f(4) = 0@At two"
Take y prime equals y times one minus y over four. At two, the rate is one. At three, three quarters. At four, the capacity, it's zero.

@words text="steepest at K/2"
So the curve is steepest at half the capacity, and flattest at the capacity itself.
