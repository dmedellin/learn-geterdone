title: Coffee doesn't cool at a steady rate

@art hot-cup
Does hot coffee lose the same number of degrees every minute? No.

@math items="−20,  −15,  −45/4,  −135/16@minus twenty"
In a twenty degree room, coffee at a hundred cools at minus twenty degrees a minute, then minus fifteen, then minus forty five quarters, then minus a hundred thirty five sixteenths.

@words text="each rate is 3/4 of the last"
Each rate is three quarters of the one before, because the rate follows the gap to the room, and the gap keeps shrinking.
