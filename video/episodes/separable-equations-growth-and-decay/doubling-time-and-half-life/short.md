title: Does a bigger amount take longer to double?

@art scaled-curves
Does a bigger amount take longer to double? No. Not even a little.

@math items="y(t + T) = y₀·e^(kt)·e^(kT) = 2·y(t)@t plus T;;T = ln 2/k@ln two over k"
At time t plus T, the amount is y of t times e to the k T. That factor is two when T is ln two over k. The start never appears.

@words text="start 1 or 5, k = 1/2:  T ≈ 1.38629"
So with k equals one half, start at one or start at five, and the doubling time is the same, about one point three eight six.
