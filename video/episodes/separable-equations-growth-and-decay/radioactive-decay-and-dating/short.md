title: After two half-lives, how much is left?
---

@art halving-squares
After two half-lives, is the sample gone? It isn't even close.

@math items="1  →  1/2@a half is left;;1/2  →  1/4@a quarter;;1/4  →  1/8@an eighth"
A half life halves what's there. After one, a half is left. After two, a quarter. After three, an eighth.

@words text="A quarter left = two half-lives"
So it never runs out in a fixed number of half lives. And it runs the other way too. A sample with a quarter left has been through two.
