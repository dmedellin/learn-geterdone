@art two-branches
y squared equals t squared plus four. Is y the positive square root? Not always.

@math items="y = ±√(t² + 4)@plus or minus;;y(0) = −2  →  y = −√(t² + 4)@minus two"
The relation gives y equals plus or minus the square root of t squared plus four. Start at y of zero equals minus two, and only the negative root passes through it.

@words text="The relation can't tell the sign" sub="the initial value can"
The square root recovers the size of y, not its sign. The starting value is what picks the branch.
