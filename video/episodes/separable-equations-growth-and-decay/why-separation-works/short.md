Separating variables isn't a trick with fractions. It's the chain rule.

@math items="d/dt (y²/2) = y·y′@y squared over two"
Differentiate y squared over two with respect to t. Because y depends on t, the chain rule gives y times y prime.

@words text="y·y′ = t  →  y²/2 = t²/2 + C"
So when an equation says y times y prime equals t, both sides are derivatives. Integrate each one, and you get y squared over two equals t squared over two, plus C.
