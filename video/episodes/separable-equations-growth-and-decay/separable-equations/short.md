@art two-sides
Separating variables? The y prime has to stay with the y.

@math items="y′ = t/y@t over y;;y·y′ = t@times y prime"
Take y prime equals t over y. Multiply both sides by y, and you get y times y prime equals t. Nothing is left behind.

@math items="y²/2 = t²/2 + C@integrate;;y(0) = 2  →  y² = t² + 4@starting at"
Now integrate each side: y squared over two equals t squared over two, plus C. Starting at y of zero equals two, that's y squared equals t squared plus four.
