Euler's method on exponential growth is just a geometric sequence in disguise.

@math items="yₙ₊₁ = (1 + kh)·yₙ@one plus k h"
For y prime equals k y, each step adds h times k times the value. So every step multiplies by the same factor, one plus k h.

@math items="y′ = y,  h = 1/4:  factor 5/4@five quarters;;(5/4)⁴ = 625/256 ≈ 2.44141@after four steps"
With y prime equals y and a step of a quarter, that's five quarters. After four steps, six hundred twenty five over two hundred fifty six, just below e.
