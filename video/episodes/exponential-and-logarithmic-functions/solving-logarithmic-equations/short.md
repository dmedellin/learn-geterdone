@art two-negatives
Every step of your algebra is right, and one answer is still wrong.

@math items="log₂(x) + log₂(x − 2) = 3@log base two of x;;x(x − 2) = 8@Condense;;x = 4    or    x = −2@four or minus two"
Solve log base two of x, plus log base two of x minus two, equals three. Condense to x times x minus two equals eight, and you get four or minus two.

@words text="x = −2:    log₂(−2) is not defined" sub="check in the original"
At minus two the product is eight, so the condensed form is happy. But log base two of minus two doesn't exist. Always check in the original equation.
