title: Log of a sum: the law that doesn't exist
---

@art broken-sum
Is the log of eight plus eight the same as log eight plus log eight?

@math items="log₂(8 + 8) = log₂(16) = 4@sixteen;;log₂(8) + log₂(8) = 3 + 3 = 6@three plus three"
In base two, eight plus eight is sixteen, and the log of sixteen is four. But log eight is three, and three plus three is six.

@words text="No law for a sum" sub="the real one:   log(M·N) = log M + log N"
The real law is for a product: the log of M times N is log M plus log N. For a sum inside the log, there's no law at all.
