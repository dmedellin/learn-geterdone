@art mirror-pair
Why does a logarithm refuse negative numbers? The answer is a mirror.

@art point-swap
The log curve is the exponential reflected in the line y equals x, so every point's coordinates swap. Two cubed is eight puts three, eight on one curve, and eight, three on the other.

@words text="bˣ returns only positives  →  log_b accepts only positives"
b to the x only ever returns positive numbers. Swap the roles, and the logarithm only ever accepts them.
