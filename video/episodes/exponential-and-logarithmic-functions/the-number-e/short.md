@art climbing-steps
Compound a dollar every second for a year. It still won't reach three.

@math items="once a year:   2@once a year;;quarterly:   2.4414…@four times;;daily:   ≈ 2.714567@daily;;every second:   ≈ 2.71828179@every second"
At a hundred percent paid once a year, a dollar becomes two. Paid four times, about two point four four. Paid daily, about two point seven one four six. And paid every second, about two point seven one eight two eight.

@words text="e ≈ 2.71828…" sub="the ceiling"
Each extra split buys less than the one before. The ceiling they all creep toward is the number e.
