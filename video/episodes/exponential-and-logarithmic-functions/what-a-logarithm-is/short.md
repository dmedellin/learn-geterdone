title: Log base two of eight is three. Flip it?

Log base two of eight is three. So what's log base eight of two?

@math items="2³ = 8    →    log₂(8) = 3@Two cubed;;8^(1/3) = 2    →    log₈(2) = 1/3@the cube root"
Two cubed is eight, so log base two of eight is three. But eight to the one third, the cube root of eight, is two. So log base eight of two is one third.

@words text="Reciprocals, not equals"
Swap the base and the argument and you get the reciprocal. Say the base out loud: it's the number being raised to a power.
