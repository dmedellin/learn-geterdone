title: Daily versus continuous compounding: about six cents

@art frequency-ceiling
Daily compounding against continuous, over ten years. The difference is about six cents.

@math items="daily:    1648.66@Daily;;continuous:    1648.72@Continuously"
Take a thousand dollars at five percent for ten years. Daily gives sixteen forty eight, sixty six. Continuously gives sixteen forty eight, seventy two.

@words text="Continuous is a ceiling, not a speed"
Compounding more often climbs, then stops climbing. Continuous compounding is the ceiling it climbs toward.
