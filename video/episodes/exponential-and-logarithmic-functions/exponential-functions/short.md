@art add-vs-multiply
Two times x and two to the x agree twice, then part for good.

@math items="x = 1:   2x = 2,   2ˣ = 2@At one;;x = 2:   2x = 4,   2ˣ = 4@At two;;x = 5:   2x = 10,   2ˣ = 32@at five"
At one, both give two. At two, both give four. That's why the mix up survives. But at five, two x is ten, and two to the x is thirty two.

@words text="A line adds. An exponential multiplies."
Two x adds two at every step. Two to the x doubles at every step. That's the whole difference.
