@art missing-key
Your calculator has no log base two key. You don't need one.

@math items="log₂(10) = ln 10 / ln 2@ln ten over ln two;;= 2.302585 / 0.693147 ≈ 3.321928@about three point three two"
Log base two of ten is ln ten over ln two. That's about three point three two, and it sits between three and four, as it should, since two cubed is eight and two to the fourth is sixteen.

@words text="Argument on top, base underneath"
Flip it and you get the reciprocal, about point three. So put the argument on top, and the base underneath.
