title: ln of e squared is two. log of e squared isn't.

@art crossed-pair
Ln of e squared is two. So why isn't log of e squared?

@math items="ln(e²) = logₑ(e²) = 2@Ln of e squared is log base e;;log(e²) = log₁₀(e²) = 2·log e@log of e squared is log base ten"
Write out the hidden bases. Ln of e squared is log base e of e squared, which is two. But log of e squared is log base ten of e squared, which is two log e, not two.

@words text="Match the base before you cancel"
A power and a logarithm only cancel when they share a base.
