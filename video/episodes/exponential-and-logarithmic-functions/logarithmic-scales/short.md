@art sound-waves
Two sixty decibel machines, running together. Is that a hundred and twenty decibels?

@math items="10·log(2I / I₀) = 60 + 10·log 2@sixty plus ten log two;;≈ 63.01 dB@sixty three"
The intensities add, so you have twice the intensity. The level is sixty plus ten log two, which is about sixty three decibels.

@words text="Adding readings multiplies the quantities"
A hundred and twenty would mean multiplying the intensities. So add the intensities first, then take the log once.
