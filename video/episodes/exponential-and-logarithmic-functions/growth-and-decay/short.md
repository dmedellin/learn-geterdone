title: Twenty percent off, twice, is not forty percent off

@art shrinking-losses
Take twenty percent off five hundred, twice. Why isn't the answer three hundred?

@math items="500 × 4/5 = 400@The first cut;;400 × 4/5 = 320@The second"
The first cut takes a hundred, leaving four hundred. The second takes twenty percent of four hundred, which is only eighty, leaving three hundred and twenty.

@words text="Down 20% means multiply by 4/5"
Repeated percentage change is multiplication, and it always multiplies the current value, not the original one.
