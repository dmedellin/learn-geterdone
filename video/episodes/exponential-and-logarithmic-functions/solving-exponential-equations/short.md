@art rejected-root
This equation factors into two answers. One of them can't happen.

@math items="e^(2x) − eˣ − 6 = 0@e to the two x;;(u − 3)(u + 2) = 0,    u = eˣ@Let u be;;u = 3    or    u = −2@three or minus two"
Take e to the two x, minus e to the x, minus six, equals zero. Let u be e to the x, and it factors as u minus three, times u plus two. So u is three or minus two.

@words text="eˣ > 0,  so  x = ln 3" sub="u = −2 is impossible"
But e to the x is positive for every x, so minus two never happens. The only solution is x equals ln three.
