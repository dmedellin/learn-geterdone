@art life-cycle
Software grows in a cycle. You prototype, you expand, you consolidate, and then you do it again.

@quote text="Design patterns thus provide targets for your refactorings." cite="Design Patterns, Chapter 6"
And that's where the Gang of Four put their catalog. Design patterns thus provide targets for your refactorings.

@art white-to-black
Use one early, and you may spare a refactoring. Find one late, and it shows you what to refactor toward.
