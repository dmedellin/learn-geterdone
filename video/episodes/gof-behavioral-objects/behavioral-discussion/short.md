@art distributed-vs-central
Observer or Mediator? The Gang of Four compared their own patterns.

@words text="Observer: easier to reuse"
Observer spreads the communication out. Its small classes, the authors found, are easier to reuse.

@words text="Mediator: easier to follow"
Mediator puts it all in one object. That makes the flow easier to follow, because observer connections are hard to see later.

@end text="Full lesson on Learn"
Neither wins outright, and the language you write in tips the balance. The full lesson is on Learn.
