title: The likeliest key shouldn't always be the root

@art likely-key-root
Put your most searched key at the root of the tree. It can be the wrong call.

@math items="k₄ at the root: 100/40@a hundred fortieths;;k₂ at the root: 95/40@ninety five"
In the lesson's four key example, key four is the likeliest. The best tree with it at the root costs a hundred fortieths. With key two at the root, it's ninety five.

@art push-down
A root at one end pushes every other key, and every miss, one level down.
