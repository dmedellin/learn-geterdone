@words text="7¹²⁸ mod 13"
Seven to the hundred and twenty eight, mod thirteen, takes just seven squarings.

@math items="7 → 10 → 9 → 3 → 9 → 3 → 9 → 3@seven squared"
Just square, and reduce each time. Seven squared is ten, mod thirteen. Then nine, three, nine, three, nine, and three.

@art bounded-box
A hundred and twenty eight is two to the seventh, so seven squarings give the answer, three. No number ever got above a hundred and forty four.
