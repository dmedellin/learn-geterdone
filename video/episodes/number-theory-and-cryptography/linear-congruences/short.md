@words text="6x ≡ 9 (mod 15)"
Six x is nine, mod fifteen. How many answers? Three.

@math items="gcd(6, 15) = 3,  3 ∣ 9@the gcd;;x ≡ 4, 9, 14 (mod 15)@four, nine"
The gcd of six and fifteen is three, and three divides nine. So there are exactly three solutions: four, nine and fourteen.

@art spaced-solutions
They're spaced five apart, fifteen over three. Change the nine to a seven, and three no longer divides it, so there are none at all.
