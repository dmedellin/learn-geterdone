@words text="gcd × lcm = ?"
The gcd times the lcm of two numbers is always their product.

@math items="gcd(48, 180) = 12@the gcd of;;lcm(48, 180) = 720@the lcm is;;12 · 720 = 8640 = 48 · 180@twelve times"
Take forty eight and one hundred and eighty. The gcd of the two is twelve, and the lcm is seven hundred and twenty. Twelve times seven hundred and twenty is eight thousand six hundred and forty, which is forty eight times a hundred and eighty.

@art min-max-bars
At each prime, the smaller exponent plus the larger one is both exponents. So the gcd times the lcm is the product.
