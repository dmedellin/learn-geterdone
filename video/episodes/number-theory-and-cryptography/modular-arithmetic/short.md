@words text="2 · 3 ≡ 2 · 8 (mod 10)"
Two times three equals two times eight. At least, it does mod ten.

@math items="6 ≡ 16  (mod 10)@six is congruent;;but  3 ≢ 8@but three"
Six is congruent to sixteen, mod ten. But three is not congruent to eight. You can't cancel the two.

@art collide
You may cancel a factor only when it's coprime to the modulus. Here the gcd of two and ten is two.
