title: To find primes up to 100, you only need four of them

@art number-grid
To find every prime below a hundred, you only need four passes.

@words text="2, 3, 5, 7" sub="√100 = 10"
Cross out the multiples of two, three, five and seven. The square root of a hundred is ten, so no larger prime is needed.

@art stop-early
Any composite below a hundred has a prime factor at most ten. So whatever survives those four passes is prime. Twenty five of them.
