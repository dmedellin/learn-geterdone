@words text="Why isn't 1 prime?"
Why isn't one a prime number? It's not because it's too small.

@math items="6 = 2 · 3@six would be two;;6 = 1 · 2 · 3@one times two;;6 = 1 · 1 · 2 · 3@one times one"
If one were prime, six would be two times three, and one times two times three, and one times one times two times three, forever.

@art prime-atoms
Every number above one factors into primes in exactly one way. Leaving one out is the decision that keeps that true.
