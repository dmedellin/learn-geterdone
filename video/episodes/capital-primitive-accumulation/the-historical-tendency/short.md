@art merging-capitals
Ten firms, nothing grows, and the biggest one ends up with sixty percent.

@math items="10 × £100 = £1,000@ten capitals;;largest absorbs smallest, 5 times@five rounds;;£600 / £1,000 = 3/5@three fifths"
Start with ten capitals of a hundred pounds. Each period, the largest absorbs the smallest. After five rounds, the total is still a thousand pounds, but the largest holds six hundred, three fifths. Marx calls this centralisation, and it's different from growth. One capitalist always kills many.
