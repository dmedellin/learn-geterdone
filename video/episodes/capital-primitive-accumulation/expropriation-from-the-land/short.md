title: The same twelve hours, a new owner
---

@art same-day
A peasant works twelve hours on her own land. Then she loses it.

@math items="own land: 0 h for anyone else@zero;;hired: 12 − 6 = 6 h surplus@six are surplus"
On her own land, every hour is for her household: zero hours of surplus for anyone else. Hired for the same twelve hours, six pay her wage and six are surplus for her employer.

@words text="Same work. New owner of the surplus."
Nothing about the work changed. That, Marx says, is what expropriation did.
