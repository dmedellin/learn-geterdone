title: The market that appeared when nobody got richer

@art household-goods
A hundred families make everything they need. How big is their market? Zero.

@math items="self-supplied: 0s;;separated: 100 × 1s 6d = 150s a day@a hundred and fifty"
Take away their land, and each must buy its one and six of subsistence a day. Now the market is a hundred and fifty shillings a day.

@words text="Same goods, now commodities"
Nobody eats more. The same goods are simply bought instead of made.
