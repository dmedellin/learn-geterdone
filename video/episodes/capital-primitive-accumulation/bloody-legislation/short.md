@art wage-ceiling
For centuries, English law fixed the most you could be paid, and never the least.

@math items="at the value, 3s: 100%@a hundred percent;;statute, 2s: 200%@two hundred"
Take a day that creates six shillings, with labour-power worth three. Paid three, the rate is a hundred percent. Hold the wage at two by law, and it's two hundred.

@words text="A maximum, but on no account a minimum"
Taking a higher wage was punished more than paying one.
