@art bonds
A bond pays three pounds a year. Interest rates fall. Its owner gets fifty pounds richer.

@math items="£3 / 3% = £100@three percent;;£3 / 2% = £150@two percent"
At three percent, three pounds a year is priced at a hundred. At two percent, the same three pounds is priced at a hundred and fifty.

@words text="Barren money with the power of breeding"
Nothing was built. Marx says the national debt gave barren money the power of breeding, paid for out of taxes.
