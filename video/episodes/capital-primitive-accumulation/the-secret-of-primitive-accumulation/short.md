@art saving-jar
Could a frugal worker save his way to owning a factory?

@math items="3s a day, a tenth saved: 3.6d@three point six pence;;£3,000 / 3.6d = 200,000 days@two hundred thousand"
Earn three shillings a day and save a tenth, three point six pence. A factory's three thousand pound wage bill takes two hundred thousand working days. That's over six centuries.

@words text="Not saving, but separation"
Marx's answer: the first capitals came from separating producers from their land and tools.
