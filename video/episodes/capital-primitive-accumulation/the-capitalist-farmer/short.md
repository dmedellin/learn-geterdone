title: How inflation made the farmer rich
---

@art long-lease
A farmer's rent is fixed by a ninety nine year lease. Then prices double.

@math items="rent £20, prices ×2 → worth £10@worth ten;;farmer's real gain: £10 from rent + £10 from wages@twenty pounds"
His twenty pound rent is now worth ten. And if wages rise by only half, he gains ten more from his labourers. Twenty pounds of real gain, without doing anything.

@words text="Golden fruit"
Marx says the farmer grew rich at the expense of both his labourers and his landlord.
