He brought fifty thousand pounds and three thousand workers, and nobody made his bed.

@words text="Capital is a social relation"
In eighteen twenty nine, Thomas Peel took money, tools and people to the Swan River. The land was free, so his workers went off to farm it. His fortune employed no one. Marx's point is that capital is not a thing, but a social relation between persons. Tools command labour only where workers have no other way to live.
