title: At 80% busy, why is anyone waiting?

@art bunching-arrivals
Your server is idle a fifth of the time. So why is work waiting?

@math items="clockwork: L = 4/5@clockwork;;random: L = 12/5@random"
With clockwork arrivals and clockwork service at four fifths utilisation, the mean number in the system is four fifths, and nothing ever queues. Make both random at the same utilisation, and it's twelve fifths. Three times as many.

@words text="Same idle time, wrong moments"
Random arrivals bunch, and the idle time lands where nobody needs it.
