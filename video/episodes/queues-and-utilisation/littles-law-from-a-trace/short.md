@art strips-and-bars
Little's Law isn't a queueing model. It's counting the same area twice.

@math items="area under N(t) = 17@seventeen;;sum of stays = 17@add up;;L = λW = 17/10@seventeen tenths"
Five customers over ten slots. The area under the count of who's inside is seventeen. Add up how long each one stayed, and you get seventeen too. Divide by the window, and L equals lambda W, seventeen tenths.

@words text="Only rule: everyone has left by T"
The only condition is a closed window.
