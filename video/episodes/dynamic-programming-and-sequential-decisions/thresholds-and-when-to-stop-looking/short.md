title: Should you get pickier as the deadline nears?
---

@art falling-thresholds
As the deadline gets closer, you should get less picky, not more.

@math items="3 left: take 30 only@three left;;2 left: take 20 or 30@two left;;1 left: take anything@one left"
Offers of ten, twenty or thirty. With three left, take only thirty. With two left, twenty or thirty. With one left, anything.

@words text="Compare with what waiting is worth"
You compare each offer with the chances still to come, and there are fewer of those every single period.
