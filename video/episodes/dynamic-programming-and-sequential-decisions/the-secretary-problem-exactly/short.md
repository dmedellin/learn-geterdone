@art look-then-leap
Throw away the first candidate unseen, and your odds go up.

@math items="take the first:  1/4@take the first;;reject one, take the next record:  11/24@reject one"
With four candidates in a random order, and only the best counting, take the first and you win a quarter of the time. Reject one, then take the next record, and you win eleven times in twenty four.

@art all-orderings
Count all twenty four orderings by hand, and it checks out exactly.
