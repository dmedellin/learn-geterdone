@art policy-flip
Same state, same options, and the best move changes with time left.

@words text="small: harvest, grow, grow, grow" sub="1, 2, 3, 4 periods left"
In the small state, with one period left, harvesting pays three right now, so you take it.

@art action-split
With two or more left, growing pays nothing now, but moves you to the large state three times in four. And that's worth more.
