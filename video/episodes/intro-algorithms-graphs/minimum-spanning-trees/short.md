@art cut-and-light-edge
The lightest edge across a cut is safe. Is it in every minimum tree?

@math items="2 minimum trees, weight 12@two minimum spanning;;light edge 1–2: in only 1 of them@only one"
No. The lesson's graph has two minimum spanning trees, both of weight twelve, and its light edge one two is in only one of them.

@words text="Safe = in some minimum tree"
Its tied rival, two three, is in the other. Safe means some minimum tree holds it.
