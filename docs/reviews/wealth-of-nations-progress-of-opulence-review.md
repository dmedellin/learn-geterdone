## Fixes

**Accuracy**

1. L1: "Smith himself says Italy, cultivated through foreign commerce, was the
   exception" did not say what Italy was an exception to. It now gives his
   claim: Italy is "the only great country of Europe" improved in every part by
   foreign commerce and manufactures for distant sale (l. 13532–13534). That is
   the inverted order succeeding, and the quiz `why` (Q4) already says the same.
2. L2: "later no inclination and &ldquo;almost always the requisite
   abilities&rdquo;" said the reverse of the text. The text: when law and order
   gave him leisure, "he often wanted the inclination, and almost always the
   requisite abilities" (l. 12487–12488). Corrected.
3. L2 concept: "the landlord, who lays out nothing" contradicted the body, where
   the proprietor supplies seed, cattle and instruments. It now reads "lays out
   nothing on an improvement", which is Smith's sense (l. 12600ff.).
4. L3: "the silk of Lucca, which in 1310 drove nine hundred families into exile"
   made the silk trade the cause. In the text, the families were driven out by
   Castruccio Castracani's tyranny, and thirty-one of them took the manufacture to
   Venice (l. 13059–13065). Corrected.
5. L3 mistakes: "a bargain whose terms he had every reason to think fair" is not
   in the text. Smith calls the grant extraordinary and explains it by the
   alliance against the lords. The mistake now says that.

**Labs and worked examples**

6. L3 worked: a fixed 100 against dues of 320 (31.25 per cent) was called "a
   third of the dues". Corrected to "less than a third".

**Quiz**

7. L2: three of four correct answers sat at position 1. Q2 now has its answer at
   3 and Q3 at 2; the `why` texts do not refer to positions. Course positions now
   run 3, 0, 2, 1 / 1, 3, 2, 0 / 3, 1, 2, 3 / 2, 1, 3, 0.
8. I tried to argue for every wrong answer in all sixteen questions. None held,
   and every `c` agrees with its `why`.

**Prose**

9. L1: "Foreign commerce, by this account, matters less than it seems whose
   capital carries it" now reads "On this account it matters less than it seems
   whose capital carries a country's foreign commerce."
10. L2: *coloni partiarii* italicised as a Latin term.
11. L3: "He gives two reasons" announced two reasons and then gave three (the
    alliance, the lords' contempt, the removal of suspicion). It now reads "He
    gives the reasons".
12. L4 step 3: "a share of 1/100 is one who lives almost entirely on someone else"
    now reads "on his other customers". L4 note: the closing clause ("where Smith
    traces the loss of the lords' power") is recast as a plain contrast with
    Capital's reading.
