Carry your marks between devices

Your marks are written to your own OneDrive, in this application's private folder, as one small JSON file. They are not stored on this server. Syncing merges both sides, so a lesson ticked on either device stays ticked.

What signing in does not do

It does not unlock anything. Every lesson, every lab and every word of the material is readable without an account, and always will be. Signing in only moves your own ticks between your own devices.

Clearing your browser data clears local marks. If you have synced, they are still in your OneDrive.
