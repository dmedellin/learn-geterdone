About the Trading courses: educational use only. Nothing in that material is trading, investment, or financial advice. Every chart, price, quote, trade, fill, and performance figure in those courses is a synthetic example generated to illustrate a concept: no lesson describes a real instrument or a real market session, and nothing there is a recommendation, a signal, or a forecast. Trading carries the risk of losing money, and real outcomes can differ from any illustration because of spread, slippage, commissions, gaps, taxes, liquidity, assignment, exercise, implied volatility, time decay, and other factors. Options in particular involve risk and are not suitable for every investor.

The one exception, and it is dated. A single worked example published under Trading is not course material and is not synthetic: it analyses real market data for a real listed security over a fixed window that ended on August 14, 2026, and it is written as of August 16, 2026. It is a point-in-time snapshot, not a live or current view, and not a forecast. It is educational analysis, not personalized investment advice, not a trade signal, and not a guarantee of any outcome, and it carries a notice of its own saying exactly that.
